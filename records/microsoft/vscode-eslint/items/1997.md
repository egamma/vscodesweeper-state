## Summary

Bulk-suppression support is implemented on current main and shipped in the 3.0.25 pre-release and 3.0.34 stable release. The new settings let users apply eslint-suppressions.json while choosing whether suppressed violations remain visible and at what severity.

## Change summary

Add support for ESLint's eslint-suppressions.json bulk-suppression feature in editor diagnostics.

## Best solution

Use the shipped `eslint.bulkSuppression.enable`, `eslint.bulkSuppression.location`, and `eslint.bulkSuppression.severity` settings with ESLint 10.1 or newer; no further extension-side work remains for the requested capability.
