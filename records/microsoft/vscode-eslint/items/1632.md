[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-eslint/issues/1632](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-eslint/issues/1632)

## Summary

This is the same Windows long-path failure already tracked by the older, broader issue https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-eslint/issues/1229, which documents the extension failing to lint paths beyond the traditional limit even though the ESLint CLI succeeds.

## Change summary

Support linting and fix-on-save for files whose Windows paths exceed the traditional path-length limit.

## Best solution

Consolidate the investigation in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-eslint/issues/1229 and ensure the server's URI-to-file-path and ESLint invocation paths work with long Windows paths under the runtime used by the extension.
