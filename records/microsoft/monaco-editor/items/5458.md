- **Current upstream code:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/main/src/vs/platform/clipboard/browser/clipboardService.ts still creates currentWritePromise without observing its rejection, cancels the previous unsettled deferred on click/keydown, and catches only clipboard.write's returned promise.
- **Cancellation semantics:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/main/src/vs/base/common/async.ts shows DeferredPromise.cancel() calling error(new CancellationError()), which rejects p. The returned settlement promise is distinct from p.
- **Independent reproduction:** The supplied October 19 comment on https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5458 confirms the same path under happy-dom with Monaco 0.56.0. Observing currentWritePromise.p eliminated rejections in six CI runs, compared with failures in four of nine earlier runs; the commenter links https://github.com/Presight-AI/vantage-frontend/pull/8023. This is a downstream patch, not an upstream fix.
