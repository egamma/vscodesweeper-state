[https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/4580](https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/4580)

## Summary

Verified duplicate of the open content-widget positioning issue: transformed or positioned ancestors cause Monaco's coordinate calculations to place hover and suggestion widgets incorrectly.

## Change summary

Marker hover tooltips choose the wrong vertical position when the editor has a transformed or absolutely positioned ancestor.

## Best solution

Consolidate widget positioning under transformed or positioned ancestors on https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/2793. Preserve this report's useful detail that the non-fixed marker hover chooses the wrong side and that `position: absolute` also reproduces it.
