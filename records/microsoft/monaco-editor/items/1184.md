[https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/1184](https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/1184)

Keep open: continued single-line comments are now supported, but the broader request for C/C++ line splicing everywhere remains uncovered. The existing grammar labels are appropriate, and the maintainer welcomed a contribution.

## Change summary
