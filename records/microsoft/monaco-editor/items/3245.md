[https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/3245](https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/3245)

## Summary

The quoted-delimiter failure is fixed by the C++ raw-string tokenizer change shipped in v0.48.0. Tracing the supplied example through current source shows that the closing delimiter now exits raw-string highlighting.

## Change summary

C++ raw strings with a quote in their delimiter incorrectly extend string highlighting beyond the closing delimiter.
