[https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/626](https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/626)

## Summary

Keep this focused touch-selection request open. Existing selection APIs do not supply native touch interaction, and the broader mobile-support discussion explicitly asks for individual reports of specific problems.
