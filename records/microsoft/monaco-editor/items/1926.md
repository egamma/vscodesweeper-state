- **Actionable report:** https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/1926 supplies two file-URI models, compiler options, the failing '@/foo' import, and the expected IntelliSense. - **Workaround does not establish resolution:** The discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/1926 distinguishes addExtraLib-based typing from cross-file definition navigation; the January 2024 follow-up reports that types disappear after switching files. The declaration-library example does not exercise the original alias.
- **Existing path-mapping surface:** src/languages/features/typescript/register.ts declares baseUrl and paths. tsWorker.ts passes compiler options unchanged to TypeScript, returns an empty current directory, and looks up models by URI string. The bundled typescriptServices.js functions getPathsBasePath and tryLoadModuleUsingPaths resolve substitutions against baseUrl; the supplied './' substitution contains no wildcard and the relative baseUrl does not identify the models' file-URI root. These are configuration leads, not proof of a general resolver defect or a landed fix.
- **Model synchronization boundary:** src/languages/features/typescript/workerManager.ts synchronizes all matching-language models when getEagerModelSync() is enabled; otherwise getLanguageServiceWorker synchronizes requested resources. Cross-file model availability must be checked separately from alias resolution.
