## Summary

The reported styling failure is plausible, but no deterministic reproduction or playground link establishes what removes or invalidates Monaco's theme stylesheet. A current-version repro is needed to distinguish an editor-core lifecycle defect from application DOM recovery behavior.

## Change summary

DiffEditor loses inherited theme CSS variables after renderer recovery or long uptime, and reapplying the theme does not restore its styling.
