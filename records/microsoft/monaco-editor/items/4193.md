## Evidence

- **Current source confirms the trigger:** webpack-plugin/src/index.ts: resolveDesiredLanguages concatenates customLanguages into the bundled language list. createLoaderRules passes every language entry to resolveMonacoPath, whose monacoEditorPath branch immediately calls require.resolve(path.join(monacoEditorPath, 'esm', filePath)). An external entry is consequently looked up beneath the installation's esm directory rather than at its own location; failure bypasses the fallback resolution branches.
