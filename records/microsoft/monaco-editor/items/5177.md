## Summary

Keep open: an upstream positioning fix addresses part of this report, but tooltip wrapping and blocked Find buttons remain independently reported. The supplied playground and layout steps suffice to retest against v0.58.0; current browser behavior was not verified.

## Change summary

Find widget tooltips misalign or wrap over their buttons in embedded layouts, flickering and preventing clicks.

## Evidence

- **Complete reproduction:** https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5177 provides a v0.55.1 playground, code, screenshots, and explicit steps: disable the minimap, add 50px body padding, then position the editor container relatively. Above-button tooltips wrap, overlap their anchors, flicker, and block clicks; scrollbar-size variations are also documented.
- **Partial upstream fix:** The merged https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/283599 replaces container-based positioning offsets with the measured context-view positioning origin and adjusts standalone tooltip fonts. Its diff still resets width to 'initial'; it does not establish resolution of the reported post-placement wrapping. Its associated https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5112 is closed and cannot serve as a duplicate canonical.
- **Remaining symptom independently confirmed:** https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5208#issuecomment-6949448670 reports the same Close-button flicker on Monaco 0.57.0 in Electron/Chromium: the tooltip shrinks from about 110px to 94px after placement, grows from 28px to 47px tall, and overlaps the button.
- **Canonical search and prior-review bar:** https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5208 is open but newer, has four upvotes versus this report's six, and has no milestone or open linked implementation PR. https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5516 is also newer, unscheduled, and narrower: its v0.57.0 playground isolates wrapping in a 400px positioned container. Neither qualifies as this re-reviewed item's duplicate canonical.
- **Current bundled source:** CHANGELOG.md identifies v0.58.0's core revision as c132f72ae9f5ff80093e7d722c36f03755865b20. At https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/c132f72ae9f5ff80093e7d722c36f03755865b20/src/vs/base/browser/ui/contextview/contextview.ts#L668-L698, ContextView.doLayout measures dimensions before positioning and still assigns width 'initial' afterward. This supports the remaining sizing concern but does not replace browser reproduction.

- **Application workarounds are not library fixes:** Merged https://github.com/SigNoz/signoz/pull/11826 offsets the Find widget with application CSS; merged https://github.com/otterscale/dashboard/pull/690 uses no-wrap styling, relative positioning, and overflow changes. Both reference this report and modify their applications rather than fix Monaco's core behavior.
