[https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5251](https://github.com/microsoft/monaco-editor/issues/5251)

## Summary

Treat this as a packaging enhancement for Shadow DOM embedding: the repository already documents document-level font registration, but its workaround does not provide the requested font-only stylesheet. Route to packaging and Shadow DOM ownership rather than close.

## Change summary

Provide a separate Codicon font-face stylesheet that web-component embedders can load in the document while keeping editor styles inside shadow roots.

## Best solution
