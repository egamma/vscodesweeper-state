## Summary

The URL-construction concerns remain plausible in current source, but the report lacks concrete product reproduction steps, which a maintainer explicitly requested. The reporter subsequently closed the proposed fix because they could no longer reproduce the network-drive behavior; request the missing trigger rather than treating that withdrawal as a fix.

## Change summary

Preserve absolute HTTP(S) destinations and ensure workspace-relative preview URLs have a slash between the port and pathname.
