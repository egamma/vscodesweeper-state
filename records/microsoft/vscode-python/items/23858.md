# Issue #23858 — Creating new venv, recommended tag

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-python/issues/23858](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-python/issues/23858)

## Summary

Keep open: the current picker can leave a newly recommended workspace venv outside the Recommended group until reopening. This team-authored, accepted issue has neither a verified open duplicate nor an issue-referencing fix.

## Change summary

A workspace venv created while the interpreter picker is open has inconsistent recommendation presentation and placement before and after reopening.

## Best solution

Make recommendation presentation consistent in SetInterpreterCommand's live-update and initial-list paths, while preserving the established protection against moving entries under the user's cursor during discovery.
