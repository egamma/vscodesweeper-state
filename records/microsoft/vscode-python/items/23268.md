The reported failure was caused by sorting extension manifests through an optional displayName field. Commit e5b47b96 added the required fallback, and the fix shipped in 2024.16.1.

## Change summary

Prevent Python: Report Issue from throwing when an installed extension omits displayName from its manifest.

Keep the shipped fallback that sorts extensions by ID when their manifest lacks a display name; no further change is needed.
