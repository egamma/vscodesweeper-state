In pytest/arguments.ts, TestFilter.removeTests obtains positional tokens through getPositionalArguments and removes them. common/argumentsHelper.ts treats unknown --test_option as valueless, so ['.', '--test_option', 'somevalue'] becomes ['--test_option']; the runner forwards the incomplete arguments to pytest.main.
- **Confirmed report and unique evidence:** The supplied discussion includes a reproduced failure and July 2023 rewrite execution logs showing --test_option without its value while discovery retains both tokens. https://github.com/philippun1/pytest-vscode-parameters supplies the reproduction; the reporter confirms equals-form arguments work and reports Ubuntu and Windows impact.
