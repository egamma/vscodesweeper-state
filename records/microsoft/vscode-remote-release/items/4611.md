## Summary

The documented behavior is for `code .` from WSL to open the folder in a WSL window, including paths under `/mnt/c`, but the original 2021 environment has not been retested. A 2025 workaround suggests command resolution or the Scoop installation may be involved, so current diagnostics are needed.
