# Issue #10370 — Port Forwarding Fails Due to 'PortsPerTunnel' Limit Despite No Active Forwards

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/10370](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/10370)

## Summary

Keep open: the PortsPerTunnel rejection is distinct from the announced connection-limit increase, and later reports describe recurring failures despite service-reinstallation workarounds. The supplied setup, steps, and quota-error log suffice for retesting; current-release resolution remains unverified.

## Change summary

Remote - Tunnels rejects new port forwards with a PortsPerTunnel quota error even when the Ports view shows no active forwards.

## Best solution

The tunnels owner should investigate whether stale server-side port registrations survive disconnections and ensure forwarding sessions reconcile them reliably. Restarting or reinstalling the tunnel service should not be necessary to restore forwarding.
