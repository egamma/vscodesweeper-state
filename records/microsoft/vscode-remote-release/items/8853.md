## Change summary

After reloading a workspace whose tunnel host went offline, the remote indicator remains green even though remote contents are unavailable.

## Best solution

The tunnels client should restore the workspace in an explicitly disconnected state after reload, with the remote indicator reflecting the lost connection and an appropriate reconnect or error path.
