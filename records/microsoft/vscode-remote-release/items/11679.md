[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11679](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11679)

## Summary

Keep open: the September resource-loading regression has a public fix, but the thread explicitly requests rejection diagnostics that remain absent. The supplied steps and network capture suffice to retest loading on current releases; September's stable-release failures do not establish current reproduction.

## Change summary

Remote extension icons, fonts, and preview resources fail to load through vscode-managed-remote-resource, with rejection reasons missing from logs.

## Best solution

A maintainer can transfer this to microsoft/vscode, which owns the Electron protocol handler, preserving the outstanding request to log rejected managed-resource requests. Confirm current resource loading separately rather than treating the loading fix as completion of the diagnostic request.

- **Public loading fix:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/365b48f9bc374b5eaadb5a38bd605f940081ae3a, committed 2026-09-09T14:15:02Z, removes the !request.referrer rejection from src/vs/code/electron-main/app.ts and delegates to createManagedRemoteResourceRequestHandler. Its replacement dispatches window-authority requests without a referrer check and supplies Buffer error bodies, addressing the captured empty-referrer rejection and misleading net::ERR_FAILED response.
- **Separate outstanding diagnostic request:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11679#issuecomment-5641214582 explicitly asks for rejection logging independently of the loading fix. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/main/src/vs/platform/protocol/electron-main/managedRemoteResourceProtocol.ts still returns a 404 for invalid window authority without logging; its warning covers dispatch exceptions only. The cited loading fix therefore does not fulfill the entire thread's remaining request.
- **Related reports do not own the diagnostic remainder:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11684, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11856, and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11839 are open and describe matching resource-loading symptoms and the useExecServer workaround, but do not explicitly own the remaining rejection-logging change. They do not establish a full-scope duplicate canonical.
- **Workaround is not completion:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11679#issuecomment-5669834192 confirms remote.SSH.useExecServer=false works around the recurrence. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/wiki/Remote-SSH-troubleshooting documents disabling this mode as troubleshooting and explains that exec-server mode enables additional remote workflows; bypassing it does not repair the default resource path or add diagnostics.
