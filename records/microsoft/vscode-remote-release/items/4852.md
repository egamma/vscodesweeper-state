[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/4852](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/4852)

## Summary

Keep open for configurable discovery of custom, including private, template collections in the Dev Containers picker. Publishing templates and manually entering registry references do not deliver the requested searchable list, and no verified canonical supersedes this accepted Backlog request.

## Change summary

Allow users to configure custom container-template sources and browse their definitions during quick setup.

## Best solution

The Dev Containers extension should let users configure template collection sources and discover their templates in the configuration picker, supporting authenticated private collections through the existing distribution ecosystem. Keep this issue as the product-side tracking item rather than folding it into spec publishing work or the narrower local-template request.
