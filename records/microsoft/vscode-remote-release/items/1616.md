[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/1616](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/1616)

## Summary

Keep open: multiple users confirmed failed WSL2 reconnection after hibernation, and the supplied steps suffice to retest. Historical successful attempts do not establish resolution, and the related Modern Standby issue has not been shown to own the full hibernation failure.

## Change summary

An open WSL2 development session fails to reconnect after the Windows host resumes from hibernation.

## Best solution

The WSL component should retest the documented hibernation sequence and collect paired client/server logs to determine why the management connection loses its reconnection token. Compare the result with the Modern Standby issue before consolidating reports or assigning an upstream cause.
