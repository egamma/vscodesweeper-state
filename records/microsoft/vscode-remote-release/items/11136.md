- **Matching workaround:** The reporter confirms successful connection with remote.SSH.useExecServer=false at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11136#issuecomment-3159949522. Users of the canonical confirm the identical workaround at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/10939#issuecomment-2949816330; this bypasses the failing path rather than proving a fix.
- **Recent affected-host evidence:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11136#issuecomment-6414574950 reports the same CLI-startup failure on SLES 15 SP7 on 2026-10-26, again resolved operationally by disabling useExecServer.
- **Related diagnosis, not a shipped fix:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11433#issuecomment-5288977176 documents a bundled-OpenSSL/system-configuration incompatibility on Tumbleweed, with command-shell hanging under seed=JITTER. The canonical links that discussion; neither thread establishes a shipped product fix.
- **Distinct commenter evidence:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/issues/11136#issuecomment-4162135718 reports Alpine arm64 with a CLI log already containing 'Listening on 127.0.0.1:45795'. That observation is not proof of the SUSE startup deadlock and should not be represented as sharing its diagnosed root cause.
- **Repository troubleshooting policy:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-remote-release/wiki/Remote-SSH-troubleshooting explicitly recommends testing with useExecServer disabled and explains that exec-server mode enables additional remote workflows. The documented fallback is therefore not equivalent to resolving the failing mode.
