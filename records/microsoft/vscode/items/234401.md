# Issue #234401 — Formatter messes up indentation (even its own)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234401](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234401)

## Summary

The issue reports two auto-indentation problems with Allman-style braces in TypeScript: Enter-key indentation and paste indentation. Recent work added an `autoIndentOnPaste` setting (June 2025) and an early-return fix for format-on-paste (April 2025) that may partially address the paste complaint, but neither commit references this issue and the Enter-key auto-indentation problem with non-K&R brace styles remains a known limitation. Keeping open as a valid bug in the editor-autoindent area.

## Change summary

TypeScript auto-indentation produces incorrect indentation when pressing Enter before a brace and when pasting code, particularly with Allman-style (next-line) brace formatting.

## Best solution

The paste-related portion may be mitigated by the new `editor.autoIndentOnPaste` setting (default false, added in commit 507316dcb42). The Enter-key auto-indentation with Allman braces remains an open gap in `src/vs/editor/contrib/indentation/` and `src/vs/editor/common/languages/autoIndent.ts`. A maintainer should verify whether the paste portion is now addressed by the new setting and narrow the issue to the Enter-key behavior if so.
