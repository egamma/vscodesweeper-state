## Evidence

- **Current source confirms the defect:** extensions/git/src/git.ts:3114-3163 routes getWorktrees() exclusively through getWorktreesFS(). Both main and linked worktrees read filesystem HEAD directly, strip 'ref: ', and infer detached state from that prefix.
