[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338101](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338101)

## Summary

Plausible but unconfirmed race: the reporter describes the Secondary Side Bar sometimes surfacing the hidden Chat (Copilot) view container instead of the last-active one (Claude Code) when toggling visibility — intermittent, so I can't confirm it from source alone, and no one else has reproduced it yet.

## Change summary

Toggling the Secondary Side Bar with CMD+Shift+B intermittently shows the hidden "Chat" (Copilot) view container instead of the last-active "Claude Code" one.

## Best solution

Needs a confirmed, deterministic reproduction (ideally with just two competing view containers) before diagnosing; likely candidate area is the pane composite bar's visibility/activation handling in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/paneCompositeBar.ts (onDidChangeViewContainerVisibility / onDidViewContainerVisible), where a late visibility-change event for Chat could race with restoring the previously hidden composite on show.
