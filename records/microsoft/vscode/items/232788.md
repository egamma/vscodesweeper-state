# Issue #232788 — Webview: throttling of `setTimeout` and `setInterval`

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232788](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232788)

## Summary

Correctly triaged as an upstream Chromium timer-throttling behavior affecting webviews. No fix on main; the existing labels and milestone are appropriate. Keep open to track the upstream issue.

## Change summary

Chromium throttles setTimeout/setInterval to 1000ms minimum in webviews that it considers hidden/inactive, even when they are visible.

## Best solution

Track upstream Chromium timer-throttling behavior. If Electron exposes a way to mark webview BrowserViews as always-active (preventing the throttle), that would be the fix path. Otherwise this waits on Chromium changes. The workaround (reassigning webview HTML) is documented in the thread.
