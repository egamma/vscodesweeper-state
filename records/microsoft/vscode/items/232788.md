[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232788](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232788)

## Summary

Keep open as an upstream webview timing issue: the thread includes a sample, an independent confirmation, and a recovery workaround. Source inspection does not establish a fix or an older same-symptom canonical issue; the supplied steps remain sufficient for retesting.

## Change summary

Prevent visible extension webviews from intermittently throttling 500 ms timers to approximately 1000 ms after reopening.

## Best solution

Identify why Electron/Chromium treats an active webview as eligible for timer and animation throttling, then resolve that visibility or scheduling defect at the responsible layer. Preserve legitimate background throttling rather than disabling it globally.
