Still reproduces on main: the gutter click hit-test is hardcoded to ±3px regardless of the configured gutter width. An open PR (#214554) already targets this issue but has not merged.

SCM dirty-diff gutter click area is hardcoded to 6px wide, ignoring the user's `scm.diffDecorationsGutterWidth` setting, making wider decorations unclickable near their edges.
