Keep open: current main still performs AHP JSONL serialization and queues diagnostic file writes from the renderer. The reported steps suffice to retest, but the intermittent stall and its precise cause remain unconfirmed; the follow-up measurements rule out attributing 270 ms to payload size alone.

Prevent AHP diagnostic logging from introducing renderer long tasks when opening or switching large agent-host sessions.
