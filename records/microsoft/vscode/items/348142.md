- **Behavior:**

- **Trace:**

  > LocalAgentHostService constructs AhpJsonlLogger in the renderer, and AgentHostIpcChannelTransport logs received frames before notifying consumers. AhpJsonlLogger.log serializes entries locally; _writeBuffers combines them into approximately 1 MiB batches and appends through IFileService. The desktop disk provider forwards those buffers to the main-process channel, whose Electron protocol calls sender.send directly. This corroborates the reported path but does not independently establish its elapsed time. Start by reproducing the logging-on/off profiles from https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348142 and separating entry serialization, coalescing, and IPC send costs. Preserve protocol delivery, JSONL ordering, redaction, bounded pending data, byte budgets, retention, and flush semantics; a smaller coalescing limit alone does not bound individual entries.
