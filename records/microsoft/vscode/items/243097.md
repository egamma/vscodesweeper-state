# Issue #243097 — UX: Quick chat resizing bug

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/243097](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/243097)

## Summary

The current source still explains the empty-state resizing jump and minimum-height restriction. Keep this team-authored, accepted bug open; the related reports do not establish a valid duplicate or a completed fix.

## Change summary

Resizing an empty Quick Chat abruptly enlarges it and prevents returning to its compact initial height.

## Best solution

Make Quick Chat sash resizing continuous from its displayed height, independent of the overlay's screen position, and allow the empty state to return to a content-fitting height. Preserve the separate automatic conversation-layout and sash-reset behavior.

## Evidence

- **Current resize path:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widgetHosts/chatQuick.ts, QuickChat.registerListeners disables dynamic layout on sash start, rejects currentY below DEFAULT_MIN_HEIGHT = 200, and passes currentY directly to widget.layout as a height. src/vs/base/browser/ui/sash/sash.ts constructs currentY from pointer pageY, an absolute coordinate rather than a height relative to the Quick Chat container. This explains a jump when the overlay has a nonzero top offset.
- **Empty-state minimum mismatch:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatWidget.ts, layoutDynamicChatTreeItemMode computes the initial empty-state height from inputHeight plus chatSuggestNextWidgetHeight, with zero message height and no 200-pixel minimum. Manual resizing in chatQuick.ts applies that minimum instead, so an initially smaller empty widget cannot be manually resized back to its initial height.
- **Older related issue is not the same symptom:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209026 is open and older, but concerns scrolling a conversation automatically enlarging the widget, not dragging the empty-state sash. Its maintainer discussion explicitly distinguishes automatic scrolling growth from fixed-height sash resizing: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209026#issuecomment-2549102031. It does not meet the same-observable-symptom duplicate bar for this re-reviewed item.
