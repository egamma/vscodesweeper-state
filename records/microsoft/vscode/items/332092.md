## Summary

Plausible, code-corroborated gap: the persisted-subagent rendering path in chatSubagentContentPart.ts only replays the final `toolSpecificData.result` text for a completed subagent invocation, not its live intermediate activity/tool-call history — which would explain why a nested (second-level) subagent's in-progress workflow disappears once the task finishes and the invocation is serialized.

## Change summary

After a task finishes, the workflow of a second-level (nested) subagent invoked from within a first-level subagent is no longer viewable, even though it was visible while running.

## Best solution
