[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326347](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326347)

## Summary

The report does not yet distinguish an actually rewritten shortcut from Windows activating the still-running portable instance. The maintainer's requested shutdown check and the shortcut target before and after launch are needed to confirm the defect.

## Change summary

Launching portable VS Code appears to make the installed copy's Start menu shortcut open the portable copy.

## Best solution

First confirm whether the `.lnk` target changes after portable startup and whether the behavior persists after closing every portable window.
