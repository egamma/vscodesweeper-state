# Issue #147105 — Clicking in the middle of a selected range, does not deselect the text sometimes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147105](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147105)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the cursor hit-testing code in mouseTarget.ts can intercept a click inside a selection when the click lands on the cursor's rendered area, preventing deselection. The related investigation issue #164484 is still open.

## Change summary

Clicking inside a selected range occasionally fails to deselect the text, especially with wider cursor widths.

## Best solution

Resolve alongside https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/164484 — removing or refining the cursor hit-testing in `_hitTestViewCursor` (src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts, lines 626–673) would eliminate the false positives that cause the selection to stick. The cursor hit test was added for an old Edge bug and may no longer be needed.

With `editor.cursorWidth: 5` the hit area is larger, explaining the reporter's observation of 16/100 vs 3/100 trigger rate.
