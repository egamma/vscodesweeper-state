# Issue #343967 — Consider Progress indicator on top level

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343967](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343967)

## Summary

Keep open for a design decision: comparison groups already summarize running attempts in text, but their stacked icon does not indicate ongoing work. The reporter explicitly weighs better visibility against visual fatigue; neither a defect nor an established decision to decline the enhancement is demonstrated.

## Change summary

Consider an aggregate progress indicator beside the top-level comparison group while its sessions are working.

## Best solution

Decide whether comparison-group activity needs an additional visual cue beyond the existing working-attempt count. Any enhancement belongs in the Sessions list comparison-group presentation and should preserve clear attention states without adding unnecessary competing animation.
