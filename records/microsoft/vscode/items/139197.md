# Issue #139197 — Notebook File/Cell Sace and Recover Problem

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139197](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139197)

## Summary

Keep open for a notebook undo/redo UX decision. Current code supports restoring deleted cells and configuring per-cell history, but those capabilities do not fully address the reporter's difficulty understanding recovery or the maintainer-raised suggestion to exclude cell creation from undo.

## Change summary

Make notebook undo/redo behavior more predictable, particularly when recovering accidentally deleted cells.

## Best solution

Agree on a clear interaction contract for cell-content undo versus notebook-structure undo, then make recovery discoverable and consistent with that contract. Evaluate the suggestion to exclude cell creation from ordinary undo without sacrificing recovery of explicitly deleted cells.

The reporter asks for clearer recovery behavior rather than specifying a single current failure. A maintainer also raised excluding cell creation from the undo stack, but did not settle that behavior. Acceptance must cover explicit deletion recovery and undo/redo after cell creation, with both per-cell history settings and both content and container focus.
- **Trace:**

  > NotebookUndoRedoContribution in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/contrib/undoRedo/notebookUndoRedo.ts delegates focused-notebook commands to NotebookViewModel.undo()/redo(), which operate on the notebook resource. notebook.contribution.ts registers notebook.undoRedoPerCell and changes cell-resource comparison keys according to that setting. cellOperations.ts records deletion and insertion operations, while SpliceCellsEdit in common/model/cellEdit.ts restores structural edits. These existing mechanisms establish recovery support, not resolution of the requested interaction clarity. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139197#issuecomment-1119932562 raises a product alternative rather than an approved patch; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/144102 addresses narrower historical defects. Preserve explicit deletion recovery and existing text-edit correctness; do not expand this into clipboard, persistence, or unrelated markdown-history work. - **Open decisions:**
