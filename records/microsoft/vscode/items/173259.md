- **Reported workaround is partial coverage:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/173259#issuecomment-1685900733 reports that Shift+AltGr works on the S9+ keyboard cover in Brave. This does not restore ordinary AltGr behavior or establish that the Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y mismatch is fixed.
