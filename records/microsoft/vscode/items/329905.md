# Issue #329905 — Flaky Tests: signals when word-buffered content has drained to the DOM

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329905](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329905)

## Summary

Team-authored flaky-test report — kept open per policy regardless, but the race is real and traceable: the test assumes exactly two rAF ticks are enough for WordBuffer to drain, when the buffer's word-reveal count depends on wall-clock elapsed time between frames and can legitimately reveal zero new words on a fast tick.

## Change summary

The `signals when word-buffered content has drained to the DOM` unit test intermittently fails, expecting `isDrained: true, drainCount: 1` but observing `isDrained: false, drainCount: 0`.

## Best solution

Fix the test itself (not the product code) in chatIncrementalRendering.test.ts: instead of awaiting a fixed two rAF frames, poll/await repeated rAF ticks (bounded) until `morpher.isDrained` becomes true, so the assertion isn't tied to how many words WordBuffer.filterFlush happens to reveal per elapsed millisecond.
