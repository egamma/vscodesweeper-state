# Issue #324416 — UX defect - too easy to close terminal window when maximizing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324416](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324416)

## Summary

Valid UX polish request: the maximize and close (hide) panel buttons sit adjacent in the panel title bar (order 1 and 2 in the same navigation group), making accidental clicks easy. No duplicate found. Keeping open for the layout/UX area to evaluate.

## Change summary

The maximize and close/hide panel buttons in the panel title bar are placed directly next to each other, making it easy to accidentally close the panel when trying to maximize it.

## Best solution

Consider adding spacing, a separator, or reordering the panel title bar actions so that maximize and close are not immediately adjacent. The relevant registrations are in `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/panel/panelActions.ts` — maximize is order 1 and close is order 2 in `MenuId.PanelTitle` group `'navigation'`. A similar review could apply to sidebar title bars.
