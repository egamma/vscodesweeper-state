# Issue #350045 — Not sure why it's called "Close all". How do I open more than one chat at a time in Chat Area?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350045](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350045)

## Summary

The chat area can retain several open chats while displaying only the active one. This can be answered by explaining Close versus Close All and the existing setting for showing tabs.

## Change summary

Clarify how multiple chats remain open in the Chat Area and why its menu offers Close All.

## Best solution

Explain that opening successive sessions retains earlier chats, and closing the active chat reveals a previous one. Users who want visible tabs can set "sessions.showChatTabs": "multiple".

  > Thanks for testing. The Chat Area shows one chat at a time by default, but opening another session keeps the previous chat open behind it. Close reveals the previous chat; Close All closes all of those chats and hides the area.
  > 
  > To show the open chats as tabs, set `"sessions.showChatTabs": "multiple"`, then open sessions from the sessions list.
