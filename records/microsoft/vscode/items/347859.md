## Summary

The synchronous startup defect remains in current main. Keep this open while the existing linked fix PR is reviewed; no additional reproduction information is needed.

## Change summary

Allow custom Monaco worker factories to call ctx.getMirrorModels() synchronously without throwing or leaving initialization permanently blocked.

## Best solution

Review and land an appropriate fix through https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/347934, covering synchronous mirror-model access and recovery after failed initialization. Merely switching to the callback's workerServer parameter is insufficient because its requestHandler is also not initialized during the factory call.
