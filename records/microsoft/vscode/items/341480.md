- **Behavior:**

- **Trace:**

  > TerminalInstance creates TerminalResizeDebouncer callbacks that resize xterm and then forward its dimensions through _updatePtyDimensions. TerminalInstance also enables ConPTY cursor-line reflow when windowsUseConptyDll is enabled. Source inspection establishes these boundaries but cannot determine whether the observed divergence originates in resize coordination, xterm, ConPTY, or shell redraw.
