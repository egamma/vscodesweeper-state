# Issue #347308 — Expanding a part of a collapsed section is not discoverable

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347308](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347308)

## Summary

Keep open as discoverability polish. Partial expansion already works through the collapsed region's upper and lower edges, but the prominent arrows toggle the entire region and do not explain the incremental interaction.

## Change summary

Make incremental expansion and dragging of collapsed unchanged sections discoverable in the Agents window multi-diff editor.

## Best solution

Improve the compact diff disclosure's affordances so users can discover incremental expansion without guessing where to click or drag, while preserving full expansion and keyboard access. The area owner should settle the arrow interaction before implementation.
