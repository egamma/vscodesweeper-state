## Evidence

- **Existing capability is partial:** src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findWidget.ts:893–970 handles CtrlCmd+DownArrow in both _onFindInputKeyDown and _onReplaceInputKeyDown by focusing the editor, not the next input. Neither handler supplies the corresponding CtrlCmd+UpArrow input-navigation path. Tab and Shift+Tab already move between the inputs.
- **Search view comparison:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/search/browser/searchActionsNav.ts:242–317 registers FocusNextInputAction and FocusPreviousInputAction with CtrlCmd+DownArrow and CtrlCmd+UpArrow, scoped to Search view or Search Editor inputs. These registrations do not cover the editor Find widget.
- **Alternative does not satisfy the request:** src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findController.ts registers StartFindAction and StartFindReplaceAction for the existing Find/Replace shortcuts. The reporter explicitly acknowledges Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H but requests the same directional navigation used elsewhere; these alternatives do not deliver that consistency.
