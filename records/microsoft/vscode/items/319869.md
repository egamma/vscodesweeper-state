## Summary

Verified duplicate: the older open canonical issue explicitly tracks configurable pauses before Copilot inline suggestions and shorter single-line suggestions, fully covering this request.

## Change summary

Allow users to delay automatic Copilot inline suggestions until they have paused typing, with less aggressive short suggestions.

## Best solution

Consolidate timing and suggestion-length feedback in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/283075.
