# Issue #343575 — Copilot harness (now default) silently lacks run_in_terminal; agents fall back to a separate shell and fail where Local works

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343575](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343575)

## Summary

Keep open: current terminal alternatives do not establish parity with Local's prepared, shared integrated terminal. The related non-TTY report does not cover this report's full observable capability and disclosure gap.

## Change summary

Provide shared integrated-terminal workflows in the Copilot harness, or clearly disclose their absence, alongside accurate migration status and Local-default guidance.

## Best solution

Establish an explicit terminal capability contract for Copilot sessions: support the prepared-terminal workflow or explain the limitation before users depend on it. Separately reconcile the migration-status and custom-agent-picker observations rather than treating terminal improvements as completing the entire report.
