[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343575](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343575)

## Summary

Keep open: current source includes broader client-tool forwarding and shell-profile support, but that does not establish that the reported shared-terminal workflow now works. The supplied steps suffice to retest; neither a verified full-scope duplicate nor a concrete fixing commit was established.

## Change summary

Switching to the Copilot harness silently breaks custom-agent commands that depend on integrated-terminal state, with additional customization and migration-status inconsistencies.

## Best solution

Verify that Copilot chats can discover and use the integrated-terminal tools without losing the user's prepared shell context; unsupported workflows should be explained before commands fail. Clarify MCP delivery versus migration status and document the supported Local-selection behavior separately.
