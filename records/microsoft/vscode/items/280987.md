# Issue #280987 — NES long distance hint: weird interaction at edge of viewport

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/280987](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/280987)

## Summary

The viewport-edge interaction remains present in current source: the long-distance hint is positioned in document coordinates without constraining its vertical rectangle to the visible editor, while the overlay clips overflow. The existing NES labels route it correctly, and the older related issue reports a different symptom.

## Change summary

Prevent the NES long-distance hint and preview widget from becoming partially clipped or transitioning awkwardly when its target line reaches the viewport edge.

## Best solution

Keep the long-distance hint fully visible and visually coherent as the target crosses either viewport edge, while preserving its current placement away from those edges. Cover the edge position with a component screenshot or focused placement regression test.
