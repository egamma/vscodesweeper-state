- **Existing lifecycle capabilities and boundary:** src/vs/platform/hover/browser/updatableHoverWidget.ts cancels the HTML-content CancellationToken when the current rendered widget is disposed. src/vs/base/browser/ui/hover/hover.ts exposes IHoverOptions.onDidHide, and HoverService._showHover registers that callback with the shown hover's disposable store.
