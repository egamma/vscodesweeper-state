Keep open: current source still exposes an untitled-content handoff that explains the blank text fallback after opening an image in Safari or Firefox. The linked open PR acknowledges the underlying limitation rather than resolving it, and no eligible older canonical was verified.

## Change summary

Review the linked PR against this full requirement; its audio/video changes alone do not resolve the reported GIF fallback.
