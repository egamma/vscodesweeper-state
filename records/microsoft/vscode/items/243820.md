# Issue #243820 — Force language auto-detect to keep checking a new document when typing a first line even if it finds another language first (looking at you, HTML).

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/243820](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/243820)

## Summary

Keep open: current source already retries automatic detection after content changes, but unlike manual Auto Detect it does not consult registered firstLine associations on that path. The supplied typing and paste steps suffice to retest; the exact STML outcome remains unverified without executing its extension.

## Change summary

Automatically recognize a completed extension-provided firstLine match even when an earlier partial line was classified as HTML.

## Best solution

Make automatic detection honor registered firstLine associations for eligible documents as their contents evolve, while preserving explicit language choices, file-association precedence, and responsive typing. Diagnose the automatic-versus-manual mismatch rather than assuming detection stops after its first success.
