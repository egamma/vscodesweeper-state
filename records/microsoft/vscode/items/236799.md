## Evidence

- **Reported trigger and confirmations:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/236799 describes copying an importable line without a selection and pasting onto a populated destination line: insertion occurs at the caret rather than above the line. The discussion independently confirms the behavior on macOS 1.107.1 and Windows 11 1.107.0.
- **Current source confirms the discrepancy:** src/vs/editor/contrib/dropOrPasteInto/browser/copyPasteController.ts records isFromEmptySelection as defaultPastePayload.pasteOnNewLine, but doPasteInline passes the original selections to getPasteEdits and applyEditAndShowIfNeeded without adjusting their columns. postEditWidget.ts forwards those ranges to createCombinedWorkspaceEdit in edit.ts, which inserts at the supplied ranges. In contrast, PasteOperation._simplePaste in src/vs/editor/common/cursor/cursorTypeEditOperations.ts:776-790 explicitly inserts eligible full-line pastes at column 1.
- **Existing open implementation candidate:** Verified https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/306565 is OPEN. Its body explicitly says Closes #236799 and proposes preserving full-line positioning in CopyPasteController.doPasteInline. An open fix attempt is not evidence of a completed fix and excludes a competing agent implementation.
