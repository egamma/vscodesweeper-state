Keep open: the supplied steps suffice to retest the intermittent loss of terminals after restarting the pty host. Current source includes restart-listener cleanup and reconnection handling, but neither the source inspection nor the later ConPTY observation establishes that the reported terminal disposal is resolved.

## Change summary

Restarting the pty host intermittently kills replacement terminal processes and closes the terminal panel.

## Best solution

Normal terminals should survive a pty-host restart as terminal instances with replacement shells, without being disposed by shutdown or reconnection events. Reproduce the reported sequence and identify the event ordering responsible before changing the terminal lifecycle.
