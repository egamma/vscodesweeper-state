- **Behavior:**

  > Opening and focusing a Markdown preview must not make Alt-based menu navigation unavailable where it works from a normal editor. Historical reports also reproduce the problem after opening Release Notes, including native title bars with a toggled menu bar. Acceptance should compare both surfaces on Windows and Linux, verify menu activation and subsequent navigation, and ensure webview editing shortcuts and focus restoration remain intact.
- **Trace:**

  > ElectronWebviewElement.handleFocusChange in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/electron-browser/webviewElement.ts invokes WindowIgnoreMenuShortcutsManager, which requests native shortcut suppression on focus and restoration on blur; disposal also requests restoration. The manager gates this behavior on native title bars or macOS, and src/vs/platform/webview/electron-main/webviewMainService.ts forwards it to Electron. Separately, browser/pre/index.html forwards keydown and keyup through WebviewElement.handleKeyEvent to the host window, whose ModifierKeyEmitter feeds the custom menubar's Alt handling. These paths are present in the checkout, but their current native-menu outcome was not executed, so the first task is to reproduce and identify which path fails. Preserve webview editing shortcut handling and existing custom-menu settings; do not assume removing all native shortcut suppression is safe. Historical confirmations and consolidation are recorded at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/142594 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149082.
