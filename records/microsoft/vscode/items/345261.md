- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter's MCP prompts remain available through Inspector, curl, and Local chat, but disappear from slash completion in Copilot-harness chat. A connected server's prompts should be discoverable and usable in that harness as well. Acceptance covers listing a known prompt, selecting it, resolving its arguments, and using its returned content without regressing Local chat.
- **Trace:**

  > In chatInputCompletions.ts, the mcpPromptSlashCommands provider reads IMcpService server.prompts but explicitly returns for agent-host-backed widgets. AgentHostInputCompletions in agentHostInputCompletions.ts instead registers completion providers from host-advertised trigger characters and converts returned items into editor suggestions. In mcpServer.ts, _populateLiveData and _setServerPrompts handle workbench-side discovery, and the absence of a prompt-count log is not evidence of failed discovery. The reporter confirmed successful server responses at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345261#issuecomment-6580167928 and isolated the Copilot harness at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345261#issuecomment-7166071325. Inspection establishes the split completion paths, not whether the failing host discovers or returns MCP prompts; begin with that distinction. Preserve Local prompt handling and existing harness ownership rather than assuming the skipped workbench provider is itself the bug.
