## Evidence

- **Verified older open canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661 is OPEN and was created on 2018-05-11, before this issue. Its body requests an option to ignore whitespace-only lines during trimming.
- **Exact same observable requirement:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661#issuecomment-526716248 explicitly requests removing whitespace at the end of non-empty lines while preserving whitespace-only lines; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661#issuecomment-526717471 confirms that exact goal. This is the same requested behavior, not merely a related indentation concern.
- **Existing capability check:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorConfigurationSchema.ts:56-59 defines editor.trimAutoWhitespace as a boolean for auto-inserted whitespace. src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/files.contribution.ts:237-247 provides files.trimTrailingWhitespace and a regex/string exclusion, but neither supplies a whitespace-only-line exclusion. In src/vs/editor/common/commands/trimTrailingWhitespaceCommand.ts, trimTrailingWhitespace sets fromColumn to 1 when lastNonWhitespaceIndex is -1, so whitespace-only lines remain trimming targets.
- **Linked implementation did not land:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/211093 is CLOSED without merging. The maintainer explained that the proposed override did not work at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/211093#issuecomment-2943713280; the PR was later closed for lack of response at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/211093#issuecomment-3679027752. It is supporting implementation history, not evidence of a shipped fix.
- **Original discussion and workaround:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/40115#issuecomment-2044780745 distinguishes preserving auto-inserted indentation from this selective-trimming request. The canonical discussion also documents a Trailing Spaces extension workaround at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661#issuecomment-1200345682; that workaround does not establish built-in coverage or justify declining the accepted request.
