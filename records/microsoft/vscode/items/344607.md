# Issue #344607 — UX / UI: Model picker became difficult to use

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344607](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344607)

## Summary

Keep open for model-picker design discussion: current source still hides inactive provider labels, and search does not replace mouse-driven browsing. A maintainer has already consolidated another usability report into this issue, making this the established discussion rather than a duplicate to close.

## Change summary

Make models across multiple providers easy to identify and browse without guessing icons, waiting for tooltips, or knowing a search term in advance.

## Best solution

Agree on a model-picker presentation that makes provider identity discoverable while preserving quick model selection and accommodating many providers. Retain this issue for the concrete browsing feedback, and assess the reported layout failure with more than six providers alongside the design work.
