## Summary

Still reproduces on current main — the BugIndicatingError at diffEditorWidget.ts:406 fires when a TextModel is disposed while the DiffEditorWidget still references it. The attempted fix PR #313937 was closed without merging, and the error continues spiking in stable (3.1x in v1.129.1, 164 users). This is a real, endemic lifecycle race that needs a proper fix.

## Change summary

The closed PR https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/313937 attempted the latter approach and could be revived or reworked.
