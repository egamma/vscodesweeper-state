# Issue #337989 — Renderer freezes during bulk text model cleanup

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337989](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337989)

## Summary

Keep open while the directly linked fixing PR is under review. The issue has a measured local reproduction, and the PR reports reducing cleanup from 6.84 seconds and 18,000 scans to 73 ms and two scans.

## Change summary

Bulk text-model disposal repeatedly scans all remaining models, freezing the renderer for several seconds when switching sessions with thousands of models.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/337990, preserving immediate model-disposal notifications while coalescing the expensive full-model scans; close this issue through that PR once merged.
