# Issue #141809 — Closing dirty custom document calls revert after the view is disposed before onDidDispose is called

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141809](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141809)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still dispatches custom-document revert without awaiting the extension callback, leaving a plausible disposal-ordering race. The report's synchronous untitled PawDraw reproduction supplies enough detail to retest, but its exact timing-dependent failure has not been confirmed against current main.

## Change summary

Closing and discarding a dirty custom document can leave its revert callback updating a destroyed webview before the extension receives the panel's disposal event.

## Best solution

Establish a consistent lifecycle between custom-document revert completion and webview-panel disposal so document updates cannot unexpectedly target a destroyed panel that the extension still considers live. Reproduce the synchronous untitled case first, then cover the ordering across the main-thread and extension-host bridge.
