# Issue #141503 — Support radio buttons in the QuickPick

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141503](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141503)

## Summary

Keep open: QuickPick still provides checkbox or ordinary list selection, not radio-button controls. This team-authored Backlog request needs a design decision alongside its explicitly requested prerequisite, not a staleness-based close.

## Change summary

Support radio-button choices in QuickPick in addition to its existing checkbox controls.

## Best solution

Define mutually exclusive choice groups for QuickPick, coordinated with the mixed checkbox/non-checkbox work in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141500. Settle grouping and acceptance semantics before implementing the control and its accessible interaction.

- **Reason:** Distinguishing mutually exclusive choices from independent toggles supports consistent, understandable interactions in an existing core widget.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/quickinput/common/quickInput.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInput.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputList.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/test/browser/quickinput.test.ts`
- **Validation:** After the interaction contract is agreed, add QuickPick browser tests for exclusive selection, independence between groups, selection events, and unchanged ordinary/checkbox modes. Manually verify mouse, keyboard, and screen-reader interaction with a picker containing the agreed group types.
- **Behavior:**

  > QuickPick should distinguish mutually exclusive choices from independent checkbox toggles using radio-button controls. Choosing another option in an exclusive group must replace that group's previous choice without changing independent choices. Acceptance checks should cover visible selected state, keyboard operation, accessible group and choice announcements, and preservation of existing QuickPick modes. Group boundaries and whether choosing an option immediately accepts the picker require a maintainer decision.
- **Trace:**

  > QuickPick.update in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInput.ts derives checkbox visibility from the picker-wide canSelectMany flag. QuickPickItemElementRenderer.ensureCheckbox in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputList.ts constructs Checkbox controls, while the accessibility provider exposes checkbox or option roles within a listbox. The item and separator contracts in src/vs/platform/quickinput/common/quickInput.ts do not define exclusive groups. The author explicitly places https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/141500 before this work; its separator-based grouping suggestion is not a settled contract. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/170639 supplies user evidence that toggle and exclusive-choice presentation are confusing when conflated. Preserve ordinary single-select and multi-checkbox behavior; whether the new capability is initially internal or exposed through the extension API remains undecided.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should radio choices form one picker-wide exclusive group, or support multiple separator-defined groups alongside independent checkboxes and ordinary items?
  - Should selecting a radio option keep the picker open for explicit confirmation, or immediately accept as ordinary single-select QuickPick does?
  - Should the initial capability be internal to workbench QuickPick consumers, or also become an extension-facing QuickPick API?
