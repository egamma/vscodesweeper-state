# Issue #256002 — Chat: UI controls are still operational even though they look disabled

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/256002](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/256002)

## Summary

The reopened keyboard-accessibility defect remains present on current main: editing adds visual and pointer-blocking overlays, but Tab can still focus and operate the controls beneath them. The accepted Backlog bug should stay open until keyboard focus follows the maintainer-defined cancellation behavior.

## Change summary

While editing a previous chat request, controls that appear disabled remain keyboard-focusable and operational.

## Best solution

When focus leaves the request-editing input for the rest of chat, end or cancel editing before another chat control receives focus; controls covered by the disabled scrim must never remain operable through keyboard navigation.
