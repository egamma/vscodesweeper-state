- **Current webview dismissal path:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webviewElement.ts:268–291, the did-context-menu handler opens MenuId.WebviewContext without an element-restoration onHide callback. Its onDidHideContextMenu subscription sends set-context-menu-visible=false; the corresponding handler in browser/pre/index.html only toggles a body class.
- **Existing restoration does not prove resolution:** src/vs/platform/contextview/browser/contextMenuHandler.ts records the workbench active element and conditionally focuses it on dismissal, while WebviewElement._doFocus and the preload focus handler focus iframe windows. The preload's lastFocusedElements restoration is specifically inside execCommand; cancelling with Escape does not execute that handler. The test in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/test/node/webviewShellMessaging.test.ts covers native-command restoration, not Escape dismissal.
- **Older related precedent:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/162909 is closed and discusses focus leaving a webview while an external context menu is displayed. The maintainer response at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/162909#issuecomment-1337911597 suggests manually tracking/restoring focus; it does not establish that losing the originating control after Escape is intended. It is not an open duplicate target.
