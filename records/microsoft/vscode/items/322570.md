## Summary

## Change summary

Terminal crashes with 'Cannot set properties of undefined (setting isWrapped)' when xterm.js resizes a scrolled viewport and leaves unallocated rows.

## Best solution

Wait for upstream fix in xtermjs/xterm.js#6063 (Buffer.resize backfill patch), then update the vendored @xterm/xterm dependency. The fix is a post-loop invariant restoration in Buffer.resize that ensures lines.length >= ybase + rows after growing.
