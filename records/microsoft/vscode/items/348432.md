## Summary

The screenshots and current source point to Batch grammar tokenization: the caret-prefixed delimiter quote starts a string, hiding the following opening parenthesis from bracket matching. Keep this open; the bundled upstream grammar remains implicated, and no matching fix or canonical issue was established.

## Change summary

Recognize the escaped quote delimiter in Batch FOR /F options without disrupting surrounding parenthesis matching.

## Best solution

Correct delimiter tokenization in the upstream Batch grammar and incorporate the correction into VS Code's bundled grammar, preserving normal exclusion of brackets inside strings.
