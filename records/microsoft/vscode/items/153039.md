# Issue #153039 — Inconsistent use of beaks in tooltip and hovers

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/153039](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/153039)

## Summary

Keep open for a shared hover-design decision: current source still supports different pointer and alignment treatments, and the discussion identifies legitimate differences between element-anchored and editor-content hovers. No verified fix or older canonical issue establishes that this cross-surface consistency work is complete.

## Change summary

Establish consistent rules for when tooltips, hovers, and peek views show beaks and how they align with their targets.

## Best solution

Define pointer and alignment guidance by surface role, preserving meaningful distinctions between element-anchored tooltips, content-aligned editor hovers, and peek views. Apply the agreed guidance across the reported surfaces rather than indiscriminately adding or removing every beak.
