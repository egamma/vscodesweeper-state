# Issue #212260 — Extension buttons are cropped

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/212260](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/212260)

## Summary

The narrow Extensions view can still clip action buttons or adjacent publisher text on current main. This remains the canonical accepted bug, with two later reports explicitly closed as duplicates of it.

## Change summary

Prevent extension action buttons and publisher text from being visibly cropped when the Extensions view is narrow.

## Best solution

Keep extension-list actions legible at constrained widths by showing a complete compact control or an ellipsized label, while allowing the publisher region to truncate cleanly instead of clipping either surface.

- **Trace:**

## Evidence

- **current source path:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/extensions/browser/extensionsList.ts places PublisherWidget and ActionBar together in the extension item's flex footer.
- **clipping mechanism:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/extensions/browser/media/extension.css gives the footer `overflow: hidden`; action labels have fixed 150px/100px maximum widths, while `min-width: 0` is applied only to disabled action items. src/vs/base/browser/ui/actionbar/actionbar.css leaves action items at intrinsic flex sizing, so constrained rows can clip the composed footer instead of shrinking its enabled action cleanly.
