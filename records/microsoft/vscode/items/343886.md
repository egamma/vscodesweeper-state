# Issue #343886 — Nesting a renamed session that used a subagent shows its old title, and Undo keeps it

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343886](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343886)

## Summary

The source still skips propagating a session rename when a subagent adds a second chat, leaving the old title available to nesting and promotion. Keep open for the assigned 1.145.0 milestone and the existing open fixing PR.

## Change summary

Preserve a renamed session's title in its main chat, nested row, Undo restoration, and Promote to Session when it has subagent chats.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/344274, preserving independent titles for genuine peer chats while excluding tool-origin and hidden chats from the single-visible-chat decision. The PR explicitly leaves repair of previously affected sessions to another rename on a fixed build.
