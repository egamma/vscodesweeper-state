- **Corroborating discussion:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52855#issuecomment-3531637369 describes repeated disappearance after installing an older version and restarting. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52855#issuecomment-3548012021 reports Process Monitor evidence of the update installer deleting main.js and leaving a corrupted installation. These support consolidation rather than treating this as an old-version support question.
- **Reporter clarification:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/266382, the reporter explicitly says reinstalling starts with an older version, but updating it does not prevent the subsequent disappearance. Waiting several minutes before shutdown sometimes also fails; neither detail establishes a separate defect from the canonical shutdown/update corruption.
- **Current source and completion limits:** src/vs/platform/update/electron-main/updateService.win32.ts contains versioned-update recovery in postInitialize and passes a session-ending flag into the update path; build/win32/code.iss checks that flag to avoid invoking the updater during shutdown. These are relevant safeguards, not proof that this report is fixed. The canonical remains open, and its maintainer update at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52855#issuecomment-3633186256 describes fix work in progress; no completed-fix claim is made.
