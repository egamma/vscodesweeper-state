# Issue #196665 — Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'completion')

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/196665](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/196665)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: `_showDetails` passes `this._list.getFocusedElements()[0]` to `renderItem` without guarding against `undefined` — the RAF scheduling means focus can clear between the call and the callback.

## Change summary

TypeError when the suggest details widget tries to render an item that is no longer focused due to a race with animation-frame scheduling.

## Best solution

Guard the `renderItem` call at line 743 in `suggestWidget.ts` — bail out early when `getFocusedElements()[0]` is `undefined`. The same guard pattern already exists at line 727 (`canExpandCompletionItem(this._list.getFocusedElements()[0])`).
