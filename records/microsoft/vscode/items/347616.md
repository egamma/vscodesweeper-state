[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347616](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347616)

## Summary

Keep open for a maintainer decision on preserving Local as an optional extension. Existing harness-selection settings and the restored Ask agent do not settle the request for continued availability after deprecation.

## Change summary
