# Issue #328507 — Chat scroll indicator doesn't go to short last message

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328507](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328507)

## Summary

The short-final-turn case remains in current source: reaching the transcript bottom can leave the timeline indicator on an earlier prompt. Keep this team-authored report open; resolving the indicator behavior needs care because the sticky header deliberately tracks the prompt at the viewport top.

## Change summary

Make chat's scroll indicator reach a short final turn when the transcript cannot scroll that turn to the viewport top.

## Best solution

Give the timeline a coherent end-of-conversation indication without restoring excessive blank padding or misidentifying the prompt owning the sticky header. Settle the bottom-of-transcript selection semantics before changing the shared active-prompt model.

- **Behavior:**

  > When the final chat turn is short, reaching the transcript bottom cannot move that prompt to the viewport top, and the timeline can continue indicating an earlier turn. The navigation should clearly represent reaching the conversation end without requiring large blank space below it. Acceptance must cover scrolling to the bottom, clicking the final timeline entry, and scrolling back upward, while keeping the sticky header truthful about the content at the viewport top.
- **Trace:**

  > PromptTimelineModel._updateActive in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/promptTimeline/promptTimelineModel.ts rejects prompts below scrollTop even when isScrolledToBottom is true. PromptTimelineWidgetContrib supplies this model's active IDs to the rails and its activePrompt to the sticky header, so these observable behaviors are coupled. PromptTimelineRulerRail._scrollFanCenter also maps the viewport top into prompt positions rather than treating the scroll endpoint separately. Preserve ordinary viewport-top tracking and avoid unrelated scrolling or padding changes. Related https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337315 reports a newer click-selection symptom; it is not a duplicate target for this older report.
- **Open decisions:**
