# Issue #335982 — Terminal replay can drop acknowledgements for live output and leave the PTY paused

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335982](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335982)

## Summary

The replay acknowledgement race remains in current source: live output can be parsed while replay is awaiting completion, and its acknowledgements are discarded. Keep this open and route it to terminal; the class-level defect is established, although its connection to the original interactive incident remains unconfirmed.

## Change summary

Prevent terminal replay from dropping live-output acknowledgements and leaving the PTY permanently paused after reattachment.

## Best solution

Ensure replay and concurrent live output preserve output order and correct flow-control accounting, without acknowledging historical replay data against the host's live-output count. Cover the same-task callback ordering with a deterministic terminal replay regression test.
