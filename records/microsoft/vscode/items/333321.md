## Summary

The reported bulk closure was the stale-Alt “Close Others” action rather than tab multi-selection, and a later fix now revalidates Alt on the close-button press before that destructive action can run.

## Change summary

Prevent a stale modifier state from turning a normal tab-close click into an unintended bulk close.

## Best solution

Keep the fix from commit c21e2a87463d70feb213f8f915b56919c76065c7, which resets the stale action at click time and includes a regression test for the exact no-mouse-movement path.
