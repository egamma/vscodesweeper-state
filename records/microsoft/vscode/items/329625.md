## Evidence

- **Confirmed reproduction and causal control:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329625#issuecomment-5273086003 documents a maintainer reproduction on the reporter's exact commit using a 2,598-line Markdown document. Switching to a text tab reduced the retained inner viewport from 844x675 to 300x150 and changed scrollTop from 12000 to 31866.5. Freezing the hidden overlay at its active dimensions eliminated the jump. The macOS offset eventually recovered; persistent Windows behavior and a possible focus contribution were not independently isolated.
- **Verified broader canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/323890 is currently OPEN and predates this report. It tracks the same retained custom-editor iframe resizing to default dimensions during text-tab switches and explicitly requires avoiding tiny or stale viewport layout. The maintainer investigation identifies this report as its scroll-state consequence, supplying direct root-cause evidence rather than a title-only association.
- **Current source remains consistent with diagnosis:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/overlayWebview.ts: claim() calls _show() before reapplying the anchor; release() hides but retains the webview; _show() sets visibility to visible. src/vs/base/browser/overlayLayoutElement.ts sizes the overlay with anchor-size(width/height), without preserved hidden dimensions. extensions/markdown-language-features/markdown-editor-src/editor.ts:534-556 persists native scroll offsets and relies on document.visibilityState for hide handling; restoration at line 602 occurs during view construction. These source observations support the investigation but are not a new runtime reproduction.
- **Open implementation candidate:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/326482 is OPEN and names https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/323890 as its fixing target. It proposes deferring visibility until a connected anchor is bound. The investigation here explains why outer iframe rectangle checks alone do not establish inner viewport or scroll stability.
- **Other related reports checked:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317033 is open and includes similar tab-switch scroll symptoms, but its primary reproduction concerns source-editor position being locked to a prior preview position; its shared cause is not established. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/271233 concerns stale preview content, not scroll retention. Neither provides a stronger canonical than the directly diagnosed shared-webview issue.
