## Summary

Keep open to investigate missing approvals in editor Chat, including the reported single-background-subagent case. Approval routing has changed since earlier related reports, but this report supplies sufficient steps to retest and no verified fix covers its remaining behavior.

## Change summary

Copilot subagent shell approvals intermittently disappear after earlier approvals succeed, leaving delegated work blocked indefinitely.

## Best solution

Identify where the pending request stops propagating between the Copilot permission handler, child-chat state, and editor Chat confirmation controls. Every outstanding request should remain discoverable and actionable without weakening Manual permissions or requiring a restart.
