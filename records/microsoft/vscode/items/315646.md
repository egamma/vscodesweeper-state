## Change summary

Removing folders from the Agents Window workspace picker does not persist — removed folders reappear when the picker is reopened.

## Best solution

Investigate the folder removal flow in `src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/sessionWorkspacePicker.ts`: verify that `_removeRecentWorkspace` and `_removeVSCodeRecentWorkspace` both correctly prevent the removed URI from appearing in the next `_buildItems` call. A likely area is the interaction between the inline `_removeItem` in the `ActionListWidget` (which splices the in-memory array) and the full `_buildItems` rebuild that happens when the picker is reopened — if the storage write or the `onDidChangeRecentlyOpened` async reload hasn't completed, the item reappears.
