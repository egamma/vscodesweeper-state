## Summary

Thanks for the report. I couldn't confirm the described flow from source, and the behavior looks like a new, documented column-paste mode that `editor.multiCursorPaste: "full"` may already turn off. More detail is needed to tell a real regression from a settings choice.

## Change summary

After copying multiple lines with multiple cursors, pasting into a single line overwrites line contents instead of inserting new lines, and the reporter says overwriteOnPaste is not respected.

## Best solution

Confirm whether the reporter copied with a column (box) selection. If so, this is the new block-paste path, and `editor.multiCursorPaste: "full"` should restore plain insertion. If it is a plain multi-cursor copy, find the real defect.
