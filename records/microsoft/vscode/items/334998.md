# Issue #334998 — In Global Search, a click on the result does not trigger editor.findMatchForeground(or background) change

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334998](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334998)

## Summary

The source still exposes both reported defects: navigating to another search result can clear its active-match decoration, and the foreground theme tokens are wired backwards. Two older open issues cover these separately, but neither covers the full report, so a duplicate close would discard part of its scope.

## Change summary

Global Search should highlight the current match after one click and apply the documented current-match foreground color.

## Best solution

Resolve the navigation highlight lifecycle alongside https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/295724 and the reversed foreground mappings alongside https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/228782. Retain this report until both behaviors are accounted for; its clean-profile macOS reproduction exercises them together.

## Evidence

- **Navigation highlight lifecycle:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/search/browser/searchView.ts:1021–1028, the result-open handler sets the selected match before calling onFocus. onFocus delegates to open, which passes the new editor selection to editorService.openEditor at lines 2240–2256. The cursor-position listener at lines 1082–1087 unconditionally clears that selected match. Moving to another occurrence therefore clears the decoration; clicking the same occurrence again need not move the cursor.
- **Decoration consequence:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/search/browser/searchTreeModel/fileMatch.ts:218–233 chooses decorations through isMatchSelected; setSelectedMatch at lines 277–289 immediately updates them. Its current-match decoration uses currentFindMatch/currentFindMatchInline, whereas other matches use findMatch/findMatchInline.
- **Foreground inversion remains in source:** src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findWidget.ts:1489–1495 applies editor.findMatchForeground to .findMatchInline and editor.findMatchHighlightForeground to .currentFindMatchInline. This contradicts the token descriptions in src/vs/platform/theme/common/colors/editorColors.ts:152–162 and explains the reporter's red active-match text.
- **Older matching navigation report:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/295724 was created on 2026-02-17 and is currently open. Its body explicitly reports that the active global-search decoration appears only after selecting a result twice; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/295724#issuecomment-6157281913 confirms the behavior in 1.141.1. It matches the navigation symptom, but does not cover reversed foreground-token mappings.
- **Separate foreground report:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/228782 was created on 2024-09-16 and remains open. It reports the same reversed foreground colors, with independent confirmations. Its linked implementation candidate, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/321258, is closed rather than merged; the reversed mapping remains in this checkout.
