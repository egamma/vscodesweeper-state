## Summary

Keep open to investigate the Fusion cache-reuse drop specifically at user-message boundaries. Current main has a stability-ordered prompt option, but its cross-session optimization does not establish a fix for this report; the supplied call sequence and expiry details suffice to retest.

## Change summary

Fusion loses substantial prompt-cache reuse on the first call of a new user message despite retaining the model and remaining within the recorded cache lifetime.

## Best solution
