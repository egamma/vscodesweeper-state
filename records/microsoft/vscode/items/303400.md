# Issue #303400 — Broken rendering when not enough space for reasoning submenu on the right

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/303400](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/303400)

## Summary

The broken submenu rendering in narrow chat views was fixed by improving the submenu positioning logic to properly fall back to the left side when there isn't enough space on the right. The fix landed shortly after the issue was filed and has shipped since 1.114.0.

## Change summary

The reasoning effort submenu in the model picker renders incorrectly (clipped/overflowing) when the chat view is too narrow for the submenu to fit on the right side.

## Best solution

Keep the shipped fix — the submenu positioning now calculates available space on both sides and flips to the left when the right side is too narrow; no further change needed.
