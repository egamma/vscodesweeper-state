# Issue #129986 — Middle Click for Fast Scroll Notebook

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/129986](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/129986)

## Summary

Keep open: middle-click scrolling now exists for text editors, but its implementation does not cover the notebook cell-margin gesture requested here. The accepted Backlog request has 24 upvotes, and no verified open canonical owns the remaining notebook work.

## Change summary

Support middle-click followed by mouse movement to scroll a notebook from the cell margin.

## Best solution

Provide notebook-level middle-click autoscrolling from the cell margin, consistent with the existing text-editor interaction while preserving cell editing, selection, and drag-and-drop behavior.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/contrib/middleScroll/browser/middleScrollController.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/notebookCellList.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/notebookCellListView.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/cellParts/cellDnd.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/notebook.contribution.ts`
- **Validation:** On Windows, open a notebook with enough cells and output to exceed the viewport; middle-click its left cell margin and move the pointer above and below the starting position. Verify notebook-level scrolling follows pointer displacement, cancellation matches the chosen interaction contract, and ordinary cell dragging, selection, wheel scrolling, and text-editor middle-click behavior remain unchanged.
- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter wants to navigate long notebooks by middle-clicking the left cell margin and moving the mouse up or down, without targeting the scrollbar or entering a cell editor. The finished behavior should scroll the notebook viewport rather than only an embedded editor or output region. Acceptance should cover long outputs, starting and stopping the scrolling gesture, and preservation of existing cell editing and drag-and-drop interactions.
- **Trace:**

  > The historical dependency chain runs from https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/129986 through https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/104183 to https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/6302. The last was completed by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/245882, but current MiddleScrollController is an ICodeEditor contribution that changes only that editor's scroll position. The notebook's outer viewport is NotebookCellList extending WorkbenchList, with NotebookCellListView extending ListView; notebook source searches found no integration with the editor middle-click option. CellDragAndDropController owns existing cell drag interactions, which must remain intact. No runtime reproduction was attempted in the read-only checkout; the remaining uncertainty is the notebook-level interaction and configuration contract, not whether the text-editor setting exists.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should editor.scrollOnMiddleClick also enable notebook-margin autoscrolling, or should notebook-level scrolling have a separate opt-in contract?
  - Should the initial notebook interaction apply only to cell margins and notebook background, or also to rendered markdown and output regions with their own scrolling?
