## Change summary

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `build/win32/code.iss`, `src/vs/platform/update/electron-main/updateService.win32.ts`
- **Validation:** On a disposable Windows ARM64 installation, force the context-menu cleanup helper to remain running during a background update. Verify the agreed timeout outcome occurs within a bounded interval, logs identify the stalled step, the existing installation remains usable, and a subsequent update is not permanently blocked; also exercise normal cleanup and context-menu operation.
- **Behavior:**

  > A background update must not remain indefinitely blocked when its context-menu cleanup helper stops making progress. The reported failure left setup active overnight and prevented later installers from applying, even after a VS Code reload. Acceptance requires a bounded, diagnosable outcome for a deliberately stalled helper, preservation of a usable installation, and the ability to retry updating. Normal context-menu cleanup and successful updates must retain their existing behavior.
- **Trace:**

  > KillContextMenuComSurrogate in build/win32/code.iss synchronously waits for the exact PowerShell command documented at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347947, without a timeout. RemoveAppxPackage invokes it before CurStepChanged signals background-update readiness on the forced-classic-context-menu path; other callers precede version-folder garbage collection. The reporter confirmed the stalled process chain, but a separate CIM query succeeded, so the evidence does not isolate PowerShell startup, CIM enumeration, or process termination as the cause. doApplyUpdate in src/vs/platform/update/electron-main/updateService.win32.ts times out polling without resolving the active installer, explaining why subsequent attempts remain blocked. Preserve CLSID-specific targeting, application-readiness ordering, and safe Appx/version-folder handling; do not broaden process termination to unrelated COM surrogates. A Windows fault-injection check is needed to establish the recovery contract and verify it.
- **Open decisions:**
  - If context-menu cleanup exceeds its deadline, should setup abort cleanly while preserving the existing installation, or continue with deferred cleanup, and should the outcome differ between Appx removal and version-folder garbage collection?
