# Issue #307703 — Settings view Shift+Tab jumps focus back to editor unexpectedly

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307703](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307703)

Keep open: a maintainer confirmed that Shift+Tab unexpectedly leaves Settings in both overlaid and docked layouts. No verified fix or older canonical reporting the same editor-focus escape was established.

## Change summary

Shift+Tab unexpectedly moves focus out of Settings instead of to the preceding Settings control.
