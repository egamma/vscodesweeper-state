## Summary

Keep open as a distinct feature request: existing comparisons support model and reasoning variants, but do not capture or enforce different skill states or versions per attempt. Neither related issue owns this controlled-customization comparison.

Allow controlled Bakeoff comparisons of enabled, disabled, and revised skills or plugins while preserving comparable execution conditions and inspectable evidence.

## Best solution
