# Issue #341724 — Chats created before upgrading from 1.141.2 to 1.142.0 close immediately

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341724](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341724)

## Summary

Keep open: two commenters independently confirm that upgrading to 1.142.0 makes existing conversations inaccessible or disappear. Related reports and an unmerged fix proposal warrant investigation, but neither a landed fix nor a sufficiently established older canonical justifies closing this report.

## Change summary

Previously saved chat sessions close immediately and disappear from the sessions list after upgrading from 1.141.2 to 1.142.0.

## Best solution

Restore reliable opening and continuation of pre-upgrade local conversations without implicitly archiving them. Evaluate the related fix proposal at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/342504 against this report's stable-release upgrade scenario before treating the regression as resolved.
