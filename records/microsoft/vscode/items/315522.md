# Issue #315522 — Feature Request: Custom Background Colors for Chat Sessions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/315522](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/315522)

## Summary

Keep open for a product decision: existing theme colors and Agents Window background customization do not provide persistent colors for individual chat sessions. Related color-group proposals are newer and cover different surfaces, so they do not justify a duplicate close.

## Change summary

Allow users to assign and persist individual chat-session background colors for faster visual identification.

## Best solution

Decide whether session identity should use theme-aware background colors or a quieter tab/list accent, preserving readability and non-color identification. Coordinate with the related colored-group exploration without treating it as coverage of per-session backgrounds.
