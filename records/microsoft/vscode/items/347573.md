# Issue #347573 — Keyboard shortcuts are dropped after switching to a lazily revived webview editor, until the webview is clicked

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347573](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347573)

## Summary

Keep open: the reported shortcut loss matches the webview focus and event-forwarding paths in the checkout. The checkout predates the report, so current-build reproduction remains unverified; the supplied steps and frame observations suffice to retest.

## Change summary

Workbench shortcuts stop working after first activating a lazily restored webview panel until a click repairs focus.

## Best solution

Ensure keyboard activation of restored webview editors preserves workbench shortcut delivery throughout content loading, without stealing focus from another control. Reproduce with a minimal serializer extension before choosing the focus-handoff change.

- **Trace:**

  > WebviewEditor.focus() in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webviewPanel/browser/webviewEditor.ts delegates to the webview.
