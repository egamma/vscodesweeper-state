## Change summary

Workbench shortcuts stop working after keyboard navigation to a lazily restored webview until the user clicks or explicitly refocuses it.

## Best solution

Ensure lazily restored webviews establish consistent workbench and content-frame focus so shortcuts continue working without a click. Preserve protection against inactive webviews forwarding keys and delayed focus stealing focus from other controls.
