## Evidence

- **Reported behavior and missing trigger:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/284590 reports personally observed intermittent oversized characters and a 1080p@100% / 4k@200% monitor setup. Monitor scaling is presented as a hypothesis; there are no comments confirming reproduction, precise triggering steps, or current-build results.
- **Current scaling lifecycle:** src/vs/editor/browser/gpu/viewGpuContext.ts initializes devicePixelRatio from getActiveWindow(), updates it on resize, and clears the shared atlas when it changes. src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLinesGpu/viewLinesGpu.ts:205-208 and 457-472 recreate the glyph rasterizer when the observed devicePixelRatio changes. These mechanisms exist, but source inspection alone does not establish which environmental transition fails in this report.
- **Diagnostic lead, not confirmed root cause:** src/vs/editor/browser/gpu/raster/glyphRasterizer.ts scales rasterized font size by devicePixelRatio, but its cacheKey includes only font family and font size. src/vs/editor/browser/gpu/atlas/textureAtlas.ts uses that key for shared glyph lookup. This warrants inspecting cache invalidation and window ownership during reproduction, but does not conclusively reproduce the reported intermittent oversized text.
