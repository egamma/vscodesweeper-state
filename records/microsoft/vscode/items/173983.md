# Issue #173983 — Selecting text highlighting in large files silently drops highlighting of occurrences

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/173983](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/173983)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: the 1000-result cap is intentional for performance, but the unresolved product gap is that selection highlighting silently omits matches, including nearby visible occurrences, without indicating truncation.

## Change summary

Improve selection-occurrence highlighting in large files so visible matches are not silently omitted when the performance cap is reached.

## Best solution

Preserve a bounded selection-highlight budget while prioritizing matches in or near the viewport and exposing a lightweight indication when additional occurrences were omitted; avoid matching the unbounded semantics of an explicit Find operation.
