## Summary

Keep open: multi-root Undo and granular review have received fixes since the older linked reports, but those do not establish that secondary-folder edits are visible in this reporter's 1.148.0 BYOK scenario. The supplied primary-versus-secondary steps suffice to retest; current source supports multiple roots, but the exact failing session state remains unconfirmed.

## Change summary
