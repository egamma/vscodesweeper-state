Keep open: the reporter confirms persistence in 1.146.0, including tablet-mode transitions requiring a refresh. The supplied keyboard-toggle steps suffice to retest; current source inspection did not establish the cause or a landed fix.

## Change summary

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/base/browser/touch.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/window.ts`
- **Validation:** On Android 17, open the editor, show and dismiss the software keyboard, then tap Explorer entries and refocus the editor. Repeat after entering and leaving tablet mode. Verify interaction and keyboard reopening work without refresh, while menus, status-bar controls, and TalkBack navigation remain functional.
- **Behavior:**

  > Showing and dismissing the software keyboard must not leave the workbench unable to accept interaction. Explorer entries and the editor must remain usable, including after switching into and out of tablet mode. Acceptance includes reopening the editor keyboard without refreshing and preserving the already-working menu, status-bar, and TalkBack interactions.
- **Trace:**

  > Gesture in src/vs/base/browser/touch.ts tracks active touches through document touchstart, touchmove, and touchend listeners; no touchcancel listener is registered there. BrowserWindow.registerListeners in src/vs/workbench/browser/window.ts handles layout resize, with visualViewport and keyboard scroll-reset behavior limited to iOS. Neither observation conclusively explains the reported Android failure, so diagnosis must first capture which input events and hit targets change after keyboard dismissal. Related gesture proposals exist at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/332629 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/342314, but their relevance must be established before parallel implementation. Preserve existing iOS keyboard handling and working accessibility navigation; do not assume the problem is a renderer-wide hang.
