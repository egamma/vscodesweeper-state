## Summary

Plausible bug in webview multi-window handling, but I couldn't conclusively confirm the defect in source — OverlayWebview.claim() already disposes and recreates the webview (and its message-port handshake) on a window change, which should in principle keep find working — so this needs a minimal repro and reporter confirmation before it can be routed with confidence.
