# Issue #343183 — Automations: a scheduled run that fails before its session is created is hidden and not retried

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343183](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343183)

## Summary

Keep open: current source conclusively hides local runs that fail before session creation and never retries those terminal runs. The linked runtime-recovery issue explains one trigger, but does not cover the independent history visibility defect or recovery of already-failed automation occurrences.

## Change summary

Show pre-session automation failures with their errors and provide a recovery path after the provider becomes usable.

## Best solution

Automations history should retain failed occurrences even without a session, display their recorded error, and support recovering the missed work. Settle whether recovery is automatic or explicitly user-initiated before changing the host's terminal-run and scheduling behavior.

The unresolved contract is how a failed occurrence becomes recoverable, not whether its failure should be visible.
