- **Current picker capability gap:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/agentSessions/agentHost/agentHostChatContribution.ts:324-331 advertises prompt and image attachment support but does not enable supportsSymbolAttachments, supportsProblemAttachments, supportsSearchResultAttachments, or supportsSourceControlAttachments.
