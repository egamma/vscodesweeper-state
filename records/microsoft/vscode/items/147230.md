# Issue #147230 — Play audio-cues for suggest widget

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147230](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147230)

## Summary

The accessibility gap remains on current main, but the signal design and potential noise alongside inline-suggestion cues still require product and user research. Keep this Backlog request open as under discussion.

## Change summary

Play distinct accessibility signals when the editor suggest widget opens and closes.

## Best solution

After screen-reader user research and sound-design review, register configurable suggest-widget open/close signals and trigger them from the widget's visibility transitions without duplicating inline-suggestion feedback.
