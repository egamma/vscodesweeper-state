- **Validation:** On Ubuntu with working IBus Chinese pinyin, compare current Snap and official .deb builds: open an editor, activate Chinese input, compose pinyin, select a candidate and commit Chinese text. Acceptance is working composition through the normal Snap entry point without regressing Latin typing or the previously working display backend.
- **Behavior:**

  > Multiple users cannot compose Chinese or Japanese text in the Snap distribution even though input works in the official .deb distribution on the same system. Normal Snap launch should permit activating the host input method, displaying candidates and committing selected characters into the editor. First reproduce with a current Snap build and IBus Chinese pinyin, using .deb as a control and recording the display backend. A successful result must not require replacing Snap or bypassing its normal launcher.
- **Trace:**

  > resources/linux/snap/snapcraft.yaml routes both entry points through electron-launch and stages GTK and libibus on a core20 classic Snap. resources/linux/snap/electron-launch preserves the original GTK_IM_MODULE_FILE, replaces it with a Snap-local cache, and queries bundled GTK modules when the Snap revision changes. The launcher also unconditionally appends --ozone-platform=x11. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/312549 reports a newer Wayland/chat failure resolved by bypassing that wrapper, but this does not prove the root cause of https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/96041. The desktop-legacy suggestion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/96041#issuecomment-1253384037 is unverified. Diagnosis must distinguish module availability, launcher configuration and upstream toolkit behavior; preserve working X11 input and do not broaden the work into unrelated Chrome OS or candidate-positioning defects.
