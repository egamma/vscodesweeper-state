# Issue #347132 — Check next to an X ... both feel "done" to me, and at a glance it's confusing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347132](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347132)

## Summary

Keep open for a design decision: Inbox rows still show adjacent checkmark and close actions for archiving and dismissing, respectively. Their implementations are intentionally different, but the reporter's concern about recognizing that difference remains unresolved.

## Change summary

Make Inbox dismissal and session archival distinguishable at a glance, or deliberately unify their user-facing behavior.

## Best solution

Agree on a clear presentation for the two outcomes in the Sessions sidebar, preferably preserving reversible Inbox dismissal separately from archival. Any decision to unify them should explicitly address the existing difference in persistence and reappearance behavior.

- **Trace:**

The uncertainty is the desired user-facing distinction, not the current execution paths; preserve ordinary non-Inbox actions and avoid changing lifecycle semantics without an explicit decision.
- **Open decisions:**
