# Issue #296153 — Unclear why I need to take action when archiving background agent

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296153](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296153)

## Summary

Keep open: the background-session archive flow still contains a generic pending-edits confirmation that does not explain its relationship to Apply. No verified fix or older canonical covers the complete reported symptom, and this team-authored item is protected from judgment closes.

## Change summary

Make archiving a background-agent session after applying its changes understandable, including the meaning of Keep/Undo and the pending-file count.

## Best solution

Make background-session archiving reflect the actual state and destination of its changes, without offering local-edit decisions that do not apply. Preserve local-chat edit safeguards and ensure any remaining confirmation explains exactly which files and location it affects.
