## Change summary

Changing visibility or protocol while forwarding two local ports can revert previously configured port settings.

## Best solution

Make local forwarding preserve each port's independently selected visibility and protocol across subsequent changes. First reproduce the two-port interaction and distinguish workbench attribute reconstruction from extension-to-CLI update handling rather than assuming the abandoned debounce proposal fixes it.
