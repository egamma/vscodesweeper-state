# Issue #327133 — Spurious failure of McpStdioStateHandler

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327133](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327133)

## Summary

The CI failure is a genuine test race that remains on main: shutdown begins before the spawned child confirms its signal handler is installed.

## Change summary

Prevent the McpStdioStateHandler SIGTERM test from failing spuriously under slow child-process startup.

## Best solution

Make the test child emit a readiness signal after registering its stdin and SIGTERM handlers, then call `handler.stop()` only after receiving that signal.
