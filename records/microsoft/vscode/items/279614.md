# Issue #279614 — Mousing over content after OSC 633 resurrects OSC 8 tooltip hover (ie, hyperlink tooltips are re-appearing when they shouldn't)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/279614](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/279614)

## Summary

Fixed on main: commit 28704d8c988 added a `leave` handler and an `onRender` listener to the OSC 8 link handler, which dismiss the hover tooltip when the mouse leaves the link area or the terminal re-renders (e.g. due to OSC 633 sequences). This directly prevents the tooltip resurrection described here.

## Change summary

OSC 8 hyperlink hover tooltip reappears ('resurrects') when mousing down from a link to a line containing OSC 633 sequences at zoom level 0.

## Best solution

Keep the shipped fix in commit 28704d8c988 (1.120.0). The `leave` handler clears the tooltip when the mouse exits the link, and the `onRender` listener dismisses it when the link area is re-rendered — both prevent the resurrection. No further change needed.
