# Issue #41022 — when writing a new CompletionItemProvider it is very hard debug as to why the item is not being offered

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41022](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41022)

## Summary

Keep open: suggest explain mode describes visible completions, but current source still discards nonmatching items without exposing their rejection reasons. This is the remaining capability explicitly acknowledged in the maintainer discussion, and no verified open canonical was found.

## Change summary

Provide extension authors with diagnostics explaining why returned completion items do not appear in the suggestion menu.

## Best solution

Extend completion diagnostics to expose rejected items and their filtering reasons without changing normal suggestion behavior. Decide with the maintainer whether these diagnostics belong in an explain-mode inspection surface, an opt-in log, or both.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/completionModel.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggest.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestModel.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestController.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidget.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidgetDetails.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/test/browser/completionModel.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Use a test completion provider returning one matching item and one nonmatching item. With diagnostics enabled, verify the rejected item is identifiable with its compared prefix, matching text, and rejection reason, including when no items remain visible. With diagnostics disabled, verify the normal visible list, ordering, insertion behavior, and existing explain-mode output remain unchanged; cover the filtering contract in completionModel.test.ts and exercise the chosen diagnostic surface manually.
- **Behavior:**

  > An extension author can discover why a returned completion item was excluded from suggestions, rather than being limited to inspecting items that survived filtering. At minimum, diagnostics identify no-match rejection and the prefix and label or filterText used for that comparison. Diagnostics remain usable when every returned item is rejected. Normal completion matching, ordering, and insertion behavior remain unchanged when diagnostics are disabled.
- **Trace:**

  > The toggleExplainMode command in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestController.ts delegates to SuggestWidget, whose toggleExplainMode requires a focused visible item. SuggestWidgetDetails.renderItem displays ranking information only for that selected item. CompletionModel._createCachedState in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/completionModel.ts skips invalid items and unsuccessful filterText or label matches, returning only surviving items through its items getter. SuggestModel logs invalid items and provider timings, while suggest.ts and SuggestModel.createSuggestFilter also govern visibility before fuzzy matching. The maintainer discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41022 explicitly distinguishes shipped explain mode from missing rejected-item diagnostics; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/75500 covers the shipped portion. The uncertainty is the user-facing diagnostic surface and how broadly it explains suppression, not whether normal fuzzy filtering should change. Preserve existing completion semantics and avoid treating the individual filterText workaround as resolution of the broader request.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should rejected-item diagnostics appear in an expanded explain-mode inspection view, an opt-in developer log, or both?
  - Should the first version explain only returned items rejected by matching and validation, or also visibility settings and provider-selection decisions that prevent items from reaching the matcher?
