# Issue #41022 — when writing a new CompletionItemProvider it is very hard debug as to why the item is not being offered

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41022](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41022)

## Summary

The shipped explain mode covers visible suggestions, but the central request remains valid: completion items rejected during filtering still have no accessible explanation.

## Change summary

Provide diagnostics explaining why completion items from an extension were filtered out of the suggest widget.

## Best solution

Extend suggest explain mode to expose rejected completion items and their failed matching criteria, likely through the completion model and suggest details UI, while retaining the existing diagnostics for visible items.
