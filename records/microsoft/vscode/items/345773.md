# Issue #345773 — Automation runs promoted to sessions should have an automation icon on the session title

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345773](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345773)

## Summary

Keep open: promoted automation sessions retain their provenance in the model, but the current session-title renderer does not display it. This team-authored request remains a distinct presentation enhancement; no fixing commit or verified canonical duplicate was found.

## Change summary

Identify automation-origin sessions with an automation icon beside their title in the Sessions list.

## Best solution

Make automation provenance recognizable beside the session title, including an accessible description, without obscuring existing progress, attention, unread, or pull-request indicators.

- **Trace:**

  > AutomationSessionsListingContribution in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/automationSessionsListing.ts already identifies runs through ISession.isAutomation and delegates listing to ISessionsListModelService. The listing setting and external-application badge do not supply automation provenance. Keep provider execution, promotion rules, persisted visibility, and shared status-icon behavior unchanged. The remaining design question is whether the title marker conveys origin alone or also identifies the originating automation.
