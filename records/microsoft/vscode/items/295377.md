# Issue #295377 — Thinking titles are sometimes not saved

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/295377](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/295377)

## Summary

Keep open: the team-authored report has a confirmed timing-dependent persistence defect, and the linked fix attempt was closed after its author confirmed it did not fully resolve the problem. No post-report fixing commit is identified.

## Change summary

Thinking-part titles generated after a chat response completes are sometimes omitted from persisted chat state and later regenerated.

## Best solution

Ensure late thinking-title mutations are represented by an immutable operation-log snapshot or observable serialization change so persistence detects them without preventing otherwise-complete requests from sealing.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/model/chatSessionOperationLog.ts`
- **Validation:** Add a chat-model persistence test that completes a response before assigning its thinking title, persists and restores the session, and asserts that the late title is retained without being regenerated; include a case where no title ever arrives to verify normal sealing/diff work terminates.
- **Behavior:**

  > When a thinking title is generated after its chat response has completed, the title must be persisted and restored exactly as generated rather than disappearing or being regenerated. Requests for which no thinking title arrives must still reach a stable persisted state without perpetual diffing. Acceptance requires a deterministic delayed-title regression test plus coverage for the no-title completion path.
- **Trace:**

  > Chat persistence compares mutations through ObjectMutationLog in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/model/chatSessionOperationLog.ts. The maintainer diagnosis is that thinking parts mutate their title in place and lack an immutable toJson snapshot, so the operation log may compare the same referenced object and miss the change. The closed attempt at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/295378 instead delayed request sealing, but the reporter found that it remained timing-dependent and raised the risk of perpetual expensive diffing when a title never arrives. Preserve normal request sealing and avoid changing unrelated response-part persistence; the uncertainty begins at how late title updates should participate in serialization and mutation tracking.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should a missing thinking title keep that response content explicitly mutable until a title arrives, or should the response seal normally while title updates remain independently persistable afterward?

## Evidence

- **confirmed defect:** The reporter reproduced the race repeatedly and observed that titles were present on ObjectMutationLog._previous while still not being saved; stepping through the code changed the outcome.
- **root-cause diagnosis:** A maintainer explained that thinking parts appear to lack toJson and are mutated when the title arrives, leaving the operation log holding the same old/new object reference so its differ cannot observe the title change.
- **incomplete fix attempt:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/295378 attempted to keep requests unsealed until thinking titles arrived, but it is closed and the author explicitly reported that titles were still sometimes regenerated.
