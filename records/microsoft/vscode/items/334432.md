## Change summary

Switching a chat session to autopilot mid-conversation injects a conditional SystemMessage block into the middle of the merged system prompt, busting prefix-cache for OpenAI-compatible third-party providers without cache-control breakpoints.

## Best solution

Extend the existing drift/freeze pattern in agentPrompt.tsx's getOrFreezeCustomizationsIndex (or a similar snapshot mechanism) to also cover the isAutopilot/isVoiceModeInput/templateVariablesContext conditional blocks, appending changes at the tail (latest user message or after conversation history) instead of merging them into the leading system prompt — a maintainer needs to decide the exact mechanism given multiple conditional flags share this pattern.
