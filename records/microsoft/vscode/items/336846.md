## Summary

Fresh feature request (0 days old, 0 upvotes, no maintainer engagement) asking the editor to render a folded multi-line block as a single line including the closing tag; current folding behavior in folding.ts/foldingDecorations.ts collapses the middle but still shows the last line as its own row, so the gap is real, but there's no evidence yet of broader demand or maintainer interest.

## Change summary
