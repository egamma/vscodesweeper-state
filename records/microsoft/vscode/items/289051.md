# Issue #289051 — Editor Hover Overflow Issue In Small Screen Devices

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/289051](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/289051)

## Summary

The initial editor-hover layout can exceed a narrow browser viewport: its content width is not constrained to the available screen width. Keep this open; the reviewed related reports do not establish a canonical duplicate or a completed fix.

## Change summary

Keep the hover for a large TypeScript type readable within the viewport on small-screen vscode.dev devices.

## Best solution

Make initial editor-hover sizing respect the available browser viewport while retaining wrapping, scrolling, and resizing. Verify the supplied TypeScript example in a narrow browser viewport and confirm the Android behavior.

## Evidence

- **Initial sizing defect:** src/vs/editor/contrib/hover/browser/contentHoverWidget.ts:308–313 sets the initial maximum width to Math.max(editorWidth * 0.66, 750, lastWidth), including when the viewport is narrower than 750. handleContentsChanged at lines 404–428 measures and assigns that content width without applying the viewport-aware _findMaximumRenderingWidth calculation; that calculation is used by the resize path instead.
- **Outer constraint does not constrain hover content:** src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/contentWidgets/contentWidgets.ts:291–299 and 547–558 constrain the outer widget to the window width, but ContentHoverWidget assigns explicit widths to its inner container, scrollbar, and contents. The horizontal placement at lines 345–370 reserves side clearance and repositions the outer widget; it does not shrink those inner dimensions. This leaves a source-level overflow path for wide content on narrow screens.
