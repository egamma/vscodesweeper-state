Compare local terminals across supported platforms and remote terminals under controlled latency. Add deterministic buffering tests for ordered delivery, burst coalescing, empty flushes, resize, and exit, plus flow-control tests for pause/resume and parse-based acknowledgements.
- **Behavior:**

  > Local integrated terminals should deliver idle output with less avoidable delay and sustain higher bulk-output throughput while preserving every character in order. Improvements must retain burst coalescing and responsive interruption of flooding processes, including Unicode workloads. Acceptance compares the reporter's latency, throughput, IPC-count, and interruption-drain measurements against an unchanged control. Remote behavior must respect the agreed connection-fairness and backpressure policy rather than inherit an untested local tuning change.
- **Trace:**

  > TerminalDataBufferer.startBuffering in src/vs/platform/terminal/common/terminalDataBuffering.ts delays the first chunk until its default 5 ms timer expires; PersistentTerminalProcess in src/vs/platform/terminal/node/ptyService.ts uses that default. TerminalProcess in src/vs/platform/terminal/node/terminalProcess.ts counts emitted characters and pauses above the shared 100000-character watermark from terminal.ts, then resumes below 5000. TerminalInstance._writeProcessData acknowledges characters after xterm parsing, through TerminalProcessManager and AckDataBufferer. These paths confirm the reporter's proposed bottleneck mechanisms, but the performance magnitudes have not been independently reproduced. Preserve output ordering, parse-based acknowledgements, resize/exit flushing, replay behavior, and remote shared-channel protection. The existing ptyService.test.ts covers serialization rather than this performance contract, so timing and flow-control coverage must be established. The replay acknowledgement defect at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335982 is distinct and should not be folded into this investigation.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should the improved batching behavior apply to both local and remote terminals, or should remote terminals retain their current behavior until separately validated?
  - Should local and remote terminals have different flow-control windows, and what interruption-responsiveness and remote-fairness limits must the selected policy preserve?
