# Issue #172902 — Extra selection getting added when you Alt + click on a multiple selection to remove the selection

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/172902](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/172902)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: Alt+clicking an existing cursor to remove it (toggle) is immediately followed by a mouse-move dispatch with inSelectionMode:true, which calls _lastCursorMoveToSelect and creates an unwanted selection extension from the last remaining cursor.

## Change summary

Alt+clicking on an existing multi-cursor to remove it also creates an unwanted selection extending from the last cursor to the click position.

## Best solution

The mouse-down handler in mouseHandler.ts (or the viewController dispatch) needs to detect when CreateCursor removed a cursor (toggle) and suppress the subsequent inSelectionMode drag dispatches. Likely fix sites: src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts (suppress drag after toggle-remove) or src/vs/editor/browser/view/viewController.ts (track that the last createCursor was a removal).
