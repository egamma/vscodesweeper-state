# Issue #336994 — Chat Customizations: 'Local', 'Remote' are confusing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336994](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336994)

## Summary

Confirmed in source: the Chat Customizations (AI Customization) editor groups skills by two overlapping taxonomies — storage location ('Workspace'/'User'/'Extensions') and, for skills specifically, harness-relative location ('Local'/'Remote', derived from 'remote-client'/'remote-host' group keys) — with no in-UI explanation of the distinction, exactly as the reporter describes.

## Change summary

The Chat Customizations skills page shows both 'Workspace/User/Extensions' and 'Local/Remote' group labels with no explanation of what 'Local' vs 'Remote' means relative to the other groups.

## Best solution

Either merge/clarify the two grouping schemes in aiCustomizationListWidget.ts (e.g. rename 'remote-client'/'remote-host' groups to something that clearly ties them to the active harness, such as 'Synced from this machine' / 'From <harness>') or add a description/tooltip explaining the relationship, so the two group families read as one coherent taxonomy.
