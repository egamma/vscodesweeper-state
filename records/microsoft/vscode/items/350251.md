## Summary

Keep open for investigation of an MCP App overlay remaining over later chat content. Current source uses visibility observers and CSS-anchor clipping; the reported scrolling sequence and screenshots suffice to begin retesting, but source inspection cannot confirm the rendering failure.

## Change summary

An MCP App form shrinks and reappears over unrelated chat content after its original output scrolls away.

## Best solution

Ensure inline MCP App overlays remain aligned with their tool output and disappear when that output leaves the visible chat area, including after tool failure. Investigate the visibility and clipping lifecycle before attributing the problem to the MCP server or Chromium.
