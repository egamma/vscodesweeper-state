# Issue #109469 — Suggest widget: do not grow horizontally to the left if I am dragging to the right

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/109469](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/109469)

## Summary

Keep open for a resize-interaction decision: current source still allows rightward dragging to widen the suggest widget while viewport containment shifts its left edge leftward. The discussion explicitly weighs that behavior's usefulness against consistency with other resize interactions; no settled decision or verified canonical supersedes this report.

## Change summary

Prevent the suggest widget from growing leftward when its right resize handle is dragged beyond the window boundary.

## Best solution

Agree whether right-edge dragging should stop widening at the window boundary, prioritizing predictable resize direction over the ability to gain additional width by shifting left. Apply the chosen behavior specifically to suggest resizing without changing ordinary overflow-widget positioning.

- **Behavior:**

The reporter wants the left edge to remain stable and further widening to stop at the boundary. The existing discussion also values the ability to obtain extra width without resizing the window, so the plan must settle that observable tradeoff. Acceptance should cover boundary dragging, reversing the drag, releasing and reopening suggestions, and ordinary resizing away from the boundary.
- **Trace:**

  > ResizableHTMLElement's east-sash listener in src/vs/base/browser/ui/resizable/resizable.ts increases width from pointer displacement. SuggestWidget._layout in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidget.ts sets a body-wide maximum, while its onDidResize listener calls _resize and then SuggestContentWidget.layout. SuggestContentWidget allows editor overflow and locks only the above/below preference during a drag. The overflow placement helper _layoutHorizontalSegmentInPage in src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/contentWidgets/contentWidgets.ts shifts left when the widened widget crosses the right viewport limit, explaining the reported outcome directly from current source. The unresolved point is product behavior, not where to place a guard: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/109469 contains opposing arguments about directional consistency and usable width. Preserve ordinary overflow containment for other content widgets, vertical placement, size persistence, and double-click sizing; none requires a redesign for this issue.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should right-edge dragging stop widening when the widget reaches the window boundary, keeping its left edge stable, or retain the ability to gain width by shifting the widget left?
