# Issue #232813 — word wrap is different for left and right sides

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232813](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/232813)

## Summary

Still present on main: in the side-by-side diff editor, the overview ruler width is subtracted only from the modified (right) editor, making its content area narrower than the original (left) editor, so word wrap breaks at different columns.

## Change summary

In the side-by-side diff editor with word wrap enabled, text wraps at different columns on the left vs. right side.

## Best solution

Equalize the effective content widths of both editors in side-by-side mode by accounting for the overview ruler when splitting the available width, so word wrap breaks at the same column on both sides. The fix belongs in `src/vs/editor/browser/widget/diffEditor/diffEditorWidget.ts` in the layout autorun.
