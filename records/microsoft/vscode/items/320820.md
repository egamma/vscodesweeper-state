# Issue #320820 — Voice input should mute mic routing to other apps (e.g. Teams) while active

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/320820](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/320820)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: current microphone capture and mute controls do not isolate Copilot speech from other applications. No verified older canonical or maintainer scope decision supports closing it.

## Change summary

Temporarily prevent calling applications from receiving microphone audio while Copilot voice is listening, then restore their previous routing.

## Best solution

Have the voice/dictation owners assess whether reliable, consent-based isolation is feasible across supported platforms, including restoration after cancellation or failure. Existing Copilot mute controls are not a substitute, and the product should not imply cross-application privacy without an enforceable guarantee.
