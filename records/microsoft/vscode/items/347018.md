[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347018](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347018)

## Change summary

Subagent delegation from a BYOK conversation unexpectedly uses Copilot models and consumes personal AI credits despite BYOK model settings.

## Best solution

Identify the actual subagent tool and session engine involved, then ensure implicit built-in delegation honors BYOK selection without silently switching to Copilot. Preserve deliberate model choices in user-authored agents and explicit model overrides.
