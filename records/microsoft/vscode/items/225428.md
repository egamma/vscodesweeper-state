# Issue #225428 — Editor GPU: Rasterizer should support sub-pixel coordinates

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/225428](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/225428)

## Summary

Horizontal sub-pixel glyph offsets are already implemented, but this item carries the protected plan-item label and must remain open for its owner to assess completion.

## Change summary

Allow GPU-rendered glyphs to vary with their fractional pixel position instead of reusing identical rasterization at every position.

## Best solution

Retain the existing position-dependent rasterization and have the GPU renderer owner determine whether it completes the planned scope or whether further coordinate support remains.
