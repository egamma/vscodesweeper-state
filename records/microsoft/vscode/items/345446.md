## Evidence

- **Concrete reproduction and workaround:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345446 reports vscode.dev on Android 17, fractional scaling such as 150%, and blur only with terminal GPU acceleration enabled. Disabling acceleration resolves it, including with extensions disabled.
- **Current renderer selection:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/xterm/xtermTerminal.ts:628-645 selects WebGL through _shouldLoadWebgl() and disposes that renderer when acceleration is disabled. _enableWebglRenderer() loads @xterm/addon-webgl and requests refreshed terminal dimensions after loading.
- **Scaling boundary:** getXtermScaledDimensions() in src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/xterm/xtermTerminal.ts:1190-1209 uses window.devicePixelRatio for terminal sizing. This identifies a relevant integration boundary, but does not prove a canvas-resolution or compositing defect.
