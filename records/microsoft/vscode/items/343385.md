# Issue #343385 — Agents window renderer grows toward 4 GB and crashes with OOM in new sessions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343385](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343385)

## Summary

Keep open: independent reports corroborate renderer OOM crashes, but neither a complete fix nor a matching canonical has been established. The linked optimization addresses large-file allocations, not conclusively the reported growth with plugins and protocol logging disabled.

## Change summary

Prevent the separate Agents window from exhausting renderer memory during active or idle remote sessions.

## Best solution

Isolate the allocation or retention responsible for new-session renderer growth, then eliminate it without requiring users to disable AI. Distinguish this from large-output, plugin-file, external-index enumeration, and growing-editor-file failures before consolidating reports.

- **Related optimization is not a demonstrated complete fix:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/345802 merged as 128ed191142f3aa054654fc1fb98b81fdc2b4464 and explicitly says 'Related to' this issue. Its actual diff optimizes buffer encoding, queues AHPFileSystemProvider.readFile calls, and avoids full oversized log serialization when byte length is known. Those changes address large resource transfers; this report does not establish that trigger and documents recurrence with plugins and AHP logging disabled.
- **Checkout limitation:** The supplied checkout HEAD is d6b179934e60dd126f1c633ad18cafc89bb1a634, dated 2026-11-02, and does not contain the November 5 optimization. Its src/vs/platform/agentHost/common/agentHostFileSystemProvider.ts readFile still requests and decodes whole Base64 resources without that limiter. Source inspection therefore cannot establish behavior after the merged optimization; no application execution was performed.
- **Canonical search:** Verified https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345353 remains open, but it concerns immediate crash-looping from a huge repeated tool-call path, not established here; it is also younger with no milestone or reactions. Open https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343006 requires large streamed shell output. Neither satisfies this re-reviewed item's duplicate bar.
- **Older related report does not establish identical scope:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324809 is open and older, but concerns the ordinary Remote-SSH workbench, with recent external-ingest enumeration evidence. This issue identifies the separate Agents renderer; no evidence connects its new-session growth to that enumeration path. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343103 instead documents remote agent-host OOM and large binary reads, a different established process and trigger.

That observation must not be treated as proof of the Agents-window leak's mechanism.
