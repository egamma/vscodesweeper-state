[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/137427](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/137427)

Keep open: the process explorer still lacks reliable service-worker identification, and the discussion identifies missing Electron/Chromium runtime metadata as the blocker. No concrete fix or qualifying older duplicate was found.

## Change summary

Distinguish webview service-worker renderer processes from workbench windows in Process Explorer.

## Best solution

Expose reliable worker-type or worker-PID metadata through Electron, then use it in VS Code's process naming pipeline. Preserve accurate workbench-window names rather than treating every unnamed renderer as a service worker.
