# Issue #100147 — Support reparenting views during drag and drop without destroying and recreating them

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/100147](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/100147)

## Summary

The framework gap remains on current main: moving a view between containers still recreates and disposes its pane, while Refactor Preview remains non-movable to protect its transient state. Keep this Backlog request open for architectural discussion.

## Change summary

Allow stateful views to move between workbench containers by reparenting their live pane instead of destroying and recreating it.

## Best solution

Add lifecycle-preserving pane transfer support to the workbench views framework, likely across ViewPaneContainer and ViewDescriptorService, then enable movement for BulkEditPane without interrupting its active setInput operation.

## Evidence

- **current lifecycle:** src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/views/viewPaneContainer.ts creates panes through createView, while onDidRemoveViewDescriptors removes them and addPane owns the pane in a DisposableStore; moving between containers therefore still destroys and recreates the instance.
- **transient state:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/bulkEdit/browser/preview/bulkEditPane.ts keeps the active provider, input, and completion callback on the pane instance during setInput, making ordinary serialization insufficient.
- **maintainer direction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/100147#issuecomment-654707731 recommends pulling live UI reattachment into the views framework rather than requiring this passive view to reconstruct its state.
