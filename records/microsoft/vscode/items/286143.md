## Change summary

Request for Microsoft to publish and maintain an official VS Code Flatpak on Flathub with proper portal integration.

## Best solution

This needs a product decision from the team: official Flatpak maintenance is a significant ongoing commitment alongside existing .deb/.rpm/snap/tarball distribution. The POC in the comments (flatpak-spawn host-remote extension) shows the sandbox-escape pattern could work as an extension in the meantime. Keep open for the community process to gauge demand.
