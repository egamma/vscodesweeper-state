# Issue #24815 — Allow to reset “Don't Show Again” preference

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/24815](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/24815)

## Summary

Keep open: current notification controls do not undo individual “Don't Show Again” choices, and the two linked management PRs were closed without merging. The request has 50 upvotes and explicit maintainer support; no verified older canonical owns the same work.

## Change summary

Provide an in-app way to reset persisted “Don't Show Again” choices without deleting unrelated state or editing SQLite databases.

## Best solution

Provide a discoverable recovery mechanism in the notification area that restores suppressed messages while preserving unrelated settings and state. Agree on selective versus bulk reset behavior and explicitly define coverage for independently stored core and extension preferences.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/notification/common/notification.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/services/notification/common/notificationService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/notifications/notificationsCommands.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/services/integrity/electron-browser/integrityService.ts`
- **Validation:** Exercise a notification with neverShowAgain: dismiss it permanently, confirm repeating its trigger produces no notification, invoke the agreed recovery action, and confirm repeating the trigger shows it again after reload. Cover the agreed application/profile/workspace boundaries, verify unrelated stored state and Do Not Disturb filters remain unchanged, and separately verify any independently stored preferences included in scope.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users need to recover messages they accidentally suppressed with “Don't Show Again” without uninstalling VS Code, deleting storage, or editing a database. A discoverable in-app recovery action should allow those messages to appear again when their normal trigger occurs, including after reload. Unrelated settings, extension state and notification filters must be preserved. The acceptance contract must state whether recovery is selective or bulk and which storage scopes and independently managed prompts it covers.
- **Trace:**

  > NotificationService.notify() and prompt() in src/vs/workbench/services/notification/common/notificationService.ts store individual suppression booleans and suppress subsequent requests before adding a notification to the model. INeverShowAgainOptions in src/vs/platform/notification/common/notification.ts identifies the preference and its scope but supplies no display metadata or reset contract. notificationsCommands.ts exposes clearing and Do Not Disturb controls, which do not reset those flags. IntegrityService in src/vs/workbench/services/integrity/electron-browser/integrityService.ts illustrates a separate core-owned suppression mechanism, and the thread also names extension-owned storage. The management attempts at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/114105 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/114114 were closed without merging. The central gap is established from source; uncertainty begins at the intended recovery UX and coverage boundary, not whether Do Not Disturb already satisfies the request. Preserve unrelated storage and existing notification delivery semantics.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should users restore individual suppressed messages, reset all suppression choices, or have both options?
  - Should recovery act on the current workspace/profile, expose explicit scope choices, or reset suppression across all supported scopes?
  - Should the initial recovery contract cover only shared neverShowAgain notifications, or also independently stored core and extension prompts named in the discussion?
