[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209632](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209632)

## Summary

Keep this diff-editor polish item open: current source centers the Stage/Revert toolbar within the entire hunk and clamps it into the viewport, rather than consistently centering it on screen for large diffs. No verified canonical or completed fix covers that remaining request.

## Change summary

Make Stage/Revert buttons predictably appear near the viewport center when reviewing a large diff hunk.

## Best solution
