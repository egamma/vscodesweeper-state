## Summary

The requested responsive behavior is implemented and shipped: secondary-side-bar title actions now progressively move into the overflow menu while actions beyond the separator remain visible.

## Change summary

Make view-container title toolbars collapse actions into the overflow menu when the secondary side bar becomes too narrow.

## Best solution

Keep the responsive auxiliary-bar toolbar behavior delivered by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/335039, which preserves the global controls and moves preceding actions into the overflow menu as space contracts.
