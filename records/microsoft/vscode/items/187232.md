## Summary

Keep open: two reporters observed the same fractional-pixel seam on macOS and Windows, and the second report ties it to specific zoom levels. No open canonical issue or concrete fixing commit was found.

## Change summary

The remote status-bar indicator can leave a white line along its bottom edge at certain window heights or zoom levels.

## Best solution

Reproduce the seam on a current macOS desktop build and vscode.dev at fractional zoom, then make the remote status-bar item's background cover its full rendered height without changing status-bar theming, hover behavior, or neighboring items.
