# Issue #300450 — Main menu is inactive and inaccessible sometimes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/300450](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/300450)

## Summary

Keep open: multiple reporters confirm intermittent custom-menu inaccessibility and incorrect window-focus styling across releases. No verified older canonical or concrete fixing commit establishes that this report is resolved.

## Change summary

The custom title bar and menu sometimes retain the wrong focus state, with menu dropdowns becoming inaccessible or appearing behind editor content.

## Best solution

Diagnose the native-to-renderer focus transition and custom-title-bar stacking behavior, then ensure the menu remains usable after window activation. Establish whether the incorrect focus appearance and obscured dropdowns share a cause before treating them as separate defects.
