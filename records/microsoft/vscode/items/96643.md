Thanks for the report. The original identity mismatch was fixed in 2020 and the reporter confirmed it, but the issue was reopened after a later race-condition report. I can't confirm from source whether a defect remains on current main.

## Change summary

TaskExecution and Task objects delivered to the task start/end callbacks are not identical (===) to the ones returned by executeTask.
