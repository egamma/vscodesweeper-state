# Issue #343902 — Auto open 'workspace' picker after picking an environment?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902)

## Summary

Keep open: automatic workspace selection was explicitly removed from the merged picker-polish PR, and the maintainer reopened this request. Current main still requires manual project selection; the desired focus behavior needs a product decision.

## Change summary

Automatically open or focus the workspace picker after switching between local and remote environments.

## Best solution

Agree on a predictable environment-to-workspace focus transition in the new-session composer, considering the suggested experimental opt-in. Preserve draft input, remembered workspaces, and safe behavior during failed or superseded remote connections.
