# Issue #343902 — Auto open 'workspace' picker after picking an environment?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902)

## Summary

Keep open for a focus-flow design decision: switching environments does not automatically advance to workspace selection. This team-authored request remains actionable; the existing manual picker command does not satisfy the automatic handoff.

## Change summary

After switching between local and remote environments, automatically open or focus the workspace picker instead of returning to the prompt.

## Best solution

Make workspace selection the natural next step after an environment change when a workspace must be chosen. Decide whether that means opening the picker or focusing its trigger, and preserve intentional workspace-less chat and compatible workspace selections.
