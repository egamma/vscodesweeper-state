# Issue #343902 — Auto open 'workspace' picker after picking an environment?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343902)

## Summary

Keep open: the merged picker-polish PR explicitly removed automatic workspace selection, and current source still leaves selection manual. This reopened, team-authored request needs a decision between opening the workspace picker and moving focus to its control.

## Change summary

After switching environments, automatically open or focus the workspace picker instead of returning to the input.

## Best solution

Agree on the workspace-picker handoff after an explicit environment change, considering the maintainer's experimental opt-in suggestion. Preserve remembered workspaces, draft content, and connection-failure behavior.
