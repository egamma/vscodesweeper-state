[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341705](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341705)

## Summary

Keep open for investigation of custom-agent application in a Windows-to-WSL workspace. The reporter supplied the requested storage path and confirmed persistence through 1.143.1; current source has customization synchronization and agent-resolution paths to investigate, but the exact failure remains unconfirmed and the supplied steps suffice to retest.

## Change summary

Selected custom agents reportedly fail to apply their instructions when their files are stored in the WSL user's ~/.copilot/agents directory.

## Best solution

Ensure the selected custom agent's actual file contents reach the active conversation across the Windows/WSL boundary, including after switching agents. First distinguish customization discovery or synchronization failure from selection resolution and conversation-history effects using a fresh canary conversation.
