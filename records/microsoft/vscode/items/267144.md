- **Validation:** On Windows with a screen reader active, enable screen-reader rendering and EditContext, create seven lines containing a through g, place the cursor on line 5, press Ctrl+G, then Escape without entering a destination. Verify the cursor remains at the original line and column. Repeat with an entered but unaccepted destination, and verify Enter still navigates to an accepted destination.
- **Behavior:**

  > With screen-reader rendering and EditContext enabled on Windows, opening Go to Line and cancelling with Escape must leave the cursor at its original line and column. The reported failure moves it from line 5 to the top of the file. Acceptance must cover cancellation without input and cancellation after entering a destination, while preserving explicit Enter navigation.
- **Trace:**

  > AbstractEditorNavigationQuickAccessProvider.doProvide in src/vs/editor/contrib/quickAccess/browser/editorNavigationQuickAccess.ts tracks editor view state on every cursor-position event and restores it on cancellation. AbstractGotoLineQuickAccessProvider.provideWithTextEditor in src/vs/editor/contrib/quickAccess/browser/gotoLineQuickAccess.ts previews through reveal and decorations, not selection changes; gotoLocation runs on acceptance. NativeEditContext forwards focus transitions to ScreenReaderSupport.handleFocusChange, which writes screen-reader content. The maintainer confirmed the Windows symptom, but the current event ordering and source of the unwanted selection change have not been established. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/271610 was closed without merging; do not assume its JUMP-filter diagnosis is correct. Preserve accepted navigation and the documented ability to update the restoration point when users intentionally move the editor cursor while quick access remains open after losing focus.
