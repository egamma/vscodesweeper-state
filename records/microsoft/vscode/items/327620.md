[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327620](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327620)

## Summary

Confirmed on current main: trajectory-based cancellation is disconnected while superseded suggestions still rely on it, leaving speculative requests alive after divergence. This team-authored bug should remain open while the false-positive scenario is identified and the cancellation lifecycle is made consistent.

## Change summary

Cancel obsolete NES speculative requests when a superseded suggestion diverges from its type-through trajectory.

## Best solution

First encode the false-positive cancellation referenced by the FIXME as a regression test, then either safely reconnect `SpeculativeRequestManager.onActiveDocumentChanged` or give `handleIgnored` explicit superseded-request handling and remove the unused trajectory machinery.
