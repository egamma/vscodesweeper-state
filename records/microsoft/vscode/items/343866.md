# Issue #343866 — Plugins and skills within plugins should show a 'last updated at' and authors

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343866](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343866)

## Summary

Keep open: repository provenance helps investigate freshness but does not provide the requested authors and update dates. Current source confirms that the metadata gap remains; this team-authored request needs a metadata contract decision.

## Change summary

Show authorship and last-updated information for plugins and their individual skills.

## Best solution

Define authoritative authorship and freshness metadata, including whether dates describe installed content or the latest available content, then expose it consistently for plugins and their skills without treating publisher identity or installation time as equivalent.
