## Summary

Verified duplicate of the older open https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316908: both report that changing modes through the chat mode picker destroys an unsent draft and submits `Start implementation` instead. The Agent-to-Ask/Plan direction is useful additional reproduction evidence but does not require separate tracking.
