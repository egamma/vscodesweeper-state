## Evidence

- **Verified older canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/293468 was created on 2026-02-06 and is currently open. Its steps describe opening a Mermaid diagram from chat in an editor, zooming in with Ctrl+mousewheel, then seeing the diagram zoom far out with one downward notch. The reporter confirms Windows with a standard mouse wheel at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/293468#issuecomment-3863379738.
- **Same observable symptom and shared implementation:** extensions/mermaid-markdown-features/preview-src/chat/index.ts and index-editor.ts both call initializeMermaidWebview in mermaidWebview.ts, which constructs the same PanZoomHandler. The editor's defaultView option changes initial framing, not wheel handling; the older report therefore covers the same failing interaction rather than merely a neighboring zoom problem.
- **Current-source defect:** PanZoomHandler.handleWheel in extensions/mermaid-markdown-features/preview-src/chat/mermaidWebview.ts treats every Ctrl-modified wheel event as a pinch and multiplies zoomFactor 0.002 by five. It computes scale * (1 - deltaY * 0.01), clamped to 0.1–5: a positive deltaY of 100 or more immediately reaches the minimum scale, whereas the opposite direction increases scale. This confirms the asymmetric jump path without executing the application.
- **Report-specific evidence to preserve:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316966 was filed while testing chat Mermaid rendering under https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316726. Its maintainer comment reports that Alt+scroll is more stable and asks whether Ctrl+scroll should be disabled; that is a design question, not an established decision to remove the interaction.
