# Issue #316966 — Ctrl + scroll to zoom out has a massive jump

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316966](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316966)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the mermaid diagram Ctrl+scroll zoom on Windows applies a pinchMultiplier of 10 to regular scroll wheel deltaY values, causing massive zoom jumps — especially visible when zooming out due to the multiplicative scale formula's inherent asymmetry.

## Change summary

Ctrl+scroll to zoom out on mermaid diagrams jumps drastically compared to zooming in, because the wheel handler treats Ctrl+scroll the same as pinch-zoom and applies a 10× sensitivity multiplier.

## Best solution

Distinguish between actual trackpad pinch gestures and Ctrl+scroll wheel events in `handleWheel` (e.g. by checking `e.deltaMode` or deltaY magnitude heuristics), and apply the 10× pinch multiplier only for true pinch gestures. The fix belongs in `extensions/mermaid-markdown-features/preview-src/shared/diagramManager.ts`.
