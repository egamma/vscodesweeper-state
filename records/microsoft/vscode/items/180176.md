## Summary

The inline-suggestion sound was replaced in January 2025, but the available evidence does not establish that the replacement resolves the reported listening fatigue. Keep this accepted accessibility request open for a listening review of the current asset; the older related issues do not establish a same-symptom duplicate.

## Change summary

Make the frequently played inline-suggestion audio cue less intrusive and tiring without losing its usefulness.
