# Issue #339344 — Improve keyboard access to Quick Pick item buttons with Tab

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339344](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339344)

## Summary

Keep open for a keyboard-interaction design decision: current item toolbars provide keyboard access, but not the proposed direct action/navigation cycle. Older reports cover individual parts of the request, not its full scope.

## Change summary

Make Quick Pick item actions easier to reach, cancel, and repeat from the keyboard while preserving filter access and meaningful item focus.

## Best solution

Define a consistent Quick Pick item-action interaction that supports returning to filtering and continuing navigation after an item is removed. Settle whether Tab may activate a single action, including destructive actions, before changing its existing focus-navigation role.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputActions.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputController.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputList.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/pickerQuickAccess.ts`
- **Validation:** After agreeing on semantics, exercise a keyboard-only picker with zero, one, and multiple item buttons: filter, navigate to a middle item, enter actions, cancel without activation, activate an action that keeps the picker open, and resume filtering/navigation. Repeat removal of middle and final items; verify meaningful successor focus, continued access to title buttons and checkboxes, Shift+Tab behavior, and screen-reader focus announcements. Check the reported Tab/arrow dismissal independently against the loop documented in issue 142157.
- **Behavior:**

  > Quick Pick item actions should participate in a predictable keyboard workflow that allows filtering, selecting an item, invoking or canceling an action, and continuing without reopening the picker or restarting navigation. When an action removes the active item and the picker remains open, users should retain a meaningful navigation position. Multiple-action items must remain operable with toolbar arrow navigation and explicit activation. Acceptance must include access to other picker controls and screen-reader users; whether Tab itself activates a single action is not yet agreed.
- **Trace:**

  > BaseQuickInputListRenderer.renderTemplate in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputList.ts creates the item ToolBar; src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputActions.ts has no Tab command for the proposed interaction. QuickInputController in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputController.ts tracks container focus and hides on blur unless ignoreFocusOut applies. In src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/pickerQuickAccess.ts, buttonTrigger handles removal by filtering the removed item out of activeItems without selecting its successor. The source establishes relevant paths, but does not prove the reported present-day dismissal across all pickers. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/207191 overlaps direct toolbar entry, and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/134000 overlaps update-related focus retention; neither absorbs the complete proposal. Preserve ordinary item acceptance, provider-requested picker closure, toolbar arrow navigation, and access to non-item controls. The planning boundary is the user-visible Tab contract and focus behavior, not a general ActionBar redesign.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should Tab only focus item actions, or activate an item's sole action directly, including Close and other destructive or extension-defined actions?
  - Should direct item-action entry apply whenever an item is active, or only after explicit list navigation, preserving ordinary traversal to title buttons and other controls while typing?

## Evidence

- **Existing capability and remaining gap:** src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputList.ts, BaseQuickInputListRenderer.renderTemplate, constructs a ToolBar for item actions. src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputActions.ts registers item navigation, acceptance, hiding, checkbox, and hover commands, but no Tab command implementing the requested action/navigation cycle. Existing keyboard accessibility therefore does not fully deliver the requested interaction.
- **Removal behavior:** src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/pickerQuickAccess.ts, buttonTrigger's TriggerAction.REMOVE_ITEM branch, removes the item and filters it out of activeItems without choosing a successor. keepScrollPosition preserves scrolling, not a meaningful replacement active item. This supports investigating the repeated-action workflow, without asserting that every picker loses selection after every action.
