This matches the reporter-observable symptom and satisfies the stronger duplicate bar for a re-reviewed item.
- **Current source explains the behavior:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/chatEditing/chatEditingEditorActions.ts: AcceptRejectHunkAction binds chatEditor.action.undoHunk to CtrlCmd+N with WorkbenchContrib+1 priority while the cursor is inside a modified hunk. Its runChatEditingCommand calls rejectNearestChange and, when chat.editing.revealNextChangeOnResolve is enabled and the file has no remaining changes, opens the next modified file through openNextOrPreviousChange.
- **Canonical covers cross-file rejection too:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/258281#issuecomment-3745609155 describes repeatedly pressing the new-window shortcut, discarding edits, and jumping through modified files. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/258281#issuecomment-3877137351 independently reports Cmd+N unexpectedly undoing multiple edits.
- **Maintainer discussion remains unresolved:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/258281#issuecomment-3473464995 describes restricting hunk shortcuts to the cursor's hunk, not removing the collision. Current source retains that restriction and CtrlCmd+N binding; this is not evidence of a completed fix.
