# Issue #199377 — Sparkle should render on new line

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/199377](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/199377)

## Summary

The positioning concern remains on current main, but the interaction tradeoff is intentionally under discussion. This team-authored Backlog issue is protected from a judgment close and should remain open until the editor owners decide whether same-line placement is preferable.

## Change summary

Place the AI code-action sparkle on the cursor's new line rather than relocating it to the preceding line.

## Best solution

Settle the interaction policy for code-action affordances at column one, then, if adopted, render the sparkle beside the cursor on an empty line without obstructing typing or pointer interaction. The relevant positioning logic is in `LightBulbWidget.update` in `src/vs/editor/contrib/codeAction/browser/lightBulbWidget.ts`.
