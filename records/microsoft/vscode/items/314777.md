- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter's toolbar-label finding is already resolved; the remaining findings concern a tabbable list container and unsupported ARIA attributes on dialog rows. Acceptance includes clean role/attribute checks for both toasts and the notification center, plus screen-reader checks that preserve understandable notification announcements and navigation.
- **Trace:**

  > NotificationsList supplies NotificationAccessibilityProvider, whose getRole() returns 'dialog' and whose missing getWidgetRole() leaves List's container role at 'list'. ListView makes the container tabbable and unconditionally adds aria-setsize and aria-posinset to every rendered row; SelectionTrait also adds aria-selected for this container role. Both NotificationsToasts and NotificationsCenter use this path. The dialog role is deliberate: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/82728#issuecomment-617865378 documents the parent-list/child-dialog arrangement. The unsupported row attributes are conclusively present in current source, but the appropriate replacement or focus behavior for the container is not settled by this report. Preserve embedded controls, existing notification announcements, and unrelated list consumers; do not reopen the already-fixed toolbar-label finding.
