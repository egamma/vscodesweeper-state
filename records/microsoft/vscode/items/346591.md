# Issue #346591 — Agents window: nesting a pinned session by drag loses its pin, and Move to New Session doesn't restore it

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346591](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346591)

## Summary

The current source explains the reported round-trip: promotion allocates a new session identity without restoring the original pin and assigns fresh timestamps. Keep this team-authored bug open; group inheritance is already intentional, while restoration versus clearer nesting feedback needs a maintainer decision.

## Change summary

Nesting a pinned session and moving its chat back to a new session loses its visible pin and original recency, placing it in the destination's group and Inbox.

## Best solution

Make accidental nesting recoverable without treating existing work as newly created, or explicitly communicate the nesting outcome before the drop. Preserve ordinary promotion and group-inheritance behavior unless the maintainer chooses a different round-trip contract.
