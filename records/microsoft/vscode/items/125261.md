The reported whitespace removal remains source-reproducible: closing-bracket handling replaces the line's indentation without respecting editor.autoIndent: "keep". Keep this accepted backlog bug open; the older related reports inspected do not track the same observable defect.

## Change summary

Preserve manually entered leading whitespace when typing a closing bracket or brace with editor.autoIndent set to "keep".
