- **Matching wallet trigger:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248664#issuecomment-2883254315, the canonical reporter identifies KWallet involvement and confirms that enabling KWallet eliminates the freeze. They link https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=504014 as a suspected upstream explanation; that attribution is a lead, not independently verified root-cause proof.
- **Diagnostic evidence to preserve:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248978 records two approximately 25-second synchronous D-Bus timeouts for org.kde.KWallet.isEnabled and close, bracketed by EncryptionMainService availability-check logs. Three independent commenters confirm the startup problem or workaround, including persistence in 1.104.0 and hangs when a wallet prompt is left unanswered or dismissed.
- **Current source boundary:** src/vs/platform/encryption/electron-main/encryptionMainService.ts, EncryptionMainService.isEncryptionAvailable(), still calls Electron safeStorage.isEncryptionAvailable() synchronously before returning Promise.resolve(result). This matches the reported log boundary and supports consolidating the same encryption-initialization freeze, but does not establish a current runtime reproduction or a landed fix.
- **Other related report:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/280093 is also open, but describes partially improved startup delays and additional profiling-port problems. The older https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248664 is the closer canonical match for this report's temporary input freeze.
