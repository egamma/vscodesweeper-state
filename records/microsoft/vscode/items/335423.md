## Summary

Verified against source: the Outline view's `outline.collapseItems` setting only supports `alwaysCollapse`/`alwaysExpand` (all-or-nothing), and `outline.showMethods: false` filters out method-level symbols entirely rather than controlling expand depth — so the reporter's ask (collapse to method level, expand individual methods on demand) genuinely isn't covered today. Already correctly labeled `feature-request`/`outline` and just entered the Backlog Candidates 60-day community process, so it should stay open on its own merits.
