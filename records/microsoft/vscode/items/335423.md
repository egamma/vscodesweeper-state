# Issue #335423 — Outline Window Missing Functionality

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335423](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335423)

## Summary

Keep open in the existing Backlog Candidates process: current Outline controls do not offer method-level collapse while preserving expandable children. The closely matching historical requests are closed, and the open follow-cursor request concerns different behavior.

## Change summary

Allow the Outline tree to show methods with their contents collapsed, without hiding those contents or collapsing their ancestor containers.

## Best solution

Provide selective Outline collapse that leaves methods visible and their children available through expansion, rather than using symbol visibility filters. Retain the existing all-expanded and all-collapsed behavior; adoption should follow the community-review and area-owner decision.

## Evidence

- **Existing-capability check:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/outline/browser/outline.contribution.ts registers outline.collapseItems with only alwaysCollapse and alwaysExpand. outline.showMethods controls method-symbol visibility, not whether method children are collapsed. Neither setting delivers the requested selective collapse.
- **Current action and tree behavior:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/outline/browser/outlineActions.ts registers outline.collapse and outline.expand, which call OutlinePane.collapseAll() and expandAll(). src/vs/workbench/contrib/codeEditor/browser/outline/documentSymbolsOutline.ts constructs a single boolean collapseByDefault from outline.collapseItems, rather than a method-kind or depth-specific policy. Individual expansion and remembered tree state in outlinePane.ts are partial workarounds, not the requested bulk control.
- **Matching historical requests are closed:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/244864, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/179484, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/212567, and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149525 request intermediate Outline expansion levels. Their discussions show closure for insufficient community upvotes, not implementation or a substantive decision that this behavior is intentionally excluded. They cannot serve as open duplicate canonicals.
- **Open related request has different scope:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/206597 is open and asks Follow Cursor to collapse the previously active method when expanding the current method. It does not own this report's request to leave methods visible while collapsing their contents independently of cursor navigation.
