## Change summary

Avoid repeated synchronous style and layout recalculations when terminal output mirrors render in large restored chats.

## Best solution

Terminal output rendering should coordinate measurements across parts without forcing a separate document recalculation after each part's writes, while preserving terminal reflow, row sizing, scrolling, and expansion behavior. Address this specific contributor independently of the broader chat-opening performance investigation.
