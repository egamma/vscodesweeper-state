# Issue #298895 — Reverse double-click bracket selection direction

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298895](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298895)

## Summary

Still present on main: `_trySelectBracketContent` in viewController.ts always creates a left-to-right selection regardless of which bracket side was double-clicked. The issue is properly triaged and belongs in Backlog.

## Change summary

Double-clicking a closing bracket should create a right-to-left selection (cursor at content start), but currently both sides produce left-to-right.

## Best solution

In `_trySelectBracketContent` (src/vs/editor/browser/view/viewController.ts, line 153), reverse the selection direction for the closing-bracket branch so the anchor is at the closing bracket and the active position is at the opening bracket end.
