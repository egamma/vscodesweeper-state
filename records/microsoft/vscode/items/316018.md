- **Report supplies actionable scenarios:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316018 describes initially correct reading followed by stale or joined braille lines after editing, navigation problems at the bottom of files, and “group” output in the sidebar and console. The reporter also supplied a video repository at https://github.com/vincentmartin84/voice_over_vs_code.
- **Independent reproduction and upstream diagnosis:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/319725#issuecomment-5283836111 supplies a three-line editing/navigation reproduction. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/319725#issuecomment-5511690655 reports matching failures in plain textareas and VS Code-like ARIA textboxes, with direct macOS AX queries showing incorrect AXLineForIndex and AXRangeForLine results. This identifies the primary synchronization failure as Chromium-owned rather than an editor accessibility-setting problem.
- **Upstream fix and current runtime:** https://github.com/electron/electron/pull/53428 merged the Chromium line-API fix: use upper_bound minus one for AXLineForIndex and forward nonzero line indexes for AXRangeForLine. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/319725#issuecomment-5537813733 reports delivery in Electron 43.6.0 and successful physical-braille testing in a local VS Code build on Electron 44. Current checkout .npmrc targets Electron 43.7.7. This supports likely resolution of the primary symptom, not verification of every reported surface.
- **Relevant editor paths inspected:** src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/screenReaderSupport.ts exposes a multiline role=textbox; SimpleScreenReaderContent.updateScreenReaderContent in screenReaderContentSimple.ts updates its text and DOM selection. The alternative textAreaEditContext.ts refreshes native textarea content on cursor changes and rendering. Both use the platform text accessibility behavior covered by the upstream diagnosis; source inspection cannot verify physical braille output.
