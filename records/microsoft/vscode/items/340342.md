[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340342](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340342)

## Summary

Keep this team-authored umbrella open to track cross-harness BYOK parity. Local bridging does not complete the multi-surface, remote, and cross-client continuity goals, and both harness-specific child issues remain open.

## Change summary

Support BYOK across agent harnesses, client surfaces, and local or remote environments while preserving session continuity between clients.

## Best solution

Retain this issue as the cross-harness acceptance tracker, with implementation and design work owned by the linked Copilot and Codex child issues. Completion should establish that configured models remain usable when a session is continued from another client, rather than relying solely on a connected VS Code renderer.
