# Issue #134190 — Not showing pencil on left margin of settings.json

## Summary

Still valid on current main: the edit-preference pencil icon in settings.json is restricted to boolean/enum settings by the filter at line 308 of preferencesRenderers.ts. A team member acknowledged this could be extended but no fix has landed.

## Change summary

Show the pencil/edit icon in the glyph margin for all settings in settings.json, not just boolean/enum ones.

## Best solution
