The idle-eviction failure remains in current source: send_message starts a turn without restoring the target's ownership registration. Keep this open; the independent reproduction and UI-reopen workaround match that path.

## Change summary

Restore an idle-evicted target before send_message starts its turn, instead of throwing Unknown chat owner.
