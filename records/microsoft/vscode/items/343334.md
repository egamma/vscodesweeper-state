# Issue #343334 — Migrate VS Code-installed agent plugins to Copilot SDK-managed installations

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343334](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343334)

## Summary

Keep open: the current migration registers marketplaces but does not migrate existing VS Code-managed plugin installations to SDK ownership. The discussion also identifies a concrete cross-harness compatibility requirement that the migration design must preserve.

## Change summary

Provide a retryable migration of existing VS Code-installed plugins to Copilot SDK ownership without losing enablement or breaking other harnesses.

## Best solution

Provide an explicit, per-plugin migration that verifies SDK ownership before suppressing Copilot client forwarding, preserves enablement, and retains compatibility for unsuccessful migrations. Settle the retention and shared-installation contract for Local, Claude, and Codex before removing legacy records or files.

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/customizationMigrationServiceImpl.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/customizationMarketplaceInstallService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/plugins/fileBackedInstalledPluginsStore.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/plugins/agentPluginServiceImpl.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/agentSessions/agentHost/agentHostLocalCustomizations.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/test/browser/aiCustomization/customizationMigrationServiceImpl.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/test/common/plugins/fileBackedInstalledPluginsStore.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Extend the customizationMigrationServiceImpl and fileBackedInstalledPluginsStore test suites to cover eligible legacy installations, already-SDK-installed plugins, preserved disabled state, unsupported sources, partial failure and retry. Acceptance must also verify exactly one Copilot contribution after confirmed migration, continued client delivery after failure, and unchanged availability to Local, Claude, and Codex under the agreed retention contract.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users with plugins recorded in VS Code's installed.json can explicitly migrate eligible installations to Copilot SDK management. Already-installed plugins are not installed twice, applicable enablement survives, and unsupported sources or individual failures remain visible and retryable. Copilot must neither lose contributions nor receive duplicates during the handoff. Existing consumers of VS Code-managed plugins must continue working under an explicitly agreed retention policy.
- **Trace:**

  > CustomizationMigrationServiceImpl currently computes marketplace-registration candidates from settings and calls marketplaceManagementProvider.add; it does not migrate installed plugin records. FileBackedInstalledPluginsStore owns the legacy inventory, MarketplaceAgentPluginDiscovery exposes its installations, and resolveCustomizationRefs forwards plugin directories with profile enablement. CustomizationMarketplaceInstallService already delegates explicit installations to a provider, but source inspection did not establish a legacy-record-to-SDK adoption flow. Preserve the existing compatibility path until ownership is verified; marketplace registration alone is not that verification. The comment on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343334 raises shared Claude/Codex usage, with link-install behavior tracked separately at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344698. The unresolved boundary is how successful Copilot migration affects shared legacy files, future updates, and uninstall behavior; SDK adoption and enablement mapping also require further tracing.
- **Open decisions:**
  - After successful Copilot migration, should the VS Code-managed installation remain independently managed for Local, Claude, and Codex, or should those harnesses share an adopted installation, and what update/uninstall behavior should users observe?
  - When may legacy installation records and files be cleaned up, and what user-visible retention or cleanup choice is required while other harnesses still depend on them?
