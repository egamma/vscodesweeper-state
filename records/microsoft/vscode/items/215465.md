# Issue #215465 — Give an option to not autoclose after word character other than "|' characters

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/215465](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/215465)

## Summary

A real configuration gap remains on current main, and the request earned 20 community upvotes and promotion to the Backlog. No existing setting or canonical issue covers suppressing additional autoclosing characters after word characters.

## Change summary

Allow languages or users to specify additional autoclosing characters, such as Scala backticks, that should not close automatically after a word character.

## Best solution

Add a language-defined, preferably pair-level control for suppressing autoclosing after word characters, rather than another global editor toggle. This likely belongs in the language-configuration autoclosing-pair model and cursor typing operation.
