# Issue #236671 — Formatting changes ending on \r result in an extra new line

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/236671](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/236671)

## Summary

A fix was merged (commit 365bd13fee5) and shipped in 1.108.0, but the reporter said it did not resolve the problem, and a maintainer (mjbvz) reopened the issue and moved it to On Deck. ## Change summary

Formatting edits from language servers whose replacement text ends with a trailing \r produce a spurious extra newline in CRLF files.

## Best solution

The editor team should investigate why the fix in commit 365bd13fee5 (stripping trailing \r when the range ends at end-of-line in a CRLF buffer) does not cover the clangd formatting scenario reported here. The reporter's LSP response shows the replacement text ending with \r where the range end is at character 15 on line 2 — it's possible the range doesn't land exactly at getLineMaxColumn, or there's an interaction with range validation that prevents the guard from triggering. A targeted reproduction with the exact LSP edit from Comment 3 would clarify.
