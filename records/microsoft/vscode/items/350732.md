## Summary

Keep open: current main still unconditionally relayouts visible Chat on unrelated part-visibility events, reaching explicit picker measurements even when its geometry and location are unchanged. The related picker optimizations do not remove this redundant caller-side work.

## Change summary

Avoid redundant Chat view layouts and picker measurements when sidebar or Zen mode toggles leave the view's layout inputs unchanged.

## Best solution

Make ChatViewPane's visibility-event handling avoid unnecessary full layouts while preserving updates for actual geometry, position, location, and visibility changes. Retain the sidebar-layout correctness addressed by the earlier fix.
