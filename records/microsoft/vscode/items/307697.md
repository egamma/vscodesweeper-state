# Issue #307697 — Allow customizing the media sound for user confirmation prompts

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307697](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307697)

## Summary

Sound replacement remains unsupported, but the maintainer has expressed concern about confusing signal meanings. Keep this team-authored Backlog request open for the accessibility owner's decision; no verified fix or canonical duplicate supports a factual close.

## Change summary

Allow users to replace the confirmation-prompt sound with a more distinguishable audio cue.

## Best solution

Have the accessibility owner settle whether improved distinguishability should come through a revised standard confirmation cue or user-selectable audio, accounting for the stated confusion concern. Existing enablement, announcement, and volume controls do not fulfill sound replacement.
