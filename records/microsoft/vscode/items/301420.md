## Change summary

Editor text disappears or renders incorrectly after changing font size when the GPU renderer exhausts its texture atlas.

## Best solution

Ensure the editor GPU renderer handles atlas capacity pressure without losing visible text, including after repeated font-size changes and across editors sharing the atlas. First establish a deterministic capacity reproduction and the recovery boundary for shared atlas consumers.
