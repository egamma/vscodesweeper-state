# Issue #316984 — Sessions sections are missing expansion state indication

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316984](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316984)

## Summary

Keep this team-authored report open and route it to agents: ordinary session sections still hide their expansion indicators until interaction. Current chevrons partially address the request, but do not make collapsed sections discoverable at rest.

## Change summary

Make session-section expandability and expansion state discoverable, including after filter changes and for users who require double-click expansion.

## Best solution

Agree on a consistent disclosure treatment for ordinary Sessions list sections that exposes their state without exploratory interaction, while retaining keyboard accessibility, section identity, and persisted collapse choices.
