- **Trace:**

  > src/vs/code/node/cli.ts adds profiling/debugging arguments and stores their original tokens in a marker file. StartupProfiler._stopProfiling in src/vs/workbench/contrib/performance/electron-browser/startupProfiler.ts reads those tokens, coordinates profile completion, and passes removeArgs to the native relaunch service. NativeHostMainService.relaunch delegates to LifecycleMainService.relaunch, which removes matching argv entries and schedules Electron relaunch on quit. Source inspection establishes the route, not whether shutdown stalls or the replacement process hangs. Preserve ordinary relaunch semantics and profile output collection.
