# Issue #109937 — Render new lines in suggest widget details

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/109937](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/109937)

## Summary

The requested combination of preserved author-provided line breaks and automatic wrapping remains absent from the suggest details header. Keep this discussion open: the current formatting policy deliberately distinguishes indented multiline details, and the verified older related issue concerns resizing rather than this behavior.

## Change summary

Preserve explicit line breaks in completion details while also wrapping long lines to the available width.

## Best solution

Make completion signatures readable without losing author-provided line boundaries, while retaining intentional formatting of indented signatures. Settle whether indented multiline details should also soft-wrap before changing the suggest details rendering policy.
