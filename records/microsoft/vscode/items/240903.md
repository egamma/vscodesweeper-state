Keep open in editor-gpu: the discussion independently confirms GPU-rendered text corruption after zooming, although not the exact positioning symptom. Current source contains zoom-refresh handling, but no verified fix or eligible canonical establishes that this report is resolved.

## Change summary

The reporter observed incorrect positioning; an independent commenter confirmed text corruption after zooming that disappeared after scrolling, but not the same positioning error.
