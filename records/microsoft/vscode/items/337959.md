# Issue #337959 — Chat created with a worktree does not show the "Worktree created" message

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337959](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337959)

## Summary

The missing confirmation is a distinct, source-traceable Agents Window bug and should remain open under the agents-window area. The older visibility requests concern persistent worktree identity or a message that scrolls away, not a creation message that disappears from the newly committed chat.

## Change summary

Preserve the successful worktree-creation confirmation at the top of a newly created chat after its preparation flow completes.

## Best solution

Ensure the worktree completion emitted during new-session preparation is represented in the committed chat transcript, without duplicating it or persisting unrelated transient progress.
