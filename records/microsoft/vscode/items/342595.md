# Issue #342595 — Copilot harness (Agent Host): "Instructions" missing from attach picker; unclear how attached/applyTo instructions are loaded and persisted

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342595](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342595)

## Summary

Keep open for explicit session-scoped instruction support and lifecycle clarification. Current source explains the missing picker, and newer documentation describes ordinary attachments, but neither establishes the requested persistence through compaction.

## Change summary

Provide explicit instruction selection for Copilot sessions with documented loading, scope, and persistence semantics.

## Best solution

Define a supported session-scoped instruction contract for the Copilot harness, expose it through an appropriate customization entry point, and document its behavior during compaction and restoration. Preserve harness-owned discovery rather than assuming Local attachment semantics transfer unchanged.
