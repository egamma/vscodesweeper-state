## Change summary

Drag selection across a view zone taller than the viewport can stall upward scrolling or make downward scrolling jump back.

## Best solution

Make drag selection reliably traverse oversized diff view zones in both directions. First repeat the reported scenario in standalone and multi-file diff editors to distinguish editor drag scrolling from outer viewport synchronization.
