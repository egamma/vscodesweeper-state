Adding Foo records foo with count 0; adding Foo-1 records foo-1; adding Foo again increments foo's count to 1 and returns the already occupied foo-1.
- **Preview rendering path:** extensions/markdown-language-features/src/markdownEngine.ts creates per-render slug builders at lines 190 and 208. Its #addNamedHeaders renderer at lines 305-310 calls the builder and directly assigns slug.value to the heading's id, carrying the collision into rendered HTML.
- **Open implementation candidate:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/332628 was verified OPEN with mergedAt null and explicitly states Fixes #332618. Its diff checks candidate suffixes against previously emitted ids, registers generated ids, and adds tests for the reported Foo, Foo-1, Foo sequence and collisions with generated ids.
