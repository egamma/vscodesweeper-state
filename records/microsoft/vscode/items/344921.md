## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: existing customization UI covers source grouping, source inspection, and enable/disable actions, but does not fully deliver the requested invocation explanations and filtering. Related open work addresses grouping and override visibility, not the complete request.

## Change summary

## Evidence

- **Existing source grouping and inspection:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/aiCustomizationListWidget.ts, groupItemsBySource, already defines User, Workspace, Plugins, Extensions, and Built-in groups. Its row renderer exposes built-in origin, extension identifier, or resource path in a hover and applies disabled styling. These capabilities partially cover the request, but do not establish full invocation-mode or precedence explanations.
- **Existing skill metadata and enablement:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/promptsServiceCustomizationItemProvider.ts, provideCustomizations, maps skill name, description, source, extension identity, enabled state, and userInvocable into customization items. src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/aiCustomizationManagement.contribution.ts registers separate Enable and Disable actions.
- **Invocation and precedence are distinct underlying concepts:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/promptSyntax/service/promptsServiceImpl.ts, computeSkillDiscoveryInfo, reads userInvocable and disableModelInvocation separately and records duplicate-name skills with duplicateOf metadata. The customization provider inspected here passes userInvocable but not those other discovery fields, so existing discovery semantics do not by themselves satisfy the requested UI.
- **Open implementation overlaps only part of the scope:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/344320 is open and proposes scope-based grouping, provenance labels, and explanations for overridden skills. It references https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341371 and other issues, not this report; its described changes do not cover the full invocation-mode, filtering, and selection-explanation request. It is not a landed fix or a full duplicate canonical.
- **Related source-discoverability request:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298998 is open and requests source location for prompts and skills specifically in the slash picker. That narrower picker request does not absorb this report's broader customization-management and invocation-semantics scope.
- **Demand and classification:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344921 has no comments, upvotes, or concrete failing example. Its proposed filters, badges, details, and conflict warnings are new product capabilities rather than an established defect in current intended behavior.
