## Change summary

Chat customization-file discovery (prompt/instructions/agent/skill locations) intermittently spawns dozens of concurrent full-workspace `rg` enumerations with `--no-ignore --follow`, thrashing and re-firing in a loop on large npm-workspaces monorepos.

## Best solution

Once the reporter confirms whether custom glob-pattern `chat.*FilesLocations` are configured and where `searchFilesInLocation` is actually invoked from, fix should land in `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/promptSyntax/utils/promptFilesLocator.ts`: (1) plumb `search.followSymlinks` into `ignoreSymlinks` on the folder query instead of always following symlinks, and (2) add debouncing/coalescing so a burst of triggers (chat editing session activity) doesn't fire many concurrent identical full-tree searches with overlapping cancel-and-refire.
