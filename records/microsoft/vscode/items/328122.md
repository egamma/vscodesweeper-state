# Issue #328122 — Chat customization discovery fires batches of ~30 concurrent full-workspace rg enumerations with --no-ignore --follow, cancelled and re-fired in a loop (1.131.0)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328122](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328122)

## Summary

The logs provide a credible current-release reproduction of a severe search-process storm in core chat customization discovery. Route this to chat and search for investigation rather than closing it.

## Change summary

Chat customization discovery repeatedly launches dozens of concurrent full-workspace ripgrep enumerations that follow symlinks and ignore exclusions.

## Best solution

Coalesce and debounce customization refreshes in the prompt discovery service, constrain searches to their known directories and patterns, and prevent these discovery queries from following symlinks or bypassing ignore files.
