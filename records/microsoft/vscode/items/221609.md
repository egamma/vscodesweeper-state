## Plan brief

Verify the response is scrollable, controls remain accessible, and closing chat restores terminal positioning.
- **Behavior:**

  > A terminal-chat response must remain readable and its controls accessible when content arrives in a newly opened window. Small terminals must not produce broken response or terminal positioning. Acceptance should cover streaming, completed responses, terminal resizing, and restoration of normal terminal layout after closing chat.
- **Trace:**

  > TerminalChatWidget subscribes to content-height and terminal-dimension changes and routes them through _relayout and _doLayout in src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminalContrib/chat/browser/terminalChatWidget.ts. Height combines a content measurement, a viewport-relative cap, and the shared InlineChatWidget.minHeight; _updateXtermViewportPosition separately uses that height and the terminal cursor to offset the terminal. InlineChatWidget._doLayout subtracts status and extra height before laying out the chat widget. The discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221609 confirms the historical defect, but source inspection alone does not establish the failing rendered measurements today. Start by reproducing the supplied flow and recording those measurements; preserve ordinary terminal scrolling, chat dismissal, and unrelated editor inline-chat behavior.
