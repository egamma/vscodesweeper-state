# Issue #186333 — Diff Editor - Show All/Fold Unchanged Region actions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186333](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186333)

## Summary

Keep this as the canonical alignment request: the standard diff editor still places expansion and folding controls in different gutter positions. Three later reports were explicitly closed into this issue, and no verified fixing commit or older equivalent canonical was found.

## Change summary

Align the unchanged-region Show All and Fold controls on the left of the lines so toggling does not require horizontal mouse movement.

## Best solution

Give the standard diff editor's per-region expansion and folding affordances a consistent gutter anchor, including in multi-diff editors. Preserve partial-reveal, scrolling, and keyboard behavior; the compact variant's disclosure control is related prior art, not complete coverage of this request.
