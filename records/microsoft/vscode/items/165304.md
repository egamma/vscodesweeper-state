[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/165304](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/165304)

## Summary

Keep open: the remaining-conflicts counter still lacks the requested visual emphasis, and no verified canonical issue covers its discoverability. This remains an unscheduled merge-editor design improvement, not a demonstrated bug.

## Change summary

Make the merge editor's remaining-conflicts counter easier to notice, particularly when unresolved conflicts are outside the visible code.

## Best solution

Give the unresolved-conflict count a clearer visual hierarchy within the Result header while retaining its text and navigation behavior. The merge-editor owner should choose an affordance consistent with workbench styling rather than introducing a one-off warning treatment or customization setting.
