# Issue #342606 — Incorrect Default for Copilot's Permission

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342606](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342606)

## Summary

Keep open for human review of permission defaults and approval scope. Another user confirms the regression, and current source corroborates session-wide approval as the primary ordinary confirmation action; the security-sensitive permission concern rules out a cleanup close.

## Change summary

Copilot-harness permission prompts default to session-wide tool approval rather than one-time approval, with reports that subsequent commands or URLs no longer prompt.

## Best solution

Establish a least-privilege confirmation contract for the Copilot harness: one-time approval should remain readily accessible, and broader grants should clearly disclose their scope and require deliberate selection. Review the primary-action ordering and tool-name-based approval persistence together, without weakening managed approvals or sandbox restrictions.
