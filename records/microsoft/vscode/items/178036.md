# Issue #178036 — Diff Editor: Highlight Entire Lines For Non-Aligned Lines

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/178036](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/178036)

## Summary

Keep open: current alignment and highlighting logic does not establish coverage of the requested treatment for mostly different lines. This team-authored Backlog request has neither a verified canonical duplicate nor an explicitly linked fixing commit.

## Change summary

Avoid aligning mostly different lines as counterparts and highlight non-aligned lines as whole-line additions or deletions.

## Best solution

Define when changed lines cease to be meaningful counterparts, then make side-by-side alignment and whole-line highlighting consistently express that distinction while preserving useful alignment of similar lines.
