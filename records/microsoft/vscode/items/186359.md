# Issue #186359 — reverted custom editor for untitled files will stay open after shutdown

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186359](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186359)

## Summary

Keep open: the shutdown discard path still clears an editable custom document's dirty state without disposing its untitled editor input, leaving it eligible for restoration. This remains a distinct, maintainer-requested follow-up; the newer editor-switching and readonly-discard reports do not supersede it.

## Change summary

Discarding an untitled custom document during shutdown should close its editor instead of restoring a blank tab on reopening.

## Best solution

Make successful discard of an untitled custom document retire its editor inputs, consistently with untitled text editors, including when shutdown reverts the working copy directly. Preserve file-backed revert behavior, cancellation, and normal hot-exit restoration.
