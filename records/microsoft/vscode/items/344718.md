Validate visible and minimized states on Linux/X11, including reduced-motion mode.

  > CodeWindow construction in src/vs/platform/windows/electron-main/windowImpl.ts sets backgroundThrottling to false specifically for Sessions windows before creating the BrowserWindow; shared window-option handling resides in windows.ts. The setup loading overlay is removed after dismissal, so its infinite pulse alone does not explain steady idle activity.
