## Evidence

- **reporter trigger and recurrence:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314797, the reporter describes repositories appearing during discovery and then disappearing from Changes. Their May 31 comment confirms recurrence on 1.122.1 after adding repositories while working and reloading the window; the May 7 comment records temporary recovery by changing git.repositoryScanMaxDepth from 1 to 0 and back across workspace reopenings.
- **verified canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334842 is currently open and reproduces the same observable loss of repository visibility after a new repository is discovered before a previously visible one. Its reproduction uses a clean profile with extensions disabled, and its real-world example involves nested repositories discovered by the depth-1 scan. It documents both repositories disappearing during restoration and the resulting partial selection persisting across restarts.
- **current source trace:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scmViewService.ts:350-425, SCMViewService.onDidAddRepository, retains previousState after encountering a new repository and resets didSelectRepository to false. A subsequently discovered previously-visible repository then clears all earlier repositories' selectionIndex values to -1. onWillSaveState at lines 569-578 persists that reduced visible set. This conclusively reproduces the canonical's failing discovery sequence from current source without executing code.
- **nested discovery path:** extensions/git/src/model.ts:314-405, Model.doInitialScan and scanWorkspaceFolders, discover repositories through concurrent startup paths and open scanned subfolders with Promise.all. git.repositoryScanMaxDepth controls which nested folders participate; changing it alters the discovery set, consistent with the reporter's temporary recovery rather than proving a fix.
- **fix remains pending:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/334845 is open, has no merge timestamp, and explicitly fixes https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334842. Its described failing sequences produce different visible subsets depending on discovery order. This is an implementation candidate, not a landed fix.
- **other leads distinguish different behavior:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/77828 concerns file-content corruption when reverting hunks, not repositories disappearing from Changes. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338547 concerns startup tree refresh and re-expansion churn, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301028 concerns intentionally hidden repositories becoming visible again; neither matches this report as directly as the selected canonical.
