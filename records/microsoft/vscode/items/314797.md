In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/275281#issuecomment-3487912685, the reporter confirms that repositories had become unselected without deliberate action and reselecting them restored the changes. This covers the disappearing-changes behavior here, not merely a neighboring SCM concern.
- **Current source explains discovery-time disappearance:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scmViewService.ts, SCMViewService.onDidAddRepository selects all repositories when a newly discovered provider is absent from previousState.all, but resets didSelectRepository to false while retaining previousState. A subsequently discovered previously-visible repository then clears every earlier repository's selectionIndex to -1. With previously visible A and B and discovery order A, new C, B, only B remains visible. onWillSaveState persists that reduced selection. extensions/git/src/model.ts scans subfolders and opens repositories asynchronously, providing the relevant nested-repository discovery path. This was traced without executing the checkout.
