# Issue #292419 — Workspace Trust - Double trust request for untrusted workspace, different dialogue content

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292419](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292419)

## Summary

Keep open for Workspace Trust UX assessment: rejecting trust when adding a folder and acknowledging the startup dialog follow separate paths, leaving the reported repetition possible. The available settings suppress prompts globally but do not unify the dialogs or remember that earlier interaction.

## Change summary

Avoid a redundant startup trust question after declining trust for an added folder, and make the two dialogs consistent.

## Best solution

Have the Workspace Trust owner establish consistent acknowledgment semantics and messaging across folder addition and startup, while preserving Restricted Mode and checks for newly introduced resources. Assess the additional Git-triggered prompts separately rather than assuming they share this cause.
