[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292419](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292419)

## Summary

Keep open as Workspace Trust UX polish: current main defaults the startup prompt to never, but the add-folder and startup dialogs remain separate and do not share acknowledgment state. Existing settings suppress the extra surfaces without addressing the requested consistency; the shallow checkout provides no identifiable fixing commit.

## Change summary

Avoid asking for trust again after an explicit refusal when adding a folder, and make the trust dialogs' explanations consistent.

## Best solution

Have the Workspace Trust owner decide whether declining trust during folder addition should satisfy the startup prompt's once-only acknowledgment, while preserving the explicit always option. Present consistent explanations without weakening Restricted Mode or granting trust implicitly.
