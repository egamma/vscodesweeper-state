# Issue #343775 — Agent Merge: CI-only repair always waits 10 minutes after it is enabled, even when the CI failures are old (the approval check keeps CI "pending")

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343775](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343775)

## Summary

Keep open: current source confirms that first observing a head starts a fresh 10-minute quiet period, even when its failures are old. This requests an improvement to deliberately tested pacing behavior; the team-authored report has no verified fix or duplicate supporting closure.

## Change summary

Avoid restarting the CI quiet period for already-old check failures, expose waiting state, and make active-turn deferrals diagnosable.

## Best solution

Make CI-only repair pacing reflect the head's actual check activity while preserving protection against actively changing CI, and make remaining waits visible. Agree on the user-visible waiting presentation and whether restart persistence belongs in this change; keep the unrelated SAML and operation-churn observations separate.
