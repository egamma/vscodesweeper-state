## Summary

Brand-new, detailed UX bug report about the free Copilot Pro upgrade flow: after upgrading on github.com, VS Code's model picker keeps showing stale 'upgrade' UI until the window is reloaded, and the in-editor upgrade CTA briefly routes to a paid Pro+ page. No maintainer signal yet; routing to the chat/entitlement area for investigation rather than closing.

## Change summary

The Copilot free-Pro-upgrade flow in VS Code's model picker doesn't refresh entitlement state after upgrading via the browser, requiring a full window reload, and briefly surfaces a misleading paid-upgrade CTA.

## Best solution

Have the chat/entitlement team (src/vs/workbench/services/chat/common/chatEntitlementService.ts) investigate whether the entitlement observable is actively refreshed after a completed upgrade flow (vs. only on next resolve/reload), and whether the model-picker upgrade CTA can distinguish 'not yet eligible' from 'already Pro' states without requiring a manual reload.
