## Evidence

- **Reporter-confirmed minimal reproduction:** The November 10 follow-up at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346024 reproduces with one repository, no installed extensions, and git.detectWorktrees disabled. Moving Repositories into a secondary-side-bar container produces two entries after clean-state startup; resetting its location produces one.
- **View-state duplication rather than repeated Git discovery:** The follow-up reports one Git repository-open log entry but persisted all:[uri,uri] and visible:[0,0]. In src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scmViewService.ts, onWillSaveState maps repository entries directly into all and computes visible using all.indexOf, which explains those duplicate indices if the in-memory list already contains duplicates.
- **Current initialization path needs diagnosis:** SCMViewService subscribes to onDidAddRepository and then enumerates scmService.repositories; onDidAddRepository and insertRepositoryView do not reject an already-present repository. SCMService.registerSCMProvider stores its repository, updates context keys, and then fires its add event. Both services are registered as delayed singletons in src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scm.service.contribution.ts. These are concrete startup-order investigation points, not proof of the exact layout-triggered failure.
