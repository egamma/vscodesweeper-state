[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327872](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327872)

A real gap remains for standalone Copilot Business organizations: newly announced user-based model targeting requires a GitHub enterprise account. Keep this open pending a decision on equivalent organization/team-level controls.
