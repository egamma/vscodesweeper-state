[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327872](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327872)

Keep open as a request for administrator-controlled model access for selected Copilot Business users. Existing model defaults and picker visibility do not provide that authorization boundary, and no verified canonical or implementing fix was found.

## Change summary
