## Summary

The requested configurable selection-highlight limit is implemented, including the multiline support requested in the discussion. Propose closing as completed while preserving the existing performance-conscious defaults.

## Change summary

Allow users to configure the 200-character selection-highlight limit and enable matching across multiple lines.

## Best solution

Use editor.selectionHighlightMaxLength to raise or remove the length limit and editor.selectionHighlightMultiline to enable multiline matches; both were implemented by commit 034724ad11429e59f66584f1a6cd6e87c8d7aafd.
