# Issue #296579 — Recommended models are all disabled

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296579](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296579)

## Summary

Team-authored UX polish request for the model picker on managed/enterprise accounts where all recommended models are disabled, leaving no enabled model visible in the top-level list. Keeping open per team-author policy; the discussion is still active with maintainer input.

## Change summary

When all recommended models are disabled on a managed account, the model picker shows only disabled entries, making it look unusable until the user digs into the dropdown.

## Best solution

Surface at least one enabled model in the recommended/top section of the model picker for managed accounts, or reduce the number of disabled models shown (e.g. cap at 1) so the picker doesn't feel dominated by unavailable options. The model picker lives in the chat contrib area (likely under `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/` in the model-picker-related files). The team discussion is split between showing fewer disabled models vs. ensuring at least one enabled model is visible — a product decision is still needed.
