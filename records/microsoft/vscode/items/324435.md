# Issue #324435 — Japanese IME reconversion at caret (no selection) duplicates committed text with EditContext

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324435](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324435)

Keep open: the report provides sufficient steps to retest caret-only Japanese reconversion, but the Windows IME event sequence remains unverified. Native EditContext refresh scheduling changed and was subsequently reverted since the report; neither that history nor the related issues establishes a fix or a qualifying duplicate.

## Change summary

Japanese IME reconversion without an explicit selection duplicates committed text instead of replacing the reconversion target.

## Best solution

Make caret-triggered reconversion replace the original target while preserving working selection-based reconversion. First capture the Windows EditContext event sequence to determine whether the replacement range is lost in Monaco's input synchronization or before the browser delivers it.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/nativeEditContext.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/editContextFactory.ts`
- **Validation:** On Windows with Japanese Microsoft IME and editor.editContext enabled, convert three middle dots to an ellipsis and commit, place the caret immediately after it without selecting, press Henkan, and commit a candidate. Confirm that only the chosen replacement remains, then repeat with an explicit selection and verify ordinary IME composition remains unchanged. Capture composition events, TextUpdateEvent replacement ranges, and editor/EditContext selections to locate the failure boundary before defining a regression test.
- **Behavior:**

  > Reconversion of already-committed Japanese text at the caret must replace the IME's target rather than append another copy. The reported case converts three middle dots to an ellipsis, commits it, and invokes Henkan immediately after it without a selection. Acceptance requires one resulting candidate rather than duplicated text, with explicit-selection reconversion and ordinary composition continuing to work.
- **Trace:**

  > NativeEditContext in src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/nativeEditContext.ts refreshes model-derived EditContext state on compositionstart and compositionend. Its _emitTypeEvent computes replacement counts from TextUpdateEvent.updateRangeStart/updateRangeEnd relative to _previousEditContextSelection, then _onType dispatches compositionType or type. The source can express replacement before a collapsed caret, so the exact Windows event sequence is necessary to determine where the target range is lost; the reporter's TSF ranges alone do not establish the JavaScript event payload. EditContext.create in src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/editContextFactory.ts constructs the browser-native implementation. Preserve working explicit-selection reconversion and avoid assuming a Chromium defect before observing the delivered ranges. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/320074 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337197 describe distinct triggers, not verified duplicates.
