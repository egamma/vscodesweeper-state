  > The historical report was created successfully, but its browser page initially returned 404 and the user believed their work was lost. A failed browser handoff should not leave the user without recoverable submission details or clarity about whether creation succeeded. Acceptance checks should cover successful submission, failed navigation after successful creation, and recovery without duplicate creation.
- **Trace:**

  > The original submission is preserved at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/165172, and the reporter explains the misleading 404 at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/165172#issuecomment-1304608547. NativeIssueReporter.submitToGitHub in src/vs/workbench/contrib/issue/electron-browser/issueReporterService.ts opens the API-returned html_url and closes the legacy reporter without observing the browser page's HTTP result. The wizard instead delegates to IssueFormService.submitIssue and remains editable after IssueReporterEditorPane marks its preview opened. Source inspection establishes these differing recovery boundaries, not the cause of the 404; diagnosis must start by reproducing the relevant handoff. Do not conflate this with the template-selection redirect in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246251, and preserve single-creation semantics rather than retrying a successful POST.
