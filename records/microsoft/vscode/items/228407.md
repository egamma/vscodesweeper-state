Confirm the service remains usable after a subsequent service restart.
- **Behavior:**

  > A tunnel running as an installed service must remain available for fresh browser connections after a successful CLI update. The reported failure allows an existing connection to continue but prevents reconnecting after refreshing or reopening vscode.dev; reinstalling alone does not recover it, while uninstalling and reinstalling does. Acceptance requires reconnecting after the update without service reinstallation and retaining a usable service after restart.
- **Trace:**

  > The inspected update RPC is handle_update in cli/src/tunnels/control_server.rs, which invokes SelfUpdate::do_update in cli/src/self_update.rs to replace the executable. The serving path in cli/src/commands/tunnels.rs saves current_exe before serving and spawns that path on Next::Respawn. Service installation and removal delegate to platform-specific managers, with materially different lifecycle operations; the report does not establish which host service manager failed. Source inspection has not confirmed a failing branch, so diagnosis must first observe the update, process handoff, and subsequent fresh connection. Preserve tunnel identity, credentials, and normal service installation behavior; do not turn service reinstallation into the normal update workflow. Related reports https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307368, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/322058, and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324151 provide comparison cases, not a verified shared root cause.

- **Current update and restart paths:** cli/src/tunnels/control_server.rs::handle_update invokes SelfUpdate::do_update. cli/src/self_update.rs stages and validates the replacement executable, renames the running executable, and installs the replacement. cli/src/commands/tunnels.rs captures current_exe before serving and uses that saved path when handling Next::Respawn. These paths identify the investigation boundary but do not conclusively explain the reported failure.
- **Service reinstallation is not equivalent to restart:** cli/src/commands/tunnels.rs::service delegates installation and removal to platform service managers. For example, cli/src/tunnels/service_linux.rs::register starts the unit, while unregister explicitly stops and disables it. The reported uninstall/install recovery is useful lifecycle evidence, not proof that a particular platform implementation is defective.
