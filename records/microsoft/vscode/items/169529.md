# Issue #169529 — Parts that are hidden should not render DOM nodes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529)

## Summary

The broader gap remains on current main: workbench startup creates every part and its content even when the layout marks that part invisible. The original native-titlebar control container is now guarded, but the native titlebar still inherits and renders the general browser titlebar subtree, so there is no basis to close the issue as completed.

## Change summary

Avoid creating DOM and associated workbench-part logic for parts that are permanently disabled or hidden, while preserving fast toggling for transiently hidden parts.

## Best solution

Distinguish parts that are unavailable for the lifetime of a window from parts that users frequently toggle: permanently unavailable parts should use a minimal service/view without rendering their normal subtree, while retaining cached DOM only where measurements show that it improves toggling performance.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts`
- **Validation:** Add a workbench startup test covering native-titlebar and initially hidden-part configurations, asserting that permanently unavailable parts do not create their normal content subtree while a toggleable hidden part can still be shown correctly; supplement it with before/after startup and resize measurements.
- **Behavior:**

  > A workbench part that cannot become visible in the current window must not render its normal DOM subtree or participate in unnecessary style recalculation. Parts intentionally retained for fast user toggling must continue to appear correctly on first show, preserve their state, and toggle without a regression. Acceptance should cover the native-titlebar configuration and initially hidden activity bar, sidebar, panel, auxiliary bar, and status bar, with measured startup or resize impact rather than DOM-count reduction alone.
- **Trace:**

  > Workbench.renderWorkbench in src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.ts currently creates every registered part and calls Part.create before src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts applies serialized grid visibility. NativeTitlebarPart in src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts inherits BrowserTitlebarPart.createContentArea, so native-titlebar windows still construct the general titlebar subtree; only window-controls-container itself is now guarded in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts. The issue discussion confirms excessive style recalculation for that control container but explicitly leaves other parts subject to measurement: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529#issuecomment-1359690682 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529#issuecomment-1367598458. Preserve service contracts, serialized layout state, and the established fast-toggle behavior for transiently hidden parts; do not generalize DOM removal without measuring those paths.

## Evidence

- **current startup path:** src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.ts, renderWorkbench(), unconditionally iterates over the title bar, activity bar, sidebar, panel, auxiliary bar, and status bar, creates each container, and calls getPart(id).create(...) before layout visibility is applied.
- **visibility is layout-only:** src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts, createGridDescriptor(), records hidden parts as serialized grid leaves with visible: false, after their DOM and content have already been created.
- **native titlebar remains rendered:** src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts defines NativeTitlebarPart as a BrowserTitlebarPart subclass without replacing createContentArea(); src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts therefore still creates the titlebar container, three content regions, drag region, and title for the native case.
- **partial mitigation only:** src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/titlebar/titlebarPart.ts now guards creation of window-controls-container with !hasNativeTitlebar(...), addressing the concrete expensive node discussed in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529#issuecomment-1359690682 but not the issue's broader hidden-part rendering behavior.
- **maintainer direction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169529#issuecomment-1367598458 distinguishes permanently unused native-titlebar rendering from parts whose retained DOM may improve toggle speed, and proposes a cheap native-titlebar view; current source does not implement that separation.
