- **Current source confirms shared mechanism:** src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/resourceProviders/fileSystemResources.ts:30-36 reads the complete resource and passes Base64 requests to the shared encodeBase64 helper. src/vs/base/common/buffer.ts:431-463 still accumulates the output through individual character concatenations, including four appends per loop at lines 443-446.
- **Report-specific evidence to preserve:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343103#issuecomment-6306118716 documents repeated remote agent-host SIGABRT OOMs near a 2 GiB heap limit, eight Base64 resource reads totaling 358,448,202 bytes, and a generated-data encoder diagnostic showing approximately 42.7 MiB additional heap for 1 MiB input. The instrumented run used a larger heap and did not itself crash; the frontend selection of workspace binaries remains unisolated.
- **Same root cause, different trigger:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343103#issuecomment-6306130355 links the canonical and its proposed encoder fix. Linux remote-host execution and untracked workspace captures differ from the canonical's Windows renderer/plugin inputs, but both reach the same complete-resource Base64 amplification; the available diagnostics do not establish a separate platform-specific defect.
- **Fix remains pending:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/339434 is open and unmerged. It replaces concatenated-string encoding with a sized output buffer, but explicitly leaves complete-payload reads and transport serialization unchanged and does not claim verification of original large-file crashes or concurrent large transfers.
