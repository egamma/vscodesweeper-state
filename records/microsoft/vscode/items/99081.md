# Issue #99081 — Settings sync - Cannot dismiss "Configure..." quick pick dialog

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/99081](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/99081)

## Summary

Keep open: stable quick picks still lack an automatic mouse/touch cancellation affordance, and this issue owns the generic improvement discussed by maintainers. The focus-loss behavior is intentional, but Escape and OK do not provide equivalent pointer-only cancellation.

## Change summary

Provide discoverable mouse/touch dismissal for quick picks that intentionally remain open when focus moves elsewhere.

## Best solution

Provide a consistent cancellation affordance through the shared quick-input UI, preserving intentional focus retention and Escape semantics. Resolve the outstanding presentation choice for titleless pickers rather than requiring each caller to supply its own close button.
