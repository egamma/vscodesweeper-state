# Issue #325330 — Undo history seems to be unlimited, but redo history is not

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325330](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325330)

## Summary

The video and repeated brace-boundary reproductions support a real undo/redo defect. Route this to undo/redo to identify which auto-insertion or formatting participant creates an edit during redo and discards the remaining future stack.

## Change summary

Repeated redo stops when braces are restored, leaving later undone edits unapplied.

## Best solution

Prevent auto-insertion and formatting participants from producing new edits in response to undo or redo events, preserving the remaining redo stack.
