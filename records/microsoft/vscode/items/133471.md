# Issue #133471 — Bracket pair colorization - options for showing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/133471](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/133471)

## Summary

Keep this accepted request open: current main supports a configurable foreground for matching brackets, but not the requested theme-independent, interaction-only bracket colorization, including selected brackets. Closely related requests are newer and cannot replace this re-reviewed item.

## Change summary

Offer bracket pair colors only when the caret is adjacent to a bracket, during relevant typing, or when a bracket is selected.

## Best solution

Provide an opt-in interaction-only bracket colorization mode in the editor, preserving existing always-on colorization and bracket matching behavior. Settle its relationship to the matching-bracket foreground override and its exact activation semantics before implementation.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/bracketMatching/browser/bracketMatching.ts`, `src/vs/editor/common/model/bracketPairsTextModelPart/colorizedBracketPairsDecorationProvider.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/bracketMatching/test/browser/bracketMatching.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Extend the bracket matching browser suite with nested-bracket cases covering caret adjacency, moving away, selecting a bracket, and typing brackets under the agreed mode. Verify active glyph colors and inactive glyph restoration in an editor, and verify that existing always-on colorization and matching commands remain unchanged.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users can opt into bracket pair colorization that appears only while interacting with brackets rather than throughout the visible code. The reported activation cases are caret adjacency, relevant typing, and selecting a bracket. Inactive brackets retain their normal syntax colors, and existing always-on colorization remains available. Acceptance checks should cover nested pairs, leaving an active bracket, selecting a bracket, and typing a pair, using the agreed activation and color semantics.
- **Trace:**

  > BracketMatchingController in src/vs/editor/contrib/bracketMatching/browser/bracketMatching.ts updates decorations from cursor and model events; _recomputeBrackets ignores noncollapsed selections and distinguishes adjacent matching from enclosing-pair matching through editor.matchBrackets. Its theming participant already supports editorBracketMatch.foreground, but this is a single theme-defined override rather than an interaction-only nesting palette. ColorizedBracketPairsDecorationProvider in src/vs/editor/common/model/bracketPairsTextModelPart/colorizedBracketPairsDecorationProvider.ts colors brackets by nesting throughout a requested range, and editorOptions.ts has no conditional colorization mode. The reporter explicitly rejected treating the foreground-token proposal at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/85775 as full coverage. Related requests https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/136646 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/150648 describe overlapping but not identical activation scopes. Preserve existing bracket commands, always-on coloring, guide settings, and foreground overrides; the unresolved boundary is the new mode's observable activation and color behavior.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should the interaction-only mode retain nesting-level palette colors, or use a single active-pair color independent of nesting?
  - Should activation show only the adjacent or selected matching pair, or also enclosing and nested pairs; what precisely counts as activation while typing?
