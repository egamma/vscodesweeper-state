# Issue #308148 — Feature Request: Auto-resize images that exceed the 2000px dimension limit

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308148](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308148)

## Summary

The failure remains possible on current main. Chat automatically resizes attachments, but the resizing algorithm can leave or make panoramic images wider than the API's 2000px many-image limit.

## Change summary

Ensure oversized Copilot Chat image attachments are downscaled within the 2000px service limit while preserving their aspect ratio.

## Best solution

Keep transparent attachment optimization, but guarantee that its final output never exceeds the service's longest-side limit and never upscales an image after applying that cap.
