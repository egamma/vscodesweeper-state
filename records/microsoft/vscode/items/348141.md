# Issue #348141 — Agents window: prompt timeline subscribes to every turn's changeset on each session open, and the agent host recomputes all turn diffs each time

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348141](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348141)

## Summary

The default-off stopgap prevents automatic timeline mounting, but enabling the timeline still eagerly subscribes to historical turn changesets and recomputes them on reopening. Keep this open for the underlying fix; the maintainer-confirmed host stalls and reconnect loop warrant retaining its importance.

## Change summary

Prevent prompt timeline presentation and session switching from eagerly subscribing to and repeatedly recomputing every historical turn's diffs.

## Best solution

Provide timeline navigation and summaries without live changeset subscriptions for every historical prompt, consistent with the maintainer's chat-outline direction. Preserve accurate on-demand diff review and account for edits arriving while historical changesets are unobserved.

## Evidence

- **Confirmed impact and proposed work:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348141#issuecomment-7040487456 confirms the same problem in editor windows: roughly 5,000 prompts stalled the host for over 20 seconds, disconnected the window, and repeated on reconnect. The full comment reports measured subscription counts and an unposted visible-row prototype, including a proposed 500-prompt contribution test.
- **Mitigation is explicitly incomplete:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/349432 explicitly describes defaulting sessions.chatTimeline.display to off as mitigation only and states that explicit opt-ins retain the cost. Current src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/promptTimeline/promptTimeline.contribution.ts registers default: 'off'; the feature and expensive enabled path remain.
- **Current eager observation path:** In src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/promptTimeline/promptTimelineWidgetContrib.ts, _createRail observes model.promptTicks for the gutter without checking flyout visibility. In promptTimelineModel.ts:185-215, _basePromptTicks maps every loaded prompt and _withDiffStats reads every tick's derived stat. _diffsForRequest at lines 664-675 observes getChangesForRequest, so opening a gutter-enabled chat activates all loaded prompts' diff sources.
- **Current subscription and recomputation path:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/agentSessions/agentHost/agentHostResponseFileChanges.ts:_createDiffsObservable reads the turn changeset; _subscribeChangeset at lines 504-513 acquires a connection subscription for its observed lifetime. src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/agentHostChangesetCoordinator.ts:298-304 invokes computeTurnChangeset on each first subscriber when loaded, while onLastSubscriber removes interest. agentHostChangesetService.ts:709-738 marks computing and reads the database without a Ready-result reuse guard.
- **Cache correctness boundary:** src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/agentHostChangesetService.ts:onToolCallEditsApplied, onTurnComplete, and _onTrackedEditsChanged schedule turn recomputation only for subscribed changesets. Retaining and reusing a Ready result alone would therefore risk stale results after late snapshots or discarded edits.
- **Maintainer direction and related navigation work:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348141#issuecomment-7042787704 favors a chat outline rather than reintroducing subscriptions and links https://github.com/microsoft/agent-host-protocol/pull/620. That proposal links the now-merged https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/349287 for bounded history navigation. These are relevant architectural groundwork, not proof that the still-present eager stat and resubscribe paths have been fixed.
