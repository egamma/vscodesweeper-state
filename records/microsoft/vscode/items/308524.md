[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308524](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308524)

## Summary

Keep open while the linked inno-updater fix is reviewed. The reporter's improvement on 1.138.0 is encouraging, but another maintainer confirmed the failure on 1.136.2 and the upstream rename still fails immediately on contention.

## Change summary

Allow Windows updates to tolerate a temporarily locked Code.exe instead of failing immediately during the current-to-old rename.

## Best solution

Review and resolve https://github.com/microsoft/inno-updater/pull/69, then verify that VS Code adopts an updater that handles transient executable locks without compromising rename rollback or persistent-error reporting.
