[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/288672](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/288672)

## Summary

Keep open with the existing upstream/native-dialog classification. Current source delegates filename selection to Electron without appending `.text`, and neither a verified open canonical nor a fix for this macOS 26.2 report was found.

## Change summary

Preserve the filename entered in the macOS native Save dialog when saving a new untitled text file, without forcibly appending `.text`.

## Best solution

Establish a minimal Electron native-save reproduction on the affected macOS version and resolve the filename coercion at the Electron/AppKit boundary. Preserve unrestricted filenames in VS Code; the simplified dialog remains a workaround, not fulfillment of the native-dialog requirement.
