Keep open in webview: multiple reporters confirm that native dragging inside externally loaded nested iframes fails on macOS, with confirmation through September 2025. No verified core fix or eligible open canonical was found; the supplied minimal example is sufficient to begin a current-build investigation.

## Change summary

Make native drag-and-drop inside an externally loaded iframe in an extension webview work on macOS as it does in a standalone browser.

The expected result is that the target receives dragover and drop and the button moves into it. In src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/pre/index.html, handleInnerDragStartEvent and handleInnerDragEvent forward file-item drags to the host; hookupOnLoadHandlers installs listeners on the content window, while the extension's externally loaded iframe is a descendant browsing context. WebviewWindowDragMonitor observes workbench-window drag events, and WebviewElement.windowDidDragStart/windowDidDragEnd toggle iframe pointer-events.
