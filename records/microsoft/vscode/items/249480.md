Keep open: current source still collapses language-server completion kinds into shell-oriented categories, including rendering Python keywords as aliases. This issue owns the icon-mapping cleanup; the broader provider-support issue does not replace it.

- **Trace:**

  > LspCompletionProviderAddon.provideCompletions converts provider kinds using mapLspKindToTerminalKind in terminalCompletionItem.ts before constructing terminal suggestions. The converter handles only seven language kinds, maps Keyword to Alias, and defaults remaining kinds to Method; its TODO links directly to https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/249480. SuggestAddon assigns icons and kind labels from terminal-kind maps, and terminalSymbolIcons.ts gives Alias a method icon, whereas editor CompletionItemKinds maps Keyword to Codicon.symbolKeyword. The remaining uncertainty is the user-visible coverage of the broader cleanup, not whether keyword presentation is wrong.
