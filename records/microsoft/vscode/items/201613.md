## Summary

Still unfixed on main: when activity bar is on top and sidebar is hidden, the compact menubar (where Alt+F triggers) is also hidden, causing the menu to either not appear or render in a broken state. Team agreed on a fix approach but no implementing commit was found.

## Change summary

Alt+F (and similar Alt shortcuts) for the compact menu don't work correctly when the activity bar is positioned at the top and the sidebar is hidden.

## Best solution
