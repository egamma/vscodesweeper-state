## Summary

The click-to-show-and-focus interaction remains missing: existing hover and toolbar controls do not provide it. Keep the accepted Backlog request open for an inline-completions interaction decision; no verified canonical owns the same work.

## Change summary

Make left-clicking ghost text show and focus its hover, and make right-clicking it open the regular editor context menu.

## Best solution
