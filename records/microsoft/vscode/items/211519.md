[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/211519](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/211519)

## Summary

Consolidate this request into the older, open SCM keyboard-shortcuts issue, whose discussion explicitly documents the same editor-versus-highlighted-resource behavior. The missing capability remains present in current source; this is a duplicate proposal, not a claim that it is fixed.

## Change summary

Allow SCM keyboard shortcuts to stage or discard highlighted resources without opening their diffs or moving focus into an editor.

## Best solution

Resolve resource-aware SCM keyboard actions under https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/24916, preserving this report's single-file navigation, multi-selection, and focus-retention requirements.
