# Issue #314576 — copilot_getNotebookSummary needs a freaking timeout and clear error

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314576](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314576)

## Summary

Keep open: current source makes the structural notebook summary await kernel-variable retrieval without a timeout, so a stalled provider can prevent the tool from returning. Older reports cover the loading symptom, but do not establish coverage of this report's broader timeout, cancellation, and stale-cell recovery requirements.

## Change summary

Prevent notebook tools from waiting indefinitely and provide actionable failure feedback without allowing subsequent edits to rely on stale cell IDs.

## Best solution

Make Copilot's notebook-summary operation complete predictably when runtime-variable retrieval stalls, while preserving current in-memory notebook structure and unsaved edits. Establish timeout and failure semantics first, and separately verify the reported execution/edit hangs and stale-cell cascade rather than assuming they share one cause.
