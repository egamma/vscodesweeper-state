# Issue #238940 — Quick open can still be over window controls when zoomed in

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238940](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238940)

## Summary

The zoomed-in overlap remains possible in current source: horizontal snapping can undo the window-controls collision constraint. Keep this with the quick-pick owner; no verified canonical or completed fix was found.

## Change summary

Prevent Quick Open from overlapping window controls when the workbench is zoomed in.

## Best solution

Keep the draggable Quick Pick clear of visible window controls after all positioning and snapping calculations, including zoomed and narrow-window layouts, without changing ordinary safe snap positions.
