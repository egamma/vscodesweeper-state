## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/model/chatSessionStore.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/services/environment/browser/environmentService.ts`
- **Validation:** In the same Codespace and client, create two named conversations containing identifiable messages, verify they appear in history, allow inactivity suspension, resume, and confirm both conversations reopen with their prior messages. Compare workspace identity, the session index, persisted session files, and active history filters before and after; repeat in web and desktop clients to establish the affected boundary.
- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter loses all visible Copilot conversations and agent context after an idle Codespace suspends and resumes. Previously saved conversations should remain discoverable and reopen with their messages when returning to the same Codespace. Acceptance should cover at least two conversations across the suspension cycle and distinguish durable conversation history from whether an interrupted agent operation automatically continues.
- **Trace:**

  > ChatSessionStore in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/model/chatSessionStore.ts locates ordinary session files under workspaceStorageHome/<workspaceId>/chatSessions and keeps the index in workspace-scoped storage. internalGetIndex produces an empty index if its storage key is absent; the source does not establish why that would happen during this reported suspension cycle. Workspace migration is registered through onDidEnterWorkspace, but whether Codespace resume changes identity or invokes that event remains unknown. The browser environment service derives workspaceStorageHome from userRoamingDataHome. Related reports https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/321290 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334076 describe different storage inconsistencies, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/300557 includes recovery through resetting filters. Start by reproducing and locating the affected storage/backend boundary; do not assume data deletion, merge unrelated recovery features, or change automatic continuation semantics.
