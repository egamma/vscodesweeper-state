# Issue #342637 — [Worktree Cleanup] Clicking on a session removed it from the list

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342637](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342637)

## Summary

Keep open as a request to preview cleanup candidates without losing their place in the cleanup workflow. The maintainer confirmed that exclusion after interaction is intentional, but acknowledged a possible exception; current controls do not provide that capability.

## Change summary

Allow inspecting a session from Worktree Cleanup without making it disappear when returning to the cleanup list.

## Best solution

Provide a cleanup-specific inspection flow that preserves the candidate's discoverability after viewing its context, while retaining protection against cleaning up genuinely active or recently used sessions. Decide whether that should be a non-activating preview or retained list context before implementation.
