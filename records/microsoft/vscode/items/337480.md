[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337480](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337480)

## Summary

The reported Dark Modern color regression is fixed on main: the legacy tab strip and inactive tabs again use their original darker background. The later comment concerns separation between background tabs, which the color restoration does not claim to change.

## Change summary

Updating to 1.139.0 changed Dark Modern's legacy tab strip and inactive tabs to a brighter gray, reducing readability.

## Best solution

Close the original palette regression as completed by ab8f31f0c0b1a4dd86d8dbee1248542f5806574d, preserving the dedicated connected-tab palette. Treat any remaining inactive-tab separation concern separately from the restored legacy colors.
