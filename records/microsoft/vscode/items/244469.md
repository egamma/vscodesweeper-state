## Summary

Keep open: current source implements recovery logging and error reporting for observable transactions, but that does not complete the broader diagnostic and notification proposal. This team-authored engineering item has no verified full-scope fix or qualifying older duplicate.

## Change summary

Make reasonable repairs of inconsistent internal state observable through developer diagnostics, error telemetry, and controlled Insiders notifications without interrupting recoverable operations.

## Best solution
