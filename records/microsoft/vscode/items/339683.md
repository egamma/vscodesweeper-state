Team-authored bug (author sandy081, MEMBER) carrying a real version milestone (1.141.0) — a hard keep-open case regardless of further evidence; filed today with no fix commit yet, so no implemented-on-main close is possible. The deferred-title refinement path in agentHostSessionTitleController.ts has several conditions that could silently skip refinement, matching the reported symptom.

## Change summary

After #337348 made deferred title generation the default, new Agent Host sessions sometimes keep their prompt-derived provisional title instead of being renamed by the background utility-model refinement after the first turn.

## Best solution

Diagnose why `refineTitleFromFirstTurn` in src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/agentHostSessionTitleController.ts is bailing out for affected sessions (e.g. a turn-count/title mismatch guard, `_deferredRefinementStarted` already set, or `successful`/`TurnState.Complete` check) and tighten the eligibility check so the background refinement reliably fires once per session.
