# Issue #177708 — How to know which extension is asking for interaction

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/177708](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/177708)

## Summary

Keep open: extension-created quick inputs still lack automatic attribution to their originating extension. The related newer report was closed in favor of this issue, and no older canonical covering the same interaction was identified.

## Change summary

Automatically identify the extension requesting input in top-center quick-input dialogs, without requiring extension authors to add their own labels.

## Best solution

Provide consistent, core-owned extension attribution for extension-created input boxes and quick picks, while preserving their existing titles and prompts. The quick-input owner should settle the presentation and scope before implementation.

- **Reason:** Consistent attribution makes extension-driven interactions easier to understand without requiring individual extensions to invent their own presentation.
