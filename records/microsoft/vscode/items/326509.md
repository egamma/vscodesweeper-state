# Issue #326509 — Can't switch profiles or prefered extension accounts

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326509](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326509)

## Summary

Compound report mixing three distinct concerns: frustration over unexpected Copilot credit consumption, a claim that BYOK/local models become unusable once cloud credits are exhausted, and an inability to switch the preferred GitHub/GitHub Enterprise account for Copilot. The account-switching piece is already tracked by a clearer, more specific open feature request (#330136), but that issue does not cover the billing/BYOK-blocking complaint, so this report isn't fully absorbed by it and shouldn't be closed as a straight duplicate.

## Change summary

User wants clarity on Copilot credit/BYOK entitlement behavior and a way to switch the preferred GitHub/GitHub Enterprise account used by Copilot without losing access when one account's credits are exhausted.

## Best solution

Split the concerns: the account-switching ask is best tracked and consolidated under https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330136 (which explicitly links back to this issue and proposes a 'Switch Account/Provider' command); route the remaining billing-transparency/BYOK-fallback concern to the Copilot/authentication area owners for a product decision on whether BYOK should remain usable when cloud credits are exhausted.
