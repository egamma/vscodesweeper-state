## Change summary

Copilot generates regex-style alternation in grep_search.includePattern when searching multiple folders, producing false no-match results.

## Best solution

Make multi-folder searches reliable without confusing content-query regex syntax with file-selection glob syntax. Evaluate the tool contract and recovery behavior with current models before choosing an approach; the maintainer explicitly cautioned that adding examples alone had not solved this.
