# Issue #345517 — Chat input in Inbox is positioned such that it's not clear what it applies to

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345517](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345517)

## Summary

Keep open for Inbox layout design: the composer remains centered below both panes, rather than visually grouped with the selected conversation's evidence. This team-authored feedback identifies a clarity improvement, not a proven functional defect or completed fix.

## Change summary

Make the Inbox reply input's conversation target clear through placement and less repetitive headings.

## Best solution

Visually associate the reply composer with the selected conversation while retaining one composer and clear new-session behavior. Agree on the wide and narrow layouts before changing placement or removing target labels.
