## Change summary

Prevent a color swatch from temporarily appearing inside the property name when Emmet expands `c:red` into `color: red;`.

## Best solution

Keep delayed color-provider computation, but ensure invalidated color swatches do not remain visibly attached to unrelated replacement text. Address this in the color detector without changing general editor-decoration tracking semantics.
