# Issue #336045 — Second click after selecting from command center interpreted as double click on underlying UI

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336045](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336045)

## Summary

The defect remains present on current main: mouse selection immediately accepts and hides the quick pick, allowing the next click in the same double-click sequence to reach an underlying surface whose intentional double-click action creates an untitled editor. Route this to the quick-pick and command-center owners.

## Change summary

Prevent the second click of a rapid command-center selection from being interpreted as a double-click on the UI revealed beneath the dismissed picker.

## Best solution

Preserve single-click acceptance while ensuring that the rest of that pointer sequence cannot activate the newly exposed workbench surface. Intentional double-click behavior on empty editor groups and tab-bar space, keyboard acceptance, and background acceptance should remain unchanged.
