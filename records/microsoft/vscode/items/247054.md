- **Actual upstream shadow implementation:** https://github.com/electron/electron/commit/3a2b7d372053d6358b3053eeab2bc7ec19454e89 implements frameless Wayland shadows after the reopening. Its `shell/browser/native_window_views.cc` diff defaults `has_shadow_` to true and preserves transparent CSD shadow regions; the added `LinuxFrameLayout::Create` selects a CSD layout for opaque Wayland windows requesting shadows, and `LinuxCSDFrameLayout::PaintWindowFrame` paints the frame. Electron v43.7.7's `ElectronFrameViewLayoutLinux::GetShadowValues` and `GetCornerRadii` provide shadows and corner radii on this path.
