Keep open: the highly supported RTL editing request remains active, and three open implementation PRs reference it. Closing before an implementation merges would interrupt ongoing work.

## Change summary

Provide reliable right-to-left and mixed bidirectional text editing for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, and similar content.

## Best solution

Consolidate the overlapping approaches in the open PRs and land one coherent editor-level direction setting and detection model, building on the decoration-based RTL infrastructure already on main.
