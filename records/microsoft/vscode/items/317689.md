- **Behavior:**

  > The chat input area should have a calm, consistent spacing hierarchy on the empty screen and while a response is in progress. Its alignment and breathing room must remain coherent when implicit-context attachments are visible, including at narrow widths. Acceptance requires visual comparison against the approved design for all four state combinations: empty/in-progress with and without attachments.
- **Trace:**

  > The outer composer spacing is currently controlled by `.interactive-input-part` in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/media/chat.css, while `.chat-input-container` owns internal input padding. ChatInputPart in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/input/chatInputPart.ts constructs the composer and its attachment/input surface stack, with adjacency rules in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/input/media/chatInputStack.css. The issue supplies the intended Figma treatment, but https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317689#issuecomment-4565477650 leaves attachment behavior unsettled. Keep compact and quick-input hosts outside the scope unless the approved design explicitly includes them, and preserve keyboard, focus, and screen-reader behavior because this is spacing-only polish.
- **Open decisions:**
  - When implicit-context attachments are present, should they occupy the same padded composer region as the empty-state input, or should the input spacing contract to preserve a fixed outer edge and vertical rhythm?
