## Summary

Design-opinion feedback against the new Modern UI experiment, asking for its permanent removal rather than just a toggle; a setting to disable it already exists, and outright removal is a maintainer/roadmap call, not something to close via triage.

## Change summary

Requests reverting and permanently removing the experimental "Modern UI" (workbench.experimental.modernUI), rather than leaving it as a toggle-able option, to preserve the classic high-density layout.

## Best solution

Keep open as design feedback for the UI modernization workstream to weigh; note that `workbench.experimental.modernUI` (see src/vs/workbench/services/layout/browser/layoutService.ts and src/vs/workbench/contrib/styleOverrides/browser/styleOverrides.contribution.ts) already lets users opt out today, and consolidate this with the other open Modern UI feedback threads for maintainer review rather than deciding removal unilaterally.
