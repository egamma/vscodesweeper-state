# Issue #298565 — Copilot Chat Sessions Not Appearing in History After Creating New Session

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298565](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298565)

## Summary

The reported local-session disappearance is addressed by a landed fix that distinguishes unloading a chat model from deleting its history. Tracing New Session on current main shows that saved conversations remain discoverable rather than being removed from the Sessions list.

## Change summary

Keep completed local Copilot conversations visible and reopenable after clicking New Session, without requiring a refresh or extension reload.

## Best solution

Treat this as completed by commit 14200868c7fb67eb932fbdf69abbe0f87b5da837: retain saved sessions when their models unload and refresh the list from live sessions plus persisted history.
