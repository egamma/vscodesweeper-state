# Issue #191560 — Test: Jump to end of notebook sticky scroll with shift click

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/191560](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/191560)

## Summary

Notebook sticky scroll still lacks the editor's Shift-hover preview and Shift-click scope-end navigation. Keep this team-authored request open; the existing settings do not provide it, and no verified canonical or fixing commit was found.

## Change summary

Support previewing and navigating to a notebook section's end through Shift-hover and Shift-click on its sticky header.

## Best solution

Provide a notebook equivalent of editor sticky-scroll scope-end navigation while preserving ordinary header navigation and folding. First settle what constitutes the notebook section endpoint and what its sticky preview should display.

- **Behavior:**

  > Notebook sticky headers currently navigate to their heading even when Shift is held, and do not preview the section end. The requested interaction should offer a notebook equivalent of the text editor's Shift-hover endpoint preview and Shift-click navigation. Acceptance should cover nested and final sections, restoring the normal header after releasing Shift, and preserving ordinary header clicks and folding. The exact endpoint and preview content require agreement before those checks can be finalized.
- **Trace:**

  > NotebookStickyLine in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/viewParts/notebookEditorStickyScroll.ts unconditionally routes header clicks to focusCell(), which focuses entry.cell and scrolls to its top. No modifier or hover-preview handling exists there. OutlineEntry in browser/viewModel/OutlineEntry.ts exposes a cell, optional range, and parent/children hierarchy, but no explicit notebook-section endpoint contract. The text-editor reference is StickyScrollController._registerMouseListeners() in src/vs/editor/contrib/stickyScroll/browser/stickyScrollController.ts, which uses stored ending line numbers for Shift-click and preview state for Shift-hover. Existing notebook coverage lives in test/browser/notebookStickyScroll.test.ts under NotebookEditorStickyScroll. Preserve normal navigation, folding, and sticky-scroll visibility/layout settings; the uncertainty is the user-visible translation from a text scope's last line to a notebook section's endpoint. Original request: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/191560.
- **Open decisions:**
  - For a notebook heading, should scope-end navigation target the final cell in its section or a precise line within that cell, including when the section boundary occurs inside a Markdown cell?
  - What should Shift-hover display as the notebook section-end preview: the endpoint's text or a cell-level representation?

## Evidence
