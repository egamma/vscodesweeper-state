# Issue #346101 — Compare models: model selection is lost when picker is dismissed or workspace changes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346101](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346101)

## Summary

Keep open: current source explicitly discards unfinished comparison selections on dismissal and resets selections when the workspace changes. This team-authored request seeks a change to existing lifecycle semantics, not behavior already covered by a setting.

## Change summary

Preserve Compare Models selections across accidental picker dismissal and workspace changes, or prevent accidental dismissal during selection.

## Best solution

Make comparison setup resilient to incidental navigation without committing unfinished choices or allowing unavailable models to launch. Agree on dismissal and incompatible-workspace behavior before changing the comparison selection lifecycle.
