[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335645](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335645)

## Summary

Confirmed on main: the download progress message builds "Downloading {0} agent" with a lowercase noun, and a sibling Codex account action hardcodes "Downloading Codex agent…" — both inconsistent with title-case "Agent" branding used elsewhere. Filed by the maintainer who introduced the recent PR, so this is a quick, well-scoped polish fix rather than a close.

## Change summary
