# Issue #305818 — Detect, surface, and recover orphaned Copilot Chat sessions from moved/renamed/deleted workspaces

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/305818](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/305818)

## Summary

This remains a well-supported Backlog feature request: 84 community upvotes and repeated reports show a real recovery gap when workspace identity changes. The related reports are newer and narrower symptoms, so they do not meet the stronger duplicate bar for this re-reviewed item.

## Change summary

Detect chat sessions orphaned by moved, renamed, deleted, or newly saved workspaces, make them browsable, and provide a safe way to relink or migrate them.

## Best solution

Provide workspace-independent discovery of persisted chat sessions, then support explicit user-controlled relinking or migration when workspace identity changes. The design should avoid automatic folder-name matching that could attach private history to the wrong workspace.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat`, `src/vs/workbench/services/workspaces`, `src/vs/platform/storage`
- **Validation:** End-to-end acceptance scenario: create a workspace chat session, rename or move the workspace, reopen it, verify the prior session is discoverable and readable, explicitly relink it, and confirm unrelated workspaces' sessions are neither exposed nor attached.
- **Behavior:**

  > Chat sessions must not become effectively lost when a folder is renamed, moved, deleted, or an Untitled Workspace is saved under a new identity. Users should be able to discover and read retained sessions whose original workspace no longer resolves, and explicitly associate recoverable sessions with the intended current workspace. Recovery must not silently expose or attach sessions from unrelated workspaces. Existing workspace-scoped history behavior should remain intact for unchanged workspaces.
- **Trace:**

  > The report identifies workspace identity as the boundary between persisted chat sessions and the Chat history UI: changing the workspace URI produces a different storage identity while old session data remains under the previous workspace storage entry. External tools cited in the thread demonstrate partial recovery, but there is no integrated discovery, browsing, or relinking contract. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301793 covers one older observable case—saving an Untitled Workspace—while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314799 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334076 are newer, narrower reports. Planning should trace chat-session enumeration together with workspace storage metadata and define a privacy-preserving identity/recovery boundary; it must not change normal history behavior for unchanged workspaces or automatically trust a folder-name match.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should orphaned sessions appear in the existing Chat history UI or in a separate global history/recovery surface?
  - Is recovery a copy, a move, or a persistent link, and should the original workspace association remain visible after recovery?
  - Which evidence is sufficient to suggest a destination workspace, and must every relink require explicit confirmation?
  - Should deleted-workspace sessions remain indefinitely discoverable, or follow a retention and deletion policy?
