# Issue #331011 — Flexible Context Window & Custom Thinking Effort for BYOK

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/331011](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/331011)

## Summary

Keep open: current main provides configurable BYOK thinking-effort levels and a fixed context window, but does not cover selectable context-window presets or the requested Max-effort persistence exception. The older related issues cover portions of this request, not its full observable behavior.

## Change summary

Allow custom-endpoint models to advertise selectable context windows and thinking efforts, with configurable defaults and remembered selections except Max effort.

## Best solution

Extend the existing BYOK model-configuration surface rather than introduce parallel controls, coordinating context-window presets with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329892. Retain this issue's distinct default-selection and persistence requirements until their product semantics are agreed.
