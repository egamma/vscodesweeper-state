The current source confirms that a configured hyphen can prevent terminal link-handler initialization. Route to the terminal area and keep open while the existing fixing PR is reviewed.

## Change summary

## Evidence

- **Source-confirmed trigger:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminalContrib/links/browser/terminalWordLinkDetector.ts:129-136 inserts escapeRegExpCharacters(separators) directly into a regex character class. The reporter's |-/ sequence therefore creates a descending range and throws; a trailing hyphen can instead form an unintended range with the appended Powerline characters.
- **Escaping helper:** src/vs/base/common/strings.ts:89-90 does not escape hyphens, confirming that the detector does not treat every configured separator literally.
- **Initialization failure:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminalContrib/links/browser/terminalLinkManager.ts constructs TerminalWordLinkDetector before assigning xterm.options.linkHandler at line 116. The detector constructor immediately calls _refreshSeparatorCodes, so the regex exception prevents installation of the modifier-gated handler. The subsequent native dialog and blank-window behavior were not runtime-verified.
- **Open fixing PR:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/349213 is verified open with merged_at null. Its body explicitly says 'Fixes #349210' and describes literal hyphen escaping plus regression cases for descending ranges and trailing hyphens; keep the issue open pending review and merge.
