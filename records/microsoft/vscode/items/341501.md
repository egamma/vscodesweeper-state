## Summary

Keep open for a visual accessibility assessment: current Dark 2026 still uses closely spaced active/inactive tab backgrounds, while the connected-tab presentation has evolved. The supplied theme, screenshot, affected surface, and environment suffice to retest; neither a fixing commit nor a qualifying duplicate was verified.

## Change summary

Dark 2026 makes active and inactive editor tabs difficult to distinguish, particularly on lower-quality displays.

## Best solution

Ensure the default Dark 2026 tab presentation clearly identifies the active editor in focused and unfocused groups. Assess the complete selection indicator, including boundaries and foregrounds, before choosing a theme-level adjustment; a background contrast measurement alone does not establish the accessibility of the whole control.
