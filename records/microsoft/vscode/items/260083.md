## Summary

Still reproducible on main: the DiffHunkWidget constructor eagerly creates a MenuWorkbenchToolBar per hunk, and the ObjectPool keeps returned widgets alive without disposing their toolbar listeners. Two fix PRs (#304152 and #309287) were closed without merging. A team member commented this may not be a true leak but deferred to Joh for a decision — keeping open for area-owner triage.

## Change summary

Listener-leak warning fires in telemetry when many DiffHunkWidgets exist because each eagerly creates a MenuWorkbenchToolBar that subscribes to menu-registry events, and the ObjectPool does not dispose returned widgets.

## Best solution

Either make toolbar creation lazy (created on first show, disposed on hide) as PR #309287 attempted, or remove the ObjectPool and dispose widgets on each render cycle as PR #304152 attempted. Joh (area owner per roblourens' comment) should decide the right approach — both PRs were closed without merging, so the fix strategy is still open.
