[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343906](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343906)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still exposes cross-session chat moves and session nesting through drag and drop without equivalent keyboard actions. The assigned 1.145.0 milestone independently requires keeping this scheduled accessibility work open.

## Change summary

Provide keyboard- and screen-reader-operable alternatives for moving a nested chat into another session and nesting a single-chat session under another session.

## Best solution

Expose both existing move operations through accessible Sessions list actions with destination selection, preserving provider eligibility, move feedback, undo, and focus behavior. Review the linked open PR separately; its stated undo-restoration scope does not establish that these missing actions are implemented.
