# Issue #177409 — Markdown/output warmup can block renderer process

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/177409](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/177409)

## Summary

Team-authored performance issue about markdown/output warmup blocking the renderer. Multiple related improvements have landed (viewport-only warmup, idle rendering, selective warmup), but no commit references this issue directly, and the `under-discussion` label suggests the architectural concern may remain open.

## Change summary

Opening a notebook with thousands of markdown cells blocks the renderer for hundreds of ms or seconds due to warmup dimension callbacks.

## Best solution

Evaluate whether the current viewport-scoped warmup (commit 2ea6f55705d, Feb 2025) and idle-rendering paths adequately address the original concern for large notebooks, or whether further batching/deferral of dimension updates from the webview is still needed.
