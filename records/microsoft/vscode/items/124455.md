[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124455](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124455)

## Summary

Keep open for a completion-ranking decision: current scoring still trades contiguous matches against word-boundary matches, and existing settings do not guarantee substring-first ordering. This team-authored request has a later corroborating report, but no verified fix or eligible canonical duplicate.

## Change summary
