[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238598](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238598)

## Summary

Keep open: the notebook outline renderer still omits the tooltip text needed to preview an item's full name. This is accepted Backlog work; neither the low vote count nor the related richer-symbol-hover request establishes a reason to close it.

## Change summary

## Best solution
