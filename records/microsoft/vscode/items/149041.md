# Issue #149041 — `retainContextWhenHidden` does not work with `when` clauses for Webview Views

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149041](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149041)

## Summary

The current source still disposes the entire webview view when its `when` condition becomes false, bypassing the retention behavior used for ordinary visibility changes. Keep this accepted, team-authored bug open; neither related item establishes a core fix or a qualifying canonical duplicate.

## Change summary

Preserve a retained webview's live context across temporary hiding caused by its contributed view's `when` condition.

## Best solution

Make conditional hiding preserve retained webview content without changing explicit user-hide disposal or retaining views after permanent removal. This needs lifecycle design across view-container removal, webview ownership, and extension-host disposal notifications.

## Evidence

- **Conditional hiding disposes the pane:** In src/vs/workbench/services/views/common/viewContainerModel.ts:593-646, onDidChangeContext evaluates the view's `when` condition and broadcasts removal of visible descriptors when it becomes false. In src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/views/viewPaneContainer.ts, onDidRemoveViewDescriptors calls removePanes; removePane disposes paneItem.disposable. addPane registers the pane itself in that disposable store, so this destroys the pane rather than merely hiding it.
- **Retention cannot survive owner disposal:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/webviewView/browser/webviewViewPane.ts owns its overlay through a registered MutableDisposable and fires onDispose when the pane is disposed. src/vs/workbench/api/browser/mainThreadWebviewViews.ts forwards that event through $disposeWebviewView and removes the handle. By contrast, src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/overlayWebview.ts:178-199 honors retainContextWhenHidden only in release(); its dispose() path tears down the overlay regardless of that option.
- **API boundary and reporter's comparison:** src/vscode-dts/vscode.d.ts:10254-10279 explicitly documents that unchecking a view in its context menu disposes it, unlike collapsing a view or switching sidebar groups. Thus the reporter's claim that context-menu hiding retains context does not match the current contract; a correction for conditional hiding must preserve the documented explicit-hide behavior.
