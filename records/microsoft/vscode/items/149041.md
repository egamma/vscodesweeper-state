# Issue #149041 — `retainContextWhenHidden` does not work with `when` clauses for Webview Views

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149041](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149041)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: when a webview view's `when` clause becomes false, the pane is fully disposed (destroying the webview), rather than just hidden — so `retainContextWhenHidden` has no effect in that path. The bug remains unfixed; keeping open with `help wanted`.

## Change summary

Webview views with `retainContextWhenHidden: true` lose their state when hidden via a `when` clause evaluation, because the view pane is disposed rather than retained.

## Best solution

The fix likely needs `WebviewViewPane` to persist its webview (or at least its serialized state) across dispose/re-create cycles triggered by `when` clause changes — either by separating the webview lifecycle from the pane lifecycle when `retainContextWhenHidden` is set, or by having the view container model hide rather than remove the pane when a `when` clause toggles. The fix touches `src/vs/workbench/contrib/webviewView/browser/webviewViewPane.ts` and possibly `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/views/viewPaneContainer.ts`.
