# Issue #328722 — Unable to collapse an expanded sbagent

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328722](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328722)

## Summary

Confirmed by two other team members and a community reporter: a subagent section, once manually expanded while the subagent is still actively working, can't be collapsed again until the subagent finishes. The assigned engineer indicated this UX is being replaced rather than patched, so this should stay open and be resolved as part of that redesign.

## Change summary

The chevron/collapse button on an expanded subagent section in chat becomes unresponsive to manual collapse while the subagent is still running.

## Best solution

Either fix the toggle interaction in ChatSubagentContentPart/ChatCollapsibleContentPart (src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatContentParts/chatSubagentContentPart.ts, chatCollapsibleContentPart.ts) so manual clicks on the collapse button always take effect regardless of `isActive`, or land the planned replacement subagent UX that justschen referenced, whichever comes first.
