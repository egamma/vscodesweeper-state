## Evidence

- **Confirmed trigger and workaround:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/208068, the reporter describes Ctrl+S on a new file followed by a custom filename, with no installed extensions. Their March 29 comment confirms that the problem disappears without Sogou IME, that Notepad++ works, and that Chrome becomes unresponsive during a comparable save operation.
- **Native crash evidence:** The maintainer-attached symbolicated dump at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/files/14796084/symbolicated-d7.log reports EXCEPTION_HEAP_CORRUPTION. Crashed thread 51 includes ntdll!RtlReAllocateHeap followed by sogoutsf.ime, Windows text-input cleanup, COMDLG32!CFileOpenSave::Show, and Electron file_dialog::ShowSaveDialogSync at file_dialog_win.cc:246. This supports a native IME/dialog interaction, without proving which native component introduced the corruption.
- **Current source boundary:** src/vs/workbench/services/dialogs/electron-browser/fileDialogService.ts delegates local saves through FileDialogService.pickFileToSave/showSaveDialog to nativeHostService.showSaveDialog. src/vs/platform/dialogs/electron-main/dialogMainService.ts delegates to electron.dialog.showSaveDialog; the inspected path contains no Sogou-specific handling. Source inspection cannot establish whether this environment-dependent native crash persists on the current runtime.
- **Existing bypass is not a fix:** FileDialogService.shouldUseSimplified in src/vs/workbench/services/dialogs/electron-browser/fileDialogService.ts honors files.simpleDialog.enable and selects the simplified save-dialog path. This bypasses the implicated native dialog, but was not exercised and does not establish resolution of the reported crash.

The older open https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/167556 concerns hangs when switching between local and Remote Desktop sessions; its Sogou mentions describe a workaround, not this failure. It does not meet the stronger duplicate bar for this re-reviewed item.
