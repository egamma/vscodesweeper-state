For the reported optional-group replacement, this preserves the literal \u preceding $1; subsequent native replacement removes the unmatched $1 but leaves \u in the output.
- **Short-capture exception in current source:** In src/vs/workbench/services/search/common/replace.ts:124–138, the loop is bounded only by caseOps.length. With replacement \u\u\u$1 and capture id or x, replacement[i] becomes undefined before the modifiers are exhausted, and the lowercase-u branch calls toUpperCase() on it.
