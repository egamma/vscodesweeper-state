## Change summary

Copilot agent tools intermittently misclassify README.md inside a samba-mapped network drive workspace (W:/Workspace) as an external file, prompting for approval even though it's inside the workspace.

## Best solution

Needs a maintainer or the reporter to confirm the exact URI forms being compared before a fix can be scoped.
