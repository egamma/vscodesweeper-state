Selecting the added lines and staging them produces an index diff that also deletes the preceding paragraph; this is not merely a stale staging indicator.
- **Current source path:** extensions/git/src/commands.ts:1769–1800 intersects working-tree changes with the selection and passes them to _stageChanges at lines 1932–1945. In extensions/git/src/staging.ts:109–146, intersectDiffWithRange retains the entire original range when the original and modified hunk lengths differ, while narrowing only the modified range. applyLineChanges then replaces that entire original range with the selected modified lines, explaining the unexpected staged deletion.
- **Verified open canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/9475 was verified OPEN. Its discussion explicitly reports that selecting added lines also stages removed lines within the same hunk: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/9475#issuecomment-2074926269. A later confirmation describes deleted lines being forced into staging whenever adjacent additions are staged: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/9475#issuecomment-2668167716. The canonical therefore owns this behavior, not only selection of standalone deletions.
- **Other candidates distinguished:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230423 concerns the gutter Stage Selection action, with commenters reporting that the context-menu command works. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/64027 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/283148 concern stale index content after successive operations.
