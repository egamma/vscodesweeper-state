Keep open: current source still permits the reported duplicate-ID refresh to corrupt tree-node mappings and prevent subsequent updates. This is a confirmed core-tree regression, and the team-authored issue is protected from judgment closes.

## Change summary

A transient duplicate sibling TreeItem.id permanently breaks subsequent tree-view refreshes until the window reloads.

## Best solution

Ensure a transient duplicate-ID refresh cannot leave the tree model inconsistent and that subsequent valid refreshes recover normally. Preserve the concurrent expansion behavior addressed by the earlier tree fixes.
