- **Current source retains a relevant conflict path:** src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/model/inlineCompletionsModel.ts:790-866 protects empty lines and multiline/full-content selections, but an empty selection in the indentation of a nonempty line does not receive that protection. tabShouldJumpToInlineEdit can consequently remain true; src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/controller/commands.ts:279-309 binds that state to Tab and calls controller.jump().
