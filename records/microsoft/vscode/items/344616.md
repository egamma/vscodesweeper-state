# Issue #344616 — Add opt-in setting to keep Primary Side Bar visible when maximizing Secondary Side Bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344616](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344616)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: current maximization deliberately hides the Primary Side Bar, and existing settings do not provide the requested opt-in behavior. The related open issue covers toggling the Primary Side Bar after maximization, not this request's full maximize-and-restore contract.

## Change summary

Allow opt-in Secondary Side Bar maximization that preserves the Primary Side Bar's visibility and width, including subsequent sidebar toggles and layout restoration.

## Best solution

Have the workbench layout owner evaluate a backward-compatible maximization mode that preserves project navigation without revealing the editor. Coordinate its toggle behavior with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/300729 while retaining this request's additional requirements for an already-visible Primary Side Bar and restoration.
