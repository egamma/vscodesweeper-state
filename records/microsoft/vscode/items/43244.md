## Evidence

- **Remaining source-visible continuation gap:** In extensions/javascript/javascript-language-configuration.json and extensions/typescript-basics/language-configuration.json, increaseIndentPattern handles unmatched opening delimiters and indentNextLinePattern handles arrow functions and control-flow headers, but neither matches the report's `var transformedValues =` or subsequent method-chain lines. src/vs/editor/common/languages/autoIndent.ts:getIndentForEnter delegates to getInheritIndentForLine; its first-line fallback preserves leading whitespace when neither increase nor indent-next-line matches. Thus the initial assignment-continuation example still receives no additional indentation through this path.
- **Partial fixes already present:** Both inspected JavaScript/TypeScript configurations contain indentNextLinePattern for if/while/for/else and arrow functions, plus an onEnterRule that outdents after a braceless body. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/251465 is merged and explicitly addresses else consistency and PHP indentation; its documented scope does not cover assignment continuations or method chains.
