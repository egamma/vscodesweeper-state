[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/253436](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/253436)

## Summary

Keep open: a maintainer investigated and confirmed the variable-font flashing during line moves, and no verified fix or matching canonical was found. The original test used a dedicated experimental decoration provider, so current rendering needs retesting before choosing a solution.

## Change summary

Moving lines with Alt/Option+Up/Down temporarily loses variable-font decorations, causing visible flashing while they are recomputed.

## Best solution

Maintain stable variable-font rendering during line moves without waiting for asynchronous symbol-provider recomputation. First retest the original decoration-driven scenario on current main and distinguish it from newer tokenization-driven font rendering before deciding whether a general decoration-preserving move operation is warranted.
