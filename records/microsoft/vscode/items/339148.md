# Issue #339148 — Dictation intermittently omits word spaces and inserts literal <en-US> tags

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339148](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339148)

## Summary

Keep open: multiple users independently confirm missing word spaces and literal <en-US> tags, including on Stable 1.143.1. Cleanup failures can leave the damaged transcript visible, but do not establish the cause of either transcription defect.

## Change summary

Built-in chat dictation should preserve spoken word boundaries and exclude language metadata from inserted text, independently of optional LLM cleanup.

## Best solution

Diagnose the raw transcription and assembly path separately from cleanup-model availability, then correct the confirmed source of each artifact. Preserve usable transcription when cleanup is disabled or unavailable rather than relying on cleanup to repair malformed input.
