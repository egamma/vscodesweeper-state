# Issue #316156 — [Agents App] Worktree/Folder dropdown is easily missed or misunderstood

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316156](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316156)

## Summary

Keep open for a focused UX assessment: current main adds explanatory isolation choices and conditional onboarding, but these do not establish that users reliably notice and understand the choice before starting. Related reports do not meet this re-reviewed item's same-symptom duplicate bar.

## Change summary

Make the Agents Window's worktree-versus-folder choice discoverable and understandable before the first prompt.

## Best solution

Assess the current new-session composer against the study's discoverability and comprehension failures, then agree whether onboarding suffices or the persistent mode control needs stronger labeling and prominence. Preserve the distinction between creating an isolated checkout and working in the existing folder.
