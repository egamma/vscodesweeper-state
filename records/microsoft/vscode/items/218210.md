## Summary

The empty-conflict marker gap remains in current source: zero-width content ranges are skipped, and conflict headers provide no overview-ruler marker. Keep this open, distinguishing the existing minimap-adjacent overview ruler from a request for new highlighting inside the minimap.

## Change summary

Keep unresolved merge conflicts discoverable in the editor overview when their contents are empty or consist of a single blank line.
