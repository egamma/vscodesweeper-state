Verified duplicate of the open editor-GPU issue https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301420: both reports produce corrupted or missing text after font-size changes and the identical 16-page texture-atlas exhaustion error.

## Change summary

Repeatedly zooming and scrolling with experimental editor GPU acceleration enabled eventually exhausts the glyph texture atlas and renders black rectangles instead of editor text and inline suggestions.

## Best solution

Consolidate the investigation in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301420, retaining this report's reliable zoom-and-scroll reproduction, Windows hybrid-GPU details, and inline-suggestion impact. The editor GPU renderer needs to prevent obsolete font-size glyphs from exhausting the shared 16-page atlas.
