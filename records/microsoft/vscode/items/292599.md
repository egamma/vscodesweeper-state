# Issue #292599 — Polish the Welcome Page for folks who've disabled AI Features

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292599](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292599)

## Summary

Keep open: the classic Welcome page still lacks the requested refreshed presentation, although recent workspaces are already available. This team-authored follow-up needs design agreement, particularly on whether a dismissible AI re-enablement message belongs on an explicitly AI-disabled surface.

## Change summary

Bring the refreshed Welcome page's visual hierarchy and walkthrough presentation to users who have disabled AI features, with a possible dismissible explanation of that state.

## Best solution

Provide a consistent, non-AI Welcome experience with refreshed header, workspace, and walkthrough presentation while preserving AI-disable behavior. Settle the optional re-enablement message's visibility and dismissal semantics before implementation.
