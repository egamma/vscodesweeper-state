## Summary

The reported read-only allowlist mismatch remains source-reproducible: nested workspace symlinks undergo confirmation without consulting additionalReadAccessPaths for their resolved targets. Keep this permission-boundary issue open for human review; the evidence shows excessive prompting, not unauthorized access.
