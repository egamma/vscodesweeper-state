[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/274166](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/274166)

## Summary

A real gap remains on main: environment-variable destinations are detected conservatively and blocked from auto-approval, but they are not resolved as requested. The issue is already correctly classified and routed.

## Change summary

Resolve environment variables such as `$HOME` when analyzing terminal file-write destinations.

## Best solution

Safely resolve shell-appropriate environment variables before workspace-boundary checks in the terminal command-line file-write analyzer, while continuing to block destinations that cannot be resolved confidently.
