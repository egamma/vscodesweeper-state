# Issue #188657 — Explore inline word highlighting in info texts

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/188657](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/188657)

## Summary

Keep this design exploration open: inline-code support already exists, but that does not establish completion of the requested shared styling, documentation, and wider adoption. The maintainer's question about whether it remains necessary has not received an answer.

## Change summary

Make variable words in informational text easier to scan through consistent inline-code styling and broader use.

## Best solution

Have the UX/theme owners assess representative informational surfaces against the existing inline-code treatment, then define any remaining styling and adoption work. Reuse the existing MarkdownString and theme-color mechanisms rather than introducing a separate highlighting feature.
