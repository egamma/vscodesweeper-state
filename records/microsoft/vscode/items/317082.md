# Issue #317082 — Mermaid: Something captures my scrolling and stops me

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317082](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317082)

## Summary

Team-authored bug, confirmed by a second contributor (mjbvz). The scroll-forwarding from mermaid webviews into the chat panel interrupts momentum-based trackpad scrolling. No fix has landed on main.

## Change summary

Mermaid diagram webviews in chat capture/interrupt momentum-based trackpad scrolling, halting the user's scroll through chat.

## Best solution

Fix the scroll-event re-transmission from the mermaid webview into the chat panel so that momentum scrolling is not interrupted. The issue likely lives in how `did-scroll-wheel` events are forwarded from the webview iframe (src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webviewElement.ts) and how the mermaid chat webview handles wheel events (extensions/mermaid-markdown-features/preview-src/chat/mermaidWebview.ts). The synthetic re-dispatch may need to preserve momentum state or avoid re-dispatching in a way that resets the scroll physics.
