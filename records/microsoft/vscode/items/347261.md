## Evidence

- **Re-entrant event loss confirmed in source:** src/vs/base/common/event.ts:1630-1645 schedules a microtask only when _queuedEvents.length becomes 1. In the supplied merged-event reproduction, the original queue remains populated during super.fire(), so the listener's 'second' and 'third' events schedule nothing and are discarded by the subsequent _queuedEvents = [].
- **Disposal cleanup missing:** PauseableEmitter, DebounceEmitter and MicrotaskEmitter in src/vs/base/common/event.ts:1544-1647 inherit Emitter.dispose() without overriding it. Emitter.dispose() at lines 1226-1257 removes listeners but does not clear these subclasses' queues or cancel DebounceEmitter._handle. Paused payloads remain retained while the emitter remains reachable; debounce timers survive disposal until their callbacks execute.
- **Qualification of the disposal claim:** Emitter.dispose() clears _listeners and _size, and Emitter.fire() does nothing without listeners. Thus the source supports retained payloads, an uncancelled timer and deferred merge work, but not the issue body's claim that already-disposed listeners receive microtask events.
- **Existing test coverage:** src/vs/base/test/common/event.test.ts contains DebounceEmitter, PauseableEmitter and Microtask Emitter tests. The Microtask Emitter test at lines 683-704 checks ordinary batching, not re-entrant dispatch. No tests were executed in this read-only review.
