## Evidence

- **Re-entrant event loss:** src/vs/base/common/event.ts:1621–1647: MicrotaskEmitter.fire schedules only when _queuedEvents.length === 1 and clears the array after synchronous listener delivery. In the supplied merged-event reproduction, the listener appends 'second' and 'third' to the still-populated array, schedules no microtask, and those entries are then discarded.
- **Disposal cleanup:** src/vs/base/common/event.ts:1544–1614: PauseableEmitter retains its LinkedList buffer and DebounceEmitter retains its timeout handle; neither overrides dispose(). Emitter.dispose at lines 1226–1257 clears listeners and the delivery queue but not these subclass resources.
- **Scope clarification:** MicrotaskEmitter likewise has no disposal override, so queued objects remain until its scheduled callback runs. However, Emitter.dispose clears listeners: the source does not support the report's claim that disposed listeners themselves are subsequently invoked. The confirmed concerns are delayed cleanup and pending callback execution.
- **Existing implementation candidate:** The supplied timeline identifies https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/347262, 'event: handle re-entrancy and cleanup on disposal in emitters', as an open PR referencing this issue. This is a reason to review the existing contribution, not close the issue or start a competing implementation.
