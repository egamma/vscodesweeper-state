## Summary

Keep open: current source confirms a retained-buffer growth path during repeated large replacements and undo, although the reported accumulation of thousands of undo entries is not explained by those steps. Investigate backing-buffer retention separately from undo-history limits before choosing a fix.

## Change summary

Prevent excessive retained renderer memory during repeated large-file edits and undo over long sessions.

## Best solution

Make memory retention proportional to live document content, necessary undo history, and outstanding snapshots rather than obsolete backing buffers. Diagnose the supplied edit cycle first; trimming undo entries alone would not address the backing-buffer growth traced here.
