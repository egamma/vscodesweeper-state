Keep open: the merged fixes remove two major amplifiers, but this team-authored report explicitly tracks additional root causes that remain visible in current source, including incomplete-worktree recovery and the unbounded session lookup contract.

## Change summary

Switching Agents Window sessions can block the renderer for seconds when a pathological large changeset drives repeated session and diff resolution.

## Best solution

Finish hardening archive/unarchive so a registered but partially deleted worktree is repaired rather than accepted as healthy, then establish a bounded lookup contract for `ISessionsManagementService.getSession()`. Preserve the merged feedback-session deduplication and deferred hidden-editor loading.
