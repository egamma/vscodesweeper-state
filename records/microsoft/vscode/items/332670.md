# Issue #332670 — [Perf] Agents Window hangs for 12s when switching sessions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332670](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332670)

## Summary

Team-authored perf report describing three compounding problems; one of the three (the per-file feedback overlay loop calling getSession() per candidate) was fixed and merged with an explicit 'Refs #332670', but the other two — worktree recreation leaving a partially-deleted checkout, and the O(n) rebuild-and-scan contract of getSession() itself — remain unaddressed on main, so this stays open.

## Change summary

Switching Agents Window sessions with a large/corrupted worktree freezes the renderer for several seconds due to a partially-deleted worktree inflating changed-file counts, a per-file feedback overlay loop, and an expensive getSession() lookup.

## Best solution

Land the two remaining fixes described in the issue: harden recreateWorktreeOnUnarchive in src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/shared/worktreeIsolation.ts so a partially-deleted worktree is detected and repaired (not just existence-checked), and reconsider ISessionsManagementService.getSession()'s O(n) rebuild-and-scan contract (still unchanged in src/vs/sessions/**) so callers like automationsView.ts, sessionsView.ts, and sessionsTitleBarWidget.ts aren't each paying full catalog-rebuild cost.
