# Issue #278442 — 3 Days of Messages Disappeared from Chat

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/278442](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/278442)

## Summary

Keep this open as the canonical chat-history-loss bug. Multiple independent reports describe conversations becoming blank or undiscoverable after restart or update, and a maintainer later identified this issue as the broader tracker.

## Change summary

Copilot Chat conversations and recent messages disappear from history after restarting or updating VS Code.

## Best solution

Ensure saved chat sessions remain indexed, visible, and readable across restart and update, including migration of persisted session-filter state. Diagnosis should distinguish genuinely missing session files from an index or filter state that merely hides recoverable sessions.
