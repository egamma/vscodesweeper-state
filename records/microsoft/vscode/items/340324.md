## Summary

Team-authored bug report with a careful log-based diagnosis of a session whose title-bar status stayed 'Working' after completion; the reporter themselves could not distinguish a missed completion-status update from a persistence/restoration bug, and no fix exists on main yet, so this stays open for further investigation.

## Change summary

A completed Agent Host session's status bit (InProgress) was not cleared, so the title bar kept showing 'Working' the next day even though the final turn had finished normally.

## Best solution

Trace why the session's InProgress status bit survived past normal turn completion — likely in the snapshot/summary persistence path (src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/agentHostStateManager.ts) or the status-flag reducer (src/vs/platform/agentHost/common/state/protocol/channels-session/reducer.ts) — and add a safeguard so a restored/re-announced summary can't resurrect a stale InProgress bit after the owning turn has ended.
