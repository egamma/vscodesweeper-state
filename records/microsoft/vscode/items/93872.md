[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/93872](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/93872)

## Summary

The discussion contains a confirmed extension-level reproduction showing that rename edits leave the rename-on-type decoration covering the wrong range. This team-authored issue should remain open because there is no referenced fixing commit or verified older canonical issue.

## Change summary

After an F2 rename replaces a synced region with longer text, the rename-on-type feedback decoration no longer covers the full renamed text.

## Best solution

Ensure the rename-on-type owner recomputes and reapplies its feedback decoration after rename edits, rather than relying on generic decoration range tracking through minimized edits.
