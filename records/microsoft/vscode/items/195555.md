# Issue #195555 — Completion widget assigns too much space to details and not enough to the completion item label

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/195555](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/195555)

## Summary

Keep open: the default completion-row layout still allows secondary details to compete with the completion label. Existing controls offer workarounds, but do not deliver the requested balance while retaining inline details.

## Change summary

Give completion labels more usable space relative to inline details when suggestion rows must truncate.

## Best solution

Agree on a consistent truncation policy for completion rows that preserves useful identifying text before secondary metadata. Apply it in the suggest widget without requiring users to hide details or continually widen the list.

- **Behavior:**

  > A completion row should preserve enough primary identifying text to distinguish suggestions even when secondary inline details are long. Inline details should remain useful when space permits, rather than requiring users to disable them entirely. Acceptance checks should cover string and structured labels, focused and unfocused rows, and user-resized narrow lists. The exact allocation and truncation priorities must be agreed before those checks become a contract.
- **Trace:**

  > ItemRenderer.renderElement in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidgetRenderer.ts maps labels, signatures, and descriptions into left and right flex columns. src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/media/suggest.css allows both columns to shrink and caps the right column at 70%; structured-label signatures also truncate inside the left column. SuggestWidget in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidget.ts enables a different label-preserving layout only through the internal fitWidthToDetails option, whose default is false in editorOptions.ts. The public showInlineDetails setting hides metadata rather than resolving the requested hierarchy. Related feedback at https://github.com/Dart-Code/Dart-Code/issues/5415 corroborates the tradeoff but does not settle the design. Preserve completion filtering, ordering, insertion, explicit widget resizing, and access to full details; the uncertainty is the observable allocation policy, not the implementation mechanism.
- **Open decisions:**
  - When a row cannot fit its contents, should the identifying label remain untruncated until secondary details have disappeared, or should details retain a bounded share while the label also truncates?
  - For structured completion labels, should the signature in label.detail share the primary label's space priority, or truncate ahead of the identifying label like right-hand metadata?
