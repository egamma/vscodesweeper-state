# Issue #186353 — Lost terminals during double reload

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186353](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186353)

## Summary

Keep open: the maintainer supplied a reliable reproduction, and the attached logs show panel processes failing to reconnect while the editor terminal survives. Current source identifies relevant asynchronous restoration paths, but does not establish a fix or conclusively reproduce the timing failure.

## Change summary

Preserve both split panel terminals and the editor terminal when the window is reloaded twice in quick succession.

## Best solution

Make terminal restoration resilient to a second reload during reconnection, preserving existing processes, buffers, and split layout. First reproduce the reported Linux scenario and establish whether layout persistence, orphan detection, or interrupted group restoration causes the loss.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/electron-browser/localTerminalBackend.ts`, `src/vs/platform/terminal/node/ptyService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/test/browser/terminalService.test.ts`
- **Validation:** On Linux with persistent sessions enabled, create two split bash panel terminals displaying distinct markers and a third editor terminal running git log. Reload twice rapidly, including while restoration is in progress; verify that all three original shell processes, their buffers, and the panel split survive. Repeat beyond the process reconnection grace period to detect delayed termination. The plan should establish a deterministic regression check for the diagnosed race.
- **Behavior:**

  > Two rapid window reloads must preserve existing persistent terminals instead of replacing the split panel pair with a fresh shell. The two panel terminals retain their distinct output and split relationship, and the editor terminal remains attached to its original process. Acceptance includes a second reload during restoration and verification after the reconnection grace period, not merely immediately after the window appears.
- **Trace:**

  > The original report at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186353 contains reliable Linux steps; its attached logs show both panel processes missing a subsequent reconnection and later expiring. TerminalService._reconnectToLocalTerminals in src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalService.ts starts _recreateTerminalGroups asynchronously, and restoration only accepts targets marked isOrphan. LocalTerminalBackend transfers layout information, while PtyService expands saved IDs and PersistentTerminalProcess._isOrphaned evaluates asynchronous renderer replies. These paths establish the investigation boundary, not a proven root cause on current main. Diagnose the second-reload timing before choosing an implementation; preserve ordinary single-reload behavior, editor-terminal restoration, and safeguards against attaching a process owned by another renderer. Hidden agent terminals and full application-exit revival are outside the reported case.
