[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/185950](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/185950)

## Summary

Keep under discussion: adjustable cursor padding already exists, but it does not satisfy the separate requests for Tab-out navigation and skipping formatting-only lines. The related reports inspected are closed and cover only parts of this request, so they do not justify a duplicate or whole-issue decline.

## Change summary

Improve keyboard navigation through paired characters and substantive code lines, provide cursor scroll padding, change Ctrl+Arrow behavior, and support angle-bracket autoclosing.

## Best solution

Separate the existing-setting guidance from the remaining product requests: use editor.cursorSurroundingLines for padding and configurable keybindings for cursor movement. Evaluate Tab-out and substantive-line navigation independently, accounting for existing extension solutions and language-specific angle-bracket behavior rather than changing all defaults together.

## Evidence

- **Existing scroll-padding capability:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts:6284–6299 registers editor.cursorSurroundingLines and editor.cursorSurroundingLinesStyle. These directly cover point 3 by keeping configurable leading and trailing lines visible around the cursor, but do not cover the other requests.
- **Current Tab behavior:** src/vs/editor/browser/coreCommands.ts:2024–2044 routes the ordinary Tab command through TypeOperations.tab. src/vs/editor/common/cursor/cursorTypeEditOperations.ts:844–881 implements indentation and insertion rather than skipping closing delimiters; snippets are not equivalent to the requested general paired-character navigation.
- **Current Ctrl+Down behavior:** src/vs/editor/browser/coreCommands.ts:1506–1528 binds Ctrl+Down on Windows/Linux to scrollLineDown and explicitly sets revealCursor:false. This matches the described scrolling behavior; requesting another default is a keybinding preference rather than proof of broken cursor movement.
- **Language-dependent angle brackets:** extensions/typescript-basics/language-configuration.json includes angle brackets in surroundingPairs and colorizedBracketPairs but not autoClosingPairs. editor.autoClosingBrackets defaults to languageDefined, so surrounding selections and automatically inserting a closing angle bracket are distinct capabilities; the report does not identify a target language.
