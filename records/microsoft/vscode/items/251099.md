# Issue #251099 — Use Word Replacement/Insertion View

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251099](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251099)

## Summary

The requested compact NES presentation remains a real gap on current main: single-line insertions before the cursor are explicitly routed to the line-replacement view. This team-authored Backlog item should remain open while the intended word-replacement versus insertion treatment is decided.

## Change summary

Render the demonstrated single-line next-edit suggestion with a compact word-replacement or insertion view instead of replacing the whole line visually.

## Best solution

Choose the compact presentation for a single-line insertion before the cursor, preserve cursor stability and acceptance behavior, and cover the demonstrated `new ` insertion with a focused view-selection regression test.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsView.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsViews/inlineEditsWordReplacementView.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsViews/inlineEditsInsertionView.ts`
- **Validation:** Add a focused inline-edits view-selection test using the issue's single-line `new ` insertion with the cursor after the edit; assert that it selects the maintainer-approved compact view, preserves the cursor, and leaves multiline, indentation-only, and diff-editor cases unchanged.
- **Behavior:**

  > For the demonstrated NES edit that inserts `new ` into an existing line before the cursor, VS Code should use the approved compact word-replacement or insertion presentation rather than a whole-line replacement view. The cursor must remain stable while the suggestion is displayed, and accepting it must produce exactly the provider's edit. Existing behavior for multiline edits, indentation-only changes, and diff editors must remain unchanged.
- **Trace:**

  > View selection is centralized in `InlineEditsView._determineView` in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsView.ts. A single-line insertion after the cursor selects `InsertionInline`, while the before-cursor branch explicitly selects `LineReplacement` because inserting inline would move the cursor; the later `WordReplacements` branch also guards against insertion-shaped edits. The renderers already exist in inlineEditsWordReplacementView.ts and inlineEditsInsertionView.ts. The uncertainty starts at the intended visual and cursor contract for before-cursor insertions; do not broaden the work to multiline, indentation-only, or diff-editor rendering.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should a single-line insertion before the cursor be presented by growing it into a word replacement, or should the insertion view gain a cursor-stable presentation for this case?

## Evidence

- **current source:** In src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsView.ts, `_determineView` routes a single-line insertion after the cursor to `InsertionInline`, but explicitly routes an insertion before the cursor to `LineReplacement` to avoid moving the cursor.
- **word replacement exclusion:** The subsequent `WordReplacements` selection in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsView.ts rejects edits that remain insertions after whitespace growth, so the issue's insertion-shaped example does not clearly reach the requested compact view.
- **available views:** The relevant renderers already exist in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/view/inlineEdits/inlineEditsViews/inlineEditsWordReplacementView.ts and inlineEditsInsertionView.ts; the unresolved work is the selection and cursor-behavior policy.
