# Issue #342110 — Customizations: mark first-party (Microsoft/GitHub) items and show publisher without drilling in

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342110](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342110)

## Summary

Keep open: Discover rows do not expose publisher metadata or a first-party marker. Existing publisher text in legacy gallery rows and first-party browse prioritization only partially address the request.

## Change summary

Expose customization publishers directly in list rows and distinguish Microsoft/GitHub first-party items without opening details.

## Best solution

Make publisher attribution visible as consistent secondary information in the shared customization lists. Define what authoritative identity qualifies an item for a first-party marker, keeping that meaning distinct from publisher verification or a safety endorsement.
