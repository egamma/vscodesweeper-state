## Change summary

Allow hiding the integrated browser's navigation toolbar, at least in compact floating windows.

## Best solution

Let the browser editor skip mounting or collapse the navigation toolbar widget, either through a setting or automatically when it is the only editor in a compact auxiliary window, with a keybinding such as focusing the address bar to bring it back. The change would live in browserEditor.ts and the navigation features under workbench/contrib/browserView.
