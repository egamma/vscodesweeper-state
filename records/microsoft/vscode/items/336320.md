## Summary

Keep open for the remaining Connected-tab design feedback: the reporter's October 10 update still identifies alignment, first-tab framing, and insufficient outline visibility after the linked refinements merged. Current source supports configurable borders, but that does not settle the requested default appearance across themes.

## Change summary

Refine Connected tabs so active and inactive content align, the first tab integrates cleanly with the editor frame, and the active outline remains distinguishable across themes.

## Best solution

Agree on the remaining Connected-tab geometry and default outline contrast, then validate them in Dark Modern and Dark 2026 at default and compact density. Preserve the active tab's open connection to the document and existing theme customizations rather than treating partial refinements as completion.
