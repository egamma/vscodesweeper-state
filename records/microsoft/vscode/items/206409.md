# Issue #206409 — Hover over quick pick

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/206409](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/206409)

## Summary

Keep this team-authored polish issue open: current main still has a path for a delayed workbench hover to appear after a quick pick opens, and the discussion leaves the desired layering or dismissal behavior unsettled.

## Change summary

Prevent a workbench hover initiated over an Explorer item from subsequently covering an opened quick pick.

## Best solution

Define a consistent overlay policy in which opening a quick pick either dismisses or suppresses unrelated workbench hovers without disabling hovers belonging to the quick pick itself.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputController.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputService.ts`, `src/vs/platform/hover/browser/hoverService.ts`, `src/vs/platform/hover/test/browser/hoverService.test.ts`, `src/vs/platform/quickinput/test/browser/quickinput.test.ts`
- **Validation:** With a delayed Explorer-item hover pending, open a quick pick without moving the pointer and verify that no unrelated hover appears over it; separately verify that tooltips for quick-pick items still appear normally.
- **Behavior:**

  > Opening a quick pick while an unrelated workbench hover is visible or pending must not allow that hover to cover the picker. Quick-pick-owned item tooltips must continue to work. Acceptance should cover both an already-visible hover and one whose delay expires after the quick pick opens.
- **Trace:**

  > Managed mouse hovers are scheduled by HoverService.setupManagedHover in src/vs/platform/hover/browser/hoverService.ts. QuickInputController.show in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputController.ts displays and focuses the quick input without coordinating with IHoverService; QuickInputService in src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputService.ts likewise has no hover-takeover policy. Context-menu opening already dismisses workbench hovers through HoverService, but whether quick inputs should dismiss the underlying hover or merely suppress it while visible remains unsettled in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/206409#issuecomment-2549197442. Preserve hovers whose targets are inside the quick pick and avoid changing general hover timing.
- **Open decisions:**
  - When a quick pick opens, should an unrelated workbench hover be dismissed permanently or suppressed and allowed to return after the picker closes?
  - Should the same policy apply to every quick input, including anchored quick inputs, or only modal overlay-style quick picks?
