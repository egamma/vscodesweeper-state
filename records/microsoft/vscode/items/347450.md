- **Behavior:**

  > Reverting a selected modification that leaves document layout unchanged must preserve the viewport and cursor position. The modified content must return to its Git baseline, while unrelated changes remain intact. Acceptance checks exercise both command-palette and keybinding invocation from a viewport away from the document top.
- **Trace:**

  > In extensions/git/src/commands.ts, CommandCenter.revertSelectedRanges restores selections after _revertChanges. The helper replaces the entire document, starts workspace.applyEdit without awaiting completion, awaits save, and reveals the previously visible range. MainThreadTextEditor.revealRange in src/vs/workbench/api/browser/mainThreadEditor.ts delegates to smooth range revelation; ViewLines._computeScrollTopToRevealRange in src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLines/viewLines.ts expands the requested range with padding and can reposition oversized ranges to their padded top.
