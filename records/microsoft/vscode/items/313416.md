# Issue #313416 — Copilot: "Could not build codebase semantic index" on macOS for GitLab repo (works on Windows)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313416](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313416)

## Summary

This is a server-side rollout issue for Copilot's 'external ingest' feature (semantic indexing of non-GitHub repos). The feature is gated by a token-level policy flag (`isBlackbirdExternalIndexingEnabled`) and experiment configs, explaining the inconsistent availability across users and platforms. Comment 12 reports it's now working, but the rollout status for all users is unclear.

## Change summary

Copilot's semantic indexing fails for non-GitHub repositories (GitLab, BitBucket, local repos) with 'No indexable repos found' even after the v1.118 release notes announced it was available to all users.

## Best solution

The Copilot team should confirm whether the external-ingest rollout is now complete for all subscription tiers and platforms, and update or close this issue accordingly. If the rollout is complete, users who still see the error may need to sign out and back in to refresh their Copilot token. The error message in `codeSearchRepo.ts` line 84 could also be improved to suggest troubleshooting steps rather than stating 'support is not available.'
