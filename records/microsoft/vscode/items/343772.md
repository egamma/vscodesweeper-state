## Summary

Keep open: existing sandbox bypass support does not establish parity with the requested proactive, command-scoped approval workflow. The credential-access and replay concerns require human review of the sandbox security boundary, which independently bars a close.

## Change summary

Restore predictable, individually approved access to the local development environment while keeping ordinary Copilot/AHP commands sandboxed and accurately reporting partially completed attempts.

Distinguish temporary parity gaps from intentional credential-isolation choices without requiring session-wide sandbox disablement.
