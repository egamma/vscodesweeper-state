- **Current copy-action path:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/actions/chatCopyActions.ts registers workbench.action.chat.copyAll. CopyAllAction.run synchronously serializes eligible conversation items through stringifyItem, joins them, converts the result with toPortableMarkdown, and invokes clipboardService.writeText without awaiting or returning its promise. This exposes a potential failure-reporting gap but does not conclusively explain this conversation's failure.
