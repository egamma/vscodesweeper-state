# Issue #250499 — Cell creation wasn't auto revealed

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250499](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250499)

## Summary

Keep open: maintainers accepted improving visibility during agent-driven notebook creation and execution, while leaving output-following behavior undecided. The existing execution-follow setting does not establish coverage of newly created cells or subsequently arriving outputs.

## Change summary

Keep agent-created notebook cells and their execution results meaningfully visible without requiring manual scrolling.

## Best solution

Define notebook creation and output visibility within the general agent-follow experience, preserving users' ability to avoid unsolicited scrolling. Resolve whether newly arriving outputs should trigger a separate reveal or continuous following, then validate that behavior across viewport sizes.

Whether output growth triggers further scrolling remains a maintainer decision.
