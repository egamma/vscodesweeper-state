## Summary

Keep this exploration open: current source still implements decorator-based constructor injection, and the discussion has not settled its replacement. The exploration/tracking protection also rules out closing it; the related DI rework issue is newer and does not qualify as a canonical for this re-reviewed item.

## Change summary

Explore replacing legacy parameter-decorator service injection with a standards-oriented approach compatible with TS-native, mangling, and low runtime overhead.

## Best solution

Agree on a replacement injection contract before planning a repository-wide migration, preserving service lifetime, dependency ordering, cycle diagnostics, and efficient service access. Coordinate with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/289077 without treating that newer, broader exploration as a duplicate target.

## Evidence

- **Current injection mechanism:** src/vs/platform/instantiation/common/instantiation.ts still defines createDecorator as a parameter decorator, stores parameter-index metadata through storeServiceDependency, and exposes it through _util.getServiceDependencies. The requested migration is not complete in the inspected checkout.
- **Dependency metadata has operational consumers:** src/vs/platform/instantiation/common/instantiationService.ts uses decorator metadata in _createInstance to resolve and position constructor arguments. _createAndCacheServiceInstance also consumes it to build the dependency graph, instantiate dependencies first, and detect cycles; replacing it is not merely a syntax change.
- **Existing accessor is not the migration:** InstantiationService.invokeFunction supplies a ServicesAccessor whose get method becomes invalid after the callback returns. This existing mechanism does not replace decorator-based createInstance construction or establish the proposed field-initializer injection contract.
- **Discussion establishes unresolved contract choices:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/276181#issuecomment-3517416384 proposes context-bound Injected calls, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/276181#issuecomment-3518294415 raises direct-construction and unrestricted-access concerns. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/276181#issuecomment-3521488866 additionally identifies mangling problems with name-based injection.

Its body broadly proposes evaluating service registration, cycles, testing, performance, and developer experience; it is newer than this 2025-11-07 report and fails the re-reviewed-item duplicate bar.
