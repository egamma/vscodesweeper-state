# Issue #45395 — Double-click selection matches some escaped characters

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/45395](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/45395)

## Summary

A real word-navigation gap remains on current main: selection and deletion classify individual separator characters rather than recognizing escape sequences or syntax-token boundaries. This accepted Backlog request already has the correct area and discussion labels.

## Change summary

Make word selection and word deletion avoid combining escape-sequence suffixes such as `n` in `\nword` with the following identifier.

## Best solution

Introduce a language-aware or pattern-aware word-boundary mechanism shared by double-click selection and word deletion, likely in the editor cursor word operations. Coordinate the broader token-boundary design with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325221 while retaining this escaped-sequence case as a concrete requirement.
