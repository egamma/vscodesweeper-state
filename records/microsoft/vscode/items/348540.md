Keep open: the discussion provides a confirmed idle-follow-up reproduction, and current source still permits a silent WebSocket stall to remain pending. The transport root cause and relationship to the other reported hangs are not yet established.

## Change summary

Agent chat requests sometimes produce no response after inactivity or during long-running operations, with cancellation and retry restoring progress.

## Best solution

Establish the failing transport path and provide bounded, explicit recovery from a silently stalled request without interrupting legitimate long-running work or replaying completed actions. Investigate the captured WebSocket case separately from missing agent-question UI and host-turn lifecycle failures.
