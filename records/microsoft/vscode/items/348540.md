# Issue #348540 — Chat won't give response

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348540](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348540)

## Summary

Keep open: the discussion supplies a confirmed idle-then-prompt reproduction and trace logs, while current source still permits an indefinite first-event wait. The evidence supports investigating client recovery, but does not establish whether the initiating failure is in the connection, proxy, or backend.

## Change summary

Copilot requests can remain pending after inactivity until the user cancels and retries.

## Best solution

Establish why the reused WebSocket stops producing responses and provide bounded, safe recovery in the Copilot networking path. Distinguish this transport stall from the separately reported missing-question UI behavior.

- **Behavior:**

  > After a successful Copilot turn, a follow-up sent after inactivity can remain pending without a response or visible error. The finished behavior must give the user a bounded outcome rather than requiring indefinite waiting followed by manual cancellation. Healthy long-running requests and normal cross-turn context reuse must remain supported, and recovery must not duplicate user-visible actions.
- **Trace:**

ChatWebSocketManager.getOrCreateConnection in extensions/copilot/src/platform/networking/node/chatWebSocketManager.ts reuses a locally open, inactive, same-model connection without checking idle age or liveness. These facts establish a silent-stall recovery gap, not the origin of the silent transport; diagnosis must distinguish proxy interruption from backend or stateful-response behavior. Preserve healthy streaming, cancellation, and conversation context, and do not treat the separately mentioned HTTP timeout concern or missing-question UI symptom as proven causes of this captured WebSocket failure.
