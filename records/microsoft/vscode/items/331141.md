Fresh, team-authored bug report (roblourens) about the new chat pet widget shifting chat content when the window loses/regains focus, with a video demonstrating the glitch; no fix exists on main yet, and team-authored reports are protected from judgment closes anyway, so this stays open for a maintainer to address.

## Change summary

Chat content appears to shift position when the pet hides/reappears in response to window focus/blur.

## Best solution
