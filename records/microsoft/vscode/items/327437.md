# Issue #327437 — Agents window: background session terminals keep their renderer-side xterm alive, growing unbounded

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327437](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327437)

## Summary

Keep open and route to the terminal area. The heap evidence and current source confirm that archived-session terminals retain their renderer-side xterm buffers without a bound.

## Change summary

Bound renderer memory retained by background terminals from archived Agents window sessions while preserving recoverable terminal state.

## Best solution

Decouple the renderer-side xterm lifecycle from the persistent pty in the shared terminal implementation, releasing xterm buffers when backgrounded and recreating them from pty replay when shown. An LRU cap in the Sessions terminal contribution is a reasonable interim mitigation.

## Evidence

- **heap analysis:** The issue reports 38 live XtermTerminal instances retaining approximately 80 MB across 10,791 buffer allocations, with counts matching session-associated terminals.
- **archive lifecycle:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/terminal/browser/sessionsTerminalContribution.ts explicitly calls moveToBackground from _hideTerminalsForSession when a session is archived, preserving the terminal rather than disposing it.
- **renderer retention:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalService.ts moveToBackground retains the complete TerminalInstance in _backgroundedTerminalInstances; TerminalInstance.detachFromElement in terminalInstance.ts only removes the wrapper element and does not dispose its xterm field.
- **intentional origin:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/e76364f9169461749f908e7fa5b33ae9e2296252 intentionally preserved Agents window terminals on archive, but introduced no renderer dehydration or background-instance cap.
