## Evidence

- **Documented incident:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348411 correlates 2,996 rg processes with active threads and approximately 64.71 GiB of private commit to one local extension host. The initiating query, command lines and cancellation sequence were not recovered; the relationship to the Windows graphics bugcheck remains unconfirmed.
- **Follow-up controls:** The complete issue body reports unsuccessful passive monitoring and bounded Markdown file-change probes. Built-in searches completed successfully during a logging control, demonstrating that interval process snapshots can miss short-lived searches; these controls do not establish the original trigger.
- **Independent corroboration:** The November 16 comment by adi1112 at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348411 reports more than 200 rg processes and resource exhaustion on the same VS Code version. Its rollback comparison also changed drivers, extension configuration and privileges; the commenter explicitly states that a deterministic reproducer and initiating extension remain unidentified.
- **Current source boundary:** src/vs/workbench/api/node/extHostSearch.ts registers extension-host file and text search providers. src/vs/workbench/services/search/node/ripgrepFileSearch.ts:spawnRipgrepCmd and ripgrepTextSearchEngine.ts:searchWithRipgrep spawn the bundled executable; fileSearch.ts:FileWalker.cancel and the text-search cancellation handler attempt child termination. The parent PID and executable path alone therefore do not identify the requesting component or prove a cancellation defect.
- **Related reports are not verified duplicates:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/307001 describes bracket-containing paths and repeated discovery scans; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/193927 describes network-drive PATH lookups and unkillable processes; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328122 supplies specific overlapping file-enumeration commands. This report has not established any of those distinguishing conditions.
- **Extension attribution remains unproven:** The reporter already links https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/38105, where worktree creation and uninstalling the Codex extension distinguish the incident. Neither condition has been reproduced here, and merely having openai.chatgpt and built-in Copilot Chat activated does not establish ownership.
