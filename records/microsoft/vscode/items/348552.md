For the reported \\?\D:\README.md, the drive colon follows the prefix rather than appearing at the start; the remaining backslash-prefixed segment cannot match a numeric suffix. The assertion rejects both the bare path and the :1:1 form.

For the reported input, this preserves the prefixed drive path; the identified rejection occurs earlier in argument validation.
