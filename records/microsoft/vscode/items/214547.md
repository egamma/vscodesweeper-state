## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/terminal/node/terminalProfiles.ts`, `src/vs/platform/terminal/node/ptyHostService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalProfileService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalView.ts`

Confirm the distribution appears after discovery completes without opening PowerShell; repeat after a reload and verify normal non-WSL profiles remain available.
- **Behavior:**

  > After a clean Windows boot, an installed WSL distribution should be available in the New Terminal profile menu once profile discovery completes. Opening another terminal must not be required to make it appear. Validate the initial dropdown and subsequent refresh while preserving ordinary terminal profiles and intentional profile exclusions.
- **Trace:**

  > LocalTerminalBackend.getProfiles delegates through PtyHostService.getProfiles to detectAvailableProfiles in src/vs/platform/terminal/node/terminalProfiles.ts. getWslProfiles enumerates distributions with a one-second command timeout, and its caller omits WSL profiles on enumeration failure. TerminalProfileService performs asynchronous throttled discovery and publishes changed results; TerminalView subscribes to those results and constructs the dropdown from availableProfiles. These paths identify the investigation boundary, but do not determine whether the reported cold-start omission is an enumeration failure or a refresh-timing problem. Preserve terminal.integrated.useWslProfiles, explicit profile exclusions, and non-WSL behavior. The closed setting-refresh report https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/188985 is context, not proof that this startup defect is resolved.
