# Issue #350116 — Copilot auto submits answers while I didnt press submit

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350116](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350116)

## Summary

Keep open for a question-carousel interaction decision: current source deliberately submits when the final single-choice answer is clicked and can save automatically selected answers when navigating. That explains the reported surprise but does not establish that the interaction should remain unchanged.

## Change summary

Prevent selecting the final questionnaire answer from unexpectedly submitting earlier answers the user did not explicitly choose.

## Best solution

Make questionnaire completion and acceptance of default answers explicit to the user. The chat owner should decide whether final-option selection should require a separate confirmation, particularly after navigating past an unanswered question.
