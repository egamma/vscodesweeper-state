- **Behavior:**

  > Users report very slow update, WSL Server, and Agents-window CLI downloads in mainland China despite normal browser installer downloads. The goal is to reduce these delays without requiring a proxy or compromising download correctness. Acceptance should compare identical artifacts under equivalent network conditions and demonstrate improvement on the affected flow. Parallel downloads are a proposed means, not an established remedy.
- **Trace:**

  > DownloadService.download in src/vs/platform/download/common/downloadService.ts uses one GET and writes its stream. WindowsUpdateService.doCheckForUpdates in src/vs/platform/update/electron-main/updateService.win32.ts has a separate single-response package-download path with progress and checksum handling. src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/wslRemoteAgentHostHelpers.ts constructs single-curl CLI installation commands. The ordinary Remote-WSL Server downloader is extension-owned and was not traced in this checkout; do not assume all three operations share DownloadService. The comments on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347631 support investigating the delivery endpoint, but do not establish a client defect or show that concurrent ranges would help. Preserve existing integrity, cancellation, proxy, and installation behavior; the unresolved boundary is delivery-side versus client-side responsibility.
