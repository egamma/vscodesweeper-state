# Issue #149264 — margin of context between right and left are different in notebook

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149264](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/149264)

## Summary

Notebook content still uses unequal left and right spacing in current source. Keep this as the existing consolidation point for notebook spacing polish; the related report was explicitly merged into this issue, not fixed independently.

## Change summary

Give notebook content balanced horizontal spacing and sufficient separation from the right scrollbar.

- **Behavior:**

  > Notebook content should have consistent horizontal breathing room rather than appearing crowded against the right scrollbar. The original report asks for balanced left and right margins; the report merged into it additionally documents long Markdown content touching the scrollbar. Acceptance should cover rendered Markdown and cell editors in compact and noncompact layouts without reducing access to notebook controls.
- **Trace:**

  > NotebookOptions in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/notebookOptions.ts supplies separate gutter, left-margin, right-margin, and Markdown preview-padding values. computeWebviewOptions() combines the Markdown gutter and left margin, while backLayerWebView.ts applies that combination as preview padding-left and the smaller preview padding as padding-right. Cell editor widths separately subtract their gutters and cellRightMargin, and notebookEditorWidget.ts generates associated layout styles. notebook.compactView reduces left spacing but does not provide symmetry. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/151366 was explicitly consolidated here and provides the scrollbar-adjacency scenario. The uncertainty is the desired visual relationship between the functional left gutter and right content spacing, not whether unequal values exist. Preserve control placement, focus indication, and notebook diff behavior unless the agreed design explicitly includes them.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should visual balance mean equal total content insets from the notebook edges, including the left control gutter, or equal content padding outside a separately reserved gutter?
