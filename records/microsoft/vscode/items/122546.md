# Issue #122546 — Sign in quick pick dialog: focus movement could be improved

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/122546](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/122546)

## Summary

Keep open: individual checkbox tab stops have been removed, but that does not establish the requested first-Tab transition to Sign in. The discussion also identifies a description-announcement problem that the checkbox fix does not address.

## Change summary

Improve keyboard focus movement and screen-reader context in the Settings Sync sign-in quick pick.

## Best solution

Make the hidden-input Settings Sync picker provide a predictable keyboard path to Sign in while preserving arrow-key navigation and Space toggling of sync categories. Confirm that its introductory description is announced when opened, without disturbing other quick-pick keyboard behavior.
