- **Proposed comment:**

  > Thanks for the question. The autocomplete popup is controlled by VS Code's editor, not by a single extension: `editor.quickSuggestions` controls suggestions while typing, and `editor.suggestOnTriggerCharacters` controls suggestions after characters such as `.`. The suggestions themselves can come from built-in language support or installed extensions; Copilot is not required for the popup.
  > 
  > To disable automatic popups, set `editor.quickSuggestions` and `editor.suggestOnTriggerCharacters` to `false`. If you mean inline ghost text instead, that is controlled separately by `editor.inlineSuggest.enabled`.
