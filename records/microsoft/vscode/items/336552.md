# Issue #336552 — Pressing "x" on a remote submenu in workspace picker should not close the menu

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336552](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336552)

## Summary

Fresh, clearly-described report (with an attached video) that clicking the remove ('x') button on a remote entry inside the workspace picker's remote submenu closes the whole picker instead of just removing that entry — plausible and in-scope, filed today with no discussion yet.

## Change summary

Clicking 'x' to remove a remote entry inside the workspace picker's remote submenu closes the entire picker instead of just removing the entry and keeping the submenu open.

## Best solution

Investigate the nested submenu ActionListWidget flow in src/vs/platform/actionWidget/browser/actionList.ts: `onListSelection`'s toolbar-click exclusion (checking `.action-list-item-toolbar`) exists to prevent exactly this, but `_showSubmenuForElement`'s `submenuDelegate.onSelect` unconditionally calls `this.hide()` — worth confirming why a remove click on a submenu row (used by the sessions workspace picker's remote entries in src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/sessionWorkspacePicker.ts, `extended.onRemove`) still reaches that path instead of being filtered out like it is in the top-level list.
