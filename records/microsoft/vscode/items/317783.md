# Issue #317783 — too fast scrolling when drag&drop into editor (holding shift)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317783](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/317783)

## Summary

The recording and two independent reports confirm a current editor drag-and-drop auto-scroll regression: short or split editors begin scrolling while the pointer is still well inside the editor, making nearby drop targets difficult to reach.

## Change summary

Dragging text or Explorer items into a short editor, especially while holding Shift, triggers excessively early and rapidly accelerating vertical auto-scroll.

## Best solution

Adjust the editor drag auto-scroll calculation so its activation zone and speed remain usable in short editor viewports, while preserving distance-proportional acceleration near the actual top and bottom edges.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewZones/viewZones.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts`
- **Validation:** Add or identify an editor browser test that simulates drag movement within a short viewport and verifies that auto-scroll remains inactive away from the effective top/bottom edges, then accelerates monotonically only as the pointer approaches or passes those edges.
- **Behavior:**

  > Dragging selected text or an Explorer item into an editor must not scroll the document merely because the pointer enters a short or split editor. Auto-scroll should begin only near the effective top or bottom edge and should accelerate predictably with distance, without moving a nearby visible drop target away before it can be reached. The behavior must remain usable when Sticky Scroll and the panel reduce the editor's content height, and Shift-modified drops must follow the same geometry.
- **Trace:**

  > The issue and recording establish that the regression is sensitive to available vertical editor space; the reporter specifically reproduces it in a split editor showing only a few lines, while the recording uses an open terminal panel. The editor's mouse/drag handling and view-zone geometry are the likely starting points, including `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts` and `src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewZones/viewZones.ts`. A maintainer noted recent work making scroll speed proportional to distance from the editor edge, so diagnosis should trace how the effective edge and proportional distance are calculated when sticky scroll or constrained layout consumes much of the viewport. Preserve normal drag-and-drop semantics, text selection dragging, and established acceleration for pointers genuinely near or outside full-sized editor edges.

- **independent reports:** The reporter reproduced the same behavior when dragging from Explorer into a split editor showing only a few lines; another user reports that the behavior changed from the previous day and becomes worse as available vertical space decreases.
