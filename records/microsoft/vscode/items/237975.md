## Evidence

- **Current-source trigger:** src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findModel.ts defines MATCHES_LIMIT as 19999. At lines 522–526, unscoped Replace All switches to _largeReplaceAll when matchesCount reaches that limit. The reporter's 30000 matching REPLACE/ME/23 lines trigger this branch; the small example does not.
- **Confirmed semantic mismatch:** src/vs/editor/common/model/textModelSearch.ts:169–172 sends non-multiline searches through _doFindMatchesLineByLine. In contrast, findModel.ts:532–569 upgrades the regex to multiline anchors and applies replace to the entire LF-joined document. For the supplied pattern, [^/] can therefore consume the newline between two KEEP/001 lines and replace both with 001, deleting otherwise unmatched content.
- **Open implementation candidate:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/341969 was verified OPEN with mergedAt null and an explicit closing reference to this issue. Its diff addresses _largeReplaceAll and adds a regression test using KEEP/001 lines followed by more than MATCHES_LIMIT matching lines. It is a proposed fix, not evidence of completion.
- **Independent reproduction:** The supplied discussion records albertosantini reproducing the original sample on 1.96.3 and narrowing the trigger to the match-count boundary. The reporter subsequently describes unintended content loss during batch replacement.
