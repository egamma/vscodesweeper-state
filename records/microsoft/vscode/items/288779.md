# Issue #288779 — FileLoggerService logger can't increase verbosity

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/288779](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/288779)

## Summary

The defect remains source-reproducible on current main: increasing a BufferLogger's verbosity after its FileLogger delegate is installed does not update that delegate, so trace messages pass the outer filter and are then discarded by the inner logger. This accepted Backlog bug should remain open.

## Change summary

Changing an extension log output channel from Info to Trace does not increase the underlying FileLogger's verbosity, causing trace messages to be dropped.

## Best solution

Keep the BufferLogger and its installed delegate at the same effective log level when callers change verbosity, while preserving buffering and delegate-originated level updates. Cover both directions of level synchronization with a focused platform log unit test.

## Evidence

- **current source:** In src/vs/platform/log/common/bufferLog.ts, BufferLogger's logger setter copies the delegate level into the wrapper and subscribes to delegate changes, but BufferLogger does not override setLevel to propagate later wrapper changes back to its _logger.
- **failing path:** src/vs/platform/log/common/fileLog.ts creates a BufferLogger and later installs a FileLogger initialized from logger.getLevel(). After installation, AbstractLogger.setLevel in src/vs/platform/log/common/log.ts changes only the BufferLogger's own level; forwarded trace calls are filtered again by the unchanged FileLogger.
- **related fix is distinct:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/287729 changed extension-specific default-level selection during logger initialization. It does not synchronize a later BufferLogger level increase with an already-installed FileLogger delegate.
