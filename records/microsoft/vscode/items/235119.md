## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/contrib/multicursor/notebookSelectionHighlight.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/notebookEditorWidget.ts`
- **Validation:** With notebook.multiCursor.enabled and editor.selectionHighlight enabled and editor.occurrencesHighlight disabled, open a notebook containing repeated words across more cells than fit onscreen. Select a word, repeatedly scroll its cell and matching cells out of and back into view without changing the selection, and verify matching highlights remain visible and continue updating when another word is selected.
- **Behavior:**

  > Selecting text in a notebook highlights matching text across its cells. Repeated scrolling through offscreen cells must not disable those highlights while the selection remains unchanged. Acceptance includes scrolling the selected cell and matching cells out of view and back, then selecting another word and confirming highlighting still updates.

  > The report at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/235119 derives its setup from https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/235047. Current remote source for NotebookSelectionHighlighter in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/contrib/multicursor/notebookSelectionHighlight.ts retains an anchor editor and registers its cursor listener on active-cell changes; non-explicit cursor events clear notebook decorations.
