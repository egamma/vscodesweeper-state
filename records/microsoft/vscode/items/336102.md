# Issue #336102 — Copilot managed OTel policy can be missed during extension activation

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336102](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336102)

## Summary

Verified against current main: the async policy-definition update listener in configurations.ts is fired without extension activation awaiting it, and Copilot's OTel service is constructed once from `policyValue` reads that can be undefined at that moment, with no later reconfiguration — matching the reporter's exact trace.

## Change summary

Enterprise-managed Copilot OTel policy can arrive after the Copilot extension has already built a non-managed OTel service for that extension host, silently exporting no enterprise telemetry for its lifetime.

## Best solution

Two independent fixes are needed: (1) in src/vs/platform/configuration/common/configurations.ts, make extension activation wait on the async `updatePolicyDefinitions` listener (or otherwise expose an awaitable barrier for late policy-definition registration) before extensions that depend on policy start up; (2) in extensions/copilot's OTel service construction (extensions/copilot/src/extension/extension/vscode-node/services.ts) and its reload watcher (extensions/copilot/src/extension/otel/vscode-node/otelContrib.ts), fall back to the core `chat.agentHost.otel.*` policy owner when the late `github.copilot.chat.otel.*` policy reference is still unresolved, and snapshot pre-construction values so late-arriving policy triggers a reload/reconfigure rather than being silently missed.

## Evidence

- **async listener not awaited:** src/vs/platform/configuration/common/configurations.ts:130 — `this._register(this.defaultConfiguration.onDidChangeConfiguration(async ({ properties }) => this.update(await this.updatePolicyDefinitions(properties), true)));` — the Emitter fire at line 48 does not await listeners, confirming the reported race.
- **one-shot OTel service construction:** extensions/copilot/src/extension/extension/vscode-node/services.ts:298-336 reads `otelSettings.inspect(key)?.policyValue` once and permanently registers NodeOTelService or InMemoryOTelService based on that single read.
- **reload watcher snapshots after the fact:** extensions/copilot/src/extension/otel/vscode-node/otelContrib.ts:126-133 takes `initialValues` snapshot after service construction and only prompts reload on subsequent changes — a policy value that resolves between the service's read and this snapshot is missed, exactly as described.
- **no existing core fix:** No barrier/await mechanism for late policy-definition registration found in configurations.ts, and no fallback-to-core-policy-owner logic found in the Copilot OTel service construction path.
