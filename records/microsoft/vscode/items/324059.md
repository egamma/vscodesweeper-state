Keep open pending review of the linked fixing PR: the evidence identifies a PowerShell grammar limitation around standalone `--`, rather than Default Approvals ignoring all configured rules. The upstream defect and open implementation candidate preclude a completed close or a competing agent session.

## Change summary

Ultimately correct the standalone-token handling in the tree-sitter PowerShell grammar; assess the reporter's separate session-wide approval observation independently.
