# Issue #349619 — Support for Navigable Groups in IntelliSense Suggestions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349619](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349619)

## Summary

Navigable, provider-defined completion groups are not supported by the current Suggest widget. The referenced prefix-grouping request was declined, but its narrower scope does not establish a decision on this generalized proposal.

## Change summary

Allow completion providers to organize suggestions into selectable groups with navigation back to the original list.

## Best solution

Have the IntelliSense owner assess whether generic completion-group metadata and enter/back navigation warrant extending the completion contract, taking the earlier prefix-grouping decision into account. Preserve ordinary completion acceptance and a consistent keyboard-accessible experience.

- **Existing capabilities do not cover the request:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts registers editor.suggest.localityBonus, selectionMode and show-kind filters. These rank, select or hide suggestions; they do not provide selectable categories with enter/back navigation.
- **Flat widget behavior:** src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestWidget.ts:234 constructs a List of CompletionItem entries; showSuggestions inserts completionModel.items at line 588.
