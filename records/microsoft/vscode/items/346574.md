# Issue #346574 — Pet bouncing game high score is made too easy by the space bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346574](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346574)

## Summary

Keep open as a game-balance improvement: keyboard bouncing is intentional, but it awards the same streak credit without the pointer's timing requirement. No maintainer decision establishes whether that scoring advantage should change.

## Change summary

Make repeated Space presses less effective at inflating the pet's bounce streak.

## Best solution

Decide whether keyboard and pointer bouncing should have comparable scoring difficulty, while retaining keyboard access. Any adjustment belongs in the bounce interaction and scoring paths, not the separate platform-jumping game.
