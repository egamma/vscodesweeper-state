# Issue #346574 — Pet bouncing game high score is made too easy by the space bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346574](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346574)

## Summary

Keyboard bouncing is explicitly supported and deliberately contributes to the bounce count. Keep this open as gameplay-balance feedback: the source explains the advantage, but does not establish that the scoring balance should remain unchanged.

## Change summary

Make rapid Space presses less effective at accumulating a pet bounce streak than skilled pointer catches.

## Best solution

Have the pet owner decide how keyboard and pointer bouncing should compare in difficulty and scoring, while preserving keyboard accessibility. Any adjustment belongs in the pet's bounce interaction rather than the shared Button control.
