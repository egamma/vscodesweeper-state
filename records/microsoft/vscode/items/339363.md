## Change summary

Central feedback thread on the removal of per-change Keep/Undo controls in chat under the Copilot harness, and on how much granular control users need over agent changes.

## Best solution

Keep this open as the single feedback hub. Use it to decide whether the Copilot harness needs a per-file or per-change accept/reject review step. Also make the Local harness switch and its settings easier to find.
