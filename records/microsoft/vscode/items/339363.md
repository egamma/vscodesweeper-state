# Issue #339363 — [Meta] Feedback on granular control over Copilot changes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339363](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339363)

## Summary

Team-authored [Meta] feedback issue, parked in the On Deck milestone, with heavy community engagement (72 reactions, 30 comments). It is a deliberately long-lived feedback collector, so it stays open.

## Change summary

Collect feedback on the removal of per-change Keep/Undo controls in the Copilot harness and on how to restore granular review and accept/reject control.

## Best solution

Keep this as the central feedback thread. The owners should decide whether to restore explicit post-edit accept/reject in the Copilot harness. They should also make the existing Local harness workaround (the `chat.defaultToCopilotHarness` and `chat.editor.preferCopilotHarness` settings) easier to discover.
