## Evidence

- **Reported trigger and workaround:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/223591 reports the warning after running xdg-open https://www.google.es in the integrated terminal during a Wayland session. Changing GDK_BACKEND from x11 to wayland removes it.
- **Independent confirmation and limited impact:** The supplied 2025-11-27 comment reports that unsetting GDK_BACKEND resolves the same warning on Debian Trixie, without observed functional problems. The 2026-01-22 commenter did not reproduce this particular warning on EndeavourOS; successful URL opening there does not invalidate the confirmed environment-specific report.
- **Current source limitation:** resources/linux/snap/electron-launch initializes wayland_available=false and checks only $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/../$wdisplay. A socket located directly at $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/$wdisplay does not enable Wayland; the later export GDK_BACKEND="wayland" is conditional on that detection. This confirms the isolated detection defect, but not its involvement in RPM or deb launches.
- **Open implementation candidates:** GitHub reads verified that https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/269449 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/339405 are OPEN, have no mergedAt timestamp, and both contain Fixes #223591. The latter also proposes absolute WAYLAND_DISPLAY support and reports socket-fixture checks; those checks were not independently executed in this read-only review.
