  > Thanks for the longstanding request and discussion. The central capability is now available through the supported extension APIs:
  > 
  > - `viewsContainers` can contribute containers to the Activity Bar, Panel, or Secondary Side Bar.
  > - Contributed views can use `TreeView` or themed webview content through `registerWebviewViewProvider`.
  > - Webview panels and custom editors cover editor-area experiences.
  > 
  > Custom view containers landed through commit 4fd4bef1355ef62ee734d5e6b0c03dbe808b7443, and stable webview views were finalized in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/f99ff58d30e518daa111e3ac595d1909d0afd131, shipped in 1.50.0. That covers the remaining example of extension-provided UI, including a bottom-panel experience, so this can close as completed. Focused requests for capabilities missing from a particular surface should continue in separate issues.
