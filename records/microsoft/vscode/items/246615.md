# Issue #246615 — Add setting to disable punctuation and capitalization

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246615](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246615)

## Summary

The requested controls remain missing from the inspected Speech settings and provider path. Preserve this accepted Backlog request and propose a maintainer transfer to microsoft/vscode-speech, the VS Code-team-owned extension tracker specified by the review policy.

## Change summary

Allow speech dictation without automatically inserting punctuation or capitalizing words after pauses.

## Best solution

Provide an opt-in Speech transcription mode that disables automatic punctuation and capitalization while preserving existing defaults. Confirm support in the extension's embedded recognition backend; changing chat submission timeouts does not satisfy this request.
