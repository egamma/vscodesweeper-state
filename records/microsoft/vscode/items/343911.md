[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343911](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343911)

## Summary

Keep open: the current source has separate announcement paths whose state does not carry across the default-chat-to-peer transition, explaining the repeated creation notice. The supplied evidence establishes misleading messaging, not a second worktree or lost data.

## Change summary

Moving a single-chat worktree session under another session repeats its worktree-created announcement on the next turn.

## Best solution

Preserve the chat's already-announced worktree state across session moves so subsequent turns do not announce creation again. Retain the original notice in restored history and preserve the existing worktree, branch, and uncommitted files.
