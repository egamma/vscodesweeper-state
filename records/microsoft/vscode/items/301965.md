# Issue #301965 — chat debug view: Unclear what 'Loaded' means for `Loaded Skills`

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301965](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/301965)

## Summary

Keep open: current discovery details still use “Loaded” for successfully discovered files, without explaining whether their contents entered the model request. The separate customization-resolution view provides partial clarification, but does not fully address this team-authored Backlog item.

## Change summary

Clarify the distinction between discovering instructions or skills, listing their references, and including their full contents in a model request.

## Best solution

Make discovery and request-context terminology unambiguous in the chat debug view, including its accessible labels and plain-text output. Explain what each stage establishes without implying that discovering a skill means its full instructions were sent to the model.
