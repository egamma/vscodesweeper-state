# Issue #315920 — Upgrade and top-up prompts lack usage-based reasoning

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/315920](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/315920)

## Summary

A real product gap remains: current quota prompts expose present-cycle consumption and generic upgrade or budget actions, but do not explain plan suitability using historical usage, repeated top-ups, or plan-cost comparisons. The newer billing meta issue is related but cannot be the canonical for this re-reviewed item, so this should remain open for product design.

## Change summary

Use historical consumption and repeated top-ups to explain whether upgrading a Copilot plan would be more economical than buying additional credits.

## Best solution

Define recommendation criteria and expose a concise usage-backed comparison in quota and upgrade prompts, while retaining generic budget actions when there is insufficient history. The design must first settle the comparison period, recommendation threshold, and source of trustworthy plan-pricing and historical-usage data.

## Evidence
