- **Current fallback vulnerability:** src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLines/viewLine.ts:103–115 removes the DOM node and clears _renderedViewLine when GPU rendering is eligible without setting _isMaybeInvalid. These boundaries support investigating strategy transitions, but do not by themselves prove this report resolved.
