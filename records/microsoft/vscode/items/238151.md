# Issue #238151 — FREETYPE_PROPERTIES="autofitter:no-stem-darkening=0" messes with product icons rendering

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238151](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238151)

## Summary

Keep open for the Electron/Linux font-rendering investigation. Independent reports confirm the same FreeType-sensitive icon distortion, but there is no verified fix; the matching open report is younger and cannot replace this re-reviewed issue.

## Change summary

Prevent FreeType stem-darkening preferences for text from distorting font-based product icons.

## Best solution

Establish a rendering approach that preserves product-icon geometry without globally clearing users' text-rendering preferences. Evaluate the reported geometricPrecision and Fontations workarounds with the Electron rendering owner before adopting either as a supported solution.

## Evidence

- **Reported trigger and maintainer analysis:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238151 reports distorted icon outlines on Fedora 41 when launched with FREETYPE_PROPERTIES="autofitter:no-stem-darkening=0", including with extensions disabled. The January 20 maintainer comment identifies Electron/Chromium's FreeType rendering of icon fonts and cautions against clearing the environment variable globally; it asks the rendering owner for further assessment rather than establishing a final scope decision.
- **Current source:** src/vs/base/browser/ui/codicons/codicon/codicon.css still loads codicon.ttf and applies text-rendering: auto with normal font weight. src/vs/workbench/services/themes/browser/productIconThemeData.ts represents product icons through fontCharacter and font definitions. This confirms the font-rendering boundary, but source inspection alone cannot establish the current visual outcome on Fedora.
