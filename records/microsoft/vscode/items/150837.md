# Issue #150837 — On save: Unable to write file, Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'lineStarts')

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/150837](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/150837)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still permits EOL normalization to invalidate an outstanding text-buffer snapshot and produce the reported undefined.lineStarts exception. The snapshot defect is demonstrable from source, although the original save-time EOL-change trigger remains unconfirmed.

## Change summary

Reading a save snapshot after the underlying piece tree rebuilds its buffers can throw instead of returning the captured text.

## Best solution

Ensure outstanding piece-tree snapshots retain their original content across EOL changes, without suppressing errors or returning truncated data. Address the bounded snapshot defect first; the original Explorer and save-conflict symptoms are not yet established as the same cause.
