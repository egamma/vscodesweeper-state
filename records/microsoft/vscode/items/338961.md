# Issue #338961 — Unicode highlight boxes outlive their highlighter when the worker result arrives after dispose

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338961](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338961)

## Summary

The disposal race remains in current main: a pending worker result can recreate Unicode decorations after their highlighter has been disposed. Keep this open as a bounded editor lifecycle bug.

## Change summary

Prevent late Unicode-highlight worker results from restoring obsolete highlight boxes after the highlighter is disposed or replaced.

## Best solution

Disposed document highlighters must no longer publish decorations or banner state, while the replacement highlighter continues honoring current options. Pin this lifecycle contract with a deterministic deferred-worker regression test in the Unicode highlighter contribution.
