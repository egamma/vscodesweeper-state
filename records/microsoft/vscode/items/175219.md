# Issue #175219 — High contrast profile badge background not aligned with other badges

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175219](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175219)

## Summary

The high-contrast styling mismatch remains in current source: the profile indicator uses an inverted filled background, while notification badges use separate colors and a contrast border. Keep this open; the source now identifies how to recreate the indicator, but its intended high-contrast treatment needs a design decision.

## Change summary

Make the profile initials indicator visually consistent with neighboring badges in high-contrast themes.

## Best solution

Agree on a consistent high-contrast treatment for the profile indicator in the workbench theme and activity-bar styling, preserving its informational role and the deliberately distinct ordinary-theme appearance.
