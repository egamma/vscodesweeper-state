## Change summary

Copy All truncates a long Copilot conversation at approximately 19,000 lines instead of copying the complete Markdown transcript.

## Best solution

Ensure long chat transcripts reach the Windows clipboard without losing their suffix. First isolate whether content disappears during transcript serialization, native clipboard transfer, or paste into the destination editor; no verified canonical issue currently absorbs this report.
