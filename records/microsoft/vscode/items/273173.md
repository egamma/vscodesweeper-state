# Issue #273173 — Search-and-replace keeps resetting to first result in Jupyter Notebooks

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/273173](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/273173)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: after a notebook replace-one operation, the content change triggers research() which recomputes all find matches and resets the current-match pointer instead of advancing to the next match, jumping the user back toward the top of the notebook.

## Change summary

Notebook search-and-replace resets the highlighted match to the first result after each single replace, forcing the user to re-navigate to their position.

## Best solution

After replaceOne completes in notebookFindWidget.ts, the follow-up research() call (triggered by the content change in findModel.ts) needs to preserve or advance the current-match index rather than falling through to findFirstMatchAfterCellIndex. The fix likely belongs in findModel.ts's _research() method — the decoration-based match restoration (lines 390–410) should be made robust against the decoration disappearing after a replace, or replaceOne should explicitly advance _currentMatch before the content-change-triggered research fires.
