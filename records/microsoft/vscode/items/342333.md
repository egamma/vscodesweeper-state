Its registration in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/chat.shared.contribution.ts offers never, onlyNew, and newAndOutdated, with newAndOutdated as the default. src/vs/workbench/contrib/mcp/browser/mcpCommands.ts also exposes an automatically-start checkbox. These controls establish that automatic startup exists, but their existence does not resolve the reported difficulty understanding the lifecycle.
- **Existing management and scope commands:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/mcp/common/mcpCommandIds.ts defines workbench.mcp.listServer, workbench.mcp.startServer, workbench.mcp.stopServer, and separate user, remote-user, workspace, and workspace-folder configuration commands. The management capabilities exist; the request concerns how users discover and understand their interaction, rather than merely adding those commands.
- **Migration explanations are only partial coverage:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/mcp/browser/mcpMigration.ts implements migration from user and remote-user settings and notifications directing users to dedicated MCP configuration. src/vs/workbench/contrib/mcp/common/mcpConfiguration.ts additionally documents discovery of Copilot CLI configuration following user-server migration.
