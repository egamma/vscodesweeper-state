Python-family extensions and C/C++ subsequently install successfully with donotVerifySignature: true.
- **current source:** src/vs/platform/extensionManagement/node/extensionSignatureVerificationService.ts maps thrown verifier errors to UnknownError with didExecute: false and logs output at Trace level. src/vs/platform/extensionManagement/node/extensionManagementService.ts:340-381 rejects that result as SignatureVerificationInternal in built Linux x64 installations. This is the shared installer path, not evidence that individual extensions caused the failure.
- **discussion qualification:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349741#issuecomment-7093104216 recommends trace logging and disabling verification. However, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338194#issuecomment-7013581524 reports that vsce-sign can hide stderr and signal-based termination even at Trace level; Executed: false alone does not establish a specific missing-library cause.
