# Issue #124820 — Proposed API checks fail due to nullExtensionDescription when symlinks (`npm link`) is used (doc needed?)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124820](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124820)

## Summary

Keep open: current source retains the extension-identity limitation for externally linked dependencies, and maintainers consolidated two related reports here. The remaining decision is whether to support that development layout or document the limitation and improve its diagnostics.

## Change summary

Make npm-linked dependencies usable with extension-specific APIs during development, or clearly explain the unsupported layout and its workaround.

## Best solution

Establish an explicit extension-development contract for dependencies outside the extension root. At minimum, document the identity restriction and explain the bundling workaround rather than leaving developers with a misleading nullExtensionDescription permission error.
