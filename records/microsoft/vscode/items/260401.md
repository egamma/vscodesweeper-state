## Summary

Keep open as a request for a keyboard-bindable NES-specific toggle. The supplied binding names a configuration setting rather than a command; current controls offer a dashboard checkbox and a broader inline-suggestions toggle, but do not establish coverage of independently toggling NES.

## Change summary
