## Summary

Keep open: the report provides sufficient steps to retest blank transcripts after switching active sessions, but current reproduction remains unverified. Session loading and visibility handling have changed since the reported build; the related visibility fix addresses a regression introduced after this report, so it does not establish resolution.

## Change summary

Switching between active sessions in the Agents Window can leave the transcript blank even though its history remains available in editor Chat and the CLI.

## Best solution

Ensure returning to an active session renders its available transcript without waiting for completion or opening another surface. Retest this older, environment-specific report first and use it as the canonical for the matching blank-on-switch symptom if the later report shares its cause.
