- **Synchronous renderer path:** src/vs/base/common/filters.ts:459–478 constructs and synchronously executes the converted RegExp before fallback matching, without collapsing wildcard runs or bounding this operation. ViewQuickAccessProvider._getPicks in src/vs/workbench/contrib/quickaccess/browser/viewQuickAccess.ts:52–62 invokes matchesFuzzy for each view label. This confirms the defective path from source; no runtime reproduction or timing measurement was performed.
