# Issue #348546 — Agents window: allow docking the entire Terminal panel on the right

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348546](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348546)

## Summary

The requested layout is not supported: the Agents window fixes the entire panel at the bottom. The related sidebar and side-by-side chat requests address different surfaces, so neither absorbs this request.

## Change summary

Allow docking and resizing the complete Output/Terminal panel to the right of the agent conversation.

## Best solution

Support right docking of the complete panel in the Sessions-owned workbench layout, preserving its tabs, terminal list, and actions, with a discoverable positioning control and resizable width.

- **Documented layout:** src/vs/sessions/LAYOUT.md describes two desktop panel alignments: below the Sessions Part and side pane, or below the Sessions Part alone. Details positioning changes content inside the side pane, not the complete Terminal panel.
