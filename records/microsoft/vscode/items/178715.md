Thanks for the detailed diagnosis. The reported failure received a merged mitigation, but the subsequent discussion leaves lifecycle concerns unresolved; retain this team-authored issue until the original Save As flow and the identified clean-model case are checked against current main.

## Change summary

Saving an untitled notebook with notebook formatting on save enabled should preserve its contents and replace the untitled editor with the saved resource.

## Best solution

Establish whether the existing disposal safeguard fully resolves the reported Save As failure, including when formatting makes no edits. If a failure remains, ensure the target notebook working copy survives the save operation without changing normal dirty-model protection.

Repeat with a formatter that changes content and one that returns no edits, and exercise the clean-model save-participant case raised in the discussion.
- **Behavior:**

  > With notebook formatting on save enabled, saving a populated untitled notebook must open the saved resource with its contents intact rather than leave the dirty untitled editor behind. This must hold whether formatting changes cell contents or returns no edits. The thread confirms a historical editor-replacement failure, not data loss; first determine whether that failure remains after the mitigation.
- **Trace:**

  > FileWorkingCopyManager.doResolveSaveTarget creates or resolves the destination working copy before saving it. FormatOnSaveParticipant.participate creates cell text-model references and releases them after formatting; notebook reference disposal can consequently reach SimpleNotebookEditorModel.dispose(), which disposes its working copy. The merged mitigation in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/180560 makes NotebookModelReferenceCollection.destroyReferencedObject await SimpleNotebookEditorModel.canDispose(), delegating stored-copy protection to StoredFileWorkingCopyManager. The later discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/178715#issuecomment-1518527229 questions clean models and participants that make no edits. Current source retains these safeguards, but the trace did not establish a remaining failure through save completion and editor replacement. Preserve dirty-model protection and normal working-copy cleanup; do not assume a general lifecycle redesign is required.
