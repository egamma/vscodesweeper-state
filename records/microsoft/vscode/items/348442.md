## Summary

Keep open: an independent reproduction confirms that sibling-root instructions are omitted, and current source still excludes these files from the explicit additional-root handoff. Discovery across multiple roots does not establish that their instructions reach the model.

## Change summary

Load workspace-wide Copilot instructions and AGENTS.md from every participating root in a multi-root Copilot session.

## Best solution

Ensure Copilot sessions receive the applicable top-level instructions from every participating workspace root without duplicate injection. Establish the runtime-loading contract before changing the discovery-to-launch handoff; customization-list display and source-navigation defects remain separate work.
