Keep open: notebook anchoring now includes upward-scroll suppression during execution, but that does not establish that scrolling into an existing matplotlib output no longer jumps. ## Change summary

Scrolling upward across the bottom of a large matplotlib cell output unexpectedly jumps the notebook viewport to the output's top.

Reproduce the recorded boundary jump on current VS Code before deciding whether the remaining defect lies in output measurement, cell anchoring, or wheel-event handling.
