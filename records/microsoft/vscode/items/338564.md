# Issue #338564 — Archiving session leaks child worktrees

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338564](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338564)

## Summary

Confirmed real gap: archiving a session has no notion of its child/forked sessions, so their worktrees leak and the children end up stuck (archived banner, can't resume) with neither desired outcome (cascade archive or leave unarchived) implemented. Author personally reproduced and confirmed in a follow-up comment.

## Change summary

Archiving a parent session with child sessions (their own worktrees) leaves the children's worktrees uncleaned and the children stuck showing an 'archived' banner that can't be resumed.

## Best solution

Decide and implement one of the two proposed behaviors (cascade-archive children and clean up their worktrees, or leave children unarchived/resumable) in sessionsManagementService.archiveSession / the agent-host provider's archiveSession, which currently has no parent-child session relationship at all.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/sessions/services/sessions/browser/sessionsManagementService.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/providers/agentHost/browser/baseAgentHostSessionsProvider.ts`
- **Validation:** A sessionsManagementService test creating a parent session with child/forked sessions with worktrees, archiving the parent, and asserting the chosen behavior (children archived+worktrees cleaned, or children left active and resumable).
- **Behavior:**

  > When a session with child sessions (each with its own worktree, e.g. created via delegation/forking) is archived, the children should end up in a consistent, usable state: either they are archived too and their worktrees cleaned up, or they are left unarchived and fully resumable. Today neither happens — children show as archived (stuck with the archived banner) but are not resumable and their worktrees are never cleaned. Acceptance: archiving a parent with children leaves no orphaned worktrees and no session stuck in a broken archived-but-unresumable state.
- **Trace:**

  > archiveSession in src/vs/sessions/services/sessions/browser/sessionsManagementService.ts (line 1262) only calls the provider's archiveSession for the single session passed in; the agent-host provider implementation (baseAgentHostSessionsProvider.ts line 5629) just toggles an archived flag. Neither has any notion of child/forked sessions — there's no parentSessionId/childSessionIds tracking anywhere in src/vs/sessions. The boundary: do not touch the fork feature's own creation flow (agentHostForkActions.ts) or single-session archive/worktree-diskusage measurement (sessionWorktreeCleanupService.ts), which are unrelated and working as designed.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should archiving a parent session cascade-archive (and clean up worktrees for) its children, or should children be left active/unarchived so they remain independently usable?
  - If cascading, should the parent's archive UI warn about or list the affected children first?

## Evidence

- **no cascade logic:** sessionsManagementService.ts:1262 archiveSession() only calls provider.archiveSession(session.sessionId) and fires onDidArchiveSession — no lookup or handling of child/forked sessions.
- **provider archiveSession is a flag flip:** baseAgentHostSessionsProvider.ts:5629 archiveSession() calls this._setSessionArchived(sessionId, true) with no worktree cleanup and no child-session awareness.
- **no parent/child tracking found:** Searched sessions services and providers for childSessionIds/parentSessionId/spawnedSession concepts — none exist outside the unrelated 'fork' feature (agentHostForkActions.ts), confirming there is no mechanism today to enumerate a session's children.
- **author confirmation:** roblourens (author) confirmed in a follow-up comment that opening B or C shows the archived banner and they can't be resumed, consistent with the traced gap.
