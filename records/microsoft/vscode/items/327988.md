# Issue #327988 — [Feature Request] Work with the AI industry to standardize the `.noai` and `.aiignore` files

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327988](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327988)

## Summary

Keep open for product discussion: existing AI-disable settings and Copilot-specific exclusions do not provide the requested portable .noai/.aiignore contract. The older related issues cover only parts of this request, and low activity alone does not justify closing it.

## Change summary

Support project-wide .noai markers and per-path .aiignore rules, and pursue interoperable semantics across AI assistants.

## Best solution

Decide whether VS Code will recognize these portable files and define their scope across built-in AI features and agent tools before implementation. Coordinate the file-exclusion portion with existing content-exclusion work without treating it as coverage of project-wide opt-out or industry standardization.
