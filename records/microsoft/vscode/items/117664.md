# Issue #117664 — Diff editor scrolls horizontally when dragging selection vertically

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/117664](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/117664)

## Summary

The surprising horizontal scroll remains explainable on current main: dragging upward across a diff view zone places the selection at the preceding line's maximum column. The desired mouse-aligned behavior is clear, but handling invisible lines and bidirectional text needs a small design pass.

## Change summary

Keep the selection at the mouse's horizontal offset when dragging vertically upward across a diff editor view zone instead of moving it to the preceding line's end.

## Best solution

Make view-zone selection dragging preserve the pointer's horizontal intent whenever an adjacent rendered line can map the coordinate, while retaining a defined fallback for offscreen lines and correct RTL/bidirectional behavior.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts`
- **Validation:** In a diff editor containing a tall view zone, drag a selection vertically upward through the zone with the pointer at a mid-line horizontal offset; verify that the selection stays near that offset without horizontal scrolling. Repeat with the adjacent line offscreen and with RTL/bidirectional text to confirm the chosen fallback remains stable.
- **Behavior:**

  > Dragging a selection vertically upward through a diff editor view zone should preserve the pointer's horizontal intent rather than jumping to the end of the preceding line and scrolling horizontally. Downward dragging and ordinary selection outside view zones must remain unchanged. Acceptance should cover a visible adjacent line, a view zone large enough to hide that line, and RTL/bidirectional content.
- **Trace:**

  > HitTestContext.getZoneAtCoord in src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts represents the position before a view zone as the preceding line's maximum column. PointerHandler._findMousePosition and _helpPositionJumpOverViewZone in src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts select that position when a drag started below the zone and crosses upward, which explains the horizontal reveal on current main. The issue discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/117664 confirms both this behavior and the intended mouse-aligned result. The boundary is view-zone hit testing during selection: ordinary line hit testing, downward crossing, scrolling behavior, and bidi/RTL coordinate correctness must not regress. Uncertainty begins with choosing the rendered-line coordinate mapping and fallback when the adjacent line is unavailable.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Which rendered line should provide mouse-coordinate-to-logical-column mapping when crossing a view zone, and what fallback should apply when that line is not visible?
  - How should the fallback preserve predictable selection behavior for RTL and mixed bidirectional lines?

## Evidence

- **maintainer confirmation:** The discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/117664 confirms the behavior and explains that upward dragging over a view zone selects the end of the line above; the maintainer agrees that preserving the mouse's horizontal offset would make more sense.
- **current source:** src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts, HitTestContext.getZoneAtCoord, still defines `positionBefore` using `getLineMaxColumn`, while src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts, PointerHandler._helpPositionJumpOverViewZone, returns that position for an upward selection crossing.
- **implementation constraint:** The current helper surface maps logical positions to visible horizontal ranges, but the requested behavior needs the inverse mouse-coordinate mapping on a rendered adjacent line; the issue discussion specifically identifies offscreen lines and RTL/bidirectional content as fallback constraints.
