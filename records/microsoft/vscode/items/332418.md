[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332418](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332418)

## Summary

Keep open for review of the linked implementation candidate. Current source still performs synchronous whole-message decoding, but transport chunking alone does not establish that oversized payloads will stop blocking the renderer.

## Change summary

Introduce chunking or streaming for oversized IPC payloads to preserve renderer responsiveness.

## Best solution

Review https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/332419 against a measured oversized-payload scenario, including final deserialization and consumer processing rather than transport alone. Establish which channel or extension-host RPC path carries the costly payload, and require responsiveness improvements without changing message ordering or protocol compatibility.

- **Existing capability check:** src/vs/base/parts/ipc/common/ipc.ts still serializes arrays recursively and objects with JSON.stringify, then decodes arrays recursively and objects with JSON.parse. ChannelClient.send constructs the complete buffer; ChannelClient.onBuffer synchronously deserializes the header and body before dispatch. This inspected path does not provide incremental decoding.
- **Candidate does not yet prove responsiveness:** The diff of https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/332419 splits an already serialized buffer in a synchronous send loop. On receipt, ChunkingProtocolAdapter concatenates all chunks and schedules delivery of the complete message with setTimeout; the existing synchronous deserialize remains unchanged. Yielding before whole-message decoding does not bound the duration of that decoding or subsequent processing.
- **Extension-host boundary:** src/vs/workbench/services/extensions/common/rpcProtocol.ts has a separate RPCProtocol receiver attached directly to IMessagePassingProtocol.onMessage; MessageIO.deserializeRequestJSONArgs uses JSON.parse. The linked PR wraps ChannelClient and ChannelServer only, so its claimed coverage of extension-host diagnostics and other RPC traffic needs to be established rather than inferred from the shared protocol interface.
- **Related performance evidence:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340071 reports renderer performance samples in ipc.ts deserialize/onBuffer. Its automated discussion at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340071#issuecomment-6019649256 reports 4,745 affected users and 10,755 hits in 1.140.0. This supports investigating the decoding hot path, but does not independently establish payload size or prove the proposed remedy.
