## Summary

The originally reported problem (diff decorations not working) has been addressed by multiple fixes since June 2023. The symlink-specific case confirmed in the comments is now handled by design — symlinks intentionally skip quick diff decorations as of e8abd52cf7c (shipped in 1.101).
