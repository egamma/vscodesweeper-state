## Evidence

- **Complete reproduction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/333303 specifies Modern UI, Center Panel alignment, a maximized bottom Panel with the editor hidden, and individual sidebar visibility toggles. The reporter also supplied a recording in the first comment.
- **Current source defect:** src/vs/workbench/services/layout/browser/layoutService.ts:getFloatingOuterEdgeOwners includes only vertical Panels in fullOrder. With the editor hidden and only the left Primary Side Bar visible beside the bottom Panel, resolution incorrectly assigns the right outer edge to the Primary Side Bar. getFloatingPaneCompositeHorizontalMargins consequently reserves a 4px outer margin there instead of the 0px trailing inner margin, adding to the Panel's leading gutter.
- **Layout consumer:** src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/paneCompositePart.ts:625-630 applies the resulting floating-part-outer-left/right classes, and getFloatingInset at lines 680-693 subtracts the computed horizontal margins from content dimensions. The incorrect ownership therefore affects actual card geometry, not merely an unused helper.
- **Open implementation candidate:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/333753 is verified OPEN with mergedAt null and explicitly says Fixes #333303. Its diff includes a visible centered horizontal Panel in the middle-section ownership order when the editor is hidden, with regression cases for sidebar positions, visibility and densities. Its author reports manual verification; those checks were not independently executed during this read-only review.
- **Related but not canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/324215 concerns resize targets sitting inside gaps, and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326129 concerns editor/chat alignment, sash overshoot and disappearing grips.
