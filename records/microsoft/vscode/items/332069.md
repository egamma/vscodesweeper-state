## Summary

Valid, easily-verified polish request: the Artifacts pill icon is literally `Codicon.archive` in chatTurnPills.ts, which does read as 'archived/inactive' rather than 'session outputs and references'. Routing to the chat/sessions area for a maintainer/designer to pick a replacement icon (and possibly a tooltip) — not something to close or auto-fix.

## Change summary

Replace the archive-box icon used on the 'Artifacts' pill in the Agents window chat input with an icon that better conveys 'session-level outputs and references', and consider adding a clarifying tooltip.

## Best solution
