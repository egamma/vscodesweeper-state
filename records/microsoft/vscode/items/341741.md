# Issue #341741 — Built-in inconsistencies logged on every start: the bundled copilot-chat wants the 'authIssuers' proposal product.json does not grant, and bundled auth providers are reported as 'not declared'

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341741](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/341741)

## Summary

Thanks for the detailed measurements. The report is complete but I could not run it. Finding 1 has a plausible cause: the copilot extension manifest declares `authIssuers`, but the stable product.json grant lacks it. Finding 2 comes from a warning in `mainThreadAuthentication.ts` that fires before the declared providers are populated. Both are log noise only; no matching fix was found on main.

## Change summary

Stable logs an error that the bundled copilot-chat requests the `authIssuers` proposal product.json doesn't grant, and warnings that bundled github/microsoft auth providers are not declared.

## Best solution

Make the product.json grant (build-time `extensionEnabledApiProposals`) match the bundled copilot-chat manifest. Separately, make the declared-provider check in `$registerAuthenticationProvider` not fire before the manifest-declared providers are populated, or suppress it for built-in extensions.
