[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342668](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342668)

## Summary

Keep open as a harness-parity request: current main explicitly documents chat.hookFilesLocations as Local-only, and the Copilot integration does not supply equivalent configured hook discovery. This team-authored item has neither a verified canonical duplicate nor a citable implementing fix.
