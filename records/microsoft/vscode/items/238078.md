## Evidence

- **Verified older canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/152042 is OPEN and was created on 2022-06-14, before this report. Its body requests the inline revert icon for side-by-side diffs; its discussion also addresses staging affordances and their placement.
- **Same observable symptom:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/152042#issuecomment-1203963254 explicitly describes the right-hand revert arrow as non-intuitive because it looks like it will stage changes, and proposes separating revert and stage placement. This matches the central confusion here and satisfies the stronger re-review duplicate bar.
- **Current source confirms the remaining design:** src/vs/editor/browser/widget/diffEditor/diffEditor.contribution.ts registers Codicon.arrowRight for non-inline mode and Codicon.discard for inline mode, with revert ordered at 5. extensions/git/package.json registers git.diff.stageHunk and git.diff.stageSelection with a plus icon and primary@10 ordering; features/gutterFeature.ts renders their toolbar vertically. The requested icon consistency and stage-first ordering are not already implemented.
- **Existing settings are not coverage:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorConfigurationSchema.ts exposes diffEditor.renderMarginRevertIcon and diffEditor.renderGutterMenu as visibility controls, not icon or action-order customization. Hiding the controls does not deliver this request.
- **Other related reports:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/213469 is open but focuses on button proximity and confirmation rather than the full icon semantics. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/269082 concerns context-sensitive captions and bidirectional copying and is newer. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313536 reports the same split-versus-inline icon inconsistency but is also newer, so neither newer issue is used as the canonical.
- **Design boundary:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/152042#issuecomment-1167370200 explains that a discard icon can misrepresent generic file comparison, where the action copies left-hand content to the right. Consolidation preserves that existing design consideration alongside this report's Git-specific feedback.
