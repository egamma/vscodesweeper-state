## Evidence

- **Current-source diagnosis:** In src/vs/workbench/services/search/common/replace.ts, ReplacePattern.getReplaceString calls replaceWithCaseOperations. That method appends case-converted capture contents directly to newReplaceString, then calls text.replace(regex, newReplaceString). For the reported search ^(foo.*)$ and replacement \U$1, captured foo$1 becomes replacement template FOO$1; String.replace interprets that embedded $1 again, yielding FOOfoo$1. The same path reinterprets $& and collapses $$.
- **Open implementation candidate:** Verified https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/334710 is OPEN with mergedAt null. Its body explicitly says Fixes #334709 and addresses literal dollars in case-converted captures; its changed files are src/vs/workbench/services/search/common/replace.ts and src/vs/workbench/services/search/test/common/replace.test.ts. The issue must remain open pending disposition of that PR.
- **Reported reproduction and distinct scope:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334709 supplies exact file contents, search/replacement expressions, preview and saved-file results, and reproduction with extensions disabled and a fresh profile. It distinguishes captured-content dollars from replacement-field dollars tracked by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/299365 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/326451, and reports that the latter's head does not fix this case.
