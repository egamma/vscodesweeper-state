# Issue #169813 — Compound launch configuration: add abiliity to ignore preLaunchTask of launch configurations.

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169813](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169813)

## Summary

This accepted Backlog request has 22 community upvotes and describes a real gap in compound debugging: a compound-level build cannot currently suppress the child configurations' redundant pre-launch tasks.

## Change summary

Allow a compound launch configuration to skip or coordinate the preLaunchTask values of its child launch configurations.

## Best solution

Extend the compound debug contract with a backward-compatible way to suppress child pre-launch tasks when the compound provides its own task; maintainers should first settle whether suppression is compound-wide or configurable per child.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/common/debug.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/debugService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/common/debugSchemas.ts`
- **Validation:** Add debug service tests covering a compound with its own preLaunchTask and child configurations that also define preLaunchTask: the compound task runs once, opted-out child tasks do not run, ordinary direct launches and existing compounds retain current behavior.
- **Behavior:**

  > A compound launch can perform one shared pre-launch task and opt out of running the preLaunchTask declared by its child configurations. Launching those configurations directly must continue to run their existing preLaunchTask, and compounds that do not opt out must remain backward compatible. Acceptance should cover successful launch ordering and failure/cancellation of the compound-level task without starting child sessions.
- **Trace:**

  > The issue identifies the observable gap in compound orchestration: child launch configurations retain useful direct-launch preLaunchTask values, but a compound that builds the full solution has no way to prevent those child tasks from also running. The likely boundary is the compound schema and debug-service launch orchestration under src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug; task execution outside debug must not change. The uncertainty starts at the public launch.json contract: the report proposes both a compound-wide boolean and richer per-child or sequencing forms, which expose different user-visible flexibility and configuration compatibility.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should suppression apply to every child in the compound, or should each compound entry independently choose whether its configuration's preLaunchTask runs?
  - Is this feature limited to suppressing child pre-launch tasks, or should the public contract also define sequencing and launch timing for those tasks?
