- **Current implementation:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsListNotification.ts implements SessionsListNotification with its own DOM surface, ten-second expiry, paused countdown, Undo button, progress state, and accessibility help. SessionsView creates it directly in sessionsContent in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsView.ts.
- **Behavior that must be preserved:** runSessionsListMove and executeSessionsListMove in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsListChatMove.ts use the local notice for progress, results, refusals, and Undo. Without that notice, failures use INotificationService.warn and successes are only announced to screen readers; Undo is prepared only when the local notice is supplied.
- **Existing capability check:** src/vs/platform/notification/common/notification.ts exposes the shared INotificationService.notify and prompt APIs. Their existence does not resolve the request: the session-move path still explicitly selects a separate surface, and the inspected contribution registrations revealed no sessions-list-notification setting that switches it to the shared mechanism.
