# Issue #186160 — Custom snippets not prioritized when quick suggestions are inline 

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186160](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/186160)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still excludes the supplied snippet from inline suggestions because its expansion does not match the typed prefix. The inline-completion preselector can also override the first suggestion's focus; no verified canonical or fixing commit was found.

## Change summary

Preserve custom snippet priority in inline quick suggestions and when opening the suggestion list with Ctrl+Space.

## Best solution

Make inline quick suggestions honor matching snippet prefixes and configured snippet ordering even when the expansion differs from the prefix, without letting a lower-ranked inline completion unexpectedly take focus in the suggestion list. Diagnose the complete CSS interaction before changing shared visibility and preselection behavior.
