## Summary

Detailed, well-illustrated design critique of the experimental Modern UI (tab active-state contrast, sidebar icon padding, divider weight, status bar height) with real community traction (7 reactions, 12 comments); the issue is already assigned to the modernization team (see the vs-code-engineering backup-assignment comment), so this should stay open as active design feedback rather than be closed or folded elsewhere.

## Change summary

Reports that the experimental Modern UI's tab coloring, sidebar spacing, dividers, and status bar thickness break visual continuity and hurt scanability compared to the legacy workbench styling.

## Best solution

Keep this open alongside the team's other Modern UI feedback threads (e.g. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329024, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/331742) as input for the modernization design pass; the region-by-region breakdown here (tab active-state contrast, sidebar icon density, divider/status-bar weight) is more granular than the shorter general complaints and can inform concrete follow-up polish items rather than being collapsed into a single duplicate.
