## Summary

A real chat-input polish gap remains on current main: Shift+Enter inserts an ordinary newline after a triple-backtick line and no chat-specific behavior creates the closing fence. Route this to the chat area for a product decision on the precise expansion semantics.

## Change summary

## Best solution
