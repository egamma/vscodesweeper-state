# Issue #291375 — Use custom elements to avoid manually having to remount webviews

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/291375](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/291375)

## Summary

The cleanup remains relevant: shared webviews still require explicit reinitialization, while chat tracks disconnections separately. Keep this team-authored debt open; the related blank-webview reports do not own the same lifecycle cleanup.

## Change summary

Move webview disconnection and remount handling into the shared webview implementation so consumers no longer manage reinitialization manually.

## Best solution

Make retained webviews recover from DOM detach/reattach through their shared lifecycle, without consumer-specific remount plumbing. Establish behavior across DOM moves, auxiliary windows, and asynchronous initialization before migrating existing callers.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/base/browser/dom.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webview.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webviewElement.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/test/browser/webviewElement.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/chatOutputItemRenderer.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatListRenderer.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatContentParts/toolInvocationParts/chatMcpAppModel.ts`
- **Validation:** Extend the WebviewElement browser suite to detach and reattach a mounted webview without an explicit reinitializeAfterDismount call, then verify a fresh ready handshake and content delivery. Cover ancestor reparenting, repeated reconnection, disposal while detached, and overlapping asynchronous mounts; manually exercise chat virtualization and auxiliary-window attachment.
- **Behavior:**

  > A retained webview should recover after its containing DOM subtree is detached and reattached without consumers explicitly requesting reinitialization. Initial attachment and subsequent reconnections must deliver content and messages through the correct window. Repeated moves must not leave blank content or duplicate initialization, and disposal must not trigger recovery. Existing overlay behavior and unrelated chat content-part remount responsibilities must remain intact.
- **Trace:**

  > IWebviewElement in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webview.ts retains the manual reinitializeAfterDismount contract. WebviewElement._createElement creates a plain iframe; mountTo initializes its URL asynchronously and registers window-specific messaging, while reinitializeAfterDismount resets that channel and reloads cached content. ConnectionObserverElement already exists in src/vs/base/browser/dom.ts, but chatListRenderer.ts uses it outside the shared webview layer, and chatOutputItemRenderer.ts and ChatMcpAppModel.remount still invoke manual recovery. The existing browser test helper also remounts explicitly, providing a starting harness rather than proof of automatic recovery. The remaining investigation is how connection notifications interact with initial detached construction, ancestor moves, asynchronous mounts, and auxiliary-window ownership across consumers. Related narrower reports are https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/331140 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335908; neither should be assumed resolved by this cleanup.
