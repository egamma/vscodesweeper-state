# Issue #344908 — Agents window: drag and drop onto a workspace revealed by "+N more workspaces" does nothing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344908](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344908)

## Summary

Confirmed in current source: revealed workspace headers accept drop feedback but are absent from the ordering scope, so reordering fails and a nested header can be detached without reaching its destination. Keep open; the full requested behavior needs a decision about ordering and capping semantics.

## Change summary

Make header drops onto workspaces revealed by '+N more workspaces' behave consistently with their advertised destination.

## Best solution

Ensure drop feedback and placement agree, without partially changing group membership on an unsuccessful drop. Define how ordering below the cap interacts with promotion, new activity, and uncapped custom groups before implementing the full requested behavior.
