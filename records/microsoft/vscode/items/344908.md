## Summary

The reported drop failure remains source-reproducible: revealed workspace headers accept drop feedback but are absent from the reorder target list. Keep this open while the two existing fix proposals are reviewed; supporting actual reordering below the cap still needs a product decision.

## Change summary

Dragging a workspace or custom group onto a workspace revealed by '+N more workspaces' shows valid drop feedback without applying the requested order.

## Best solution

Review https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/344972 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/345018 to address misleading drop feedback and unintended ungrouping. Explicitly decide whether revealed workspaces should support persisted reordering and demotion before treating those guards as satisfying the full requested behavior.
