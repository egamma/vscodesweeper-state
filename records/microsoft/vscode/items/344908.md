# Issue #344908 — Agents window: drag and drop onto a workspace revealed by "+N more workspaces" does nothing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344908](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344908)

## Summary

The drop-target/order mismatch remains in current main, matching the maintainer's confirmed reproduction. Keep open: the two linked PRs were closed without merging, and rejecting drops alone would not deliver the requested reordering.

## Change summary

Allow workspace and custom-group headers to be reordered relative to workspaces revealed through '+N more workspaces', without silent failures or unintended regrouping.

## Best solution

Make accepted header drops produce the indicated placement, with persisted ordering for revealed workspaces. Settle how downward moves interact with activity-based capping and uncapped custom groups before implementing the full behavior.
