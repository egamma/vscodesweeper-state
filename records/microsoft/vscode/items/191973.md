Compare frame durations, dropped frames, and sticky-scroll-attributed style/layout work; after identifying a bottleneck, repeat the identical scenario and verify improvement without changing displayed scopes, folding controls, or horizontal scrolling.
- **Behavior:**

  > The report demonstrates additional dropped frames when scrolling with sticky scroll enabled compared with disabled. The goal is to retain sticky scope headers without avoidable rendering overhead. Establish a repeatable current-main comparison before choosing a change, then demonstrate reduced attributable work while preserving sticky-line content, positioning, folding, and navigation.
- **Trace:**

  > StickyScrollController._readConfiguration registers a scroll-top listener that invokes _renderStickyScroll(); _updateState computes widget state and calls StickyScrollWidget.setState(). The widget skips equal states, otherwise _renderRootNode updates or rebuilds line DOM. RenderedStickyLine now captures scrollWidth during construction before DOM attachment, and hover listeners are cleared before replacement, so the original layout hypothesis and the later accumulation report need remeasurement. The closed, unmerged https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/252342 is not a proven fix, and its array substitution must not be assumed to eliminate live layout reads. Related performance reports were consolidated here through https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/192523 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230314. Preserve existing product behavior; uncertainty starts at which work still dominates current renderer traces, not at the desired outcome.
