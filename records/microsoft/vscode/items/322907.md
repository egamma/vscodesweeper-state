[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/322907](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/322907)

## Summary

Keep open: existing Outline filters do not distinguish exported from local symbols, and an open proof-of-concept PR already addresses this request. Review that contribution against the reporter's broader export cases rather than closing the issue or starting a competing implementation.

## Change summary

Add an Outline-view filter for exported or publicly exposed symbols, preferably accessible from the view's header menu.
