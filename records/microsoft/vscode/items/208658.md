# Issue #208658 — Responsive layout panels or access to layout service

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/208658](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/208658)

## Summary

Keep open under discussion: existing resize commands and centered-layout settings do not provide resolution-aware sizing or restorable widths for the full workbench. The related issues inspected do not cover the same monitor-switching problem.

## Change summary

Provide a way to maintain or restore Explorer, editor, and terminal widths when switching between laptop and ultrawide displays.

## Best solution

Agree on a supported way to recover the user's preferred three-column sizing across window-size changes, in the workbench layout area. Choose the user-facing contract first: responsive sizing, explicit layout restoration, or extension-accessible sizing.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/actions/layoutActions.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.contribution.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/services/layout/browser/layoutService.ts`
- **Validation:** After selecting the contract, exercise an Explorer/editor/right-hand-terminal layout through wide → laptop-sized → wide window transitions and confirm preferred sizing is recovered through the chosen automatic or explicit mechanism. Repeat with manual sash adjustments and minimum-size constraints; confirm existing default resizing and centered-editor behavior remain unchanged when the new mechanism is not enabled.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users switching between laptop and ultrawide displays repeatedly lose their preferred Explorer/editor/terminal widths and must resize the columns manually. The desired outcome is a supported way to recover useful preferred sizing across those transitions. Responsive sizing, saved layouts, and extension-controlled sizing are alternative proposals, not three independently required features. Acceptance must cover shrinking and expanding the window, respect minimum dimensions, and define how manual sash adjustments interact with the selected mechanism.
- **Trace:**

  > src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts creates the workbench grid with proportionalLayout: false and implements internal getSize, setSize, and resizePart operations. IWorkbenchLayoutService declares those methods in src/vs/workbench/services/layout/browser/layoutService.ts; src/vscode-dts/vscode.d.ts does not expose an equivalent public sizing contract. BaseResizeViewAction in src/vs/workbench/browser/actions/layoutActions.ts offers focused-part increments rather than absolute or proportional layout restoration. The centered-layout settings in src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.contribution.ts concern the editor, not the full three-column arrangement. The source establishes the capability gap, but the reported macOS shrink/expand sequence was not executed. Preserve existing default resizing, centered-editor semantics, and unrelated layout consumers; changing a shared grid option is not by itself evidence that the requested outcome is satisfied. Related requests at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/75017 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/158603 cover narrower sidebar behaviors, not this full monitor-switching workflow.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should preferred sizing be recovered automatically on window-size changes, explicitly through saved-layout restoration, or through an extension-facing sizing contract?
  - Should preferred widths be absolute, proportional to available space, or breakpoint-specific, and should manual sash adjustments redefine the saved preference?
