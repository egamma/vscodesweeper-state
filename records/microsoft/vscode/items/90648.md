Keep open: 52 community upvotes support retaining this request, and external licensing workarounds do not deliver the integrated premium-extension experience discussed here. No verified canonical issue or landed implementation was found.

## Change summary

Provide first-class support for publishing and distributing premium extensions, rather than requiring publishers to assemble external payment and licensing infrastructure.

## Best solution

Establish the supported premium-extension model with the Marketplace owners, distinguishing purchase and trial support from publisher-managed licensing and copy protection. Preserve this issue as the demand record; sponsorship links and externally licensed extensions are partial alternatives, not completion of the request.
