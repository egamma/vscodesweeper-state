## Summary

The requested improvement remains relevant: manually requesting another batch still inserts suggestions ahead of the existing list rather than replacing it. Keep this team-authored polish issue open; no verified canonical or fixing commit was found.

## Change summary

Make manually regenerated rename suggestions replace the previous suggestions instead of accumulating them.
