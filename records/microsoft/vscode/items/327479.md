- **Registry warning is not a root-cause diagnosis:** src/vs/workbench/services/extensions/common/extensionDescriptionRegistry.ts:345–389 implements the lock through promises and emits the warning from a 30-second timer. src/vs/workbench/services/extensions/common/abstractExtensionService.ts:466–481 holds that lock while awaiting _resolveAndProcessExtensions and releases it in finally. These paths explain delayed extension initialization, but do not establish that the lock synchronously freezes the whole application or implicate Copilot policy fetching.
- **CLI diagnostic dependency:** src/vs/code/electron-main/main.ts:447–455 handles --status by awaiting getMainDiagnostics and getRemoteDiagnostics over the existing instance's diagnostics IPC channel before printing. src/vs/platform/diagnostics/electron-main/diagnosticsMainService.ts:79–115 gathers window and process information. A stalled --status is therefore useful evidence for investigating shared-process or IPC delays, not proof of a particular network call.

That narrower fix does not establish resolution of this report's whole-application freeze, and the closed issue cannot be a duplicate canonical.
