## Change summary

A terminal warning hover locked with Alt/Option is incorrectly dismissed when the pointer crosses another terminal action.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/310899 so attempts to show sibling hovers cannot dispose the currently locked hover.
