Retain normal complete-backup restoration and distinguish legitimate document shortening from corruption.
- **Behavior:**

  > Following an OOM crash while editing a large text document, recovery must not silently present an incomplete backup as the recovered document. Preserve the last complete recoverable content when an update is interrupted, and do not overwrite the saved original during recovery. Acceptance must include interruption during a large backup update, normal recovery, and intentional document shortening. Diagnose the recurring renderer OOM separately rather than assuming backup protection eliminates it.
- **Trace:**

  > WorkingCopyBackupServiceImpl.backup writes a preamble and document stream through IFileService.writeFile without atomic options; FileUserDataProvider does not enforce atomic writes for backup resources. WorkingCopyBackupServiceImpl.resolve checks for the preamble terminator but has no complete-content integrity check, and TextFileEditorModel.doResolveFromBackup loads its returned stream as a dirty model. NativeWorkingCopyBackupService joins writes on orderly shutdown, which does not demonstrate protection against abrupt renderer termination. These paths make interrupted writes a credible hypothesis, but neither the OOM allocation source nor the exact interrupted operation has been established. Preserve legitimate document shortening, existing backup compatibility, and ordinary file-save semantics. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/256665 records the earlier backup concern; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/98063 discusses broader atomic-save work, not a verified duplicate of this re-reviewed symptom.

## Evidence

- **Repeated and independently confirmed data loss:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345244 documents a 28 MB document recovering with 414,103 rather than approximately 427,300 lines, with the backup itself similarly truncated. Follow-up comments report recurring crashes on Insiders and stable 1.144; another commenter independently reports OOM-associated loss of most of a document and shutdown waiting on 'Backup working copies'.
- **Current backup write path:** src/vs/workbench/services/workingCopy/common/workingCopyBackupService.ts writes prefixed document content directly through fileService.writeFile(backupResource, backupBuffer), without requesting atomic replacement. src/vs/platform/userData/common/fileUserDataProvider.ts enforces atomic writes only for profile settings, keybindings, tasks, and extensions resources, not backup files.
- **Current recovery integrity boundary:** WorkingCopyBackupServiceImpl.resolve in src/vs/workbench/services/workingCopy/common/workingCopyBackupService.ts rejects a missing preamble terminator but does not verify complete document content. TextFileEditorModel.doResolveFromBackup in src/vs/workbench/services/textfile/common/textFileEditorModel.ts:503-519 turns the returned stream into a dirty editor model. This supports an interrupted-write hypothesis, not a conclusive diagnosis of these crashes.
- **Shutdown protection is not crash protection:** src/vs/workbench/services/workingCopy/electron-browser/workingCopyBackupService.ts joins pending backup operations during orderly shutdown specifically to avoid partial writes. That orderly-shutdown safeguard does not establish safety when a renderer terminates with OOM.
