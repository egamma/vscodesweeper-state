## Summary

Two independent reporters (with a screenshot and cross-platform confirmation) see empty subagent title boxes during multi-agent orchestration in Chat; a commenter traces this to the tool-presentation batching added for subagent reconstruction, and the current source does have the exact `_toolPresentationDirty`/`beginToolPresentationBatch`/`endToolPresentationBatch` machinery described, though I could not confirm the precise early-return mechanism verbatim on current main.

## Change summary

## Best solution

A Copilot Chat maintainer familiar with `ChatSubagentContentPart`/`chatListRenderer.ts` should trace why a subagent row's title is not updated for reconstructed (already-completed) tool invocations under the new batching path — the code in `chatSubagentContentPart.ts` (trackToolState/updateActiveToolPresentation/_updateToolPresentation, and the begin/endToolPresentationBatch pairing in chatListRenderer.ts) is the right area to instrument, since it deletes completed invocations from `activeToolPresentations` without guaranteeing `updateTitle()` runs before the batch flushes in every code path.
