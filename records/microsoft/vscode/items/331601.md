Keep open: independent reporters confirmed empty blocks during subagent activity, including outside the Sessions view. Current rendering differs from the proposed historical diagnosis, and neither a verified fixing commit nor an eligible older canonical establishes that this report is resolved.

## Change summary

- **Behavior:**

  > Invoking subagents must not accumulate contentless boxes in the parent transcript. Meaningful subagent labels and results must remain available during execution, after completion, after list recycling, and after reopening the session. The report is independently confirmed across WSL and macOS; the same checks should cover ordinary Chat and Sessions.
- **Trace:**

  > The commenter attributes the regression to https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/328926, but current ChatSubagentContentPart initializes its presentation in the constructor and updates presentation before trackToolState returns for completed tools. ChatListRenderer still batches subagent reconstruction through beginSubagentToolPresentationBatch, while appendToolInvocation defers collapsed child content. These are investigation entry points, not a conclusively failing current path. Compare https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339302 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339784 before deciding whether the boxes are orphaned markdown fences or subagent presentation failures. Preserve meaningful tool output, lazy rendering, and confirmation behavior; first reproduce and identify which rendered part owns each empty box.
