## Summary

Fresh, team-authored bug report on the in-progress conditional-auth (sign-in gate) work for the Agents window; the reporter gives a concrete two-step repro (modal on first open, and reappearing on second open despite an already-used signed-out session) with no fix yet on main.

## Change summary

With `chat.agentHost.allowSignedOutWhenUsable` enabled, the Agents window still shows a non-obviously-dismissible sign-in modal on first open, and shows it again on a later open even after a signed-out session was already used.

## Best solution

Likely belongs in the window-gate logic in src/vs/sessions/browser/sessionsAuthGate.ts and its consumers (contextkey `SessionsWelcomeVisibleContext`, e.g. src/vs/sessions/contrib/accountMenu/browser/account.contribution.ts) — the gate should not re-trigger once a signed-out session has been used, and the modal needs a discoverable dismiss affordance beyond an undocumented Escape.
