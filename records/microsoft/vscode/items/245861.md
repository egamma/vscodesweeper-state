## Change summary

Opening the Windows home folder through WSL produces a continuous stream of npm multiple-lockfile notifications.

## Best solution

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `extensions/npm/src/tasks.ts`, `extensions/npm/src/npmMain.ts`, `extensions/npm/src/preferred-pm.ts`
- **Validation:** In a disposable WSL workspace, use a folder with multiple package-manager lockfiles and several package.json scripts, leave package-manager and script-runner selection automatic, and exercise startup discovery and task refresh. Record notification counts and verify that discovering multiple scripts does not continually re-present the same warning, task commands remain correct, and Do not show again remains effective.
- **Behavior:**

  > Opening a home-folder workspace through WSL must not produce a continuous stream of identical npm multiple-lockfile notifications. Package-manager detection and script discovery must continue working, and Do not show again must retain its existing effect. Acceptance should cover startup discovery with several scripts, repeated discovery, and dismissal or suppression of the warning.
- **Trace:**

  > The recording in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/245861 identifies the multiple-lockfile information message emitted by detectPackageManager in extensions/npm/src/tasks.ts. NpmTaskProvider.provideTasks enables warnings, and provideNpmScriptsForFolder forwards that flag separately for every script through createScriptRunnerTask, getRunScriptCommand, and getScriptRunner; installation-task construction can also invoke detection. The warning checks persistent suppression but has no local per-folder deduplication. npmMain.ts invalidates discovery caches through package.json watchers and configuration changes; whether those events drive the reported sustained stream remains unconfirmed. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/253142 independently describes per-script warning amplification but was closed for missing information, not fixed. Preserve package-manager selection, task construction, and existing suppression semantics; do not conflate this with the false-positive detection reported in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/171943.
