## Evidence

- **Measured native-heap fragmentation:** The full report at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337444 documents 595.21 MiB committed with 38.51 MiB live after selected timing-mark cleanup and deterministic GC, leaving 556.70 MiB unused. Its independent free-list accounting reports 547.08 MiB, and critical memory pressure produced no observed reclamation during approximately 35 seconds.
- **Verified upstream reclamation constraint:** Direct inspection of https://github.com/v8/v8/blob/15.0.245.31/src/heap/cppgc/sweeper.h confirms Sweeper::CanDiscardMemory() returns false under V8_OS_WIN. The accompanying comment says discarding does not decommit memory on Windows and can become expensive enough to cause hangs. This corroborates an engine limitation, not the complete allocation root cause.
- **Application-side lead is not a complete fix:** Current src/vs/base/common/performance.ts forwards browser mark() calls to native performance.mark(); the six reported editor/model mark names remain in src/vs/workbench/services/textfile/common/textFileEditorModel.ts and src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/editors/textFileEditor.ts. The report's controlled removal of 3,708 marks recovered only 16.25 MiB, approximately 3% of the unused capacity, so mark cleanup cannot substantiate closing this report.
