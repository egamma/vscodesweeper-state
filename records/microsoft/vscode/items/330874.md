## Best solution

Keep tracking as a known shared-widget race in CompressedObjectTreeModel.setChildren (src/vs/base/browser/ui/tree/compressedObjectTreeModel.ts:163-166): an async refresh calls setChildren for an element whose compressed-node entry was already evicted by an interleaving refresh/collapse.
