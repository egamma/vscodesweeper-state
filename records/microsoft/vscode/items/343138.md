[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343138](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343138)

## Summary

The recovery gap remains in current source: host-token lookup failures are logged and retried without reaching the editor's token-failure handling. Keep this issue open while its explicitly linked fixing PR remains unmerged.

## Change summary

Restore UI-started Remote Tunnel Access automatically after an expired-token 401 following a relay disconnect.

## Best solution

Review and land the recovery work in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/343199 after confirming it handles the reported expired-token reconnect path. Treat recovery with no editor running as a separate acceptance case, since editor-based renewal cannot cover it.
