# Issue #265482 — Copilot Suggestion Struggles With non-ASCII Characters

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/265482](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/265482)

## Summary

Keep open for diagnosis in inline completions: the reporter confirmed repeatable Unicode partial-accept failures and supplied a pinned Lean file. No concrete fixing commit or verified older canonical was found; current-main reproduction still needs confirmation.

## Change summary

Word-by-word acceptance of Copilot suggestions briefly displays broken Unicode glyphs and discards the remaining suggestion, while whole-suggestion acceptance works.

## Best solution

Make partial acceptance preserve valid Unicode text and the unaccepted remainder in the editor's inline-completion pipeline. First isolate the reported Lean case with a deterministic completion provider, distinguishing word-boundary handling from ghost-text diffing and provider continuation.
