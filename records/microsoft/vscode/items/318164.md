## Summary

The malformed tip prefix remains present in the current Traditional Chinese translation, and the rendering path explains why its Markdown markers appear literally. Keep this open for a localization correction; no verified fix or matching canonical was found.

## Change summary

## Best solution

- **Current rendering path:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/chatTipService.ts:870-886, ChatTipService._createTip, substitutes the raw tip message into localize('tipPrefix', "**Tip:** {0}") and constructs a MarkdownString. src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatContentParts/chatTipContentPart.ts, _renderTip, passes that content directly to the Markdown renderer.
- **Source-confirmed delimiter behavior:** src/vs/base/common/marked/marked.js:1110-1128 recognizes Unicode punctuation and classifies punctuation followed by ** followed by a letter as a left delimiter. Its emStrong implementation at lines 782-815 skips such a run as a closing delimiter, explaining the literal markers in **提示：**使用. Moving the colon outside the emphasis gives a valid closing delimiter.
