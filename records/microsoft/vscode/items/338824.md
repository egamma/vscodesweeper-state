## Summary

Credible, specific repro (duplicate 'Haiku 4.5' entries within the same Copilot group in Manage Models) with a plausible root cause in the view model's cross-vendor merge logic; needs a maintainer to confirm which vendors are producing the duplicate and decide the right dedup/display policy before implementation.

## Change summary

The 'Manage Models...' dialog shows duplicate entries for the same model (e.g. 'Haiku 4.5') within the Copilot group, not just a separate 'two Copilot entries' issue.

## Best solution

Confirm in chatModelsViewModel.ts/addVendorModels whether multiple vendors (e.g. 'copilot' and 'copilotcli', or multiple agent-host session vendors) are each registering the same underlying model, which then get merged into one visual 'Copilot' group via COPILOT_PROVIDER_GROUPS without any identity-based dedup, and decide whether to dedupe by model identifier/name within a merged group or to stop merging those vendors.
