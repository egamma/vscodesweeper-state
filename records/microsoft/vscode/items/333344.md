- **Proposed comment:**

  > Thanks for the report. What you're seeing — unrelated words like `caches` showing up while typing `ca` — is VS Code's built-in word-based suggestion feature, which suggests words found in the open document(s) independent of language semantics. This is intentional, not a bug, and it's configurable: try setting `editor.wordBasedSuggestions` to `off` or `currentDocument` (Settings > search 'word based suggestions'). Language-aware completions (e.g. for TypeScript/JS) come from the language server and are ranked separately from these word matches. Closing as a question, but feel free to reopen if the setting doesn't address what you're seeing.
