# Issue #350700 — Make repeating progress cues opt-in and sound controls easier to discover

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350700](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350700)

## Summary

Existing controls can silence progress independently, but do not fulfill the requested opt-in default and contextual discoverability. Keep this open for an accessibility-owner decision; the verified related issues cover different requirements.

## Change summary

Make repeating progress sounds opt-in and provide discoverable, progress-specific sound controls without disrupting existing accessibility preferences.

## Best solution

Make progress-only muting easy to discover through the existing accessibility controls. Have the accessibility owner decide whether changing screen-reader defaults and adding a one-time hint preserves essential feedback without introducing unwanted prompts.

  > Thanks for the request. Progress sounds can be disabled independently by setting `accessibility.signals.progress` to `{ "sound": "off", "announcement": "off" }`, without reducing the volume of other signals.
