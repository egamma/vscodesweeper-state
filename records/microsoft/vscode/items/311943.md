## Evidence

- **Verified open canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229970 is OPEN and reports the same Source Control symptom: pressing Up past the beginning replaces the current message through history navigation, sometimes making recovery difficult. Its scope covers in-progress messages regardless of how they were populated.
- **Current source explains the replacement:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scm.contribution.ts:517–521 binds UpArrow to scm.viewPreviousCommit when scmInputIsInFirstPosition is true. src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/browser/scmInput.ts:645–655 sets that context at the first visual line and column. SCMInput.showPreviousHistoryValue in src/vs/workbench/contrib/scm/common/scmService.ts:166–175 selects a history value, and scmInput.ts:438–451 replaces the editor contents. This path does not distinguish generated messages from manually entered messages.
- **Recovery and classification:** The replacement is implemented as history navigation, not proven irreversible deletion: scmInput.ts:444–445 records it through undoable edit operations. A maintainer discussed Undo recovery at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229970#issuecomment-2388796088, and another reporter confirmed Undo restored their message at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229970#issuecomment-2451335227. The established navigation behavior needs UX polish; permanent data loss is not established here.
- **Prior consolidation precedent:** A closely matching report, https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/222005, was explicitly closed by a maintainer as a duplicate of the same canonical at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/222005#issuecomment-2557233644.
- **Workaround already documented:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229970#issuecomment-2635325604 documents removing the Up-arrow binding for scm.viewPreviousCommit in keybindings.json. This mitigates accidental navigation but does not resolve the default interaction.
- **Other lead is unrelated:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/273271 is open but requests generation-model selection and chat context, not preservation of input during arrow-key navigation.
