# Issue #314132 — Panel closes immediately

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314132](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314132)

## Summary

The exact error — `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'dimensions')` during terminal resize/dispose race — was fixed on main two days after this report by guarding all resize callbacks against disposed state.

## Change summary

Toggling the panel causes it to open and immediately close due to an unhandled xterm.js TypeError during a terminal resize/dispose race.

## Best solution

Keep the shipped fix (commit 644c7175527); no further change needed. The reporter was on 1.119.0-insider (April 30 build), which predates the fix. The fix shipped in 1.120.0.

The fix landed in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/644c7175527 (with follow-up hardening in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/e62fe6028de) and shipped in VS Code 1.120.0.
