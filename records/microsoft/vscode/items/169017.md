# Issue #169017 — `search.maxResults` does not affect how many results are found/highlighted

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169017](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169017)

## Summary

The reported mismatch remains in current source: editor Find caps its count and highlights at 19,999 independently of search.maxResults. Keep this confirmed, team-authored issue open; neither the closed predecessor nor the newer regex report owns the remaining work.

## Change summary

Changing search.maxResults does not change the result-count or highlighting limit in the current-file Find widget.

## Best solution

Make the supported relationship between search.maxResults and editor Find explicit and consistent, addressing the reported configurable-limit expectation without sacrificing large-file responsiveness or operations beyond the highlighted matches.
