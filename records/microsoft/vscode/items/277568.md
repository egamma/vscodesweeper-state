# Issue #277568 — Inline view of delegated sessions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/277568](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/277568)

## Summary

Keep open: the inspected cloud and background delegation paths still direct users to the sessions list rather than showing the delegated session's live state inline. Related improvements do not establish completion of this team-authored request; the remaining presentation and notification scope needs a maintainer design decision.

## Change summary

Make a delegated cloud or background session discoverable, navigable, and visibly distinct from the originating local conversation without leaving that conversation.

## Best solution

Provide an identifiable, accessible inline representation of the delegated session in the originating chat, with navigation and meaningful session state. Decide whether session-list badges and audio cues belong to this change or separate follow-ups, without duplicating the delegated transcript.
