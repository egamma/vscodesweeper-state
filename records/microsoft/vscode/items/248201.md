# Issue #248201 — Show squiggles and hovers at the location of related information for diagnostics

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201)

## Summary

A real editor gap remains: related diagnostic locations are navigable but receive no decoration or hover on current main. The accepted Backlog request is already routed to the correct diagnostics area.

## Change summary

Decorate diagnostic related-information ranges and expose their relationship through editor hovers.

## Best solution

Extend the marker-decoration and hover pipeline to create subtle decorations for related-information ranges in loaded models, associate them with the originating diagnostic, and update them correctly across resources without adding duplicate Problems entries.
