# Issue #248201 — Show squiggles and hovers at the location of related information for diagnostics

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201)

## Summary

Keep open: current marker decorations and hovers do not automatically identify diagnostic-related locations, so the requested capability remains missing. The issue is accepted into Backlog, and the verified related issue concerns a different observable experience rather than duplicating this request.

## Change summary

Automatically indicate diagnostic-related source ranges and provide contextual hovers without requiring additional diagnostics from language servers.

## Best solution

Extend the editor diagnostics experience so related locations are discoverable directly in source, including across files, without adding Problems entries. Agree on the secondary indicator and hover content before implementation, while preserving primary diagnostic presentation.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/common/services/markerDecorationsService.ts`, `src/vs/editor/common/services/markerDecorations.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/hover/browser/markerHoverParticipant.ts`
- **Validation:** Publish one diagnostic with related locations both elsewhere in the same document and in a second document, without publishing additional diagnostics. Verify that each related location has the agreed indicator and contextual hover, that removing or replacing the parent diagnostic removes or updates those presentations, and that Problems retains only the original diagnostic entry. Include shared related locations referenced by multiple parent diagnostics and retain existing primary marker behavior.
- **Behavior:**

  > A source range referenced by diagnostic related information should be discoverable when the primary diagnostic is outside the visible editor or in another file. Hovering the related range should explain its relationship to the diagnostic. This must not require language servers to publish duplicate diagnostics or add separate Problems entries. Acceptance covers same-file and cross-file relationships, parent diagnostic updates and removal, and unchanged primary diagnostic presentation.
- **Trace:**

  > MarkerDecorationsService._updateDecorations in src/vs/editor/common/services/markerDecorationsService.ts reads markers by the model's own URI; MarkerDecorations.update maps their primary ranges to decorations. MarkerHoverParticipant.computeSync in src/vs/editor/contrib/hover/browser/markerHoverParticipant.ts depends on those decorations, while _renderMarkerHover only lists related targets inside the primary hover. Consequently, relatedInformation alone does not create discoverability at its target. The Hint workaround in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201 requires independently published markers and does not meet the automatic-association requirement. Cross-file target lookup and invalidation need further tracing during planning; preserve marker ownership, primary severity presentation, Problems counts, and existing navigation. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/64566 concerns interactive diagnostic messages rather than this target-location experience.
- **Open decisions:**
  - What secondary indicator should related ranges display, and what should their hover show to distinguish the related message from the originating diagnostic and provide navigation back to it?

## Evidence

- **Missing editor capability:** In src/vs/editor/common/services/markerDecorationsService.ts, MarkerDecorationsService._updateDecorations reads markers belonging to the current model resource, and MarkerDecorations.update creates one decoration from each marker's primary range. Neither projects relatedInformation ranges into decorations, including related locations in other files.
- **Existing hover coverage is directional:** In src/vs/editor/contrib/hover/browser/markerHoverParticipant.ts, computeSync obtains markers from existing marker decorations. _renderMarkerHover displays relatedInformation as navigation links within the primary diagnostic's hover; it does not independently provide a diagnostic hover at the related target.
- **Existing controls do not satisfy the request:** editor.renderValidationDecorations in src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts controls whether validation decorations render, not whether related ranges receive decorations. The Problems tree and gotoErrorWidget expose related-location navigation, but neither supplies the requested persistent source indicator.
- **Workaround acknowledged, not equivalent:** The discussion on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/248201 proposes additional Hint diagnostics. The author explicitly distinguishes that workaround from automatic handling of existing relatedInformation, particularly cross-file invalidation. MarkerDecorationsService already renders ordinary Hint markers as subtle dots, but only when those separate markers exist.
- **Canonical search:** Verified https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/64566 is open and older, but its requested experience is expanding diagnostic text, syntax coloring, and navigation within messages—not indicating related source ranges. Searches for related diagnostics and related information found no verified canonical owning this request; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/55253 concerns producing related information from problem matchers, a different capability.
- **Independent motivating scenario:** The open discussion at https://github.com/dart-lang/sdk/issues/60668 describes name collisions whose diagnostic can be outside the visible screen or in another library part. This reinforces the discoverability problem, but does not replace this repository's request for generic rendering of diagnostic-related locations.
