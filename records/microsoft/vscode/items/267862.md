# Issue #267862 — All small buttons should have a height of 24 (minimum click-target for a11y)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/267862](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/267862)

## Summary

The shared small-button style still permits a 22px-high text button, so the requested accessibility improvement remains outstanding. Keep this team-authored request open; the related UI-refresh discussions do not establish a qualifying duplicate or an issue-referencing fix.

## Change summary

Increase small-button click targets to at least 24px high while preserving their compact text and icon sizing.

## Best solution

Give the shared small-button variant a minimum 24px click-target height, with consistent results across text-only, icon-bearing, and dropdown buttons. Preserve compact typography and verify that surrounding layouts do not clip the larger targets.
