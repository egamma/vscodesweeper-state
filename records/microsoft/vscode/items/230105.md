# Issue #230105 — Conflict resolver worked not expected when I click accpect incoming or current

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230105](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230105)

## Summary

Thanks for the report. The merge-conflict CodeLens passes a cached conflict object into the accept command, and that object's ranges can be stale when a prior accept is still being applied. The report stays open as a real bug.

## Change summary

Accepting the second of two merge conflicts right after the first can do nothing or apply a stale edit.

## Best solution

Have the accept command re-resolve the conflict against the current document, or wait for the pending edit to settle, instead of trusting the cached 'known-conflict' ranges. The change belongs in extensions/merge-conflict/src/commandHandler.ts and codelensProvider.ts.
