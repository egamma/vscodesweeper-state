# Issue #238210 — Inconsistent line copy-paste on Chromebook

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238210](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238210)

## Summary

Still open on main: the empty-selection copy-paste metadata (isFromEmptySelection) is lost on Linux/ChromeOS, causing line paste to insert inline instead of on a new line. No fix has been committed. The issue is correctly labeled and should stay open for the editor-clipboard area owner.

## Change summary

On Linux/ChromeOS, copying a line with no selection (Ctrl+C) and pasting (Ctrl+V) inserts inline instead of pasting on a new line above the cursor.

## Best solution

Investigate why the `vscode-editor-data` custom MIME type (carrying `isFromEmptySelection`) is stripped by the Linux/ChromeOS clipboard pipeline, and why the `InMemoryClipboardMetadataManager` fallback also fails. The fix likely lives in `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/clipboardUtils.ts` or the platform clipboard service. A platform-specific workaround (similar to the existing Firefox line-ending normalization) may be needed.
