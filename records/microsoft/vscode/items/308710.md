[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308710](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308710)

## Summary

Keep open: exhausting Copilot completions should not disable local language suggestions. Suggestion defaults and fallback handling have changed since the reported 1.115.0 build; the supplied quota trigger, disabling comparison, and recording suffice to start retesting, but current source does not conclusively establish the defect or its resolution.

## Change summary

Local variable suggestions reportedly stop appearing when Copilot's inline-completion allowance is exhausted, returning when Copilot or its language-specific suggestions are disabled.

## Best solution

Verify that local language completions remain available when Copilot returns no suggestions because its quota is exhausted, and resolve any confirmed suppression in the editor/Copilot completion integration. Preserve intentional suppression while a usable inline completion is actually showing.
