## Summary

A maintainer symbolicated the reporter's crash dump and identified a renderer V8 out-of-memory termination caused by unbounded external-ingest workspace discovery over a broad remote SSH workspace; an open PR already implements the fix, so this should stay open until that PR lands.

## Change summary

VS Code's renderer crashes (code 5) mid-response over Remote SSH, and Copilot chat usage/credits are still charged for the incomplete response.

## Best solution

Land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/331053, which caps external-ingest workspace discovery at 100,000 raw search results and skips ingestion on truncated/failed discovery, directly addressing the V8 heap exhaustion traced in the crash dump (`MainThreadSearch.$handleFileMatch` -> `SearchOperation.addMatch` -> `URI.toString`).
