# Issue #281611 — Cell keeps getting cursor stuck at a line and will not let me move it

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/281611](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/281611)

## Summary

The cursor-stuck-in-cell bug was partially addressed by PR #284253 (shipped in 1.108.0), but community reports through April 2026 (v1.115.0) confirm it still reproduces. The issue is already assigned and labeled — it should stay open for further investigation.

## Change summary

Cursor gets stuck at a line in a notebook cell after click-drag scrolling, preventing normal cursor movement and causing unintended selection behavior.

## Best solution

The existing fix in `src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/cellParts/codeCell.ts` (pointer state management and self-healing) is not fully covering all edge cases. The assignee (Yoyokrazy) should investigate remaining scenarios where `_pointerDownInEditor` / `_pointerDraggingInEditor` flags fail to reset — possibly when the scroll is triggered by the editor's own auto-scroll-on-drag rather than a wheel event.
