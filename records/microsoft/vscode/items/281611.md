## Evidence

- **Confirmed reproduction:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/281611, Numendacil describes opening a long notebook cell and dragging a selection until the window scrolls, with a recording at https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6027661e-5d28-443b-98e0-6ef0db5df4a4. Another commenter independently identifies holding the left mouse button while wheel-scrolling beyond visible cell text as the trigger.
- **Persistence and recovery:** The supplied discussion confirms the problem on versions 1.115.0 and 1.136.0. The September 2026 confirmation reports that switching cells does not recover normal interaction, while reloading the notebook does.
- **Current source boundary:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/cellParts/codeCell.ts gates scroll and cursor-selection reactions on _pointerDownInEditor and _pointerDraggingInEditor. CodeCell.registerMouseListener resets these flags on mouseup, pointerup, pointercancel, window blur, Escape, and mouse movement without the left button pressed; CodeCellLayout also suppresses programmatic editor scrolling during dragging. These safeguards identify the relevant path but do not conclusively explain or rule out the reported lock.
- **Related fix is insufficient closure evidence:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/e3523eda7dbe2ca725a5ea1ea6ab96e0858e053a introduced separate drag tracking and additional pointer-state cancellation paths in December 2025. Later confirmations in this issue report persistence, so that historical change does not justify treating the issue as completed.
