## Change summary

Make agent sessions, including archived sessions, discoverable and understandable when no folder or workspace is open.

## Best solution

Define a consistent empty-window session-discovery contract across providers, preserve access to archived sessions, and make each session's workspace or repository association understandable. Resolve the empty-list presentation separately from discovery, since the thread does not agree on adding a welcome state.
