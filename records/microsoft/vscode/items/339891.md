# Issue #339891 — Measuring more worktree than asked to clean up?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339891](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339891)

## Summary

Current source explains the extra measurement: completing selected cleanup reloads the editor and can rescan all session worktrees, with a separate notification. Keep this team-authored report open to resolve the confusing distinction between selected cleanup and whole-list refresh; the source does not indicate that unselected sessions are cleaned up.

## Change summary

Make worktree measurement after selected cleanup clearly distinguishable from the cleanup operation and its selected count.

## Best solution

Preserve selection-scoped cleanup while making the subsequent storage refresh understandable. Decide whether a full remeasurement should remain automatic after cleanup or be deferred, then ensure its progress cannot be mistaken for additional cleanup.
