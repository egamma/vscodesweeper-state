- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatListRenderer.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatContentParts/chatElicitationContentPart.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/accessibility/chatAccessibilityService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/test/browser/widget/chatListWidget.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/test/browser/accessibility/chatAccessibilityService.test.ts`
- **Validation:** With chat accessibility sounds enabled, create a history long enough to recycle list templates and repeatedly scroll old responses out of and back into view, both after completion and with an already-announced pending interaction. Verify scrolling alone produces no additional signals while a genuinely new request, response, or required interaction still produces its configured signal; capture the identified case in a targeted browser unit test.
- **Behavior:**

  > Scrolling existing chat history should not replay event-based accessibility sounds solely because a row becomes visible or is reconstructed. New requests, received responses, and newly required interactions must retain their configured audible feedback. Reproduce the supplied scenario first, identify the sound, and verify repeated viewport traversal does not increase its playback count without a new event.
- **Trace:**

  > ChatListItemRenderer.renderElicitation constructs ChatElicitationContentPart, whose constructor calls ChatAccessibilityService.acceptElicitation; the service checks Pending state but does not remember whether that elicitation was already announced. This is a candidate, not a confirmed explanation of the recording. Renderer disposal retains parts for reuse, so reconstruction depends on template reassignment rather than every scroll. Question-carousel notifications already have stable-key deduplication in _notifyOnQuestionCarousel. Progress scheduling was separately changed by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/9e5647a2343f2ad284de1d9c0eb11f1d6bd500c3, which does not prove this report resolved. Preserve legitimate new-event notifications and user signal settings; do not suppress all chat sounds as a workaround.

There are no confirming comments; the reported interaction was not executed during this read-only review.

These paths prevent treating every scroll as a conclusively reproduced signal replay.
