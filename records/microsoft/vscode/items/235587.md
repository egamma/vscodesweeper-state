## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/platform/menubar/electron-main/menubar.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/window.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/desktop.contribution.ts`
- **Validation:** On Pop OS with COSMIC, launch current VS Code with --verbose --disable-extensions, exit deliberately, and relaunch from both a terminal and the launcher without pressing Ctrl+Q. Verify the window remains open and no unsolicited workbench.action.quit dispatch appears; then verify deliberate Ctrl+Q still quits normally.
- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter can initially launch VS Code on Pop OS/COSMIC, but subsequent launches show a window that immediately disappears. Launching without a quit gesture must leave the window usable, including when extensions are disabled. Acceptance requires repeated launches from the terminal and desktop launcher to remain open while deliberate Ctrl+Q continues to quit normally.
- **Trace:**

  > The attachment at https://github.com/user-attachments/files/18055409/vscode_exits_on_popos_cosmic.txt shows repeated native-menubar Ctrl+Q invocations followed by workbench.action.quit and orderly lifecycle shutdown. Current Menubar.createMenuItem and Menubar.runActionInRenderer in src/vs/platform/menubar/electron-main/menubar.ts forward accelerator callbacks to vscode:runKeybinding, which src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/window.ts dispatches through the keybinding service. The Linux quit registration in src/vs/workbench/electron-browser/desktop.contribution.ts then calls nativeHostService.quit. The unresolved boundary is the origin of those unexpected callbacks: the log does not distinguish VS Code behavior from Electron or COSMIC input handling. Reproduce on the affected desktop before choosing ownership or a fix; preserve normal menu activation, keyboard shortcuts, and shutdown confirmation semantics. The GBM warnings are a separate lead, not proof of a graphics crash.
