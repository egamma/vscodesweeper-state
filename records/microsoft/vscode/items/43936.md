- **Existing capability and limitation:** src/vs/platform/theme/common/colors/miscColors.ts registers scrollbar.background as the scrollbar track background color, with a null default. src/vs/base/browser/ui/scrollbar/media/scrollbars.css applies that token to the track, but also makes the entire invisible scrollbar opacity: 0 with pointer-events: none. A color customization therefore improves the revealed track, not its persistent visibility.
- **Current Explorer path:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/views/explorerView.ts constructs a WorkbenchCompressibleAsyncDataTree without a verticalScrollMode override. src/vs/base/browser/ui/list/listView.ts defaults that mode to ScrollbarVisibility.Auto; AbstractScrollableElement in src/vs/base/browser/ui/scrollbar/scrollableElement.ts reveals on mouseover and calls beginHide after mouseleave when not dragging.
