# Issue #196884 — Cannot replace with positive lookbehind including `\n`

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/196884](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/196884)

## Summary

The reported single-replace failure remains traceable in current source: forward multiline search discards the preceding newline needed by the lookbehind, then selects another match instead of replacing the selected one. Keep this correctly routed issue open; the older related reports concern different observable failures.

## Change summary

Single replacement should replace a selected match of `(?<=\n)b`, consistently with highlighted results and Replace All.

## Best solution

Make forward multiline matching retain the context needed to recognize the selected match, while preserving search-start filtering, wraparound, capture groups, and LF/CRLF range mapping. Cover both the text-model search contract and single replacement in the existing find-model tests.
