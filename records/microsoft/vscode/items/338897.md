# Issue #338897 — Side-by-side chats have no left border when scrolled

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338897](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338897)

## Summary

Confirmed from the attached screenshot: when two chat sessions are shown side by side in the Agents Window, the vertical divider border between the panes is only drawn near the header/title row and disappears for the rest of the scrolled conversation content — a real, fresh visual bug in the new side-by-side sessions layout.

## Change summary

In the Agents Window, opening two chats side by side and scrolling causes the left border/divider of the right-hand pane to vanish below the title area.

## Best solution

Extend the pane divider so it spans the full height of the editor content area (not just the title/tab row) in the Agents Window's side-by-side chat layout; likely a CSS selector scoped to a header/title container in src/vs/sessions/browser/media or src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat that isn't applied to the full scrollable content region.
