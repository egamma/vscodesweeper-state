# Issue #86457 — iPadOS: cannot move panes around (activity bar, panel)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/86457](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/86457)

## Summary

Keep open: current drag observers still depend on native drag events, without the mouse-event fallback proposed in the discussion. View-movement commands offer a workaround, but do not deliver the requested drag interaction; current iPadOS behavior needs device validation before planning a change.

## Change summary

Support dragging views and activity-bar/panel entries to reorder or relocate them in Safari on iPadOS, including with an attached mouse or trackpad.

## Best solution

Provide reliable view and container drag-and-drop on iPadOS while preserving desktop and external drag behavior. First establish which events current Safari delivers, then determine the compatibility work needed in the shared drag infrastructure and its workbench consumers.

- **Behavior:**

  > The requested capability is direct manipulation of workbench views and containers in Safari on iPadOS. Existing command-based movement and desktop drag behavior must remain available.
- **Trace:**
