[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/190584](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/190584)

## Summary

The inconsistency between vscode-uri (required fields) and VS Code's internal UriComponents (optional fields) still exists on current main. A contributor indicated vscode-uri should be fixed, but no action has been taken in either repo.

## Change summary

UriComponents in vscode-uri defines authority/path/query/fragment as required, while VS Code's internal version makes them optional, creating type mismatches for consumers of both.

## Best solution

Align UriComponents in microsoft/vscode-uri to make authority/path/query/fragment optional, matching VS Code's internal definition (src/vs/base/common/uri.ts). The fix belongs in the vscode-uri repo, as jrieken indicated.
