# Issue #313107 — Agents: Consider surfacing pinned sessions at the top

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313107](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313107)

## Summary

This team-authored Backlog feature remains unresolved: pinned sessions appear in the sidebar, but the title-bar surface and its picker do not distinguish or directly expose them. The presentation choice between persistent tabs and a compact entry point still needs a maintainer decision.

## Change summary

Surface pinned agent sessions at the top of the Agents Window, either as tabs or through a dedicated entry point.

## Best solution

Provide a title-bar-level pinned-session affordance backed by the existing list-owned pin state, without duplicating session identity or persistence. A maintainer should first choose whether pinned sessions are continuously visible or exposed through the existing sessions picker.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsTitleBarWidget.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsActions.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/test/browser/sessionsTitleBarWidget.test.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/test/browser/sessionsActions.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Add focused title-bar or picker tests showing that pinned sessions are surfaced in the selected presentation, update reactively when pin state changes, and open the selected session while existing unpinned-session navigation remains available.
- **Behavior:**

  > Pinned sessions should be reachable directly from the top of the Agents Window without searching the sidebar or an undifferentiated all-sessions list. Pinning and unpinning must update that surface immediately, selecting an entry must open the corresponding session, and unpinned sessions must retain their existing navigation path. The presentation should remain usable when no sessions or many sessions are pinned.
- **Trace:**

  > Pin state is local presentation state owned by ISessionsListModelService in src/vs/sessions/services/sessions/browser/sessionsListModelService.ts. The sidebar consumes it to build its Pinned section in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts. The top command-center surface is SessionsTitleBarWidget in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsTitleBarWidget.ts; it currently renders the active session or blocked-session state and delegates ordinary navigation to sessions.showSessionsPicker. That picker is implemented in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsActions.ts and groups sessions by attention and recency rather than pin state. Preserve ISessionsListModelService as the sole pin-state owner, the existing blocked-session priority behavior, and provider-neutral session handling; the uncertainty begins at whether pinned sessions should occupy persistent title-bar space or appear through a compact picker entry.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should pinned sessions be continuously visible as top-level tabs, or exposed through a dedicated entry point or group in the existing sessions picker?
  - When blocked sessions temporarily replace the active-session title-bar state, should pinned-session access remain visible or yield to the existing attention indicator?

## Evidence

- **current pin ownership:** src/vs/sessions/services/sessions/browser/sessionsListModelService.ts persists pin state through ISessionsListModelService, explicitly allowing title-bar and other views to consume it.
- **existing sidebar behavior:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts creates a Pinned section at the top of the sidebar, while src/vs/sessions/SESSIONS_LIST.md defines pinned placement only for the primary Sessions list.
- **remaining title-bar gap:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsTitleBarWidget.ts renders the active-session pill and opens sessions.showSessionsPicker; src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/sessionsActions.ts groups that picker by needs-input, unread, recently opened, and other sessions, with no pinned group or pinned-only entry point.
