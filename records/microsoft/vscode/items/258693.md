Still reproduces on main: terminal suggest details flash because items are resolved twice — once in `simpleSuggestWidget.ts` on focus change and again in `terminalSuggestAddon.ts`. An open PR (#258838) exists to fix this but has not merged.

## Change summary

## Best solution
