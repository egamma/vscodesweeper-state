# Issue #49661 — Setting to ignore whitespace-only lines

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/49661)

## Summary

Keep open: current settings do not provide selective trimming that preserves whitespace-only lines, and disabling automatic whitespace cleanup does not control document formatting. This accepted Backlog request has 52 upvotes and a maintainer-directed consolidation; no verified older issue covers its full scope.

## Change summary

Provide configurable preservation of indentation on whitespace-only lines while removing trailing whitespace from nonempty lines, including the formatting workflow.

## Best solution

Provide an opt-in, language-overridable whitespace-preservation policy without changing existing defaults. Resolve its scope across built-in trimming and formatter-provided edits explicitly, so enabling it delivers the reported workflow rather than only a save-time workaround.
