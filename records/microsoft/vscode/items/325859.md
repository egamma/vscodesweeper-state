# Issue #325859 — editor.inertialScroll misclassifies high-resolution mouse wheels as touchpads

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325859](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325859)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: MouseWheelClassifier misclassifies high-resolution mouse wheels as touchpads because its non-integer delta penalty and modulo-1 clamping make sub-unit fractional deltas indistinguishable from touchpad input, bypassing the isPhysicalMouseWheel() guard added in 9f399eb4d1e.

## Change summary

High-resolution mouse wheels (e.g. Logitech MX Master on Wayland) producing fractional deltas are misclassified as touchpads by MouseWheelClassifier, causing unwanted synthetic inertia when editor.inertialScroll is enabled.

## Best solution

Improve the MouseWheelClassifier heuristics in scrollableElement.ts to handle sub-unit fractional deltas from high-resolution physical wheels: either adjust the modulo check to work with fractional values instead of clamping to 1, or reduce the non-integer delta penalty when deltas are consistently small fractions of a repeating value. The fix is localized to _computeScore and possibly _isAlmostInt. The side-note issues (60Hz timer, hold gesture, non-editor scrollables) are separate work items.
