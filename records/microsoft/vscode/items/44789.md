# Issue #44789 — Overview ruler becomes redundant

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/44789](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/44789)

## Summary

The apparent offset is intentional: VS Code enlarges the scrollbar slider to a grippable minimum while overview-ruler markers retain their proportional document positions.

## Change summary

Change or suppress the overview ruler when its markers no longer align visually with the minimum-sized scrollbar slider in large files.

## Best solution

Keep the standard minimum-sized scrollbar behavior; users who find the combined presentation distracting can hide the cursor marker with `editor.hideCursorInOverviewRuler` or disable decoration lanes with `editor.overviewRulerLanes: 0`.

  > Thanks for the detailed report and suggestions. This offset is intentional: the scrollbar slider has a minimum size so it remains grippable, while overview-ruler markers retain their proportional document positions. The same behavior and trade-off were confirmed by the editor maintainer in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/129122. ## Evidence

- **maintainer decision:** A later report of the same large-file offset was closed as designed at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/129122; the maintainer explains that the slider is deliberately enlarged for mouse gripping and that the viewport transitions from the slider's top through its center to its bottom.
- **current source:** src/vs/base/browser/ui/scrollbar/scrollbarState.ts retains a 20px `MINIMUM_SLIDER_SIZE` and adjusts the slider travel ratio, while src/vs/editor/common/viewModel/overviewZoneManager.ts maps ruler decorations proportionally over the complete document height.
- **existing controls:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts registers `editor.hideCursorInOverviewRuler` and permits `editor.overviewRulerLanes` to be set to 0.
