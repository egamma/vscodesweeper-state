# Issue #342071 — Terminal active tab highlight disappears when clicking within the tabs area

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342071](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342071)

## Summary

The reported background disappearance follows the current source: clicking empty list space clears selection and focus without changing the active terminal. Route to terminal owners to resolve the distinction between selection highlighting and persistent active-terminal identification; no verified fix or duplicate supports closing.

## Change summary

Keep the active terminal identifiable after clicking empty space within the terminal tabs list.

## Best solution

Ensure the terminal tabs retain a clear, theme-compatible indication of the active terminal independently of keyboard focus. Preserve deliberate multi-selection and empty-area context-menu behavior; terminal owners should settle whether empty-area clicks should retain selection or only retain a separate active indication.
