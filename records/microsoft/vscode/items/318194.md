# Issue #318194 — TAB bricked by copilot inline autocomplete

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/318194](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/318194)

## Summary

Verified duplicate: the open canonical issue tracks the same conflict where pressing Tab to indent accepts a distant Copilot next-edit suggestion and moves the cursor elsewhere.

## Change summary

Copilot inline suggestions intercept Tab during indentation and jump the cursor to an unrelated suggested edit.

## Best solution

Consolidate on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/256851 and ensure next-edit suggestions do not intercept indentation-oriented Tab presses or unexpectedly move the cursor away from the active edit.
