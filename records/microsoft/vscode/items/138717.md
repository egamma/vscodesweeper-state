[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/138717](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/138717)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still permits automatic language switching for extensionless files with tens of thousands of lines, but the reported YAML misclassification and subsequent freeze are not confirmed. This team-authored backlog issue is protected, and the maintainer has left the large-file policy unresolved.

## Change summary

Prevent automatic language detection from making large extensionless log files unresponsive after incorrectly selecting YAML.

## Best solution

Establish whether the slowdown comes from classification, model synchronization, or work activated by switching to YAML, then address that path in the language-detection/editor area. Any size-based suppression should preserve useful detection of large JSON documents, as requested in the maintainer discussion.
