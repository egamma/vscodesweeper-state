# Issue #255143 — NES should not jump when trying to align brackets

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/255143](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/255143)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the `_tabShouldIndent` guard in `inlineCompletionsModel.ts` only treats empty lines as indent-worthy (line 806: `getLineLength === 0`), so pressing Tab at the start of a non-empty line (e.g. a misaligned closing bracket) triggers NES jump/accept instead of indenting. A team member acknowledged and tagged the area owner.

## Change summary

When pressing Tab to fix bracket indentation, NES intercepts the keypress and applies a completion instead of indenting the line.

## Best solution

Broaden the `_tabShouldIndent` check in `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/model/inlineCompletionsModel.ts` to also return `true` when the cursor is at or before the indentation column of a non-empty line — so Tab indents rather than triggering NES in bracket-alignment scenarios. @hediet was tagged as the area owner.
