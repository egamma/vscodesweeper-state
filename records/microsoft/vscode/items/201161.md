# Issue #201161 — Tooltips are semantically static/poor

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/201161](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/201161)

## Summary

The diff-editor whitespace toggle still uses a fixed “Show” label even when whitespace differences are already visible. Keep this accepted Backlog polish item open; the requested state-aware wording remains missing.

## Change summary

Make the diff-editor whitespace toggle tooltip describe the action available in its current state.

## Best solution

The tooltip should offer to show leading/trailing whitespace differences when they are hidden and hide them when they are visible, without changing the toggle's behavior or checked-state indication.
