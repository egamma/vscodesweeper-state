# Issue #326268 — Support import, export, and backup of Chat sessions across workspaces in VSCode Copilot

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326268](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326268)

## Summary

Current VS Code supports single-session export and import, but not the requested bulk backup, durable imported sessions, or complete workspace-move recovery. No single canonical issue owns this broader scope, so it should remain open and route to the Chat area.

## Change summary

Provide portable export, import, backup, restore, and workspace reassociation for Chat sessions.

## Best solution

Extend Chat session management with bulk export and durable restore while coordinating with the existing portable-path and orphan-recovery work. Imported sessions should be persistable and safely reassociated with another workspace rather than disappearing after their imported editor closes.

- **Evidence:** The durable AI-assisted development theme and current agent-experience priorities both support reliable multi-session continuity.

## Evidence

- **existing capability:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/actions/chatImportExport.ts registers `workbench.action.chat.export` and `workbench.action.chat.import`, but export operates only on the last-focused session.
- **persistence gap:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/chatService/chatServiceImpl.ts explicitly excludes imported sessions from persistence and history (`shouldStoreSession` and `shouldBeInHistory`).
- **bulk export tracker:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/312058 is open and specifically tracks exporting all sessions, but does not own backup, restore, or workspace reassociation.
- **orphan recovery tracker:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/305818 is open in the Backlog and owns recovery after moved, renamed, or deleted workspaces, but not the full import/export and backup scope.
- **portable import tracker:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/283951 is open and tracks portable paths across environments, but is narrower than this request.
