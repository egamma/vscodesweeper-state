Keep open: current source explains why signed-out BYOK follow-ups remain scheduled while Send Now works. Automatic run admission incorrectly checks a new-session model template that existing-conversation schedules intentionally omit.

Scheduled follow-ups in existing signed-out BYOK conversations fail to dispatch automatically at their deadlines.
