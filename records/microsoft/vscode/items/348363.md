# Issue #348363 — Send Later follow-ups stay pending past their time in signed-out BYOK chats

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348363](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348363)

## Summary

Keep open: current source applies new-session model readiness to existing-conversation schedule admission, silently skipping signed-out BYOK follow-ups. The reported contrast with successful new-session schedules and Send Now matches this path.

## Change summary

Scheduled follow-ups in signed-out BYOK conversations remain pending after their deadline despite working manual delivery.

## Best solution

Make automatic admission honor existing-conversation execution eligibility rather than requiring a captured new-session model. Preserve exact-chat queue delivery, execution-time configuration, and once-only dispatch.
