# Issue #182173 — Cannot see the diff anymore in the Light Modern theme

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/182173](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/182173)

## Summary

The immediate blocker was fixed by PR #188668 (falling back to Light+/Dark+ diff colors), but a team member explicitly said to leave the issue open to track finding better dedicated accessible colors for the modern themes. The aspirational goal remains unaddressed on current main.

## Change summary

Colorblind users cannot distinguish diff colors (inserted vs removed) in Light Modern and Dark Modern themes.

## Best solution

Design dedicated diff colors for Light/Dark Modern themes that are accessible to users with deuteranomaly and other common color vision deficiencies, rather than permanently falling back to the legacy Light+/Dark+ palette.
