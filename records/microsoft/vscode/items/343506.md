Keep open as a feature request: the inspected HydraFusion controls enable routing modes and tier preferences, but do not expose a selected-model restriction. No verified canonical or concrete implementation was established.

Allow users to restrict HydraFusion to selected models so it cannot choose expensive models such as Opus 5.5.
