## Change summary

Make extension-created build tasks preserve project paths containing spaces, consecutive spaces, and parentheses across Windows task shells.

ProcessExecution is a concrete alternative for direct dotnet invocation, but its suitability does not establish that the reported ShellExecution defect is fixed.
