- **Behavior:**

  > The reporter sees a normal-sized mouse pointer jump up and left when entering Snap-installed VS Code at fractional display scales, with clicks and selections targeting away from the visible hotspot. The same setup works at 100% and 200% scaling and with the Debian package. The intended outcome is accurate pointer targeting and continuous pointer position across window boundaries at fractional scales. Acceptance must cover text selection and workbench controls while preserving the working package and integer-scale cases.
- **Trace:**

  > resources/linux/snap/snapcraft.yaml invokes resources/linux/snap/electron-launch for both entry points. The launcher modifies desktop environment variables, conditionally sets GDK_BACKEND=wayland, and ends with exec "$@" --ozone-platform=x11; this establishes a package-specific backend boundary but does not conclusively explain cursor scaling. The first diagnostic step is to reproduce with matching current builds and identify the effective backend and cursor environment. Do not assume a Monaco-only defect because the report also affects buttons, and do not change default backend policy without establishing its relevance. The related open reports https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/136390 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/257958 should be compared after diagnosis; neither is a verified canonical for this case.
