Keep open: the filter dropdown still uses active-toggle decoration, but current source makes that decoration conditional rather than permanently enabled. The report's notebook-open scenario and screenshot suffice to retest; no concrete fixing commit or matching canonical was established.

## Change summary

## Best solution
