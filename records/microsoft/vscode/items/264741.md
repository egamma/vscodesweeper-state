## Summary

Still open and unfixed on main — terminal suggestions break after clearing the terminal (Ctrl+L) or deleting an inserted suggestion (Ctrl+Backspace). Multiple team members have reproduced it. No fix commit references this issue.

## Change summary

Terminal suggest completions stop appearing after clearing the terminal with Ctrl+L or deleting inserted text with Ctrl+Backspace, until the user presses Enter.

## Best solution
