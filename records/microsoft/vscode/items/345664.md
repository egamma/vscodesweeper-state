# Issue #345664 — Permissions picker: locked sandbox switch and disabled Allow all don't expose their organization reason to keyboard/screen reader users

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345664](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345664)

## Summary

The reported accessibility defects remain in current main: locked sandbox rows lose their reason and unavailable state, while disabled permission choices are skipped and their labels interpolate a boolean as the reason. Keep this open for a bounded accessibility fix.

## Change summary

Make organization-imposed restrictions in the permissions picker understandable to keyboard and screen reader users without enabling restricted actions.

## Best solution

Expose each locked control's unavailable state and organization explanation through the row's accessible presentation, and provide keyboard access to disabled permission explanations. Preserve policy enforcement and the existing disabled-row navigation defaults for other action-widget consumers.
