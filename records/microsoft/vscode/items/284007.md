In src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findState.ts:254-263, FindReplaceState.change uses negated range equality inside some(), followed by a negated every(): a different new single range is incorrectly considered unchanged, preserving the old scope and suppressing its change event. This directly accounts for returning to the find input after changing the selection still searching the old range.
- **Search propagation:** src/vs/editor/contrib/find/browser/findModel.ts:151-177 recomputes the scoped search when searchScope changes. It is not evidence of a landed fix, and its full interaction coverage still needs review.
