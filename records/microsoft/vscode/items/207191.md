# Issue #207191 — Quick Pick: make buttons easier to tab to

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/207191](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/207191)

## Summary

Keep open: current Quick Pick navigation does not provide the proposed context-sensitive shortcut to buttons. This is a team-authored Backlog item, and no verified fix or eligible duplicate establishes that the work is complete.

## Change summary

Reduce Tab presses needed to reach item buttons after list navigation and the custom button after typing.

## Best solution

Make Quick Pick button access more direct while preserving predictable keyboard traversal and screen-reader behavior. Agree on whether Tab should follow the user's last interaction or whether item actions should have a separate focus shortcut.
