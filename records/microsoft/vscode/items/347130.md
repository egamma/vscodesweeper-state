# Issue #347130 — Worktree cleanup duplicates worktrees with multiple sessions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347130](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347130)

## Summary

Worktree cleanup still inventories sessions rather than distinct checkouts, inflating rows and counts when sessions share a worktree. Keep open under the assigned 1.147.0 milestone while the explicitly linked fixing PR is reviewed.

## Change summary

Show each shared worktree once, count its storage once, and derive its last activity from all associated sessions.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/347378, ensuring shared checkouts have deduplicated counts and storage, latest-session activity, and eligibility that respects every associated session.
