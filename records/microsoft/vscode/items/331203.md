## Change summary

Make it clear and reliable to drag a chat from a separate chat group back into its original group.

## Best solution

Dragging the separated chat tab onto the center of another chat group should visibly merge it into that group and automatically collapse the emptied group, matching familiar editor-group interactions. Verify that gesture in the live Agents Window and add focused regression coverage if the current drop path remains unreliable or undiscoverable.
