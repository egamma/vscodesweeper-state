# Issue #329789 — '<' and '>' are not colored correctly in left shift assignment and right shift assignment

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329789](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329789)

## Summary

Confirmed bug in bracket-pair colorization (only when bracket pair colorizing is enabled, per maintainer comment); an open fixing PR already targets it, so this should stay open pending review/merge rather than being closed.

## Change summary

'<' and '>' in `<<=`/`>>=`/`>>>=` shift-assignment operators are incorrectly matched as bracket pairs by the bracket-pair colorizer.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/329810, which adds a lookahead-only exclusion in prepareBracketForRegExp (src/vs/editor/common/model/bracketPairsTextModelPart/bracketPairsTree/brackets.ts) so `<`/`>` aren't treated as brackets when they unambiguously form `<<=`, `>>=`, or `>>>=`.
