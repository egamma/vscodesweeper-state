# Issue #214194 — editor.find.autoFindInSelection does not treat block selection as multiline

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/214194](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/214194)

## Summary

The rectangular-selection case remains reproducible from current source: automatic selection-scoped find checks only the primary, single-line selection. Keep this open in editor-find; the existing multiline setting promises activation when multiple lines of content are selected.

## Change summary

Automatically enable Find in Selection when opening Replace with a nonempty rectangular selection spanning multiple lines and editor.find.autoFindInSelection set to multiline.

## Best solution

Make automatic scope activation and the Find in Selection toggle consistently recognize the reported multi-line rectangular selection, preserving the separate selected ranges rather than searching their enclosing rectangle.
