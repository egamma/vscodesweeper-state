[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/245121](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/245121)

## Summary

Confirmed visual bug still present on main — activity bar icons from extensions lack consistent hover/focus outlines in high contrast themes. An open PR (#245404) is attempting a fix; keeping open pending that PR's resolution.

## Change summary

Activity bar icons contributed by extensions do not show consistent hover/focus outlines in high contrast themes, unlike built-in icons.

## Best solution
