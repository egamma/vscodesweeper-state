# Issue #344558 — Restoring a large chat regenerates hundreds of thinking titles via the utility model

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344558](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/344558)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still allows completed, restored thinking groups with expired or evicted titles to enqueue utility-model requests, and each cache operation reloads the shared profile blob. This is a distinct, diagnosed performance problem, not merely the broader remote-chat switching slowdown.

## Change summary

Restoring large completed agent-host chats regenerates hundreds of thinking titles and repeatedly processes a globally capped title cache.

## Best solution

Make completed-history restoration reuse durable titles without triggering a transcript-wide regeneration queue, and avoid whole-cache processing per group. Agree on whether uncached historical titles remain fallbacks or generate on explicit visibility or expansion before choosing the persistence boundary.
