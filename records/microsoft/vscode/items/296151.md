# Issue #296151 — [Bug] “Word wrap” lacks `overflow-wrap: break-word`, for ligatures whose widths exceed that of the viewport.

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296151](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296151)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the DOM line breaks computer uses `word-break: inherit` (line 140 of domLineBreaksComputer.ts), which doesn't break wide ligatures. An open PR (#309258) proposes changing it to `break-all`; keeping open pending that PR's review and merge.

## Change summary

Word wrap fails to break lines within wide ligatures (e.g. repeated Tamil ௌ U+0BCC) that exceed the viewport width, causing horizontal overflow.

## Best solution

Review and land the open PR https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/309258, which changes `word-break` from `inherit` to `break-all` in the DOM measurement container. The maintainer should verify CJK and normal wrapping are unaffected.
