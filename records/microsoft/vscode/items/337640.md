## Summary

Legitimate, well-documented follow-up to a just-landed quarantine (test.skip is present on main in chatPetWidget.test.ts) — the flaky teleport-animation test still needs a real fix and re-enable, so this stays open as the tracking issue for that work.

## Change summary

The Chat Pet 'teleports to the top of the movement area and falls onto the new input' unit test is flaky on CI (drops intermediate sprite frames) and was quarantined with test.skip; this issue tracks diagnosing and fixing the timing sensitivity so the test can be re-enabled.

## Best solution

Investigate the real-time, performance.now()-driven sprite frame scheduling in chatPetWidget.ts (frame stepping around line 567-588) to determine why intermediate frames get skipped under CI load, then either make the animation scheduling deterministic under test or adjust the test's frame-sampling assertion to tolerate/verify frame continuity without depending on wall-clock timing, and remove the test.skip in chatPetWidget.test.ts.
