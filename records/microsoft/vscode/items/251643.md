# Issue #251643 — Option to accept/discard hunk does not show when opening diffs from SCM view

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251643](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251643)

## Summary

Keep open for the agreed UX discussion: SCM diffs still exclude the chat hunk controls, but exposing them selectively needs to make pending AI edits distinguishable from other uncommitted changes. The thread explicitly postpones implementation until the team agrees on that experience.

## Change summary

Make per-hunk Keep/Undo controls consistently available when reviewing pending AI edits through Source Control diffs.

## Best solution

Agree on how SCM diffs distinguish pending AI edits from accepted AI edits and manual changes, then provide review controls whose scope is clear. Preserve ordinary SCM staging and discard behavior.

- **Behavior:**

  > Pending AI edits have per-hunk Keep/Undo controls in chat review diffs but not when the same file is opened from Source Control. The desired experience is consistent review access without implying that chat actions apply to every uncommitted change. Acceptance must cover a file containing both manual and pending AI edits, preservation of unrelated edits, and an understandable state after an AI hunk is accepted.
- **Trace:**

  > ChatEditingCodeEditorIntegration derives enabledObs from the modified model URI and, inside diff editors, isTextDiffEditorForEntry. The boundary must preserve chat review semantics and ordinary SCM staging/discard, not reinterpret all SCM changes as pending AI edits. The unresolved product decision is documented at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251643#issuecomment-3715146362: the team must agree how selective controls communicate their scope in a mixed diff.
- **Open decisions:**

## Evidence

This is an experience improvement rather than an accidental rendering defect.
- **Explicit agreement to postpone:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251643#issuecomment-3715146362 accepts postponement until the team agrees on a solution that distinguishes AI and non-AI changes. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/251643#issuecomment-2993408652 supports consistent hunk controls.
