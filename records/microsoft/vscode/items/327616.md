# Issue #327616 — NES: gutter indicator is focusable but has no role, no accessible name, and cannot be operated by keyboard

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327616](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327616)

## Summary

The gutter indicator still exposes a focusable, unnamed div with mouse-only activation and menu opening on current main. Keep this team-authored accessibility bug open; the related reports do not own the same observable defect.

## Change summary

Make the Next Edit Suggestions gutter indicator identifiable and operable by keyboard, including access to its menu.

## Best solution

Give the gutter indicator meaningful accessible semantics and keyboard activation equivalent to its mouse action, with a discoverable keyboard route into its menu. Preserve existing jump/accept behavior and coordinate with the separate menu-navigation issue at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/327617.
