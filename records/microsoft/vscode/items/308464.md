# Issue #308464 — Copilot extension breaks auto complete

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308464](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308464)

## Summary

Keep open and route to the suggest area. The reporter explicitly distinguishes this from the intentional quick-suggestion default: manual Ctrl+Space and an explicit `editor.quickSuggestions: "on"` also fail while Copilot ghost text is enabled, which is not the designed behavior.

## Change summary

Copilot ghost-text suggestions prevent both automatic and manually invoked completion lists, even when quick suggestions are explicitly enabled.

## Best solution

Reproduce the interaction with a controlled completion provider and Copilot inline completion, then ensure manual Trigger Suggest and explicit `editor.quickSuggestions: "on"` remain authoritative while preserving the intentional `offWhenInlineCompletions` default behavior.

## Evidence

- **distinct reported behavior:** The reporter states at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308464#issuecomment-4914834700 that Ctrl+Space produces no completions and `editor.quickSuggestions: "on"` does not restore them; later comments confirm the setting still does not work.
- **multiple affected environments:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/308464#issuecomment-4314889399 independently reports native completions disappearing or becoming substantially delayed with Copilot inline suggestions, across local and Remote SSH editors, while disabling inline suggestions restores normal behavior.
- **not covered by intended default:** The maintainer explanation at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316305#issuecomment-5109798770 says only automatically triggered suggestions are intentionally suppressed; Ctrl+Space and `editor.quickSuggestions: "on"` are expected to remain available.
- **current source contract:** On current main, src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestController.ts registers Ctrl+Space as `editor.action.triggerSuggest`, and SuggestModel.trigger in src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestModel.ts directly requests completion items without applying the quick-suggestion suppression path. An explicit all-`on` configuration also bypasses `_waitForInlineCompletionsAndTrigger`.
