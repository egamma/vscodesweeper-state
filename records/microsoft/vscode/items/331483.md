- **Open fixing PR and confirmed reproduction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/332012 is verified OPEN, unmerged, and explicitly says 'Fixes #331483'. Its geometry probe reports a clean-to-dirty width change from 135px to 155px with wrapTabs enabled and tabActionReserveSpace disabled, plus reproduction with hidden close actions. These are contributor-reported measurements, not checks executed during this review.
- **Current source:** src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/editorTabsControl.ts:664-677 derives the tab-actions-reserve-space class solely from tabActionReserveSpace, without a wrapTabs condition. src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/media/multieditortabscontrol.css:471-474 hides clean close-action-off action containers, while dirty tabs expose their indicator; lines 525-539 also change their padding. MultiEditorTabsControl's wrapping layout uses actual tab widths and container height, so transient width changes affect row composition.

This helps ordinary Modern UI tabs, but reservation selectors in src/vs/workbench/contrib/modernUI/browser/media/tabs.css exclude close-action-off tabs, and the setting does not establish stable file-decoration badge space.
