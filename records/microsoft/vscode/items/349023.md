- **Trace:**

  > extensions/markdown-language-features/markdown-editor-src/editor.ts wires EditorController to vscodeLocalKeyboardProfile, vscodeHostKeyboardProfile, and ImagePasteController. The latter delegates clipboard handling to AsyncClipboardStrategy from @vscode/markdown-editor, whose implementation was not available locally. The Electron webview forwarding listener in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/pre/index.html should prevent native clipboard handling and forward this chord; webviewElement.ts then redispatches it to the workbench. The report's missing V event suggests earlier consumption, but that is not proven. This supports investigating consumption before the forwarding listener rather than assuming clipboard shortcuts are intentionally excluded.
