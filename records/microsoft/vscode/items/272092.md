# Issue #272092 — `ref` auto-complete moves to top of list

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272092](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272092)

## Summary

Keep open for the suggestion-ranking decision already raised by the maintainer. Current source still boosts full matches, and existing snippet and selection settings do not provide general control over that boost.

## Change summary

Reconsider exact-match promotion that puts unused completions such as `ref` ahead of more useful suggestions and causes accidental acceptance.

## Best solution

Decide whether exact-match promotion should remain unconditional or become configurable in editor suggestions, then validate the chosen behavior against both reference completions and ordinary symbols. Preserve unrelated fuzzy-matching behavior and existing snippet-order controls.
