# Issue #325459 — Extensions can pollute process.env inherited by unrelated extensions and child processes

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325459](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325459)

## Summary

The cross-extension environment leak is present on current main, and an open PR explicitly fixes this issue by fencing synchronous extension startup. Keep the issue open while that human-owned fix is reviewed.

## Change summary

Prevent one extension's startup-time mutations to the shared extension-host process environment from affecting unrelated extensions and their child processes.

## Best solution

Review and either land or close https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/325460, confirming whether its synchronous CommonJS and activation fence is the intended platform boundary given that asynchronous continuations remain outside its scope.
