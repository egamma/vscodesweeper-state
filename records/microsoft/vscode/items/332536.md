[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332536](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332536)

## Summary

Keep open: the standard suggestion widget still clips a rectangular focused-row outline against its rounded list viewport. The related padding-consistency request is newer and is not a valid duplicate canonical for this re-reviewed report.

## Change summary

Prevent the focused completion item's outline from appearing cut off or disconnected at the suggestion widget's rounded corners.

## Best solution

Make the suggestion widget's boundary-row focus indicator render continuously within the rounded viewport, preserving keyboard-focus visibility and theme colors. Keep the broader cross-menu padding discussion separate.
