## Change summary

Archiving a completed background agent leaves its worktree present and its active chat visible and usable.

## Best solution

Archiving should remove the session from the active UI while preserving recoverable changes, with safe worktree cleanup where possible. Confirm the existing implementation against the original flow before deciding whether any further change is necessary; do not equate archiving with irreversible deletion.
