# Issue #311971 — inlayHints and documentSymbols are requested twice on every key press + inlayHints are called again on every show

## Summary

Keep open and route for investigation: redundant inlay-hint requests remain explainable from current source, and another contributor confirmed duplicate document-symbol requests with autosave disabled. The older related reports cover only parts of this issue, not its full scope.

## Change summary

Avoid repeated inlay-hint and document-symbol provider requests for unchanged document content, including requests triggered by showing or hiding cached hints.

## Best solution

Reuse valid provider results when only hint visibility or cursor-stability rendering changes, preserving refresh events and viewport coverage. Diagnose why document-symbol requests bypass or invalidate the existing shared outline cache before changing that path.
