# Issue #276982 — Give context into what helper is being installed or updated

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/276982](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/276982)

## Summary

Keep open while the linked macOS update PR is reviewed: multiple users confirm the unexplained, recurring administrator prompt. The proposed change addresses unsolicited prompts and adds privilege context, but it has not merged and its coverage of the helper-identification request still needs assessment.

## Change summary

Explain what the macOS update helper does and why authorization is needed, without repeatedly interrupting users who decline it.

## Best solution

Review and validate https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/341183 on macOS, including standard-user installations. Ensure the resulting update experience explains the helper's purpose before requesting authorization, rather than treating suppression of automatic prompts alone as full resolution.
