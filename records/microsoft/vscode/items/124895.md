That scope does not establish that the subsequent Wayland failures are fixed.
- **Runtime delivery is not resolution:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124895#issuecomment-2688190173 states that the February 2025 Insiders build contains the Chromium fixes and asks affected users to confirm resolution; it does not declare the issue resolved.
- **Failures reported after delivery:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124895#issuecomment-2960439631 reports unresponsive scrollbar dragging on VS Code 1.100.2 with Ubuntu/GNOME Wayland. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/124895#issuecomment-2965439807 independently reports broader dragging failures on 1.100.3 with Arch/KDE Wayland, including across multiple instances.
