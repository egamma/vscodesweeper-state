# Issue #221444 — Minor indentation inconsistency - trimAutoWhitespace - difference between tab and shift-tab

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221444](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221444)

## Summary

The Tab/Shift+Tab trimming inconsistency remains traceable in current main: outdenting discards the pending auto-whitespace status, leaving indentation behind on the next Enter. Keep this open in editor-autoindent; the older related reports concern different observable behavior.

## Change summary

Preserve automatic whitespace trimming when Shift+Tab reduces indentation on an automatically indented blank line.

## Best solution

Make outdenting an automatically indented blank line preserve its eligibility for trimming on the next Enter, consistent with Tab. Preserve existing handling of manually authored whitespace, nonblank lines, and disabled trimming.
