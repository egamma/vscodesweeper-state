# Issue #314743 — "Light 2026" theme does not have shadows, but VS Code shell does have shadows

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314743](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/314743)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still separates transparent Light 2026 shadow colors from workbench elevation shadows that webviews cannot access. Modern UI and workbench.shadows suppress part shadows, but preserve notification shadows, so neither fully addresses the request; a maintainer should settle the shared elevation contract.

## Change summary

Make Light 2026 shadow treatment consistent between workbench surfaces and extension webviews, either by exposing matching elevation styles or by removing the unmatched workbench shadows.

## Best solution

Establish a supported, theme-aware elevation contract for equivalent workbench and webview surfaces. Decide whether that means exposing the workbench shadow tiers to webviews or changing Light 2026's shadow treatment, while preserving intentional distinctions between pane depth and floating overlays.

- **Behavior:**

  > Light 2026 exposes transparent legacy shadow colors to extension webviews while workbench floating surfaces retain separate elevation shadows. Acceptance must distinguish pane-depth shadows from floating-overlay shadows and cover theme changes, the existing shadow setting, and high-contrast presentation.
- **Trace:**

  > WebviewThemeDataProvider.getWebviewThemeData() in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/themeing.ts exports registered colors and sizes, not the elevation declarations scoped to .monaco-workbench in browser/media/style.css. - **Open decisions:**
