# Issue #346206 — Weird twitch when tab wrapping is enabled and the currently open tabs take up nearly the entire row

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346206](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346206)

## Summary

Keep open: the Windows confirmation documents unwanted overflow and scrollbar flicker with tab wrapping enabled, independently of saving. Current source identifies the relevant layout path, but does not establish a fix or conclusively explain both triggers.

## Change summary

Prevent tab-bar twitching and unintended horizontal overflow when wrapped tabs nearly fill a row.

## Best solution

Make wrapped-tab layout stable near row-width boundaries while preserving dirty indicators, toolbar clearance, and legitimate non-wrapped scrolling. Investigate the saving and stationary-hover cases separately before assuming they share a cause.
