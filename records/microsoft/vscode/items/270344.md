# Issue #270344 — "Compare selected" on two fairly small text files never completes computing a comparison

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/270344](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/270344)

## Summary

Keep open: the attached short, long-line logs have a confirmed pathological comparison, and current source still permits synchronous, unlimited character diffing on the shared editor worker. No fixing commit or older canonical covering this specific reproduction was established.

## Change summary

Comparing two small log files containing very long lines takes minutes without completing when diffEditor.maxComputationTime is zero.

## Best solution

Make pathological long-line comparisons complete or remain recoverable without blocking subsequent comparisons. Diagnose the supplied files in the character-diff and worker paths, and consider the timeout-action UX separately without silently changing the documented meaning of a zero timeout.
