# Issue #330520 — Dictation becomes a Pasted text attachment that is dropped when sent to a side chat with btw

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330520](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330520)

## Summary

Detailed, specific first-hand repro from a contributor: dictated text becomes a Pasted-text attachment, and when handed to a side chat via /btw the attachment content is not carried over, leaving a bare unresolved `#attachment:...` reference and silently losing the message. Real chat/sessions data-loss bug, worth routing to the area owners.

## Change summary

Dictated long-form input becomes a Pasted-text attachment, and that attachment's content is lost (not resolved) when the turn is forwarded to a side chat via /btw.

## Best solution

Ensure attachment content (not just a reference) survives the side-chat hand-off in src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/btwSlashCommand.contribution.ts / sideChatOrchestration.ts and the provider's sendRequest path (src/vs/sessions/contrib/providers/copilotChatSessions/browser/copilotChatSessionsProvider.ts), likely by resolving/materializing paste-kind variable entries before or during createSideChatInSession, mirroring how agentHostSessionHandler.ts already materializes `kind === 'paste'` entries. Separately, consider not auto-converting dictated text into a Pasted-text attachment in the first place, since the author notes these are two separable issues.
