# Issue #175890 — v1.76 ZoneWidget right-click context is affected by sash and scrollbar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175890](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175890)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the scrollbar (z-index: 11) and sash (z-index: 35) sit above the ZoneWidget (z-index: 10), intercepting right-click context menus when they overlap. No fix has landed.

## Change summary

Right-click context menu in ZoneWidget is intercepted by the scrollbar and sash due to z-index layering.

## Best solution

Adjust the z-index layering so that the ZoneWidget's interactive content (or at least its context-menu hit area) is not obscured by the scrollbar and sash overlays. The fix likely lives in `src/vs/editor/contrib/zoneWidget/browser/zoneWidget.css` or requires a more holistic z-index refactor across scrollbar/sash/zone-widget layers.
