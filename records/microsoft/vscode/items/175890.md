[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175890](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/175890)

## Summary

The stacking defect is fixed on current main: context menus from fixed-overflow embedded editors now mount at the window root, outside the ZoneWidget stacking context and above its sash and scrollbar.

## Change summary

Right-click menus opened in a ZoneWidget's embedded editor were crossed or obscured by the widget sash and scrollbar.
