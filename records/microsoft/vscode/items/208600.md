## Evidence

- **Reported trigger:** The screenshot in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/208600 shows a four-line listprocesses.bat diff with a final-line change. The Revert Block button is visible, but the next gutter action extends below the file's bottom boundary and is clipped.
- **Current positioning defect:** src/vs/editor/browser/widget/diffEditor/features/gutterFeature.ts, DiffToolBar.layout, centers the measured toolbar on itemRange. Its viewport adjustment runs only when both preferred ranges exist and the preferred parent range has positive length. A short final-line hunk cannot satisfy that condition when the toolbar is taller than the hunk, so its bottom can extend outside viewRange. The compact-toolbar condition additionally requires mapping.original.startLineNumber === 1, excluding a final-line hunk later in the file.
