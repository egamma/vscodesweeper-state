## Summary

Fixed on main: the root cause was the `e.detail > 1` guard in BaseDropdown that rejected rapid repeated clicks — removed in commit dfc6a2ab333, shipped in 1.105.0. The commenter (sbatten, the fix author) confirmed this affected all dropdowns.
