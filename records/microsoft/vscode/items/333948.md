Keep open: the two-folder opening sequence provides a concrete scenario to retest, although the intermittent cross-window attachment was not confirmed from source. Current main retains the historical workspace-ID fix, and the related shutdown PR remains open; neither establishes that this regression is resolved.

## Change summary

Opening another folder sometimes attaches its window to an existing window's terminal, causing shared output, incorrect resizing, and termination when the second window closes.

## Best solution

Restore workspace-isolated terminal attachment in the local terminal backend and PTY restoration flow, with closing either window leaving the other window's process unaffected. Review https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/307472 for the shutdown symptom, without treating its proposed mitigation as a demonstrated fix for incorrect attachment.
