[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330258](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330258)

## Summary

The requested compact/flat alternative now exists: setting window.density.layout to compact retains Modern UI while removing inter-panel and perimeter gaps and squaring the workbench containers. Propose closing as completed rather than treating the existing whole-experiment opt-out as sufficient.

## Change summary

Remove distracting, space-consuming gaps around Modern UI workbench parts, or provide a compact alternative without those margins.

## Best solution
