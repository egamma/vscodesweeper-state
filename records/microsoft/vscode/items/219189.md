## Summary

Keep open in issue-bot: existing documentation partly explains reconsideration, but does not clearly cover older requests outside the bot pipeline or requests that reach the voting threshold after closure. The maintainer's guidance against duplicate submissions should be reflected in a consistent public explanation.

## Change summary

Clarify the documented triage lifecycle for older feature requests and closed requests that subsequently attract community support.

## Best solution

Publish consistent guidance across the triage wiki and bot messages explaining how older requests are reviewed, how reconsideration after closure is requested, and whether later votes trigger any automated action. Preserve the original discussion rather than encouraging duplicate submissions, and distinguish reconsideration from guaranteed reopening.
