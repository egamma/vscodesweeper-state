# Issue #349253 — Inbox: enter after promt goes to other session

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349253](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349253)

## Summary

Inbox intentionally opens another session after sending a reply. The existing sessions.list.inboxAutoAdvance setting lets the reporter stay in the current conversation instead.

## Change summary

Sending a prompt with Enter in Inbox automatically switches to another session.

Keep the intentional Inbox navigation behavior and explain that setting sessions.list.inboxAutoAdvance to "off" preserves the current conversation after replying.
