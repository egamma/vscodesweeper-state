## Evidence

- **Documented codec boundary:** extensions/media-preview/README.md explicitly states that MP4 previews do not support AAC audio tracks; supported audio formats include MP3, WAV, and Ogg.
- **Current preview implementation:** extensions/media-preview/media/videoPreview.js creates a native video element with controls enabled and sets muted only according to autoplay. extensions/media-preview/src/videoPreview.ts supplies the media URL and autoplay/loop settings; neither establishes why an Opus track would be unavailable.
- **Second affected surface:** extensions/simple-browser/src/simpleBrowserView.ts embeds the supplied URL in an iframe. Failure there as well makes a Video Preview-only explanation insufficient, but source inspection cannot verify this Ubuntu installation's decoding or audio output.
- **Verified related issue, partial scope only:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/167685 is open and older, and tracks silent MP4 playback caused by missing AAC support in webviews. It does not establish the cause of this reporter's failed Opus conversion, so it cannot yet absorb the full report.
- **Maintainer codec explanation:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329811#issuecomment-5247405894 explains that licensing restrictions limit the shipped FFmpeg codec set. This supports the AAC explanation, not a blanket conclusion that all reported audio failures are intentional.
- **Missing distinguishing reproduction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339573 supplies clear steps and external-player comparisons, but no sample, conversion command, resulting stream metadata, or independent confirmation. The reported Opus conversion copied the video and therefore is not a supported-format control that isolates audio playback.
- **Different packaging report:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/240329 concerns silent WAV playback in the Snap package and explicitly reports working playback with the .deb package. It is not a verified canonical for this .deb report.
