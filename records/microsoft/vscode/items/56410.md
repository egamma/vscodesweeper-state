# Issue #56410 — Warn when reloading VSCode if Issue Reporter is open

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/56410](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/56410)

## Summary

Keep open for protection of unfinished reports when closing or restarting VS Code, rather than ordinary window reloads. Current reporter implementations provide some close confirmation, but the inspected paths do not establish that the clarified application-exit scenario is covered.

## Change summary

Warn before application shutdown discards an unfinished issue report, or preserve and restore the draft across restart.

## Best solution

Ensure unfinished issue reports are protected when their owning VS Code window or application closes, with a cancellable warning at minimum. Treat persistent draft restoration as a separate enhancement rather than requiring it to deliver the minimum requested protection.
