# Issue #44941 — Proxy parameters are ignored and user-settings are missing a proxy-bypass option

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/44941](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/44941)

## Summary

The central asks — a proxy-bypass setting and proper handling of --proxy-bypass-list — are now implemented on main and shipped in 1.91.0. Proposing close as completed.

## Change summary

Proxy bypass parameters (--proxy-bypass-list) were ignored and no user-settings proxy-bypass option existed.

## Best solution

Keep the shipped implementations: `http.noProxy` setting (commit e3e15e15e82) provides the user-settings bypass list, and commit 8993f76c9fc passes --proxy-bypass-list as an argv argument to Electron. No further change needed.
