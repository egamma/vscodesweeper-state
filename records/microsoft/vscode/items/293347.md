# Issue #293347 — Nicer transition from Stop to Steer icon

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/293347](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/293347)

## Summary

Keep open for a focused chat-input design pass: current source still introduces a separate steering split button when typing during a response, rather than providing the seamless transition requested. A maintainer supports exploring this, and the related UI-refresh tracker explicitly retains it as unfinished work.

## Change summary

Make the transition between stopping a response and submitting a steering message visually smoother, with less icon movement and discontinuity.

## Best solution

Provide a stable, visually coherent chat-input action transition when typing during an active response. Agree on the visible Stop/Steer arrangement and transition treatment with the design owner while preserving cancellation, steering, and queueing semantics.
