- **Trace:**

  > CommentingRangeDecorator.updateHover calls _doUpdate in src/vs/workbench/contrib/comments/browser/commentsController.ts; _doUpdate splits commenting ranges around the emphasized line and replaces decorations through deltaDecorations.
