# Issue #182642 — Clipboard APIs do not work inside iframes in webviews

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/182642](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/182642)

## Summary

Still open and unfixed on main. The webview iframe sandboxing architecture sets clipboard permissions on its own two iframe layers, but extension-created iframes nested within the webview content cannot inherit clipboard access across origins. The team has acknowledged the bug with `help wanted` and it has strong community interest (24 upvotes).

## Change summary

Clipboard APIs (navigator.clipboard.writeText) fail with 'Write permission denied' inside iframes embedded within webview content, despite the iframe specifying allow="clipboard-write".

## Best solution

The Permissions-Policy for clipboard-read/write needs to propagate through the full iframe chain. The fix likely involves ensuring the inner content iframe in `src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/pre/index.html` propagates the clipboard permission policy to descendant frames, possibly by adjusting how the sandbox or allow attributes interact, or by intercepting clipboard calls via the webview messaging bridge. The related issue https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/182011 (Simple Browser copy/paste) shares the same root cause and could be addressed together.
