# Issue #229498 — `Turn on Remote Tunnel Access` account view could show suggestions for accounts to authenticate with 

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229498](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/229498)

## Summary

The account-suggestion gap remains in current source: Remote Tunnel Access lists accounts only from sessions matching its required scopes. Keep this team-authored, maintainer-supported improvement open; no verified canonical or fixing commit was identified.

## Change summary

Suggest already signed-in accounts in the Remote Tunnel Access picker even when they lack a session with the required tunnel scopes.

## Best solution

Let users select an existing account independently of its currently granted scopes, then obtain the authentication required for Remote Tunnel Access before starting the tunnel. Preserve direct reuse of suitable sessions and the option to sign in with another account.
