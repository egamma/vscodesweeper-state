# Issue #338770 — Focus outline in marketplace entry seems a bit off

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338770](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338770)

## Summary

Confirmed from source: the focus outline in the AI Customization discovery/browse cards is drawn only around the inner `.customization-discovery-card-primary` button, not the padded row the hover highlight covers, so it sits visually inset relative to the highlighted background — matching the screenshot's misaligned outline.

## Change summary

The keyboard-focus outline on a marketplace/discovery entry (e.g. "Azure AI Foundry" card) is drawn tighter than the row's hover/selection background, making it look misaligned.

## Best solution

Align the focus ring with the row bounds — either move the outline to `.customization-discovery-card` on `:focus-within` (matching how the hover background already works) or expand the primary button's hit area to match the card's padding, consistent with the inset (-1px) outline convention used elsewhere in aiCustomizationManagement.css.
