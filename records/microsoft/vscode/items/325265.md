# Issue #325265 — Webview resources fail silently with ERR_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES when a webview loads thousands of files (Linux renderer keeps default 1024 nofile soft limit)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325265](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325265)

## Summary

Keep open: independent Linux reports confirm descriptor exhaustion after both resource-loading mitigations shipped. Current source leaves cache lookups and cached-response lifetimes outside the host-body concurrency limit, but their contribution to the remaining failure still needs diagnosis.

## Change summary

Prevent high-volume webview resource loading from exhausting Linux renderer descriptors and leaving extension interfaces unable to start.

## Best solution

Diagnose the remaining descriptor pressure across webview streaming and CacheStorage, then ensure large resource bursts complete reliably under the normal Linux renderer limit. Preserve cache correctness and avoid changing limits inherited by unrelated child processes.

- **Behavior:**

  > A Linux webview loading thousands of local resources can exhaust its renderer's 1024-descriptor limit and never become ready, even with the existing buffer-pool and concurrency mitigations. The expected behavior is reliable completion with intact resources under normal desktop-launch limits, for both cold and warm caches. Acceptance should include repeated startups with descriptor measurements, successful interface readiness, and unchanged ordinary resource-loading behavior.
- **Trace:**

  > WebviewElement.loadResource in src/vs/workbench/contrib/webview/browser/webviewElement.ts obtains streams through loadLocalResource in resourceLoading.ts and transfers their chunks to the webview. In browser/pre/service-worker.js, processResourceRequest performs cache lookup before acquiring the 32-slot host-body semaphore; cached 304 responses release that slot before their bodies are consumed. Independent October reports confirm exhaustion after the merged mitigations, but do not identify the remaining peak allocation source. The cache-bypass result is confounded by worker-registration reset and must not be treated as a proven diagnosis. Preserve existing streaming, caching, range, and cancellation contracts; do not assume globally raising inherited descriptor limits is harmless. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346625 tracks a distinct close-during-load queue-lifecycle problem that should remain separate.

## Evidence

- **Additional confirmation and qualified cache hypothesis:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325265#issuecomment-6415800538 independently confirms Linux x86-64 failure with an affected webview renderer at 1024 descriptors, including hundreds of shared-memory objects. Cache bypass correlated with successful starts, but worker registration was also reset and no controlled restore/reapply experiment was performed; cache causality is not established.
- **Merged mitigations are not a completed fix:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/326272 merged as 17d3b86b9361cba49d8d0238787d9aa590e657ca and limits active host-backed response bodies to 32. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/326334 merged as 8a7abeba6e03ea3af87bfbce9a1b7e48fed567b8 and restores the Linux 8 KiB Node buffer pool. It reports successful cold loading without that close/reopen trigger, so it does not absorb this report's Linux descriptor exhaustion during startup.
