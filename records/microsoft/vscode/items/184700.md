# Issue #184700 — Alignment is incorrectly replaced by tabs

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/184700](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/184700)

## Summary

The alignment-space conversion remains in the current Enter implementation, including editor.autoIndent="keep". Keep this accepted backlog bug open: the reporter supplied a concrete example, and the older related issues do not establish a qualifying duplicate for this re-review.

## Change summary

Preserve existing tabs-for-indentation and spaces-for-alignment when Enter inherits the preceding line's whitespace.

## Best solution

Preserve the tab-and-space composition of inherited indentation when Enter continues an aligned line, without changing explicit indentation conversion or language-requested indentation increases and decreases. Cover the reporter's mixed-whitespace case in the editor cursor tests.
