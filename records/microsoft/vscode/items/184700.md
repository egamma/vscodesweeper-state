# Issue #184700 — Alignment is incorrectly replaced by tabs

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/184700](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/184700)

## Summary

The reported conversion remains reproducible from current source: Enter normalizes the inherited whitespace, replacing alignment spaces with additional tabs. Keep this accepted Backlog issue open; the older reports inspected do not establish a canonical with the same observable symptom under the stronger re-review rules.

## Change summary

Preserve tabs used for indentation and subsequent spaces used for alignment when inserting a new line.

## Best solution

Enter should preserve the preceding line's mixed tab-and-space prefix when continuing at the same indentation level, without changing explicit indentation-conversion commands or unrelated editing behavior. Address this narrow continuation case in the editor's Enter operation rather than requiring a complete Smart Tabs implementation.
