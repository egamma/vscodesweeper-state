The timing defect remains in current main, and an open PR explicitly targets this issue. Keep it open while that human-owned fix is reviewed or revised.

## Change summary

Invalid-regex validation appears before the animated editor Find widget reaches its final position, temporarily obscuring the search field.

## Best solution
