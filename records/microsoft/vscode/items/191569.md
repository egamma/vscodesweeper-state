[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/191569](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/191569)

Keep open: current source still mishandles surrounding indented code when the selection starts at column 1. The discussion confirms that trigger, and no verified fix or older same-symptom canonical was found.

## Change summary

Surround with Do-While should preserve the enclosing indentation and correctly indent the selected statements when their leading whitespace is selected.
