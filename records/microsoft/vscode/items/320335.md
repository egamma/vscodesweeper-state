[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/320335](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/320335)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still describes account-entitlement restrictions as organization management, matching the diagnostic evidence in the discussion. The disabled checkbox is consistent with the returned entitlement, but its explanation is misleading; the separately reported “Enabled” status still needs verification.

## Change summary

Explain why account eligibility disables session sync without falsely attributing the restriction to an organization or implying that sync is enabled.

## Best solution

Make Settings and session-sync status accurately describe account restrictions while preserving entitlement and enterprise-policy enforcement. Document eligibility only to the extent supported by the authoritative entitlement contract, rather than inferring a universal subscription requirement from these reports.
