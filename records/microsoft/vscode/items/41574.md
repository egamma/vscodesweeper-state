# Issue #41574 — Can we get a few more pixels for clicking the cursor at the beginning of a line?

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41574](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/41574)

## Summary

Still reproduces on current main: the margin/content boundary in the mouse hit-test code is precisely at `contentLeft` with no tolerance, so clicking a pixel or two to the left of the first character lands in the margin area instead of placing the cursor at column 0. Team-acknowledged bug with a duplicate (#127025) already consolidated here.

## Change summary

Clicking at the very beginning of a line often misses the text content area and lands in the margin, making it frustratingly hard to place the cursor at column 0.

## Best solution

Widen the content hit-test area by a small tolerance (a few pixels) to the left of `contentLeft` in `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts`, so that clicks near the boundary reliably place the cursor at column 0 rather than falling into the margin. The fix is localized to the `BareHitTestRequest` constructor's `isInMarginArea`/`isInContentArea` logic around line 411.
