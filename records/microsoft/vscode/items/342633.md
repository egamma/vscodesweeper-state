# Issue #342633 — [Worktree Cleanup] I want to view all worktrees, not just those that are more recent than 7 days old

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342633](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342633)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: Worktree Cleanup still displays only cleanup-eligible sessions, and its inactivity selector cannot show worktrees used within seven days. Viewing all managed worktrees separately from cleanup recommendations remains a real gap.

## Change summary

Allow users to inspect and manage all agent-session worktrees, with older inactive worktrees distinguished as cleanup recommendations rather than being the only visible entries.

## Best solution

Separate worktree visibility from cleanup eligibility in the Agents Window management surface, clearly distinguishing recent and protected worktrees from recommended cleanup candidates. Explain the scope of scan and displayed counts without weakening existing cleanup protections.

## Evidence

- **Existing-capability check:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/chat.contribution.ts registers Open Worktree Cleanup through command sessions.chat.manageAgentSessionWorktrees. In src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessionInputBanners/browser/sessionWorktreeCleanupEditor.ts, MINIMUM_AGE_OPTIONS is [7, 15, 30, 60, 90]; there is no all-ages option.
- **Current visibility restriction:** SessionWorktreeCleanupEditor.renderRows() filters worktrees to cleanupState === 'eligible', renders only those entries, and hides the table when none are eligible. Recent, active, and pinned sessions therefore cannot be inspected in this table even when they were included in measurement.
- **Measurement and display have different scopes:** src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessionInputBanners/browser/sessionWorktreeCleanupService.ts measures sessions with worktree-backed workspace folders and counts distinct worktree URIs within each session. _getWorktrees() excludes unmeasured and archived entries, and the editor further restricts visible rows to eligible entries. This explains why scan and visible counts can differ, but does not establish the exact cause of the reporter's 71-versus-28 totals.
- **Intended existing boundary:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342065 describes testing eligible inactive sessions alongside active, pinned, and recent sessions, and requires protected worktrees to remain intact. The request expands the management surface rather than identifying a violation of its existing eligibility rules.
- **Related reports are distinct:** The open https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342635 requests workspace identity in existing rows, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342637 reports rows disappearing after previewing a session. Neither owns the request to inspect all worktrees regardless of inactivity.
- **Broader tracking is not a duplicate:** The open https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336620 discusses visible worktree lifecycle management, but explicitly says it is not intended to replace narrower reports. It provides related design context, not a canonical duplicate for this specific cleanup-view request.
