# Issue #187369 — Option-key navigation between words separated by a non-breaking space on a Mac

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/187369](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/187369)

## Summary

The defect remains on current main: U+00A0 is classified as a regular character, so word navigation treats text joined by non-breaking spaces as one word. Route this confirmed, bounded editor bug to word navigation.

## Change summary

Option+Arrow and Option+Shift+Arrow on macOS skip across multiple words when those words are separated by non-breaking spaces.

## Best solution

Make default editor word movement and selection recognize U+00A0 as a word boundary, with regression coverage for movement in both directions and selection, while preserving existing behavior for ordinary spaces, tabs, and configured separators.
