## Summary

Verified duplicate: the remaining inability to set a breakpoint over an existing gutter decoration is owned by the open GlyphMarginWidget adoption issue.

Consolidate this scenario on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/185814 and complete breakpoint adoption of GlyphMarginWidgets there, preserving automatic breakpoint range tracking while giving breakpoint interactions a dedicated widget.
