# Issue #166024 — Cursor selection scroll down not working when status bar is hidden and in full screen mode

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/166024](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/166024)

## Summary

The bug is plausible — macOS fullscreen with hidden status bar means the editor extends to the very bottom pixel, so drag-selecting downward may never produce a mouse position 'outside' the editor to trigger auto-scroll. A team member could not initially reproduce, and the reporter clarified it requires fullscreen mode, but there has been no further confirmation. Keeping open as it is a valid scenario worth investigating, though the niche conditions and lack of community signal make it low priority.

## Change summary

Drag-selecting text downward does not auto-scroll when the status bar is hidden and VS Code is in macOS fullscreen mode.

## Best solution

Investigate whether the editor's `_getPositionOutsideEditor` in `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseHandler.ts` correctly detects mouse-below-editor when the editor extends to the very bottom of the screen in macOS fullscreen (no status bar, no dock). The `TopBottomDragScrolling` mechanism depends on `e.posy > editorContent.y + editorContent.height` — in fullscreen the mouse may never exceed that boundary. A small tolerance zone or a timer-based fallback when the mouse sits at the absolute bottom edge could fix it.
