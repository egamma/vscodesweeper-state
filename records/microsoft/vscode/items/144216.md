# Issue #144216 — Cannot open menu via shortcut when activity bar is hidden

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/144216](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/144216)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: in compact menu bar mode, when the activity bar is hidden, the menu shortcuts (Alt+letter) stop working because the compact menu lives in the activity bar and becomes inaccessible. A team member acknowledged the issue in 2022 but no fix has landed.

## Change summary

Alt+letter menu shortcuts do nothing when menu bar is set to compact and the activity bar is hidden.

## Best solution

When the activity bar is hidden and menuBarVisibility is compact, either fall back to rendering the menu bar in the title bar area, or ensure mnemonics still trigger the compact menu even when its container is not visible. The fix likely belongs in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/titlebar/menubarControl.ts and/or the layout service that coordinates activity bar visibility with compact menu placement.
