- **Current indentation behavior:** src/vs/editor/common/cursor/cursorTypeOperations.ts routes indent and outdent through ShiftCommand. In src/vs/editor/common/commands/shiftCommand.ts, the useTabStops branch computes a replacement for the leading whitespace; shiftIndent and unshiftIndent return pure tabs when insertSpaces is false. - **Existing-capability check:** src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts registers editor.useTabStops with default true. Setting it to false makes ShiftCommand prepend an indentation unit or remove a leading unit without rewriting the remaining whitespace, preserving alignment in the reported tab-prefix case. However, it also changes general tab-stop insertion/deletion behavior; it is a broader workaround rather than a dedicated alignment-preservation option. No preserveAlignmentSpacesOnIndent registration exists in current editor source.
