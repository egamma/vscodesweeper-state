Keep open while the linked navigation fix is reviewed: the current checkout still routes through the active editor group rather than explicitly targeting the auxiliary editor's group. The reported two-window scenario is sufficient to retest, but the intermittent focus transition was not confirmed through execution.

## Change summary

Selecting an Outline symbol for an auxiliary-window editor sometimes activates the main window without navigating the auxiliary editor.
