## Summary

Keep open in notebook-debugging: multiple reporters confirm that Debug Cell becomes unavailable after debugging, with reports continuing through October 2026. The related context-key investigation is a useful diagnostic lead, but neither a verified fix nor an eligible older canonical was established.

## Change summary

Debug Cell and sometimes Run by Line disappear after a notebook debugging session, and the debug keyboard shortcut also stops responding.

## Best solution

Restore reliable notebook debugging availability after a session finishes and when notebook focus or editor placement changes. Diagnose the context-key boundary between the notebook workbench and Jupyter before choosing the owning implementation; preserve this established report rather than transferring or closing it on an unconfirmed ownership hypothesis.
