# Issue #278262 — It's not obvious you can hide a model in Manage Models

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/278262](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/278262)

## Summary

The discoverability gap remains on current main: visibility controls are deliberately hidden until row hover, focus, or selection. This team-authored UX discussion should remain open under its existing area and discussion labels.

## Change summary

Make model visibility controls in Manage Models discoverable without requiring users to hover over a row.

## Best solution

Have the chat model-management owners decide on a persistent affordance, such as a labeled visibility column or clearer control, while retaining the existing keyboard and context-menu paths.
