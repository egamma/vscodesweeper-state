## Change summary

When a Claude agent-host session is restored, its customizations (agents, plugins and most skills) are not loaded, unlike a freshly created session.

## Best solution

Make restored Claude sessions re-seed and sync the active client's customizations and plugins the same way a newly created session does. The sync lives in the Claude agent's customization path (claudeAgent.ts and the customizations/ folder).
