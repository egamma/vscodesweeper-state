# Issue #343908 — Undo after nesting a pinned session brings it back unpinned and in the other session's group

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343908](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343908)

## Summary

Keep open: Undo recreates the nested session without restoring its original placement, and an open PR already targets this defect. The assigned 1.145.0 milestone independently bars a close proposal.

## Change summary

Undoing the nesting of a pinned session should restore its original pin and custom-group membership rather than inherit the destination session's group.

## Best solution

Review and land https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/344298, confirming that Undo restores both the original pin and group state, including an originally ungrouped session. Preserve ordinary promotion's existing group-inheritance behavior.
