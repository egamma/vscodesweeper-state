# Issue #329456 — Pane padding appears slowly

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329456](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329456)

## Summary

Fresh, team-authored visual bug: with the experimental Modern UI (`workbench.experimental.modernUI`) pane-inset padding enabled, toggling the sidebar shows the new padding animate/grow in rather than appear instantly — reporter attached a video repro, and no evidence yet points to a specific fix.

## Change summary

Toggling the side pane makes the (Modern UI) pane content padding visibly animate/grow in instead of appearing immediately.

## Best solution

Investigate whether the sidebar/grid resize animation (`src/vs/base/browser/ui/splitview/paneview.css` `.monaco-pane-view.animated` width/height transitions, or the workbench grid's own resize transition) is interacting with the new inset rules in `src/vs/workbench/contrib/styleOverrides/browser/media/padding.css`, likely by ensuring the padding/inset rules aren't tied to a size that itself animates.
