# Issue #147394 — Unable to drag views to reorder or move to different pane

## Summary

iPad/iOS drag-and-drop for views is a known platform limitation with no evidence of a fix on main. The issue is on the On Deck milestone and still appears valid for the web/iPad surface, though it lacks recent confirmation on current builds.

## Change summary

Dragging views to reorder or move to different panes does not work on iPad (Safari) with trackpad or mouse input.

## Best solution
