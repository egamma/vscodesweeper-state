## Evidence

- **grouping entry point:** extensions/copilot/src/extension/prompt/node/defaultIntentRequestHandler.ts getAvailableTools() only skips ToolGrouping when `endpoint.supportsToolSearch` is true; for BYOK family names this should normally evaluate false (extensions/copilot/src/platform/endpoint/common/chatModelCapabilities.ts modelSupportsToolSearch only matches specific GPT/Claude/Kimi family prefixes), so this isn't a confirmed cause.
- **no-fallback categorization/embeddings path:** extensions/copilot/src/extension/tools/common/virtualTools/virtualToolGrouper.ts _generateEmbeddingBasedGroups explicitly rethrows on embeddings failure ('Let the error bubble up as requested - no fallback'), and addGroups calls the hosted 'copilot-utility-small' endpoint for group descriptions — but it's unclear whether such a throw would actually result in the *raw, ungrouped* tool list being sent (matching the reporter's provider-side 128-tool error) or would abort the turn with a different error.
- **maintainer precedent:** On the related, closed-as-duplicate https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/290356, connor4312 (owner of virtualTools telemetry) stated: 'This is fixed with virtual tools, however if the embeddings service has an outage then groupings may not be generated' — the same general failure mode, but not confirmed as BYOK-specific.
- **corroboration:** A second reporter (Slion) independently hit the same symptom with local LLMs after installing several extensions, also linking HARD_TOOL_LIMIT in configurationService.ts.
