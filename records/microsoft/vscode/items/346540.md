# Issue #346540 — Copilot Chat model picker shows "Upgrade" for Claude/GPT models despite active Copilot Max (sku max_monthly_subscriber_quota)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346540](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346540)

## Summary

Keep open: the current picker still misclassifies missing Max models as upgrades, but the account-specific loss of model access remains unconfirmed. Model discovery has evolved since the report; the supplied steps and token warning suffice to guide a retest.

## Change summary

Copilot Max subscribers lose access to included models and receive misleading upgrade prompts.

## Best solution

Restore discovery and selection of models included in the authenticated Max account, and ensure unavailable models never advertise an upgrade that cannot unlock them. Diagnose the catalog/authentication failure rather than treating a badge-only change as resolution.
