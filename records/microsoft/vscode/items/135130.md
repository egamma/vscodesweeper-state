# Issue #135130 — Links on extension pages don't work in safari or Firefox

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/135130](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/135130)

## Summary

Upstream browser bug in Safari and Firefox popup-blocking behavior within iframes/webviews. Team-authored, already correctly labeled as upstream, and the linked browser bugs remain open. Stays open to track resolution.

## Change summary

Links on extension pages in vscode.dev don't open in Safari (blocked silently) or Firefox (blocked with notification) due to browser popup-blocking policies in iframes.

## Best solution

Monitor the upstream WebKit bug (https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=98951) and Firefox bug (https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=1736717) for resolution. If browsers don't fix this, a workaround in the webview link-handling layer (e.g. user-gesture-preserving postMessage flow) could be explored, but that's a non-trivial change to the webview architecture.
