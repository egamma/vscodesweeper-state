- **Source-traced launch asymmetry:** In abstractTaskService.ts:919–939, _findWorkspaceTasks() collects matching configured tasks in traversal order. quickResolve() at lines 1971–1996 selects foundTasks[0] without sorting; _executeTask() around lines 2076–2088 re-resolves user-launched tasks through getTask(), which applies the invalid sort. This establishes the divergent resolution paths without executing the checkout.
- **Broader resolver behavior:** In abstractTaskService.ts, _createResolver() builds fullResolve lookup maps with data.label.set(task._label, task), so later matching labels overwrite earlier entries. Correcting getTask's comparator alone does not establish consistent semantics for every fallback or cross-scope collision raised in the report.
