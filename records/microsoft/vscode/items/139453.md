# Issue #139453 — iPad: Can't search for settings

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139453](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139453)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the `!platform.isIOS` guard in settingsEditor2.ts unconditionally prevents auto-focusing the search input on iPad, even when triggered via a hardware keyboard shortcut (Cmd+,). The nuanced fix — distinguishing hardware-keyboard activation from touch taps — was never implemented.

## Change summary

On iPad with a hardware keyboard, pressing Cmd+, to open settings does not auto-focus the search input, unlike desktop behavior.

## Best solution

Detect whether settings were opened via a keybinding (hardware keyboard) vs. a touch tap, and only suppress auto-focus for the touch case. The guard at lines 581 and 627 of `src/vs/workbench/contrib/preferences/browser/settingsEditor2.ts` needs refinement rather than blanket iOS suppression.
