## Evidence

- **Confirmed upstream reproduction:** The final supplied comment on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/247522 reports working mouse echo in Chrome and vscode.dev, and a standalone Electron reproduction that works with Electron 29.4.6 but fails with modern Electron. The same findings are documented at https://github.com/electron/electron/issues/42945#issuecomment-5575765450.
- **Native hit-testing defect:** https://github.com/electron/electron/issues/42945 is open and independently identifies Electron 30.0.0-beta.8 and https://github.com/electron/electron/pull/41256 as the regression boundary. The full-window contentView container intercepts Windows accessibility hit tests before they reach the underlying WebContentsView.
- **Unmerged fix candidate:** https://github.com/electron/electron/pull/53460 is open with no merge commit. Its diff introduces ContentViewTargeterDelegate in shell/browser/native_window.cc and installs it in NativeWindowViews::SetContentView, allowing hit tests to reach web content where no child view covers the point. The author reports Linux hit-test verification but outstanding Windows NVDA verification at https://github.com/electron/electron/pull/53460#issuecomment-5807487793.
- **Current checkout and unsuccessful workaround:** The checkout's .npmrc targets Electron 43.7.3; dependency history includes bump commit 0d9ade07b60, which is not a behavioral fix. src/main.ts configures Chromium feature switches, but the supplied discussion reports that --enable-features did not restore mouse echo. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/320877 only proposed persistent feature-switch configuration and is closed without merging.
