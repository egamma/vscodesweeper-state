Keep open as the accepted background/cloud session lifecycle request. Existing archive controls and delegation handling cover parts of it, but the related discussion identifies transition timing and parent-chat discoverability that are not demonstrably resolved.

## Change summary

Improve archive and parent-conversation lifecycle handling when delegating chats to background or cloud agents.

## Best solution

Define the remaining lifecycle expectations for successful, cancelled, and failed delegation, including when the sidebar resets and how the parent conversation remains discoverable. Preserve reversible archiving and distinguish it from deleting session artifacts.
