## Summary

Keep open for the unresolved claim that identical content is detected differently depending on the numeric filename. Current detection includes configurable history weighting and two detection models; the supplied steps suffice to retest, but source inspection does not confirm that filename-specific discrepancy.

## Change summary

Investigate inconsistent JavaScript auto-detection across extensionless numeric filenames with identical content.

Preserve intentional extensionless-file detection and history weighting; the separately tracked icon problem does not resolve this remaining question.
