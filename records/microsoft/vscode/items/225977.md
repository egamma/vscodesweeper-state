# Issue #225977 — All content read as expanded in walkthrough for SR users

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/225977](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/225977)

## Summary

Keep open: walkthrough descriptions and interactive controls are still nested inside the expansion button, and activating an expanded step does not collapse it. The earlier fix predates the reopening and does not resolve the clarified accessibility requirements.

## Change summary

Make walkthrough steps keyboard-expandable and collapsible, with independently labeled completion controls and readable content outside the expansion control.

## Best solution

Deliver the accordion behavior accepted in the discussion: independently operable expansion and completion controls, with expanded content following the header in reading order rather than nested inside its button. Verify the result with NVDA and other supported screen readers, not only through an Accessible View workaround.
