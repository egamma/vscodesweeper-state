# Issue #164484 — Investigate removing cursor hit testing

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/164484](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/164484)

## Summary

The investigation remains necessary: current main still performs cursor-specific hit testing, exports last-rendered cursor geometry, and hard-codes the hit width to 2. This is also a protected team-authored exploration item, so it should remain open until the editor owners decide whether the workaround can be removed.

## Change summary

Investigate eliminating cursor-specific pointer hit testing and its dependence on potentially stale render data.

## Best solution

Determine whether the historical browser hit-testing workaround is still required; then either remove the cursor render-data coupling or make its geometry accurate, while preserving correct selection behavior across cursor styles and widths.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewCursors/viewCursor.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewCursors/viewCursors.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/view.ts`
- **Validation:** Exercise clicking directly on selections and cursors with line, thin-line, block, and underline styles, including configured cursor widths 0 and 5 and multiple cursors; repeated clicks must consistently place the pointer and clear the selection without regressions.
- **Behavior:**

  > Pointer targeting in the editor must remain accurate when clicking on or near a rendered cursor, without relying on incorrect or stale cursor geometry. Repeated clicks inside a selected range must consistently place the cursor and clear the selection. Acceptance should cover all cursor styles, default and wide line cursors, and multiple cursors.
- **Trace:**

  > The current pointer path in src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts invokes `MouseTargetFactory._hitTestViewCursor`, which reads `PointerHandlerLastRenderData.lastViewCursorsRenderData`. That data originates in `ViewCursor.render` and is accumulated by `ViewCursors.render`, then exposed through the helper in src/vs/editor/browser/view.ts. `ViewCursor.render` reports a hard-coded width of 2 despite having the computed `_renderData.width`; the coordinate hit test therefore differs from the painted cursor and may consume stale geometry between render and input handling. The workaround's comment attributes its coordinate branch to historical Edge DOM hit-testing behavior, but source inspection alone cannot establish whether current supported browsers still need it. Preserve ordinary view-line targeting, text-area targeting, cursor rendering, and the behavior tracked by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147105.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Is the historical Edge cursor-target workaround still required by any currently supported browser or editor embedding?
  - Should cursor-specific hit testing be removed entirely, or retained with accurate current-render geometry?
  - What browser-level or integration coverage can reliably detect stale render-data targeting and the intermittent selection behavior from https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147105?

## Evidence

- **current source:** src/vs/editor/browser/controller/mouseTarget.ts still calls `_hitTestViewCursor` before view-line hit testing; that method consumes `lastViewCursorsRenderData` for both DOM-target and coordinate-based cursor checks.
- **incorrect geometry remains:** src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewCursors/viewCursor.ts still returns `width: 2` from `ViewCursor.render`, even though `_renderData.width` is computed from the configured cursor style and width.
- **render-data coupling:** src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewCursors/viewCursors.ts stores the most recent cursor render results, and src/vs/editor/browser/view.ts passes them through `PointerHandlerLastRenderData` to the mouse-target factory.
- **related behavior:** The issue identifies https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/147105 as a possible symptom; that issue remains open and reports intermittent incorrect selection clearing, with wider cursors increasing its frequency.
- **originating PR:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/159325 was merged for a separate misplaced-cursor fix; its discussion identified the hard-coded width and explicitly created this issue to track the remaining investigation rather than resolving it.
- **protected exploration:** The issue was authored by a repository member and is explicitly framed as an investigation. Team-authored exploration artifacts are kept open until their owners resolve or abandon the investigation.
