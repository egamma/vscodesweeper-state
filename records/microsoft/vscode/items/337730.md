## Summary

Keep open for a focused interaction-design decision. Current main partially addresses navigation separation and Customizations status indication, but still shares header styling between shortcuts and collapsible groups; no issue-referencing fix or verified canonical establishes completion of this team-authored report.

Make Automations and Customizations clearly distinguishable from collapsible session groups, with understandable destination and status affordances.
