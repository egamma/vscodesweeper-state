## Summary

Fresh dogfooding feedback on the still-in-development Automations feature (gated by chat.automations.enabled): imported automations default to disabled but the disabled state is visually easy to miss on the card, risking accidental non-activation. Real UX concern worth routing to the feature owners, not something to close.

## Change summary
