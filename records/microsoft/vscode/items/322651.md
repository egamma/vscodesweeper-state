# Issue #322651 — Org-managed policy distribution and audit

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/322651](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/322651)

## Summary

Keep open: current main already provides enterprise-managed settings distribution and override diagnostics, but those capabilities do not establish the requested versioned policy packs and cross-repository audit trail. No verified canonical owns the full request.

## Change summary

Provide centrally distributed, versioned organization policy packs with visible overrides and auditing of agent changes and policy decisions.

## Best solution

Define the remaining policy-version identity and audit requirements against the existing managed-settings pipeline, rather than introducing a parallel distribution system. Keep runtime enforcement and decision reporting in the shared runtime contract, with VS Code presenting the relevant policy provenance.
