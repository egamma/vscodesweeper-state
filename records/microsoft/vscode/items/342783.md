However, the intermittent occurrence has no identified trigger or shareable provider setup, so request the missing reproduction inputs rather than infer a fix or upstream ownership.

## Change summary

Prevent idle provider lifecycle churn from temporarily removing available canvases or unnecessarily reloading open canvases.

## Best solution

Identify the operation repeatedly initializing providers, then ensure background rediscovery does not publish incomplete canvas availability as a completed registry update. Preserve genuine provider removals; investigate open-panel reloads separately because this capture does not establish them.
