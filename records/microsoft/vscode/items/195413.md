# Issue #195413 — indicate the number of available completions in inline completions accessible view aria label

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/195413](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/195413)

## Summary

Keep open: the inline completions Accessible View still exposes neither the available completion count nor the current index. The discussion establishes demand, but announcement placement and the cost of querying alternatives remain product decisions.

## Change summary

Expose the available inline completion count and current position to screen reader users in Accessible View.

## Best solution

Provide discoverable completion-position information in the inline completions Accessible View without delaying suggestion content or adding unnecessary provider requests. Settle whether counts are fetched on entry or only through explicit interaction, and whether they belong in the ARIA label or after the content.

- **Trace:**

  > InlineCompletionsAccessibleViewContentProvider in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/inlineCompletionsAccessibleView.ts supplies only suggestion text or an inline-edit patch; navigation awaits model.next/previous indirectly and then fires onDidChangeContent. - **Open decisions:**
