Detailed, plausible report of a remote-AHP reconnect/reconciliation gap after laptop sleep, but it's a same-day single report with no captured logs and no commenter confirmation — asking for logs/repro confirmation before routing is the right next step.

## Change summary

After a laptop sleeps mid-turn and reconnects to a remote AHP host session, the Agents window shows the turn as stuck even though it completed and its side effects landed remotely; only a full window reload reveals the finished turn.
