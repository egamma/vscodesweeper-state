- **Conditional five-line margin:** src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLines/viewLines.ts:720–739 computes surroundingLines as Math.max(cursorSurroundingLines, stickyScrollEnabled ? maxNumberStickyLines : 0), then applies it to cursor-reveal padding. editorOptions.ts:3200 defaults Sticky Scroll to enabled with maxLineCount 5. This explains values 0–4 behaving alike under that configuration, but not an unconditional five-line minimum.
