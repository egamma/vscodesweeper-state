## Change summary

Externally formatting a saved notebook updates its disk contents but leaves the open notebook editor displaying the previous cell source.

## Best solution

Saved, clean notebooks should reflect external cell-source changes without requiring reopening, while unsaved local edits remain protected. Reproduce the Black scenario and identify whether file-change delivery, working-copy state, or notebook deserialization prevents the refresh.
