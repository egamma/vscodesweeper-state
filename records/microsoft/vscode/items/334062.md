## Evidence

- **Confirmed trigger and diagnosis:** The report isolates failure to gtk-modules=appmenu-gtk-module, including with extensions disabled and a clean profile. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/334062#issuecomment-6068399440 identifies Electron's GTK-module pinning change as unloading the module while a pending GIO callback still references its code.
- **Verified upstream reproduction:** https://github.com/electron/electron/issues/54782 is open and documents a minimal BrowserWindow reproduction under Ubuntu/Xvfb: Electron 42.9.3 succeeds and 42.10.0 crashes, with boundary versions tested three times. Removing the GTK module setting prevents the crash; an AppMenu registrar is not required.
- **Current checkout does not prove resolution:** .npmrc targets Electron 43.7.7, and build/lib/electronVersion.ts:getElectronVersion reads that target for runtime selection. The upstream report also reproduces the defect in 43.7.9; a newer major-version target is therefore not evidence of a fix. No runtime execution was performed in this read-only checkout.
