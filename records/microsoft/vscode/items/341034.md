## Change summary

After one window signs in to GitHub, every other window shows the Copilot sign-in modal, and each of those sign-ins repeats the cycle in the rest.

## Best solution

Then make the replacement handling in agentHostAuth.ts silent, using the existing rejected-session and token-cache logic.
