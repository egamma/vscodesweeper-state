# Issue #247340 — Some active breakpoints can be removed only from the Breakpoints menu

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/247340](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/247340)

## Summary

The original missing-marker defect remains present on current main for breakpoint variants that lack a combined breakpoint-and-stack-frame glyph. Keep this open in the debug area; the later Jupyter report appears to describe a separate breakpoint synchronization problem.

## Change summary

Keep an active breakpoint visible and operable in the editor gutter when its line is also the current instruction pointer.

## Best solution

Ensure every applicable active line-breakpoint variant retains its breakpoint identity when combined with the current-stack-frame decoration, without adding another gutter lane. Separately triage the Jupyter case where a removed breakpoint still stops execution, because that indicates adapter synchronization rather than glyph composition.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/media/debug.contribution.css`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/breakpointEditorContribution.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/test/browser/callStack.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/test/browser/breakpoints.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Add or identify a component-level screenshot check that pauses on each supported active line-breakpoint variant and verifies that the combined gutter glyph preserves both breakpoint and stack-frame information; also manually confirm that clicking the combined glyph toggles the breakpoint.
- **Behavior:**

  > When execution pauses on a line containing an active breakpoint, the editor gutter continues to communicate both the current instruction position and the breakpoint's presence and type. The combined glyph remains clickable through the normal breakpoint interaction, while lines without a breakpoint and breakpoints away from the paused line remain unchanged. The Jupyter-specific case where execution stops after removal is outside this visual fix and requires separate reproduction.
- **Trace:**

  > BreakpointEditorContribution creates the breakpoint decoration in src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/breakpointEditorContribution.ts, while CallStackEditorContribution creates the current-frame decoration in src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/callStackEditorContribution.ts. Both use GlyphMarginLane.Right and zIndex 9999, so GlyphMarginOverlay in src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/glyphMargin/glyphMargin.ts combines their classes. The combined selectors in src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/media/debug.contribution.css cover regular and conditional breakpoints but not every active icon returned by getBreakpointMessageAndIcon. This is the same composition mechanism discussed in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/180439 and introduced by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/180448. Preserve the shared-lane behavior and existing standalone glyphs; do not broaden this work into debug-adapter breakpoint synchronization.

## Evidence

- **current source:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/callStackEditorContribution.ts and src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/breakpointEditorContribution.ts place stack-frame and breakpoint decorations in GlyphMarginLane.Right at zIndex 9999; src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/glyphMargin/glyphMargin.ts combines their class names into one glyph.
- **incomplete combined states:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/debug/browser/media/debug.contribution.css defines combined stack-frame rendering only for regular and conditional breakpoint classes, while debugIcons.ts and getBreakpointMessageAndIcon in breakpointsView.ts expose additional active variants including log, pending, unsupported, and unverified breakpoints.
- **established expectation:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/85148 documents the same observable failure and records the maintainer expectation that a line containing a breakpoint should retain a breakpoint dot even when it is the paused line.
- **glyph composition infrastructure:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/180448 merged commit 2c10a8e083523d11fbf2c8375ac381c40af6682b to combine breakpoint and debug-frame decorations, but https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/180439 explicitly notes that the combined decoration did not cover every breakpoint type.
