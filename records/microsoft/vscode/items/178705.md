## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/views/explorerView.ts`, `src/vs/base/browser/ui/tree/asyncDataTree.ts`
- **Validation:** In Code OSS, select and focus a non-root Files Explorer row, hide the primary sidebar with workbench.action.toggleSidebarVisibility, reopen it with workbench.files.action.focusFilesExplorer, and verify the same row remains selected and receives keyboard focus; repeat while an Explorer refresh is in flight.
- **Behavior:**

  > The Files Explorer must retain its selected and focused row when the primary sidebar is hidden and subsequently reopened and focused. Reopening must place keyboard focus on that row rather than only on the tree container. Existing explorer.autoReveal behavior for active editors must remain unchanged. Acceptance is the supplied toggle/focus keybinding sequence preserving the row both normally and during an overlapping Explorer refresh.
- **Trace:**

  > ExplorerView in src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/views/explorerView.ts reacts to onDidChangeBodyVisibility by awaiting setTreeInput() and then selecting the active file. setTreeInput() captures the current AsyncDataTree view state, while AsyncDataTree.getViewState() and setInput() in src/vs/base/browser/ui/tree/asyncDataTree.ts serialize and restore focus and selection identities. Commit https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/1bd2fc7937140bf342459ff895a87dff9b219eec added serialization for concurrent setTreeInput calls after a separate empty-state race, making current runtime reproduction the uncertainty boundary. Keep the investigation within Explorer visibility/refresh state; do not alter global tree focus semantics or active-file auto-reveal unless reproduction shows those paths are responsible.

## Evidence

- **confirmed reproduction:** A maintainer confirmed that hiding and revealing the Explorer tree loses its selection and labeled the report as a bug: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/178705#issuecomment-1503957539.
