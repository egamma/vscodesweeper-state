# Issue #150767 — Explorer -> Open Editors scroll issues

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/150767](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/150767)

## Summary

The scrollbar defects remain traceable on current main: horizontal-scrolling lists always reserve vertical space, while Open Editors sizes its body to the rows alone. The accepted `bug` should remain open, with the shared-list impact assessed before implementation.

## Change summary

Prevent unnecessary vertical scrolling and keep the final Open Editors row unobscured when horizontal scrolling is enabled.

## Best solution

Make the Open Editors viewport reserve interactive space for a horizontal scrollbar only when horizontal overflow exists, while ensuring the final row remains fully visible and selectable. Preserve horizontal scrolling behavior for other workbench lists.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/views/openEditorsView.ts`, `src/vs/base/browser/ui/list/listView.ts`, `src/vs/base/test/browser/ui/list/listView.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/test/browser/openEditorsView.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Add list and Open Editors tests covering a row set that exactly fits the pane: enabling horizontal scrolling without horizontal overflow must not create vertical overflow, and with real horizontal overflow the final row must remain fully visible and selectable at the bottom.
- **Behavior:**

  > When Open Editors rows fit within the available body height, no vertical scrollbar appears merely because horizontal scrolling is enabled. If a long path creates genuine horizontal overflow, the horizontal scrollbar must not cover the final row's visible or clickable area. Long labels remain horizontally scrollable, and ordinary vertical overflow continues to scroll normally.
- **Trace:**

  > OpenEditorsView lays out its WorkbenchList in `layoutBody` and calculates body constraints in `getMinExpandedBodySize` and `getMaxExpandedBodySize` from row count times `OpenEditorsDelegate.ITEM_HEIGHT`. The underlying `ListView.scrollHeight` unconditionally adds 10 pixels whenever horizontal scrolling is enabled, even when no horizontal overflow exists; this makes an exactly fitted Open Editors body vertically scrollable. The same shared list behavior places the horizontal scrollbar over the viewport while relying on extra scroll height to expose the final row, matching the overlap described here and in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/67441. Planning must enumerate other `ListView` consumers before changing shared scrollbar semantics; behavior of lists without horizontal scrolling and lists with genuine vertical overflow must not change.

## Evidence

- **current source:** src/vs/base/browser/ui/list/listView.ts:1147-1149 adds 10 pixels to `scrollHeight` whenever `horizontalScrolling` is enabled, regardless of whether the content currently overflows horizontally.
- **Open Editors sizing:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/files/browser/views/openEditorsView.ts:598-623 computes minimum and maximum body sizes solely as the number of rows multiplied by `OpenEditorsDelegate.ITEM_HEIGHT`; it does not account for the list's additional horizontal-scrollbar space.
- **earlier diagnosis:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/67441 documents the same interaction: reserving scrollbar space creates vertical overflow, while the horizontal scrollbar can still obscure the final Open Editors row. That issue is closed and therefore cannot serve as a duplicate canonical.
