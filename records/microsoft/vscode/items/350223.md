# Issue #350223 — Agents window: pinning a session moves it out of its group

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350223](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/350223)

## Summary

Pinning currently overrides group placement by an explicit, documented contract, without deleting group membership. Keep this team-authored report open for an owner decision about preserving visible grouping while pinned rather than closing it as designed.

## Change summary

Allow pinning a session without moving its sidebar row out of its group.

## Best solution

Have the sessions-list owner decide how pinning should coexist with visible group membership. Preserve saved membership and unpin restoration regardless of the chosen presentation.
