# Issue #250072 — Minimap overlaps content by the width of the vertical scrollbar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250072](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250072)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the minimap overlaps content by the vertical scrollbar width due to an off-by-one in the layout calculation. Confirmed by a team member, and the community PR to fix it was closed without merging.

## Change summary

When the minimap is on the right side, it visually overlaps the editor content area by the width of the vertical scrollbar.

## Best solution

Fix the layout math in `EditorLayoutInfoComputer.compute()` in `src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts`: the minimap is positioned at `outerWidth - minimapWidth - verticalScrollbarWidth` but `contentWidth` is `remainingWidth - minimapWidth`, which doesn't account for the scrollbar gap — the content extends under the minimap by `verticalScrollbarWidth` pixels. The content width or the minimap position needs adjusting so they don't overlap.
