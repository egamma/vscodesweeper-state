# Issue #347602 — Chat: "This is taking a little longer than usual" gives no indication of what the agent is stuck on

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347602](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347602)

## Summary

The contextual stall-status request remains unmet on current main. The foreground-tool fix prevents misleading warnings during active tools, but the remaining inactivity message still provides neither a wait reason nor elapsed time.

## Change summary

Show the known waiting activity and elapsed waiting time when an Agent-mode response stops producing progress.

## Best solution

Provide evidence-backed waiting context and elapsed time in the chat progress footer, explicitly distinguishing unknown activity from a confirmed model, tool, or retry wait. This needs a product and provider-data contract decision rather than diagnosing a particular service stall.
