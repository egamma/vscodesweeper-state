# Issue #343087 — Automations: Support automations flagging warnings on themselves

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343087](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343087)

## Summary

Keep open: current automation tools and cards do not expose an automation-authored attention warning. Existing enablement controls cover disabling schedules, but not the central request; this team-authored proposal needs a decision on warning lifecycle and suspension behavior.

## Change summary

Let an automation flag itself as needing attention on its card, potentially suspending subsequent scheduled runs.

## Best solution

Provide an agent-reportable attention state surfaced on the owning automation card, distinct from execution failure and provider availability. Establish how warnings are cleared and whether reporting one can suspend scheduling before implementing the tool and provider-backed state.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/automations/automation.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/automations/automationService.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/automations/browser/automationTools.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/automations/browser/providerAutomationService.ts`, `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/automationsView.ts`
- **Validation:** Add focused automation-tool and card-rendering tests showing that a run can report attention for its owning automation and that the card displays an accessible warning. Verify clearing and persistence against the agreed lifecycle, and verify scheduling changes only under the agreed suspension behavior while manual execution remains consistent with the existing contract.
- **Behavior:**

  > An automation that detects a problem can explicitly mark itself as needing human attention, and its card makes that condition discoverable without opening run history. The indication must be distinguishable from a failed run or an unavailable provider and understandable without relying on the symbol alone. Acceptance checks must cover reporting, displaying, and clearing the warning, plus any agreed effect on future scheduled execution.
- **Trace:**

  > The current agent-facing surface is in src/vs/sessions/contrib/automations/browser/automationTools.ts; configuration supports enabled but no attention warning. IAutomationDescriptor and IUpdateAutomationOptions in the shared automations contracts contain no dedicated warning state, while IAutomationRun has separate execution and input-needed state. AutomationsCards.updateCard and updateCardRunState in automationsView.ts do not consume an automation-authored warning. ProviderAutomationService routes definition operations, and AUTOMATIONS.md places durable state and execution authority with the owning provider or host, not the card renderer. The remaining uncertainty is the warning's lifecycle and its relationship to scheduling; do not assume reporting a warning cancels an active run or disables the entire Automations feature. Request: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343087.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Does an attention warning persist until explicitly acknowledged or cleared, or does a subsequent successful run clear it automatically?
  - Is reporting attention informational only, or may the automation also suspend future scheduled runs?
