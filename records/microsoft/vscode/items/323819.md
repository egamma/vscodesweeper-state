## Evidence

Reproduction status reflects these reports, not an executed current-main reproduction.
- **Terminal source inspection:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/common/terminalConfiguration.ts:329-332 registers terminal.integrated.scrollback with a default of 1000. src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/xterm/xtermTerminal.ts:246-252 passes the configured or overridden scrollback to xterm, and line 605 updates it.
