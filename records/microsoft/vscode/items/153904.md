## Summary

Keep open: current source still allows a period query to match colons and spaces, although it now suppresses highlighting of those nonidentical separators. The linked PR was closed without merging, and the maintainer's suggestion to change period matching remains unsettled.

## Change summary

Searching punctuation in the Command Palette unexpectedly matches other separators and was reported to shift label text as additional periods are entered.
