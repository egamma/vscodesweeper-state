Keep open for MCP management review: the reporter identified overlapping configurations and confirmed that removing them stopped the prompts. Current same-name collision handling mitigates duplication, but does not establish that the full cross-source identity and prompt-clarity concern is resolved.

## Change summary

Reduce redundant MCP startup and trust prompts when extension-provided servers overlap with manually configured or Cursor-discovered servers.

## Best solution

Retain existing same-name collision suppression and distinguish configuration sources clearly when interaction is required. The MCP owner should determine whether any additional cross-source deduplication is appropriate when launch arguments differ, rather than treating those definitions as interchangeable automatically.
