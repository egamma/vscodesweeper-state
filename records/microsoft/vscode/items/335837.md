[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335837](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/335837)

## Summary

The Agents Window settings still lack independent bounded and wordWrapColumn modes. Keep this team-authored follow-up open: the original implementation deliberately specified a three-value override, so expanding its contract needs an owner decision rather than a judgment close.

## Change summary

## Best solution
