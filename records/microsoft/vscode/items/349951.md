[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349951](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349951)

## Summary

Keep open for a quieter comparison-discovery treatment. Current source deliberately gates the footer on configuration and persisted discovery state, which can explain different windows, but does not resolve the concern about its prominence; this team-authored issue needs a design decision rather than a judgment close.

Reduce the prominence of the model-comparison discovery button and clarify when it appears.
