## Change summary

Opening a canvas creates an empty chat that appears unread despite having no conversation to read.

## Best solution

Ensure opening a canvas without sending a message does not create misleading unread-chat attention. Investigate canvas-first session graduation and initial read state while preserving genuine unread messages; draft-session support is a design lead, not an established fix.
