# Issue #153722 — Provide speech feedback when commenting or uncommenting a line.

## Change summary

Automatically announce the resulting line after commenting or uncommenting it, without requiring a separate screen-reader read-line command.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/contrib/comment/browser/comment.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/textArea/textAreaEditContext.ts`, `src/vs/editor/browser/controller/editContext/native/screenReaderSupport.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/accessibility/browser/accessibilityConfiguration.ts`
- **Validation:** With screen-reader optimized mode enabled, place the caret on a JavaScript line and invoke editor.action.commentLine twice: verify the resulting commented and uncommented line is spoken without a read-line command, duplicate speech or focus movement. Repeat with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on macOS, and check the agreed multi-line/multiple-cursor behavior and unchanged editing and undo semantics.
- **Behavior:**

  > A screen-reader user can toggle a single line comment and hear the resulting line without issuing a separate read-line command. The discussion accepts line content alone rather than a mandatory 'commented line' prefix. Feedback must not move focus, alter the selection or undo behavior, or cause duplicate announcements. Acceptance includes commenting and uncommenting with supported screen readers and an explicit expectation for multi-line selections and multiple cursors.
- **Trace:**

  > CommentLineAction.run in src/vs/editor/contrib/comment/browser/comment.ts is shared by toggle, add and remove line-comment commands and executes one LineCommentCommand per normalized selection without dedicated speech feedback. TextAreaEditContext refreshes the accessible textarea, while native ScreenReaderSupport delegates content updates to its simple or rich renderer; these updates are not proof of automatic speech, so the first planning step is to observe the exact action with supported screen readers. No matching comment-action signal or setting was found in the accessibility configuration and signal registrations. Preserve language-specific comment edits, selection handling and undo grouping. The original thread settles single-line wording as resulting line content; navigation-time comment detection discussed later, and in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/226092, is separate work rather than a prerequisite.
- **Open decisions:**
  - For multi-line selections or multiple cursors, should post-action feedback read only the primary resulting line or provide a concise summary of all affected lines?
