# Issue #272109 — Highlighting text right to left(⬅️) and wrapping around parens or brackets, should put the cursor to the most left

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272109](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272109)

## Summary

Keep open under discussion: automatic surrounding still normalizes the selection to a right-hand active endpoint, and editor.autoSurround does not configure that behavior. No verified canonical owns this request; preserving selection direction and adopting DrRacket's always-left behavior are distinct product choices.

## Change summary

Allow bracket surrounding to preserve the selection's active endpoint, or support left-side cursor placement for Lisp-style editing.

## Best solution

Decide whether automatic surrounding should preserve selection direction generally or offer language-specific left-side placement. Resolve that editing contract in the editor's surrounding-selection behavior without conflating it with bidirectional text support.
