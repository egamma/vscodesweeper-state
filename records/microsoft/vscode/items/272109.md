# Issue #272109 — Highlighting text right to left(⬅️) and wrapping around parens or brackets, should put the cursor to the most left

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272109](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272109)

## Summary

Valid request: after surround-selection with a bracket/paren, the cursor is always placed at the right end regardless of selection direction. The user asks that cursor placement respect the original selection direction (left-to-right vs right-to-left). No existing setting or duplicate covers this.

## Change summary

When text is selected right-to-left and then wrapped with brackets/parens, the cursor should be placed at the left side of the selection, preserving the original selection direction.

## Best solution

Modify `SurroundSelectionCommand.computeCursorState()` in `src/vs/editor/common/commands/surroundSelectionCommand.ts` to check the original selection direction (`this._range.getDirection()`) and swap anchor/position accordingly, so the resulting selection preserves the user's selection direction after wrapping.
