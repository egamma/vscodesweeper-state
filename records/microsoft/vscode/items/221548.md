# Issue #221548 — Improve HTML writing experience with Emmet

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221548](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221548)

## Summary

Keep open: current Emmet settings offer broad suppression, but do not cover selective suppression of common words and punctuation while retaining useful HTML abbreviations. The request is already routed to Emmet; neither the closed dot-abbreviation report nor the related open reports owns its full scope.

## Change summary

Allow ordinary HTML prose to avoid unwanted Emmet expansions through selective abbreviation controls or context-sensitive suggestions.

## Best solution

Provide a supported way in the built-in Emmet integration to suppress unwanted prose-like abbreviations without disabling useful expansions generally. The Emmet owner should settle whether that behavior is explicit customization or contextual filtering, preserving deliberate abbreviation expansion.

## Evidence

- **Requested scope:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/221548 explicitly requests selective suppression of words and punctuation, with contextual HTML-parent filtering as an ideal outcome. Its feature-request template and proposed new controls describe an enhancement rather than an established contract being violated.
- **Existing capabilities are broader workarounds:** extensions/emmet/package.json and package.nls.json register emmet.showExpandedAbbreviation, emmet.excludeLanguages, emmet.showAbbreviationSuggestions, emmet.triggerExpansionOnTab, and emmet.extensionsPath. Disabling expanded suggestions or excluding HTML suppresses useful abbreviations too; the registered controls do not expose a selective abbreviation denylist or a common-abbreviation switch.
- **Current completion path:** extensions/emmet/src/defaultCompletionProvider.ts, provideCompletionItemsInternal, extracts and validates abbreviations and passes helper.doComplete results into completion items. Its symbol-based noise filtering is restricted to JavaScript/TypeScript variants. extensions/emmet/src/abbreviationActions.ts, isValidLocationForEmmetAbbreviation, checks markup boundaries but does not implement the requested common-word or option-parent filtering.
- **Closed precedent does not resolve this enhancement:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/87985 is closed. In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/87985#issuecomment-588502899 the maintainer acknowledged the absence of customization to remove one expansion; https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/87985#issuecomment-591123303 explains that dot expansion follows Emmet behavior. That decision does not reject the full configurable and contextual enhancement requested here, and a closed issue cannot be a duplicate canonical.
- **Canonical search:** Open-issue searches for Emmet prose, suggestions, and disabling abbreviations found related reports but no verified full-scope canonical. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/51537 is open but concerns Emmet intruding into JavaScript portions of JSX/TSX/PHP, not ordinary HTML prose. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/227444 is open but newer and limited to dot expansion after quotes, so it cannot absorb this older, broader re-reviewed request.
- **Upstream subproblem remains tracked:** https://github.com/emmetio/emmet/issues/728 is open and requests removal of obsolete isindex, applet, and keygen abbreviations. It addresses only a subset of this request, not punctuation, other common words, or contextual suggestion behavior.
