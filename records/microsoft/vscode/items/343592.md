Healthy pending dialogs must remain deduplicated, and dialog operation in other windows must remain unchanged.
- **Trace:**

The pending operation also occupies the queue, so removing only the lock cannot restore dialog display. NativeHostMainService.pickFolderAndOpen in src/vs/platform/native/electron-main/nativeHostMainService.ts proceeds only when paths are returned. Preserve normal modal deduplication and do not treat every repeated request or fullscreen transition as proof of an orphan.
