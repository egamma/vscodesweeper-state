## Evidence

- **Repeatable driver-dependent regression:** In https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/238747, the reporter confirmed that downgrading from NVIDIA 570 to 560 restored responsiveness. The February 2025 follow-up reproduced delayed typing and scrolling with VS Code 1.97.2, both Debian and snap installations, and extensions disabled.
- **Workarounds do not establish resolution:** The reporter's May 2025 comment says NVIDIA 575.51.03 still exhibited lag on Ubuntu 24.04 Wayland, while switching the desktop session to X11 removed it. Another commenter reported severe delayed input on Ubuntu 25.04 under X11 in August 2025 despite disabling acceleration and extensions; a full system restart helped. The Linux Mint comment reports acceleration disabling as effective only for that user's case.
- **Current-source boundary:** src/mainImpl.ts:251–305 implements the persistent disable-hardware-acceleration option through Electron's app.disableHardwareAcceleration(). src/vs/base/node/osDisplayProtocolInfo.ts:getCodeDisplayProtocol distinguishes native Wayland, XWayland, and X11 using the ozone-platform argument. A Wayland desktop session alone therefore does not identify VS Code's actual backend, and these paths do not prove that software rendering eliminates this environment-dependent latency.
- **Older related issue is not a verified canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169504 is open and describes similar NVIDIA/Wayland input problems, but its diagnosis includes driver-library loading failures and a Chromium defect that a maintainer said was fixed in Chromium 113: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169504#issuecomment-1680358066. This report persisted with Chromium 128 and later includes an X11 case; matching symptoms do not establish that the older issue owns the same remaining defect.
