- **Behavior:**

  > An unavailable-automation warning should explain what is affected, why it is unavailable, and what the user can do next. Current main already supplies grouped provider names and reason text, but does not offer a contextual recovery action. Acceptance should cover each supported unavailability reason and distinguish known affected definitions from a provider whose catalogue cannot currently be read. Available providers and their automations must remain usable.
- **Trace:**

  > AutomationsCardsSection.renderPartialState and renderUnavailableMessage in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/automationsView.ts render text produced by getUnavailableAutomationsReasons in automationCataloguePresentation.ts. IAutomationProviderDescriptor in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/common/automations/automationService.ts carries provider identity and explanations but no recovery action. Current presentation therefore supports diagnosis at provider level, not contextual recovery controls. Preserve grouped explanations and equivalent Accessible View content in automationsAccessibility.ts; do not change execution ownership or silently select another provider. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337514 addressed message clarity, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330974 concerns a separate model-selection failure. The remaining uncertainty is the user-facing recovery contract and how to represent affected automations when the unavailable catalogue cannot expose their identities.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Which unavailability reasons should offer an immediate recovery action, and which should provide navigation or explanatory guidance instead?
  - When a provider's definitions cannot be retrieved, should the warning identify only the affected host, or also show previously known automation names with an explicit stale-information indication?
