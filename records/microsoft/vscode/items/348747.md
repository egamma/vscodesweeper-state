# Issue #348747 — Agent Host: IPC close during connect leaves the connection attempt pending

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348747](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348747)

## Summary

The pending-connect defect remains in current main: IPC close or disposal emits onClose without settling connect(). The issue is correctly routed and has a concrete component reproduction suitable for regression coverage.

## Change summary

Ensure an IPC connection attempt rejects when its transport closes or is disposed while connection establishment is pending.

## Best solution

Make the IPC transport's connection lifecycle settle promptly on close and disposal, without allowing late channel completion to reopen it. Preserve normal connection behavior and verify that interrupted reconnect attempts can advance to another attempt.
