# Issue #329330 — Custom menus and submenus close immediately on Windows, but temporarily recover after an RDP connection

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329330](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329330)

## Summary

This is the same regression already fixed on main: the button-menu close animation (added in #325190) left the closing context view focusable/interactive, letting it steal focus/mouse events from a newly opened menu or submenu and close it immediately — fixed by making the closing view `inert` during the animation.

## Change summary

On Windows with custom title bar/menu style, clicking a top-level menu or submenu makes it flash open and close immediately, unless briefly 'fixed' by an RDP session.

## Best solution

Close as completed — the fix already shipped to main in commit b966285933b (PR https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/329327); confirm the reporter's symptom disappears once they update to an Insiders/1.133+ build.
