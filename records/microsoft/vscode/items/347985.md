## Evidence

- **Reporter trigger:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347985 supplies a Rust sample and screenshot showing inconsistent colors between Default::default() and r.contains(&1), despite both being default-library calls.
- **Current source diagnosis:** src/vs/platform/theme/common/tokenClassificationRegistry.ts registers function.defaultLibrary and member.defaultLibrary with support.function, but no method.defaultLibrary rule. Ordinary method probes entity.name.function.member before support.function. The deprecated member type inherits from method, not the reverse, so its defaultLibrary rule does not cover method tokens.
- **Observable color resolution:** src/vs/workbench/services/themes/common/colorThemeData.ts:getTokenStyle applies matching semantic defaults, and resolveScopes returns the first successful probe. extensions/theme-defaults/themes/2026-dark.json styles entity.name.function as #d2a8ff, while its inherited dark_plus.json explicitly styles support.function as #DCDCAA. These paths explain the reported purple method versus yellow function.
- **Open implementation candidates:** The supplied timeline identifies https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/348011 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/348229 as open. Their fetched content directly addresses this issue: the former adds the missing method.defaultLibrary default; the latter also addresses inherited 2026 theme support scopes and describes regression tests. Neither proposal establishes a landed fix.
