Still reproduces on main: the horizontal scrollbar does not appear immediately after Alt+Z toggle. The `_addScrollbar` method creates the `DomScrollableElement` but its initial rendering appears to require a subsequent resize event to become visible. No fix commit found.

## Change summary

Related to the broader https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/133962 but this is a distinct, narrower bug.
