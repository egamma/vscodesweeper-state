- **Current implementation:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalInstance.ts:2245-2303 routes toggleSizeToContentWidth through _refreshScrollbar and _addScrollbar. _addScrollbar recalculates terminal dimensions and awaits _resize before constructing the first DomScrollableElement, then sets its width and scrollWidth and adds bottom padding. This identifies an initial-layout ordering lead, but does not conclusively prove the reported rendering failure.
