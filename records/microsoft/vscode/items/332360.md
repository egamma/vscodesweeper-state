# Issue #332360 — more than 2 external sessions are shown

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332360](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332360)

## Summary

Team-authored (COLLABORATOR) test-plan bug report, filed 1 day ago against the new `chat.agentSessions.showExternal: 'recent'` behavior, with the reporter's own follow-up comment confirming the cap of 2 is not being respected once several external sessions accumulate — this stays open regardless, and the hard keep-open rule for team-authored items applies anyway.

## Change summary

With `chat.agentSessions.showExternal` set to `recent` (documented to show at most the 2 most recent external sessions), 4 external sessions are shown after several more external chats are added.

## Best solution

Trace why the top-2 cap computed in `AgentService._getRecentSessionKeys`/`_shouldIncludeSession` (src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/agentService.ts) isn't holding once multiple external sessions are discovered in sequence — likely candidates are the reconciliation/broadcast bookkeeping in `_reconcileExternalSessions`/`_announceSurfacedSession` not retracting previously-surfaced sessions promptly, or renderer-side accumulation in `agentHostSessionListStore.ts` / `baseAgentHostSessionsProvider.ts` not applying the freshly-capped list. A maintainer with a live repro should add a targeted test reproducing >2 discovered external sessions and watch which stage stops enforcing the cap.

- **reconciliation complexity:** The visible set is also gated by a separate, event-driven reconciliation/broadcast path (_reconcileExternalSessions, _announceSurfacedSession, agentService.ts:2422-2518) that could plausibly let stale entries linger between discovery batches even though a fresh listSessions() call would cap correctly.
