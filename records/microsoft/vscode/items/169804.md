## Summary

This remains a valid Backlog feature request: it received the required 20 community upvotes, and current main still offers only zero, same, +1, or +2 wrapping indentation—not the requested negative hanging indent.

## Change summary

Allow wrapped lines to use one tab stop less indentation than their parent line.
