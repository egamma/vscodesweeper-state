## Change summary

Completing an unsaved merge after reverting a large line-ending change reportedly produces a save conflict and stages unresolved content.

## Best solution

Reproduce the EOL-revert scenario and determine whether completion is proceeding after an unsuccessful save or another writer is changing the result file. Complete Merge should stage the saved resolution only after saving and closing succeed, while preserving unsaved edits when saving fails.
