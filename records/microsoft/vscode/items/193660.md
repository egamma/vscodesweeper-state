# Issue #193660 — Put density related options under the same namespace: tabs, notebooks

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/193660](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/193660)

## Summary

Keep open: tab density was migrated, but current main still registers and consumes notebook compactness under `notebook.compactView`, leaving the requested namespace consolidation incomplete.

## Change summary

Consolidate editor-tab and notebook compactness settings under the `window.density` namespace, with a shared density option.

## Best solution

Migrate notebook compactness to `window.density.notebookCompactView`, preserving compatibility with and deprecating `notebook.compactView`; separately resolve the proposed aggregate `window.density` behavior through the broader density design issue.
