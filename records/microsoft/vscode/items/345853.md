## Summary

Keep open for the newly started community-review period. Native Claude credentials provide a workaround, but they do not satisfy the request to reuse Model-provider configuration or explain the mismatch in the picker.

## Change summary

Make Anthropic BYOK configuration usable or clearly understandable when switching from the Copilot harness to the Claude harness.
