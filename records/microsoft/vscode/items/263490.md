[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/263490](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/263490)

## Summary

Keep open: current AI statistics remain workspace-scoped and do not offer configurable reporting periods. This team-authored Backlog request still describes a real capability gap; no qualifying canonical duplicate or fixing commit was established.

## Change summary

Allow users to choose project-specific or cross-project AI statistics and reporting periods longer than one day.

- **Behavior:**

  > Users can distinguish project-specific AI statistics from statistics combined across projects and select a reporting period longer than one day. Switching projects must not present a workspace-only zero as an all-project total. Displayed counters and charts must consistently reflect the selected scope and period, with persistence and retention behavior agreed before implementation.
- **Trace:**

  > AiStatsFeature in src/vs/workbench/contrib/editTelemetry/browser/editStats/aiStatsFeature.ts persists aiStats in workspace storage and caps history at 288 five-minute sessions. Its aiRate uses all retained sessions, while acceptedInlineSuggestionsToday filters from local midnight. createAiStatsHover in aiStatsStatusBar.ts only changes chart grouping between days and sessions; aiStatsChart.ts consumes the same retained data. editTelemetry.contribution.ts exposes only editor.aiStats.enabled for this surface. The uncertainty is the product contract for combined scope and longer history, not whether the current controls satisfy the request. Keep export from https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/292307 separate, and do not conflate local statistics presentation with telemetry transmission or consent.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should all-project statistics cover workspaces on the current machine, the current profile, or a synchronized account, and should simultaneously open workspaces contribute live?
  - Which reporting periods and retention limit should be supported, and should a selected period govern every counter and chart or preserve a separate today-only counter?
  - Should existing workspace-only behavior remain the default, and where should the user's scope and period choices persist?
