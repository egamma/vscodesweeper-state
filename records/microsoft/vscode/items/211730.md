[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/211730](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/211730)

Keep open: extension-host notebook saving still defaults on only for non-stable builds, so the requested stable-default rollout remains incomplete. The merged preparation PRs do not complete this team-authored issue.
