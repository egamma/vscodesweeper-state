## Summary

Keep open: the current block-decoration renderer still excludes a conflict decoration when its model range leaves the viewport, even if its view-zone highlight remains visible. This team-authored report remains the canonical issue for the confirmed scrolling defect.

## Change summary

Merge-editor conflict highlighting disappears prematurely when scrolling through an alignment view zone.

## Best solution
