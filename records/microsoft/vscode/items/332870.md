# Issue #332870 — BYOK Chat Completions: historical reasoning_content dropped across turns (Custom Endpoint & extension providers; Kimi K3 / GLM / DeepSeek)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332870](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332870)

## Summary

Traced and confirmed on current main: the historical-thinking gate in toolCalling.tsx unconditionally excludes `chat-completions`, and groupThinkingParts() in languageModelAccessPrompt.tsx drops thinking parts with no id (which extChatEndpoint.ts forwards as `chunk.id || ''`). Both defects are real and unfixed, affecting BYOK/thinking-mode models that require preserved reasoning_content across turns.

## Change summary

Historical reasoning_content/thinking is dropped from follow-up requests for BYOK Chat Completions models (and extension-contributed providers whose thinking parts have no id), breaking Kimi K3, GLM, and DeepSeek thinking-mode conversations.

## Best solution

Extend `apiSupportsHistoricalThinking` in extensions/copilot/src/extension/prompts/node/panel/toolCalling.tsx to include chat-completions endpoints that declare thinking support, and add an id fallback (e.g. generateUuid()) in extChatEndpoint.ts / tolerate id-less parts in groupThinkingParts() (languageModelAccessPrompt.tsx) so extension-contributed thinking models don't lose reasoning across turns.
