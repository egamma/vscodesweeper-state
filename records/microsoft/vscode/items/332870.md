# Issue #332870 — BYOK Chat Completions: historical reasoning_content dropped across turns (Custom Endpoint & extension providers; Kimi K3 / GLM / DeepSeek)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332870](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/332870)

## Summary

Well-diagnosed, source-verified bug: the historical-thinking gate in toolCalling.tsx excludes chatCompletions models, and groupThinkingParts() in languageModelAccessPrompt.tsx drops id-less thinking parts, so BYOK/extension thinking-mode models lose cross-turn reasoning_content exactly as described.

## Change summary

For BYOK Chat Completions models with thinking enabled (Kimi K3, GLM, DeepSeek) and for extension-contributed providers whose thinking parts arrive without an id, reasoning_content from prior turns is stripped from outgoing requests, breaking or degrading providers that require it.

## Best solution

Extend apiSupportsHistoricalThinking in extensions/copilot/src/extension/prompts/node/panel/toolCalling.tsx to include chatCompletions endpoints that declare thinking support, and make groupThinkingParts() in extensions/copilot/src/extension/conversation/vscode-node/languageModelAccessPrompt.tsx (plus the id: chunk.id || '' fallback in extensions/copilot/src/platform/endpoint/vscode-node/extChatEndpoint.ts:282) tolerate/generate an id so id-less thinking parts survive into rebuilt prompts.

- **apiType enum confirms chatCompletions exists as a distinct path:** extensions/copilot/src/platform/endpoint/node/chatEndpoint.ts:347 defines apiType as 'responses' | 'messages' | 'chatCompletions', matching the gate check in toolCalling.tsx.
- **id-less thinking parts dropped:** extensions/copilot/src/extension/conversation/vscode-node/languageModelAccessPrompt.tsx:29-33 (groupThinkingParts) skips any part where `!part.id`.
