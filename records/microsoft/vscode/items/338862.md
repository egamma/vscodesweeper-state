# Issue #338862 — plugin marketplace search: can not see the matching text

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338862](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/338862)

## Summary

Keep open for a search-design decision: current Discover results still lack matching-text emphasis and place installed items before available results. The discussion identifies a genuine constraint—semantic results may have no literal matching text—so highlighting, ranking, and marketplace-only discovery need an agreed contract.

## Change summary

Make customization marketplace search results explain their relevance and prioritize discovering new plugins and connectors.

## Best solution

Agree on a coherent Discover search experience that distinguishes literal matches from semantic relevance and makes available customizations easy to find. Decide whether independently matched installed items belong in discovery results, retaining installation controls for catalog results that are already installed.
