# Issue #343466 — Inconsistent block padding in the Status (Bottom) Bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343466](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343466)

## Summary

Keep open as optical-balance feedback for Modern UI: the investigation distinguishes the surrounding gutter from actual Status Bar item centering. The attempted gutter-removal fix was withdrawn, and no theme-independent replacement design has been established.

## Change summary

Improve the perceived vertical balance of Status Bar content when the bar and surrounding workbench backgrounds blend together.

## Best solution

Evaluate a theme-consistent footer treatment that preserves centered Status Bar items, coherent spacing between floating surfaces, and a bar flush with the window bottom. Do not restore the asymmetric padding or adopt the withdrawn gutter-removal proposal without resolving their demonstrated regressions.
