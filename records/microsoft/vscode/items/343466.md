# Issue #343466 — Inconsistent block padding in the Status (Bottom) Bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343466](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/343466)

## Summary

Keep open as optical-balance feedback for Modern UI. The full investigation distinguishes symmetric internal padding from the surrounding gutter; the proposed gutter removal was rejected and never merged.

## Change summary

Improve the perceived vertical balance of Status Bar items when the bar blends into the surrounding window background.

## Best solution

Agree on a theme-independent footer treatment that preserves centered items and consistent separation between workbench surfaces. Do not restore asymmetric padding or revive the rejected gutter-removal proposal without resolving its contrasting-theme drawbacks.

Acceptance requires native comparisons across matching and contrasting themes and both densities, with centered hover surfaces and no gap below the Status Bar.
- **Trace:**

Preserve internal centering, the window-bottom attachment, and Classic UI; uncertainty starts at the replacement visual treatment, not at how to implement a padding change.
