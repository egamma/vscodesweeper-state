## Change summary

Make release-note videos wait for explicit playback, with a prominent Play control, rather than automatically starting and looping.

## Best solution

Resolve the release-notes playback policy across the website and in-product viewer, explicitly addressing readers who need motion-free reading without hiding the notes. Reconcile the earlier support for changing autoplay with the recent decision not to proceed with the submitted documentation change.
