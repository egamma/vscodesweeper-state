# Issue #13516 — Horizontal scrollbar doesn’t shrink after the long line getting shortened

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/13516](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/13516)

## Summary

Keep this accepted request open: current source still retains the historical maximum line width when only part of the document is rendered. The matching open report is newer, so it cannot replace this older, re-reviewed issue as the canonical.

## Change summary

Automatically reduce the editor's horizontal scroll extent after its longest line is shortened or deleted, including in documents larger than the viewport.

## Best solution

Make horizontal scroll extent recover after shortening or deleting previously measured long lines without requiring wrapping or a layout change, while preserving editor virtualization and responsiveness. Keep this issue as the older tracking report and coordinate the overlapping work with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169485.

Check both DOM and GPU rendering, retain access to remaining long lines, and measure editing/scrolling responsiveness on large files.
- **Behavior:**

  > After a previously rendered longest line is shortened or deleted, the horizontal scroll extent should recover without a layout workaround, even when the entire document is not visible. Unwrapped content must remain accessible and any remaining long lines must still be horizontally scrollable. Acceptance checks should cover shortening and deletion, partial viewports, and both rendering modes. Large-document typing and scrolling must retain their responsiveness.
- **Trace:**

  > ViewLines in src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLines/viewLines.ts measures rendered lines in _updateLineWidths and clears _maxLineWidth only when every document line is visible and measured; _ensureMaxLineWidth otherwise preserves the historical maximum. onLinesChanged and onLinesDeleted forward invalidation to the visible-line collection without resetting that maximum. Layout and wrapping changes reset it, explaining the workarounds and the short-document partial coverage. ViewLinesGpu in src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/viewLinesGpu/viewLinesGpu.ts resets its own maximum on deletion but not shortening, and ViewLines delegates width tracking to it when GPU rendering is enabled. The shared scroll extent is maintained by ViewLayout.setMaxLineWidth in src/vs/editor/common/viewLayout/viewLayout.ts. The maintainer explains the virtualization/performance constraint at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/169485#issuecomment-1361658986; that newer report overlaps but cannot replace this older re-reviewed issue. The trace establishes the limitation, not a bounded solution for unrendered widths; preserve virtualization, unwrapped presentation, and access to remaining long lines.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Must the contracted scroll extent reflect all remaining document lines immediately, including offscreen lines, or may it converge as lines are rendered while still contracting after the reported edit?
