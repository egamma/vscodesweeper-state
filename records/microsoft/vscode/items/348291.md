## Change summary

Reloading and reopening a resumed draft creates additional text-only saved entries instead of retaining one complete composition.

## Best solution

Preserve the relationship between a resumed saved draft and its recovered composer so reopening does not save a new independent copy. Preserve inline file references and attachments through the same reload/resume sequence without changing recovery of genuinely independent unsaved input.
