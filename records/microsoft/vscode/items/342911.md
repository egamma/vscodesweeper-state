Keep open: current source explicitly expands inactive connected-tab fills on hover, including the multi-selected and modified states reported here. This is a bounded visual regression, not merely general feedback about the connected-tab design.

## Change summary

Prevent inactive connected tabs from visibly growing on hover when multi-selected or highlighted as modified.

## Best solution

Keep inactive connected-tab geometry stable across hover transitions while preserving selection and modified-state indicators, active-tab connections, and existing keyboard-focus affordances.
