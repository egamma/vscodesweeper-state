# Issue #235795 — Add Cell toolbar remains sticky in blank notebook

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/235795](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/235795)

## Summary

Keep open: the supplied steps suffice to retest, and current source exposes a plausible lifecycle gap that can leave the empty-notebook toolbar visible after cells are inserted. No verified fix or canonical duplicate was found; the initial empty-state visibility is explicitly styled, but the persistence after insertion remains actionable.

## Change summary

The top Add Cell toolbar remains visible without hover after an initially empty notebook gains cells.

## Best solution

Ensure the top insertion toolbar reflects the notebook's current cell count throughout initial attachment and subsequent edits, returning to normal hover and keyboard-focus visibility after cells are added. Preserve the empty-notebook insertion affordance unless the notebook owner explicitly chooses different behavior.
