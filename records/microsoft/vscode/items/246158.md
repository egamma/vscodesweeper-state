## Summary

Still open and unfixed on main. The `Ctrl+Space` keybinding in a multi-select quick pick toggles the checkbox instead of (or in addition to) toggling the hover. No commits reference this issue, and the keybinding registrations in `quickInputActions.ts` have not been changed to address this. Team-authored issue in Backlog — keeping open.

## Change summary

Pressing `Ctrl+Space` in a multi-select quick pick toggles the checkbox when it should only toggle the hover.

## Best solution

Fix the keybinding conflict in `src/vs/platform/quickinput/browser/quickInputActions.ts` so `toggleCheckbox` (line 283, bound to plain `Space`) does not fire on `Ctrl+Space`, or investigate whether the list/tree widget's internal key handling intercepts `Ctrl+Space` before the keybinding resolver routes it to `toggleHover`.
