# Issue #329917 — Selecting prompt and changing it makes the other onboarding prompts unselectable

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329917](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329917)

## Summary

Reproducible in source: `NewSessionPromptOptions` intentionally disables the other onboarding-prompt options once the input text diverges from the generated prompt, to avoid silently overwriting a user's edits — a contributor already explained the rationale in comments, but the reporter's question (and the existing `under-discussion` label) shows the UX tradeoff isn't settled yet.

## Change summary

Editing a selected onboarding prompt greys out and disables the other prompt options, and the reporter is asking why/whether that's the right behavior.

## Best solution

Keep this open as a UX discussion in src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/newSessionPromptOptions.ts — consider whether a confirmation step (instead of full disablement) would let users switch options without losing typed edits; a maintainer/product call is needed before deciding.
