- **Current built-in behavior:** src/vs/platform/keybinding/common/abstractKeybindingService.ts:393-396 passes commandArgs directly to ICommandService.executeCommand. src/vs/workbench/services/commands/common/commandService.ts passes those arguments to the registered handler without general variable expansion. In src/vs/workbench/browser/actions/quickAccessActions.ts, workbench.action.quickOpen forwards a supplied string prefix directly to quickAccess.show; it does not resolve ${selectedText}. A literal prefix is supported, but does not satisfy dynamic selection substitution.
