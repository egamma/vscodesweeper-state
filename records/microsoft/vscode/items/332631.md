## Evidence

- **Current source confirms both defects:** src/vs/workbench/services/search/common/search.ts:787–816 still implements includedInQuery by mapping excludes into inclusion verdicts and combining them with .some(). A synchronous matching exclude is ignored by the non-thenable branch, while an asynchronous matching exclude returns false that another expression's true inclusion verdict overrides. includedInQuerySync at lines 771–781 instead rejects any matching exclusion.
- **Production result-filtering path:** src/vs/workbench/services/search/common/textSearchManager.ts:145–168 constructs the asynchronous sibling callback, calls includedInQuery for provider results, and forwards results when its verdict is true. The incorrect verdict therefore permits excluded results through this filtering stage.
