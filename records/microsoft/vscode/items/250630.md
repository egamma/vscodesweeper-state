# Issue #250630 — Setting to change image preview background

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250630](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/250630)

## Summary

Keep this accepted feature request open: the preview still hard-codes its checkerboard colors, and no existing setting independently customizes them. It has 32 upvotes and was promoted to Backlog; the older grid-scaling issue does not cover the same requested behavior.

## Change summary

Allow users to customize both transparency-checkerboard colors in the built-in image preview.

## Best solution

Expose image-preview-specific checkerboard colors through the existing theme customization system, preserving current defaults and keeping the pattern within the image bounds. A maintainer should settle the public customization contract before implementation.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `extensions/media-preview/media/imagePreview.css`, `extensions/media-preview/package.json`, `extensions/media-preview/src/imagePreview/index.ts`
- **Validation:** Open transparent PNG and SVG samples in the built-in Image Preview, customize each checkerboard color independently, and verify that both colors are applied at fit and explicit zoom levels. Reset customization and verify existing light/dark defaults, image-bounded checkerboard coverage, and unchanged backgrounds in ordinary text editors.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users can independently customize the two colors behind transparent images in the built-in preview so small or intricate image details remain legible. Without customization, current appearance remains unchanged. The checkerboard remains bounded by the image rather than filling the editor. Acceptance covers PNG and SVG previews, fit and explicit zoom levels, and isolation from ordinary text-editor backgrounds.
- **Trace:**

  > The built-in media-preview extension owns this surface. extensions/media-preview/media/imagePreview.css applies a fixed conic-gradient background directly to the image, with separate hard-coded light and dark foreground colors and transparent alternate squares. extensions/media-preview/package.json has no image-background setting, and ImagePreview.getWebviewContents in extensions/media-preview/src/imagePreview/index.ts supplies no background configuration. General editor.background customization cannot independently replace both checkerboard colors. The remaining uncertainty is the supported public customization contract, not whether the capability currently exists. Preserve image-bounded coverage and existing zoom behavior; grid scaling is separate work tracked at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/226095, whose prior fix was reverted for regressions.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should the two checkerboard colors be exposed as theme color IDs through workbench.colorCustomizations or as dedicated image-preview settings?

## Evidence

- **Existing-capability check:** extensions/media-preview/media/imagePreview.css sets the image background to a conic gradient using hard-coded rgb(230, 230, 230) in light themes and rgb(20, 20, 20) in dark themes, alternating with transparent squares. Neither checkerboard color is exposed as a theme variable.
- **Configuration and commands:** extensions/media-preview/package.json contributes only video autoplay and loop settings.
