## Summary

Keep open: current source still makes extension-backed MCP provenance clickable in the Agents Window, but its target editor is not registered there. Related provenance and duplicate-row reports do not cover this broken navigation.

## Change summary

Make extension-contributed and built-in MCP source links usable in the Agents Window, or present their provenance without unsupported navigation.

Decide whether extension provenance should be non-interactive or lead to a supported destination; investigate the GitHub derived-row observation separately without assuming equal names mean duplicate identities.
