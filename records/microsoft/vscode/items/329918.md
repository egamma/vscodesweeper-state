## Summary

The original reporter withdrew the bug claim, attributing the failure to their org's `editor_preview_features="0"` policy gating image attachments in the Copilot Chat prompt element — but a second commenter with the identical policy flag still sees the bug specifically with the 'Local agent' selected while the 'Copilot agent' works fine, which the preview-flag explanation does not cover.
