# Issue #227484 — Custom controls need to provide proper textual name, role, and state information 

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/227484](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/227484)

## Summary

The folding controls still lack accessible role and expanded-state information in current source, and their containing margin is hidden from assistive technology. Keep this reopened issue open; existing folding commands and announcements do not resolve the reported control semantics.

## Change summary

Expose code-editor folding controls with an accessible name, button role, and accurate expanded or collapsed state.

## Best solution

Make folding controls discoverable and operable by assistive technology, with semantics synchronized to folding state. Address the hidden margin ancestry and editor focus model together rather than adding ineffective attributes to hidden descendants.

## Evidence

- **Reported trigger:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/227484 reports that screen reader users cannot identify the editor's expand/collapse controls or determine their state. The report identifies VS Code 1.92.2 on macOS 14.6.1 and includes screenshots; it was reopened because the problem persisted.
- **Current control rendering:** src/vs/editor/contrib/folding/browser/foldingDecorations.ts selects collapsed or expanded icon classes and localized linesDecorationsTooltip strings. src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/linesDecorations/linesDecorations.ts, LinesDecorationsOverlay.prepareRender(), renders these as div elements with class, title, and positioning styles, but no button role or aria-expanded.
- **Hidden accessibility ancestry:** src/vs/editor/browser/view.ts attaches LinesDecorationsOverlay through marginViewOverlays beneath Margin.getDomNode(). src/vs/editor/browser/viewParts/margin/margin.ts sets that ancestor to role="presentation" and aria-hidden="true", excluding the folding controls from the accessibility tree regardless of their tooltip text.
- **Interactive behavior:** src/vs/editor/contrib/folding/browser/folding.ts, FoldingController.onEditorMouseDown() and onEditorMouseUp(), recognize GUTTER_LINE_DECORATIONS clicks and toggle folding regions. These are interactive controls, not merely decorative icons.

These support keyboard folding and folded-area awareness but do not expose the gutter controls' name, role, and state.
