- **Current source confirms mitigation only:** src/mainImpl.ts:251-305 accepts disable-hardware-acceleration from argv.json and calls Electron app.disableHardwareAcceleration(). This bypasses accelerated rendering rather than correcting mouse-pointer composition. Reports say it helps many users but not everyone; no concrete behavior-fixing commit was identified in the available checkout history.
