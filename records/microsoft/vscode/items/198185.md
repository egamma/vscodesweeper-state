## Summary

Keep open: current source includes viewport-preservation mechanisms, but these do not establish that toggling hidden regions preserves the change being viewed. The supplied steps suffice to retest; no concrete fixing commit or verified canonical was found.

## Change summary

Enabling Hide Unchanged Regions after scrolling to a change loses the diff editor's viewing position.

## Best solution

Preserve the user's place at the viewed change when toggling unchanged-region visibility. First reproduce with current viewport anchoring, including a viewport whose first visible line becomes hidden, before identifying the remaining defect.
