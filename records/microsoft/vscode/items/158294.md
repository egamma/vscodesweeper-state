[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/158294](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/158294)

Keep open: the linked maintainer report confirms incorrect Shift-click selection after replacing multiple search results, and current source still separates replacement-driven focus/selection from the range-selection anchor. ## Change summary

After replacing multiple search results, Shift-click selects additional results outside the expected range from the newly selected result.

## Best solution

Make subsequent Shift-click selection agree with the visible post-replacement selection in the Search results tree. Establish the anchor state across asynchronous replacement and tree refresh, then cover the reported interaction without changing ordinary list multiselection semantics.
