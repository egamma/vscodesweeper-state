The selection collapse remains traceable in current source: word selection clears column-selection metadata, and the subsequent character-selection command reconstructs the selection from the primary cursor alone. Keep this correctly routed bug open; neither candidate canonical reports the same symptom.

## Change summary

Preserve all selected lines when character-wise selection follows word-wise selection in column selection mode.

## Best solution

Make the transition from word-wise to character-wise selection retain every participating line and adjust each selection by one character, without changing ordinary word navigation or existing rectangular-selection behavior.
