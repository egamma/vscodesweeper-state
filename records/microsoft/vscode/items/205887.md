# Issue #205887 — Column selection is reset when selecting with arrow keys

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/205887](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/205887)

## Summary

The reported selection loss remains traceable in current source: word selection clears the column-selection metadata, and the next Shift+Arrow rebuilds selections from the primary cursor alone. Keep this open in its existing editor areas; the older related requests do not report the same symptom.

## Change summary

Preserve every line's selection when switching from word-wise to character-wise selection in column selection mode.

## Best solution

Make character-wise selection after word-wise selection retain the participating cursors and adjust each line's selection as requested, without changing ordinary rectangular selection or word-navigation behavior.
