## Summary

Still relevant: the two WordOperations tests remain skipped on Firefox (with `this.skip()` referencing this issue) in current main. The underlying Firefox word-segmentation behavior difference is unresolved; the workaround is just a test skip.

## Change summary

Firefox's Intl word segmentation treats Japanese text differently from Chromium/WebKit, causing cursorWordLeft/Right 'Recognize words' tests to fail.

## Best solution

Investigate whether VS Code's word operations should normalize word boundaries across browsers (likely in `src/vs/editor/common/cursor/cursorWordOperations.ts` or word-segmentation utilities), or whether the Firefox behavior is acceptable and the tests should assert browser-specific expected values. Once resolved, re-enable the skipped tests.
