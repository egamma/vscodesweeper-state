Keep open: multiple reporters confirmed failed sign-in on the stable iOS PWA while Insiders worked, and no verified fix or canonical duplicate was found. Current-device confirmation and callback diagnostics are still needed to establish whether the failure persists and where it originates.

## Change summary

Signing into GitHub or Microsoft from the vscode.dev iOS PWA closes the authentication popup and reloads the app without completing sign-in.
