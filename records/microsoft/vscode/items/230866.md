## Change summary

Pasting a replacement JSX tag name causes repeated tag renaming during undo and prevents restoration of later edits with redo.

## Best solution

Keep this defect with the third-party Auto Rename Tag extension. Disable it and use editor.linkedEditing for supported JSX/TSX tag-pair editing instead.
