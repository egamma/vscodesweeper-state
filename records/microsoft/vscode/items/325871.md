# Issue #325871 — Nested mermaid diagrams do not block scroll event in chat panel

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325871](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/325871)

## Summary

Confirmed current bug in nested chat-output scrolling: wheel gestures are unconditionally forwarded to the conversation even when the Mermaid webview can scroll. Keep it open and route it to the Mermaid chat area.

## Change summary

Prevent the outer chat conversation from scrolling while a trackpad gesture is being consumed by a scrollable Mermaid diagram.

## Best solution

Give chat output webviews the notebook-style nested-scroll behavior: forward a wheel event to the conversation only when no inner scrollable element can consume it, including preserving trackpad gesture ownership until the inner scroll completes.
