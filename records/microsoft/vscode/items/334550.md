## Summary

This is a Remote-SSH extension regression (icons vanish/unclickable over SSH, worked around via the extension's own `remote.SSH.useExecServer: false` setting), and the Remote Development family is tracked in microsoft/vscode-remote-release, not here — proposing this be moved rather than judged as a core close, per the team's established practice for its own extensions.
