# Issue #30406 — Autoindent inserts extra undo item, requires double undo after paste

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/30406](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/30406)

## Summary

Keep open for a decision on paste undo semantics. Current source still separates automatic reindentation from the paste; editor.autoIndentOnPaste can disable reindentation, but does not provide single-step undo while retaining it.

## Change summary

Make one undo remove the pasted content together with its automatic indentation adjustment.

## Best solution

Resolve whether paste-triggered indentation should share the paste's undo transaction, explicitly considering users who currently undo only the indentation. Document the chosen behavior and cover paste, undo, and redo in the editor indentation tests.

- **Behavior:**

  > Pasting code that needs indentation adjustment currently permits undoing the adjustment separately from removing the pasted content. The reporter wants one undo to restore the document before the paste while retaining automatic indentation during normal pasting. Other participants rely on the intermediate undo state to retain clipboard indentation. Acceptance must establish the approved single-undo and redo results, including selection replacement, without conflating indentation matching with formatter-driven formatting.
- **Trace:**

- **Open decisions:**
  - With paste auto-indentation enabled, should one undo remove the entire paste or continue to undo only its indentation adjustment?
