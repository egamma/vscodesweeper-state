# Issue #337904 — Claude agent host: steering messages sent mid-turn are never shown in the conversation

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337904](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/337904)

## Summary

The missing steering message is confirmed in current source: SDK handoff removes its pending representation, but no conversation entry replaces it. Keep this open; the related steering-delivery and browser-tool reports do not establish a canonical for the full reported behavior.

## Change summary

Show Claude steering messages in the conversation when sent during an active turn, retaining a readable record afterward.

## Best solution

Give consumed Claude steering text a durable conversation representation visible during the running turn and in restored history. Preserve steering delivery and active-task semantics; making the message visible must not silently turn it into a replacement task.
