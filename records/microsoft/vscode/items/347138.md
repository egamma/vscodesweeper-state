# Issue #347138 — Controls area for worktree cleanup could use some design love

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347138](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347138)

## Summary

Keep open for a focused design pass: current source retains the redundant headings, inline filter controls, scan text, and repeated storage estimates identified in the report. This team-authored feedback needs design agreement, not a judgment close.

## Change summary

Simplify the Worktree Cleanup controls and clarify the hierarchy of filters, progress, eligibility, and storage estimates.

## Best solution

Agree on a calmer, more consistent controls layout that makes the worktree table and cleanup action primary, while preserving understandable progress and eligibility information. Keep the work scoped to the cleanup editor rather than changing cleanup semantics.

- **Behavior:**

  > The Worktree Cleanup controls should feel calm, focused, and consistent with other VS Code filtering surfaces. Users must still understand the active filters, measurement state, selected worktrees, and effect of cleanup without competing headings or repeated estimates. The agreed presentation must remain usable in narrow layouts and with keyboard and screen-reader navigation.
- **Trace:**

  > SessionWorktreeCleanupEditor.createEditor constructs the reported controls in src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessionInputBanners/browser/sessionWorktreeCleanupEditor.ts; load, renderRows, and updateCleanupButton populate their changing text. The owning stylesheet is browser/media/sessionWorktreeCleanupEditor.css, and the browser test suite explicitly asserts summary and button text. Eligibility is not uniformly true: measuring and pending rows can remain visible without being selectable, while removal issues are filtered. Preserve cleanup eligibility, selection, confirmation, and service behavior; the uncertainty is the intended visible presentation, not the underlying cleanup algorithm. The originating test plan is https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346604; the broader UX overview at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/336620 does not supersede this narrower feedback.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should age, workspace, and sorting remain visibly labeled controls or move into toolbar menus?
  - Which progress, eligibility, and storage information should remain persistently visible, and which belongs alongside the table or in contextual explanations?

These are the surfaces identified in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/347138.
