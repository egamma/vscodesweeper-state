# Issue #139968 — When characters are typed at a rapid rate, textDocument/onTypeFormatting fails to fire

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139968](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/139968)

## Summary

Keep open for discussion of making on-type formatting more resilient to rapid follow-on typing. Current source retains deliberate cancellation when subsequent edits interfere with formatting, matching the maintainer's explanation; it does not establish that language-server change batching loses the trigger.

## Change summary

Allow on-type formatting triggered by the final 'd' in 'end' to remain useful when the user immediately presses Enter.

## Best solution

Establish whether formatting can safely survive the reported 'end' followed immediately by Enter sequence without applying stale edits or delaying typing. Distinguish cancellation of an already-started request from failure to dispatch the language-server request before choosing any behavior change.
