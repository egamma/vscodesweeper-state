## Summary

Keep open as a macOS/Electron regression: two independent reports confirm that VS Code's process tree loses LAN connectivity while other terminals work, although they disagree on whether stable 1.131 is affected.

## Change summary

Restore local-network access for terminal and Remote-SSH subprocesses on macOS 26.

## Best solution

Compare the signed app's Info.plist, entitlements, and Electron version across 1.130, 1.131, and current Insiders, then correct the packaging or upstream integration so Local Network permission consistently covers spawned processes.
