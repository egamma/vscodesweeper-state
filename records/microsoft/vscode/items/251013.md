## Summary

Keep open: the thread contains confirmed reproductions of delayed word-completion acceptance deleting trailing text, and an open PR explicitly targets this issue. The merged logging PR did not resolve the defect.

## Change summary

Accepting a word-based completion after typing during a suggestion delay can overwrite the closing quote or other trailing text in HTML.
