[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234262](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234262)

## Summary

The visual presentation of experimental settings in settings.json still uses a subtle Hint-severity marker rather than the more prominent yellow underline (Warning-severity) the author requested. The related hover-text issue (#234265) was fixed, but this UX discussion about marker severity and alternative presentations (inlay hints) remains unresolved.

## Change summary

Experimental settings in settings.json should be presented more prominently — potentially with a yellow (warning) underline or inlay hints — rather than the current subtle hint marker.
