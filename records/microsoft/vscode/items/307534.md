## Summary

The reporter's claim that the bundled x64 DLL is actually a 32-bit binary cannot be verified from source alone (the DLL is a pre-built artifact fetched from an external release), but the diagnosis is plausible and no fix has landed on main. The issue provides unique root-cause evidence distinct from the broader #304214 report.

## Change summary

The Windows 11 modern context menu entry ('Open with Code') fails to appear because the bundled code_explorer_command_x64.dll is allegedly compiled as a 32-bit binary.

## Best solution

Verify the architecture of the DLL artifact at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-explorer-command/releases/download/v8.0.0-398351/code_explorer_command_x64.dll — if confirmed as 32-bit, rebuild and publish a correct x64 binary in the upstream vscode-explorer-command repo, then bump the version/checksums in build/win32/explorer-dll-fetcher.ts and build/checksums/explorer-dll.txt.
