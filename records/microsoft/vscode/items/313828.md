# Issue #313828 — Improve the UI experience of the Customizations menu

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313828](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/313828)

## Summary

Well-articulated UX feedback on the Agents window Customizations surface — rename, icon, entry point, workspace scoping, and full-window layout. Related to the canonical #264631 but distinct enough in its specific proposals (keyboard shortcut behavior, drawer removal, workspace-scoped navigation, modal-to-full-page transition) to stand on its own as detailed design feedback rather than a duplicate.

## Change summary

Redesign the Agents app Customizations entry point and layout: replace the home icon with a gear, remove the collapsed count badge, rename to Settings, bind Cmd+comma, replace the bottom drawer with a single link, make workspace scoping explicit, and use a full-window surface instead of a modal.

## Best solution

Route to the ai-customizations area owner for design consideration alongside https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/264631. Several of the concrete proposals (gear icon, removing the aggregate badge, explicit workspace scoping, full-window surface) are actionable design decisions the Agents window team can evaluate as part of the ongoing Customizations UI iteration in `src/vs/sessions/contrib/aiCustomizationTreeView/`.
