## Change summary

After closing VS Code (Snap build), the `chrome_crashpad_handler` and a `dconf watch /system/proxy/` process linger, keeping the snap scope active and blocking pending snap refreshes until manually stopped.

## Best solution

Snap-area owner should investigate whether electron-launch (resources/linux/snap/electron-launch) or the crash-reporter/dconf watcher spawn needs explicit cleanup on exit (e.g. killing crashpad_handler's process group, or having systemd's scope properly track/reap these children) so `snap.code.code-*.scope` fully exits and doesn't block snapd refreshes.
