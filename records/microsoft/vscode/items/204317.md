[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/204317](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/204317)

## Summary

The requested header-click interaction is implemented on current main. Commit 3e807d95f1dbe022ee1d72e43de6555c2d71ae5b enabled it in the regular workbench multi-diff editor, so this can be closed as completed.

## Change summary

Allow clicking a collapsed multi-diff entry's file title or header to expand it, rather than requiring the chevron.

## Best solution

Keep the implemented header-click behavior: clicking the title expands or collapses the entry, while toolbar actions and the chevron retain their separate handling. No additional change is needed for this request.
