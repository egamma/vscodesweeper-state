[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342665](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342665)

## Summary

Keep open: current source contains a matching workspace-folder-change path that replaces and destructively disposes a started session's backend. The reconciliation messages do not establish the deletion's origin; the stronger lead is the provisional-session service retaining rebound sessions.

## Change summary

Adding or removing a workspace folder can destroy an active Copilot Agent Host conversation and delete its persisted history.

## Best solution

Started conversations must retain their backend identity and durable history across workspace-folder changes, including while awaiting user input. Preserve legitimate untitled-draft recreation and the existing non-destructive working-directory synchronization for live sessions.
