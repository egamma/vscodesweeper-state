# Issue #340856 — Modern UI: Connected editor tab icons disappear for short filenames with `tabSizing: "shrink"`

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340856](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340856)

## Summary

Thanks for the report. The cause is visible in source: connected tabs are marked narrow by width alone, and the narrow rule hides the icon, so short-filename shrink tabs lose their icons.

## Change summary

With the connected tab style and tabSizing 'shrink', icons are hidden on short-filename tabs even though there is room for them.

## Best solution

Mark a connected tab narrow only when its content is actually constrained, not when its width is under a fixed 102px. Short-name shrink tabs would then keep their icons.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/multiEditorTabsControl.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/modernUI/browser/media/connectedEditorTabs.css`
- **Validation:** Manual: set workbench.experimental.modernUIEditorTabStyle to connected and workbench.editor.tabSizing to shrink, then open short-named files. Icons must show when there is room and hide only when the tab is truly squeezed.
- **Behavior:**

  > In connected tab style with tabSizing 'shrink', short-filename tabs keep their icons whenever the icon and label fit. Icons may be hidden only when the tab is genuinely too narrow, such as when many tabs are squeezed. Fit and fixed sizing and sticky-compact tabs behave as they do today.
- **Trace:**

  > layoutConnectedTabLabels in multiEditorTabsControl.ts uses the fixed width < 102 as the narrow heuristic. For shrink tabs a short name yields a small width that is not a sign of squeezing. connectedEditorTabs.css hides the icon for .connected-tab-narrow. Don't change the other sizing modes or the sticky-compact handling. Where the uncertainty starts: whether to compare against the label's natural width, or to apply narrow only when the tab is below its natural content width.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should the narrow rule hide the icon only when the tab is squeezed below its natural content width, or keep a fixed width threshold with an adjusted value?

## Evidence

- **trigger:** layoutConnectedTabLabels in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/multiEditorTabsControl.ts toggles 'connected-tab-narrow' whenever tab.offsetWidth < 102 for any tab that is not sticky-compact or sizing-fit. A shrink tab with a short filename is naturally narrower than that.
- **effect:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/modernUI/browser/media/connectedEditorTabs.css (around line 530) sets display:none on the icon (.tab-label::before, .monaco-icon-label-iconpath) for .connected-tab-narrow, which matches the screenshot.
