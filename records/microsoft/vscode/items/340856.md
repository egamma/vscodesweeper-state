# Issue #340856 — Modern UI: Connected editor tab icons disappear for short filenames with `tabSizing: "shrink"`

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340856](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340856)

## Summary

Thanks for the report. This is still present on main: in connected tab mode, `layoutConnectedTabLabels` hides the icon on any non-fit tab narrower than 102px, even when the filename is short and there is plenty of space.

## Change summary

With the connected editor tab style and `tabSizing: shrink`, tab icons disappear for short filenames even though there is room to show them.

## Best solution

Collapse the icon only when the label would actually be truncated or crowded by the action column, rather than from a fixed 102px tab-width threshold. Short names in shrink tabs, which are about 80px wide, should keep their icon.

## Evidence

- **root cause:** src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/multiEditorTabsControl.ts `layoutConnectedTabLabels` (about line 2137) toggles `connected-tab-narrow` when `tab.offsetWidth < 102` for every non-fit, non-sticky-compact tab. Shrink tabs have a minimum width of 80 (TAB_WIDTH.shrink), and connectedEditorTabs.css sets `min-width: max(var(--connected-tab-min-width, 80px), ...)` for shrink and fixed tabs. Short-name shrink tabs therefore stay under 102px.
- **icon hiding:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/modernUI/browser/media/connectedEditorTabs.css (about lines 531-535) applies `display: none` to `.connected-tab-narrow > .tab-label::before` and `.monaco-icon-label-iconpath`, which hides the icon.
