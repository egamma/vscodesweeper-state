# Issue #174547 — Explorer hover text can become sticky when primary side bar is positioned on the right

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/174547](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/174547)

## Summary

The bug was confirmed in the experimental 1.76 Explorer hover, but its current status is unclear because the experimental setting is gone and the shared hover implementation now dismisses after the pointer leaves its target. A current-build confirmation is needed before further investigation.

## Change summary

Prevent long Explorer hovers from becoming stranded or clipped after horizontal repositioning, particularly with the primary side bar on the right.

## Best solution

Confirm the right-side Explorer scenario on the latest build; if it still reproduces, correct the shared workbench-hover positioning and mouse-leave behavior so a displaced hover remains readable and dismisses promptly.
