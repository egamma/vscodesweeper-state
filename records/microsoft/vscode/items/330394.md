# Issue #330394 — YAML indentation rules do not work

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330394](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/330394)

## Summary

Confirmed, well-diagnosed bug across three related sub-issues in the YAML language configuration; the root cause of problem 1 is traced to a package.json default that silently disables indentationRules entirely, and problems 2/3 are separate regex defects in the same file.

## Change summary

YAML's `indentationRules` (increase/decrease indent patterns) don't take effect: new lines after `key:` don't indent at all, an anchor after a key breaks the pattern due to a stray `&amp;` instead of `&`, and block scalars (`|`/`>`) aren't handled by the pattern at all.

## Best solution

Two independent fixes in the YAML extension: (1) change `editor.autoIndent` from `"advanced"` to `"full"` in the `[yaml]`/`[dockercompose]` `configurationDefaults` in extensions/yaml/package.json so `indentationRules` are actually consulted (per src/vs/editor/common/languages/autoIndent.ts:74, indentationRules are skipped below `EditorAutoIndentStrategy.Full`); (2) fix the `increaseIndentPattern` regex in extensions/yaml/language-configuration.json to use `&` instead of the mis-escaped `&amp;`, and extend it to also match block-scalar indicators (`|`, `>`).

## Evidence

- **root cause of problem 1:** extensions/yaml/package.json sets `"editor.autoIndent": "advanced"` as a configurationDefault for `[yaml]`/`[dockercompose]`. src/vs/editor/common/languages/autoIndent.ts line 74 (`if (autoIndent < EditorAutoIndentStrategy.Full) return null;`) means `indentationRules` are ignored whenever autoIndent is below `Full`; `Advanced = 3` and `Full = 4` per src/vs/editor/common/config/editorOptions.ts:42-48.
