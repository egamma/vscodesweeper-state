[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234355](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/234355)

## Summary

Keep open as a mitigated Node.js/V8 address-space issue: the thread identifies a native CodeRange allocation failure under the HPC login node's 32 GB limit, not Copilot as the primary cause. Raising the limit restores operation but does not establish a runtime fix.

## Change summary

Remote extension-host activation exhausts the login node's virtual-address-space allowance and terminates the process when Copilot starts additional workers.

## Best solution

Establish an upstream Node.js/V8 diagnosis for the large virtual-memory reservations and verify constrained-address-space behavior with the currently bundled runtime. Retain the administrator-confirmed limit increase as a mitigation, not evidence that the underlying issue is fixed.
