# Issue #279415 — Scrolling through long single line files is terrible.

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/279415](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/279415)

## Summary

Keep open: current mouse hit-testing still maps movement above or below the document to its endpoints, explaining the abrupt selection jump in an unwrapped single-line file. Existing settings do not provide the requested tolerance, and the related reports inspected do not own this specific behavior.

## Change summary

Make mouse-drag selection in long, unwrapped single-line files tolerate small vertical deviations without jumping to the document endpoints.

## Best solution

Improve editor-core selection hit-testing so small vertical deviations during horizontal selection do not unexpectedly select to the start or end of a single-line document. Establish the intended tolerance while preserving deliberate selection to document boundaries and normal multiline selection.
