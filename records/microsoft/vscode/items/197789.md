# Issue #197789 — Shouldn't "integrate" the first view into its container icon when in the secondary side bar

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/197789](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/197789)

## Summary

A real UI-consistency gap remains on current main: a lone secondary-side-bar view is merged into its container and loses its visible view header. The straightforward layout change was rejected because it misaligned the sidebars and had received negative feedback, so the desired title treatment needs a maintainer design decision.

## Change summary

Keep the title and description of a single secondary-side-bar view visible instead of representing the view only through its container icon and tooltip.

## Best solution

Settle a consistent title treatment that preserves the identity of a lone view without misaligning the Primary and Secondary Side Bars or degrading the common Chat layout, then cover the single- and multi-view states with focused workbench tests.

  > With exactly one view in a Secondary Side Bar container, the view's title and relevant description must remain visibly discoverable without relying on an icon tooltip. The result must remain visually aligned with the Primary Side Bar and must work for the default Chat container.
- **Trace:**

  > Current main places the auxiliary composite bar in the title row for ActivityBarPosition.DEFAULT in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/auxiliarybar/auxiliaryBarPart.ts.
