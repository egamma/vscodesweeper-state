A real find-widget interaction gap remains on current main, with no fixing commit, canonical duplicate, or existing command covering horizontal wheel scrolling. Keep it open under discussion in the editor-find area.

Allow horizontally overflowing Find and Replace fields to respond to horizontal mouse-wheel input.
