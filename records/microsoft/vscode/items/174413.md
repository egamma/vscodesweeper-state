# Issue #174413 — When the window is too small IHoverService doesn't take into account width of item

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/174413](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/174413)

## Summary

The width-awareness gap remains in the mouse-anchored hover path: it reduces the target to a point, preventing context-view layout from avoiding the full target. The issue should remain open for a bounded workbench-hover fix.

## Change summary

When horizontal space is insufficient, place a workbench hover above or below its target instead of covering the target.

## Best solution

Make hover placement retain the target rectangle when deciding whether to flip from a horizontal position to above or below, while preserving intentional cursor-relative positioning and pointer alignment.
