# Issue #103487 — Text fails to drop after dragging a little too far to the left of the first column

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/103487](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/103487)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still permits insertion feedback for targets that the drop handler rejects. Two open PRs explicitly address this issue; their differing acceptance behavior needs review before either can resolve it.

## Change summary

Dragging selected text beyond the editor's left boundary leaves an insertion marker visible even though releasing the mouse does not drop the text.

## Best solution

Make the insertion marker accurately reflect whether releasing the dragged text will be accepted, preserving normal content and gutter drops. Review the two linked PRs to settle whether outside-editor drops should be accepted or their feedback hidden, then land the chosen solution.
