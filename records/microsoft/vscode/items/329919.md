# Issue #329919 — on narrow layout the third option is much wider

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329919](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/329919)

## Summary

Confirmed in source: the container-query CSS makes the third onboarding option grow to fill its own row when wrapped at narrow widths, and a maintainer has already acknowledged this is a known, not-ideal tradeoff — this is an active design discussion, not something to close.

## Change summary

On a narrow Agents window, the third new-session prompt option wraps onto its own row and stretches full-width instead of matching the width of the first two options.

## Best solution

In src/vs/sessions/contrib/chat/browser/media/newSessionPromptOptions.css, the `@container (max-width: 720px)` rule sets `flex-basis: calc((100% - gap) / 2)` for all three `.new-session-prompt-option` items, and since `flex: 1 1 basis` still carries `flex-grow: 1`, the lone third item on row two stretches to fill the full row width. A maintainer-owned fix would either cap flex-grow at that breakpoint (e.g. `flex-grow: 0` with a fixed basis) or center a same-width third item, per the reporter's suggestion — this is a design call already under discussion between the reporter and the feature's author, not a mechanical bug.
