# Issue #35744 — Customizable status bar item locations

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/35744](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/35744)

## Summary

Keep this accepted, well-supported request open: current status bar controls support hiding items, but not user-controlled ordering or moving items between sides. Related arrangement requests were explicitly consolidated here, and the canonical search found no older issue owning the same work.

## Change summary

Allow users to reorder status bar items and choose whether each appears on the left or right.

## Best solution

Provide supported, accessible customization of status bar item order and alignment in the workbench, preserving existing defaults for users who do not customize it. A maintainer should settle the interaction and persistence contract before implementation.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarModel.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarPart.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/workbench.contribution.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/test/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarModel.test.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/test/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarPart.test.ts`
- **Validation:** After the product contract is agreed, add status bar model/part tests for moving an item between sides, ordering items independently of contributor priorities, retaining customization when entries disappear and return, and restoring default behavior. Manually verify moving Git branch information right and cursor position/encoding left, including a keyboard-accessible interaction and the agreed reload behavior.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users want to choose the side and relative order of built-in and extension status bar items, such as placing Git branch information on the right and cursor position or encoding on the left. Hiding items alone does not satisfy this request. Acceptance should demonstrate cross-side movement and within-side ordering through a supported, keyboard-accessible surface, with uncustomized layouts unchanged. Persistence and the treatment of compact item groups must follow an explicitly agreed product contract.
- **Trace:**

  > StatusbarPart.addEntry() in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarPart.ts accepts contributor-supplied alignment and priority. StatusbarViewModel.sort() in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarModel.ts resolves numeric and relative priorities, while getEntries() partitions by alignment; the model currently persists only hidden item IDs. StatusbarPart.getContextMenuActions() exposes visibility and extension management, not rearrangement, and workbench.contribution.ts exposes whole-bar visibility rather than item locations. The model permits multiple entries with the same ID, and relative-location entries may form compact groups, so customization must define observable behavior for these cases. Existing ordering tests are in src/vs/workbench/test/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarModel.test.ts. Preserve contributor defaults, existing hiding behavior, and accessible focus navigation when no customization is applied; do not expand this into relocating the entire status bar or a general overflow redesign. Related requests https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/80102 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/83841 were consolidated into https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/35744.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should customization be exposed through settings, direct manipulation with keyboard equivalents, or both?
  - Should item locations persist per profile or per workspace, and should they synchronize across machines?
  - Should compact or relatively positioned item groups move together, or may users independently position their constituent items?
  - When multiple entries share an ID, should a location preference apply to all of them together or distinguish individual instances?
