Keep open for Windows multi-conversation focus investigation. Notification handling has changed since the report, but the supplied steps suffice to retest; neither a matching fix nor a qualifying canonical duplicate was established.

## Change summary

Background Copilot prompts must not redirect typing or confirmation keystrokes into another conversation.

## Best solution
