## Change summary

Have Agent Host agents discover overlapping work across repository worktrees and coordinate before editing or creating another session.

## Best solution

Establish and evaluate a metadata-first coordination workflow that identifies relevant peer sessions across worktrees, inspects plausible overlaps, and coordinates ownership without delaying unrelated work. Use the closed proposal as design context, not as evidence that this behavior shipped.
