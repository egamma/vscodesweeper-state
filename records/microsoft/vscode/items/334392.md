## Change summary

Copilot shows inconsistent change summaries and apparently repeats Keep prompts after reopening conversations whose edits were previously retained.

## Best solution

Establish whether explicitly accepted edits lose their review state when the conversation is reopened, distinguishing that from saving files or displaying historical changes. If confirmed, preserve acceptance consistently across chat editing restoration and session summaries.
