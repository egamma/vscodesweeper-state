# Issue #246094 — Leake disposable: RangesLimitReporter

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246094](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246094)

## Summary

Two fixes have landed addressing parts of this leak (PR #246125 in April 2025 for emitter disposal, and commit 9e7c19077e3 in September 2025 fixing the critical .dispose vs .dispose() typo in LanguageStatusEntry), but a team member commented in December 2025 that the issue persists. Keeping open for verification against current main.

## Change summary

Disposables created by subscribing to RangesLimitReporter/DecoratorLimitReporter onDidChange events in the limit indicator contribution are leaked.

## Best solution

Verify whether the September 2025 fix (commit 9e7c19077e3 — `.dispose` → `.dispose()` in LanguageStatusEntry) resolved the leak. If the GC-based disposable tracker still reports the leak, the remaining path is likely the subscription disposable becoming orphaned when the editor's FoldingController/ColorDetector is disposed before LanguageStatusEntry.onActiveEditorChanged runs again — fix by listening for editor disposal and eagerly clearing the listener.
