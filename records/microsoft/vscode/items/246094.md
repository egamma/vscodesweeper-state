# Issue #246094 — Leake disposable: RangesLimitReporter

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246094](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246094)

## Summary

The disposable warning persisted after the original fix and the issue was explicitly reopened. Current source still contains the implicated subscription path, while no post-reopen fix, canonical issue, or open fixing PR was found, so this should remain open for diagnosis.

## Change summary

Prevent folding-range and color-decorator limit-reporting event subscriptions from being garbage-collected without disposal.

## Best solution

Determine which editor or contribution lifecycle transition lets the subscription created by `LanguageStatusEntry.onActiveEditorChanged` become unreachable without disposal, then cover both folding and color reporters with a lifecycle regression test while preserving their status-indicator behavior.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/limitIndicator/browser/limitIndicator.contribution.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/folding/browser/folding.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/colorPicker/browser/colorDetector.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/limitIndicator/test/browser/limitIndicator.contribution.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Add and run a focused `LimitIndicatorContribution` test that installs disposable tracking, exercises active-editor replacement and teardown for folding and color limit reporters, and verifies that no listener disposable remains unowned; the existing status-indicator behavior must continue to pass.
- **Behavior:**

  > In a development build with disposable tracking enabled, creating, switching away from, and disposing editors with folding-range or color-decoration limit reporters must not produce `[LEAKED DISPOSABLE]` warnings. The language-status indicators must continue to update when limits change and disappear when they no longer apply. Acceptance must cover both `RangesLimitReporter` and `DecoratorLimitReporter`, including contribution teardown.
- **Trace:**

  > The warning is created by the subscription returned from `info.onDidChange` in `LanguageStatusEntry.onActiveEditorChanged` in `src/vs/workbench/contrib/limitIndicator/browser/limitIndicator.contribution.ts`. That class retains one subscription in `_indicatorChangeListener`, disposes it before replacement, and disposes it during contribution teardown. Commit 48cb4f823e7c81ea628e810dad56c57bc8ade92f made `RangesLimitReporter` and `DecoratorLimitReporter` disposable and registered their emitters and owning instances, but https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/246094#issuecomment-2970272580 reproduced both warnings after that commit, so the pre-reopen fix is insufficient. The uncertainty begins at the lifecycle or reentrant path that makes a subscription unreachable without invoking `LanguageStatusEntry.dispose`; diagnose that path before choosing an implementation. Keep the boundary within the limit-indicator and reporter lifecycle—do not weaken `GCBasedDisposableTracker` or change folding, color detection, or language-status semantics.
