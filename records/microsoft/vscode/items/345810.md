# Issue #345810 — Feature Request: Improve Queue Management in Local Harness

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345810](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345810)

## Summary

Keep open: current queue actions do not provide explicit reordering controls, and editing removes and requeues the message without retaining its position. The related collapse/expand request covers only part of this issue.

## Change summary

Provide alternatives to drag-and-drop queue reordering and preserve queued messages' positions when editing.

## Best solution

Keep queued-message edits order-preserving and establish a consistent, keyboard-accessible alternative to dragging long prompts. Coordinate the reordering experience with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326123 without losing the separate edit-position requirement.

## Evidence

- **Current reordering boundary:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatPendingDragAndDrop.ts:175–215 implements drag-based reordering through setPendingRequests, preserving steering-before-queued grouping; this does not supply the requested non-drag alternative.
- **Edit-position behavior:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/widget/chatWidget.ts:3959–3965 removes an edited pending request and retains its queue kind, not its index. chatServiceImpl.ts:1518 adds the replacement through ChatModel.addPendingRequest; chatModel.ts:2952–2973 appends ordinary queued messages. This supports the reported position change.
- **Related issue is narrower:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326123 is open and requests collapsing long queued prompts to facilitate reordering. Its comments, including https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/326123#issuecomment-5430082434, reiterate reordering difficulty but do not cover preserving position after editing; it cannot absorb this report's full scope.
