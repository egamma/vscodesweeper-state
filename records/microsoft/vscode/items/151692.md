Current main explicitly translates an unqualified `.gitignore` entry such as `bin` to match directories at any depth, but the old report does not provide enough detail to determine whether a distinct mismatch remains. The reporter should confirm the behavior on the latest build with a concrete directory tree.

## Change summary

Make Explorer exclusions produced by `explorer.excludeGitIgnore` follow Git's ignore semantics rather than the different glob semantics of `files.exclude`.
