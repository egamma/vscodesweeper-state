[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342635](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/342635)

## Summary

Keep open: the Worktree Cleanup table still omits workspace identity, requiring users to open sessions to identify their parent workspace. The related cross-surface worktree discussion explicitly preserves narrower reports rather than replacing them.

## Change summary
