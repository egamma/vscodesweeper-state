# Issue #203806 — Editor Highlight text only shows 999 instances

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/203806](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/203806)

## Summary

Still present on main: the 999-match limit in `LIMIT_FIND_COUNT` (textModel.ts:125) is unchanged, so selection highlighting still caps at 999 instances. A team member confirmed the root cause and expressed interest in investigating improvements.

## Change summary

Editor selection highlighting stops after 999 matches due to a hardcoded limit in the text model's findMatches function.

## Best solution

Explore raising the limit or implementing viewport-relative highlighting so matches near the selection are always highlighted, even when total matches exceed 999. The fix lives in the selection highlighter's call into `findMatches` and the `LIMIT_FIND_COUNT` constant in `src/vs/editor/common/model/textModel.ts`.
