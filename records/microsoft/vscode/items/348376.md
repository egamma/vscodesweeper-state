# Issue #348376 — Agents window: allow arranging/moving the side bars (sessions list and Files/Changes panel)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348376](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348376)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: current Agents-window layout explicitly prevents swapping the sidebars or moving views between them. Existing Details-position controls do not cover this request, and the suggested canonical concerns arranging conversations rather than sidebars.

## Change summary

Allow users to rearrange the Agents-window sessions sidebar and Files/Changes panel, with the chosen arrangement retained across reloads.

## Best solution

Consider a supported, persistent sidebar arrangement in the Sessions-owned desktop layout, preserving the current default. Decide whether swapping the two sides suffices or whether individual views should also be movable.
