# Issue #230122 — Inconsistent button order between editor and panel

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230122](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/230122)

## Summary

The differing order is intentional: panel and side-bar close actions operate on the whole container, while editor close operates on the view inside its container. The established design keeps global container actions after view actions and visually separates the two scopes.

## Change summary

Make the overflow and close buttons use the same order in editor, panel, and secondary side-bar headers.

## Best solution

Keep container-wide controls at the trailing edge after view-specific actions, with the existing separator communicating the scope boundary; do not reorder them to imitate the editor's view-close control.

## Evidence
