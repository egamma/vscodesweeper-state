## Summary

Keep open: current source still fails to restore editors revealed in other groups during Quick Open symbol preview. The linked fix PR was closed without merging, and the maintainers' agreed Solution C remains unimplemented.

## Change summary

Quick Open symbol previews change another split's scroll position or visible tab and leave that reference view changed after acceptance or cancellation.

## Best solution

Complete Solution C: preserve existing cross-group symbol previews and final-opening behavior, but restore the other groups' original visible editors and view states and clean up temporary editors when accepting or explicitly cancelling Quick Open.
