# Issue #135453 — LSP `textDocument/documentSymbol` is delayed by ~350ms

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/135453](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/135453)

## Summary

The 350ms minimum remains on current main, and no canonical issue owns the request to make that floor adaptive. Keep this open under discussion while balancing responsiveness against outline stability and provider load.

## Change summary

Allow fast document-symbol providers to refresh sooner than the fixed 350ms minimum after edits.

## Best solution

Evaluate a lower or adaptive minimum in the outline debounce service while preserving protection against transient syntax states and excessive provider requests; coordinate with the open LSP cancellation proposal where appropriate.
