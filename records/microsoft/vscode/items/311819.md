Keep open: multiple reporters confirm first-keystroke loss after switching away with Alt+Tab under KDE Wayland, including in the integrated terminal. No verified fix or canonical duplicate was found; the event sequence needs diagnosis before attributing it to Electron or changing menu handling.

## Change summary
