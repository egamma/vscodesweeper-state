[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209132](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/209132)

## Summary

Keep open: changing canSelectMany after displaying populated items still leaves the list's item models in single-selection mode. The maintainer-confirmed reproduction has a concrete failing path in current source, and no verified canonical issue absorbs it.

## Change summary

Changing a visible, populated QuickPick to multi-selection should immediately display working checkboxes with consistent selectedItems.
