## Evidence

- **Verified older canonical:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/114866 is open and was created on 2021-01-24, before this report. Its body describes shrinking a long output while viewing its bottom, retaining the old scroll coordinates, and consequently showing cells much farther down the notebook.
- **Same re-execution symptom:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/114866#issuecomment-768134021 explicitly reports that re-executing a cell with long output removes that output, moves the viewport much farther down the notebook, and requires scrolling back. This matches the observable re-execution failure here, rather than merely sharing a notebook area or a speculative root cause.
- **Maintainer expectation:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/200981#issuecomment-2610788784 recommends Ctrl+Enter for remaining on the executing cell, but separately states that the next focused cell should remain anchored when the notebook resizes. The workaround does not resolve that acknowledged defect.
- **Current source path:** ExecuteCellSelectBelow in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/controller/executeActions.ts focuses the next cell in container mode before execution. Output-height changes reach NotebookCellLayoutManager.layoutNotebookCell and NotebookCellList.updateElementHeight2. NotebookCellAnchor.shouldAnchor in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/browser/view/notebookCellAnchor.ts rejects negative height deltas for a container-focused cell, so shrinking an output intersecting the viewport falls through without a focused-cell anchor.
- **Existing coverage and inspection limits:** The 'updateElementHeight with anchor' test in src/vs/workbench/contrib/notebook/test/browser/notebookCellList.test.ts explicitly expects no anchoring when cells shrink; notebookCellAnchor.test.ts likewise expects false for a negative delta without editor focus. This supports the shared shrink-anchoring gap. No runtime reproduction was performed, and the shallow checkout exposes no usable historical fixing commit.
- **Other older reports distinguished:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/196373 concerns the initial Shift+Enter reveal policy, while https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/142439 concerns jumping during manual upward scrolling. Neither is the selected canonical; the re-execution comment on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/114866 supplies the matching symptom required for this re-review.
