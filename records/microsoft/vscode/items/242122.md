[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/242122](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/242122)

## Summary

The tab-width instability remains on main, and maintainers have identified tabular numeric spacing as the appropriate polish. This team-authored issue should remain open until that change lands.

## Change summary

Prevent diagnostic-count decorations from shifting editor-tab contents when their digits change.

## Best solution

Apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to numeric file-decoration badges in editor tabs, likely through a tab-scoped rule in `multieditortabscontrol.css`, without changing badge behavior elsewhere.
