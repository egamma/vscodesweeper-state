# Issue #218939 — Inline diff is at wrong position

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/218939](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/218939)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still anchors the quick diff peek after the first visual segment of a wrapped line, rather than at the deletion boundary. No existing setting covers the requested placement, and the closest related reports do not qualify as duplicate canonicals.

## Change summary

Place the deletion-only SCM quick diff peek after the entire preceding wrapped line, immediately before the next surviving line.

## Best solution

Make the quick diff peek respect the logical deletion boundary when the preceding line wraps, within the SCM quick diff widget. Preserve other peek widgets' position-based anchoring and existing change-navigation behavior.
