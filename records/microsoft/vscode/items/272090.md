# Issue #272090 — Suggest triggers when deleting code

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272090](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/272090)

## Summary

Confirmed and under active investigation by jrieken. Quick suggestions incorrectly trigger on deletion because `_onCursorChange` treats a selection-collapse from `deleteLeft` as a rightward cursor move, firing `_doTriggerQuickSuggest()`.

## Change summary

Auto-complete popup incorrectly appears when deleting selected text that leaves a tag like `<div>`.

## Best solution

In `suggestModel.ts` `_onCursorChange`, the condition at line 366 (`prevSelection.containsRange(currentSelection)`) should not trigger quick suggest when the cursor moved LEFT due to deletion of a selection — only when the cursor moved right due to typing. The fix likely belongs in `src/vs/editor/contrib/suggest/browser/suggestModel.ts`.
