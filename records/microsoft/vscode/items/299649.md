# Issue #299649 — Resource label during dnd are clipped

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/299649](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/299649)

## Summary

Still reproduces on main: the `.monaco-drag-image` CSS in `src/vs/base/browser/ui/dnd/dnd.css` applies `max-width: 120px` with `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis`, clipping most resource labels during drag. No fix has landed since the issue was filed.

## Change summary

Resource labels are clipped and faded during drag-and-drop from the explorer over the editor area due to an aggressive 120px max-width on the drag image element.

## Best solution

Increase or remove the `max-width: 120px` on `.monaco-drag-image` in `src/vs/base/browser/ui/dnd/dnd.css`. The comment notes that browsers apply visual effects to large drag images — a wider limit (e.g. 300–400px) would accommodate typical filenames while still avoiding the browser rendering issue. The opacity/fade appearance may also be browser-applied on the oversized element.
