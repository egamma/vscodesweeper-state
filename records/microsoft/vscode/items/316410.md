[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316410](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/316410)

## Summary

Enterprise marketplace allowlisting now exists through managed settings, but the specifically requested ADMX-backed marketplace policy is absent. Keep open for a decision on the remaining deployment/template requirement, not for new allowlist enforcement.

## Change summary

Provide an enterprise policy for configuring approved plugin marketplace sources without modifying personal settings.

## Best solution

Reuse strictKnownMarketplaces for restrictions and extraKnownMarketplaces for managed additions. Resolve whether the enterprise policy templates should expose this existing functionality, rather than introduce a parallel marketplace authorization mechanism.
