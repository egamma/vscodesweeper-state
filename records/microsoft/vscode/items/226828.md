Keep open: the proposed API still exposes the separate ignore-file switches and exclude-setting modes questioned here. The maintainer explains their rationale but does not settle the simplification request; no verified canonical duplicate or issue-referencing fix was found.

## Change summary

Simplify file-search filtering options so extension authors can choose user-configured filtering or unfiltered enumeration without reasoning about individual ignore-file locations.

## Best solution
