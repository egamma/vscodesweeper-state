- **Behavior:**

  > On iPadOS, command-bearing status-bar items at the first and last positions should respond to touch, including the notification bell at the bottom-right corner when the software keyboard is hidden. Maintainers reproduced failures at those positions even after general status-bar touch handling was repaired. Acceptance requires successful corner-item activation without duplicate commands, while interior items and external-mouse activation continue working.
- **Trace:**

  > StatusbarEntryItem in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/statusbar/statusbarItem.ts registers labelContainer with Gesture.addTarget and executes commands from both click and TouchEventType.Tap listeners. These are the changes introduced by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/commit/fcbf1eb903ad019fbd003d754067d0728d79d498, after which https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/125686#issuecomment-1047133404 explicitly reports that the original corner problem remains. The uncertainty starts at whether current iPadOS delivers the expected touch sequence at those screen positions; source inspection cannot establish that. Preserve the existing general touch support and avoid changing shared gesture semantics without evidence that they cause the corner-specific failure.
