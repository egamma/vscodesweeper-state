# Issue #345619 — Needs attention unclear actions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345619](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345619)

## Summary

Keep open: Worktree Cleanup already offers conditional recovery actions, but they do not cover every Needs attention state. The remaining request is to make those rows understandable and actionable without weakening cleanup safeguards.

## Change summary

Provide clear inspection, verification, or recovery actions for worktrees listed under Needs attention.

## Best solution

Make each Needs attention row explain its restriction and offer an appropriate next step in the cleanup editor. Define inspection and stale-entry handling separately from destructive removal, preserving existence checks and confirmation requirements.
