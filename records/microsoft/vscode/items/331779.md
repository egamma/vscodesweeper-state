# Issue #331779 — Panels are right shifted in the window.

## Summary

Real visual bug in the still-in-development Modern UI Update experiment: panels render right-shifted with a thicker left border and no right border. A contributor's workaround (disabling `workbench.experimental.modernUI`) was confirmed by the reporter, pinning the root cause to that feature rather than being a general workbench regression.

## Change summary
