Keep open to investigate the reported loss of both conversations after forking. Current main distinguishes destructive deletion from non-destructive release, but source inspection does not establish what triggered deletion here; the supplied steps, timeline, and disk observations suffice to start a current-main reproduction.

## Change summary

Forking a Copilot CLI conversation reportedly causes both the source and fork to lose their persisted history and become permanently unopenable.

## Best solution

Ensure that forking, client reconnection, and idle release preserve both conversations and their durable history, while explicit deletion remains destructive. Diagnose the initiating lifecycle operation before treating the misleading restore error as the primary defect.
