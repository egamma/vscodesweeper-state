- **Remaining CLI behavior:** The November 4 contributor comment on https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/345493 attributes blank ═║ rendering to Copilot CLI and promises notification when its fix becomes available for testing. The discussion does not establish a delivered, verified fix.
- **Settings do not resolve the entire report:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/common/terminalConfiguration.ts:597–613 exposes customGlyphs and rescaleOverlappingGlyphs, both enabled by default.
