# Issue #47661 — Add a toggle to maximize a single view

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/47661](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/47661)

## Summary

Keep open: editor-group maximization exists, but temporarily maximizing one terminal split and restoring its previous layout remains unsupported in the inspected source. This accepted Backlog request has 78 upvotes and maintainer-confirmed duplicates; existing panel maximization does not cover its central ask.

## Change summary

Provide a reversible toggle that temporarily maximizes the focused terminal split or view without losing the previous layout.

## Best solution

Deliver reversible terminal-split maximization that preserves sessions, split membership, and prior sizes, consistent with existing editor-group maximization. Resolve whether the remaining work should initially cover terminal splits or provide a shared arbitrary-view interaction before implementation.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalGroup.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalActions.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/common/terminal.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/layout.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/editorPart.ts`
- **Validation:** In a running VS Code build, create three terminal splits with unequal sizes and focus the middle split. Invoke the agreed toggle and verify that only the focused terminal occupies the agreed maximized area and its terminal dimensions update. Toggle again and verify that split order, prior sizes, focus, and running sessions are preserved. Repeat with the panel at the bottom and at the side; existing editor-group and whole-panel maximization must remain unchanged.
- **Behavior:**

  > The focused terminal split can temporarily occupy the maximized area while sibling splits are hidden, without closing or regrouping their sessions. Invoking the same toggle restores the previous split arrangement and sizes, and terminal content responds to the changed dimensions. Acceptance includes unequal split sizes and both horizontal and vertical arrangements. Whether maximization stops at the terminal container or also hides other workbench parts remains a product decision.
- **Trace:**

  > SplitPaneContainer in src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalGroup.ts manages terminal splits with SplitView; its current resize operations retain siblings and do not store a reversible zoom state. TerminalGroup.setVisible affects all instances, and TerminalGroup.layout forwards dimensions to the split container. TerminalGroupService.unsplitInstance changes group membership, so it is not the requested temporary operation. Existing editor-group maximization uses EditorPart.toggleMaximizeGroup and gridWidget.exitMaximizedView, whereas Layout.toggleMaximizedPanel operates on the entire panel. Preserve terminal processes, split membership, normal resize behavior, and existing editor/panel commands. The uncertainty begins at the public scope and maximization extent: https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/47661 starts with terminal splits, while maintainer comments broaden it to editors and possibly arbitrary views.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should the initial capability target terminal splits, or expose one focused-view maximization interaction across terminal splits and other workbench views?
  - Should terminal-split maximization fill its existing terminal container, or also hide other workbench parts to fill the window, restoring those parts on the second toggle?
