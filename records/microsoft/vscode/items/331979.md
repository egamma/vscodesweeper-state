## Change summary

After an OS-level Alt-Tab away and back, a tab's close button can stay stuck in its 'Close Other Editors' swapped state even though Alt is no longer held, causing an unintended mass-close on click.

## Best solution

Land the fix in src/vs/workbench/browser/parts/editor/multiEditorTabsControl.ts — clear `hoveredTabIndex` on window blur (mirroring ModifierKeyEmitter's existing altKey reset on blur), or require a fresh in-focus Alt keydown to re-arm the 'Close Others' swap — then let the verification-found workflow confirm the fix before closing.
