[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346240](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346240)

## Summary

The per-chunk reasoning fragmentation is fixed in current main: untimed BYOK deltas are merged while streaming, not merely after completion. The later stable-version reports describe the old splitting condition, which the cited fix replaces.

## Change summary

Aggregate streamed BYOK reasoning into one collapsible block instead of creating a separate timed block for each token.
