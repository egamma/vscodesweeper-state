## Summary

Keep open: current source confirms that valid headless Judge submissions are rejected, while malformed submissions receive feedback that omits the offending field. These are distinct, actionable failures, not duplicates of the parent test plan.

## Change summary

Compare Models cannot reliably persist Judge verdicts because headless calls lose participant recognition and invalid-input feedback does not identify required corrections.

## Best solution
