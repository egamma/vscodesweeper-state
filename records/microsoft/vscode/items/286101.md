Keep open: current source still allows closing the original repository to dispose the worktree that Close Other Repositories explicitly selected to retain. The related reports do not own this symptom, and no qualifying fixing commit was found for this team-authored issue.

## Change summary

Close Other Repositories invoked on a worktree also closes the selected worktree through its original repository's disposal.
