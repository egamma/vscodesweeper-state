## Summary

Keep open: existing cloud session sync and local indexing are relevant capabilities, but the inspected source does not establish searchable remote-harness transcript retention after permanent teardown. No canonical duplicate was verified.

## Change summary

Provide opt-in retention of remote Copilot transcripts that remain readable and discoverable through past-conversation search after the remote environment is deleted.

Evaluate remote-harness coverage of existing session sync and search before adding another archive system.
