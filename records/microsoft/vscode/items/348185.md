# Issue #348185 — Preserve searchable Copilot harness conversations after remote environment teardown

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348185](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348185)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: existing export and host-side archival do not establish searchable conversation retention after the remote host and its storage are destroyed. No matching canonical issue was verified.

## Change summary

Provide opt-in retention of remote Copilot transcripts that remain readable and discoverable through past-conversation search after remote teardown.

## Best solution

Define a durable, independently stored transcript archive integrated with harness history search, preserving source identity and distinguishing archived reading from executable resumption. Specify retention, deletion, policy enforcement, synchronization completeness, and failure reporting before implementation.
