# Issue #348185 — Preserve searchable Copilot harness conversations after remote environment teardown

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348185](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/348185)

## Summary

Keep open as a feature request: existing indexing, cloud sync, and saved-chat reading do not establish the requested client-retained, harness-searchable archive after permanent remote teardown.

## Change summary

Retain remote Copilot conversations independently of disposable environments, with searchable transcripts, provenance, explicit archive status, and retention controls.

## Best solution

Evaluate durable remote-history retention alongside existing Chronicle indexing and session sync, preserving a clear distinction between reading retained history and resuming remote execution. Establish whether those mechanisms can satisfy teardown-safe retrieval before designing a separate client archive.
