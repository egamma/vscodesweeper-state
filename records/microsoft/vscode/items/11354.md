# Issue #11354 — Editor selection colors should be simplified

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/11354](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/11354)

## Summary

Keep open for a focused UX decision on selection and highlight hierarchy. Current theme tokens and settings address several original requests, but do not establish that overlapping states have been simplified; the related open reports are newer and cannot replace this re-reviewed item.

## Change summary

Simplify overlapping selection, search-match, and symbol-highlight colors so users can reliably distinguish their actual selection.

## Best solution

Agree on a theme-compatible visual hierarchy that keeps actual selections identifiable when search and occurrence highlights overlap, then validate representative combinations across light, dark, and high-contrast themes. Preserve the thread's settled decision to use VS Code-specific theme properties rather than pursue Sublime theme equivalence.
