# Issue #296652 — Feedback on tips in chat

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296652](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/296652)

## Summary

Keep open for a focused design decision: current tips address prioritization and session dismissal, but still occupy the empty-chat composer rather than resolving the placement and attention concerns. This team-authored feedback has maintainer support, and neither a fully covering fix referencing this issue nor an eligible older duplicate was established.

## Change summary

Make chat tips less intrusive, better timed to the user's learning journey, and clearer about dismissal and future appearances.

## Best solution

Agree on tip placement and frequency that preserve prompt entry as the primary task, then address the remaining UX gap while retaining existing eligibility, prioritization, and disable controls. Coordinate the contextual-placement design with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298997 without treating that younger, narrower request as a duplicate target.

- **Behavior:**

  > Chat guidance should support progressive learning without competing with starting a session. Relevant tips should appear when their action is useful, and dismissal should communicate whether only the current tip, the session's tips, or all future tips are affected. Current prioritization and session dismissal already address part of the feedback. Acceptance must cover the chosen placement, recurrence across sessions, dismissal clarity, and continued ability to disable tips.
- **Trace:**

  > ChatWidget.isGettingStartedTipEligible restricts getting-started tips to the empty state and constructs ChatInputTipPresenter using the input's tip container. ChatInputTipPresenter docks ChatTipContentPart within the input stack and yields to higher-precedence notices. ChatTipService._selectTipByTier prioritizes foundational tips; dismissTipForSession combines persistent dismissal of the current tip with suppression for the session, while disableTips changes chat.tips.enabled. ChatTipContentPart exposes navigation, dismissal, and disable actions. The source establishes current behavior, not a conclusively broken implementation; the uncertainty is the desired placement and cadence. Preserve existing eligibility and disable behavior, and do not turn the speculative instruction-file discussion into a separate bug without reproduction. Coordinate with the open contextual-placement request at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298997 and account for the completed ordering follow-up at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/297018.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should general onboarding tips remain at the empty-chat composer, move into an agent response, or appear at the relevant feature surface, given the tradeoff between prompt-entry distraction and tips being missed?
  - Should tip recurrence remain per conversation, be limited by a daily or other spaced-learning cadence, or depend on onboarding milestones?
  - Is the current icon-toolbar dismissal and disable presentation sufficient, or should these distinct outcomes be explained with visible text actions?
