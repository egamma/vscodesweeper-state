# Issue #304054 — Forking a chat should include an affordance

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/304054](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/304054)

## Summary

Keep open: fork titles and internal lineage metadata do not provide the requested link back to the original chat. Current source has a source-navigation affordance for side chats, but that path explicitly excludes ordinary forks.

## Change summary

Show a provenance affordance in a forked conversation that identifies and links to its original chat.

## Best solution

Provide a quiet, keyboard-accessible source-chat affordance for forks, with durable lineage across reopening. Coordinate the Copilot CLI metadata boundary and shared chat presentation, preserving existing fork history, titles, and session-list placement.

- **Validation:** Fork a Copilot CLI conversation both from a checkpoint and with /fork; verify the new conversation identifies its immediate source and activating the affordance by mouse or keyboard opens that source. Reopen the fork and verify provenance remains available. Confirm copied history, original-chat contents, top-level session placement, and existing side-chat navigation remain unchanged.
- **Behavior:**

  > After forking a conversation, the fork should visibly identify its original chat and offer a direct way to return to it. A 'Forked:' title alone does not meet this requirement. Acceptance should cover checkpoint and /fork entry points, keyboard activation, reopening the fork, and unchanged original conversation contents.
- **Trace:**

  > CopilotCLISessionService.forkSession persists lineage through ChatSessionMetadataStore.storeForkedSessionMetadata, including parentSessionId and kind: 'forked'. CopilotCLIChatSessionsContribution reads that lineage but only exposes parent linkage for sub-sessions, intentionally leaving forks at the top level. Shared ForkConversationAction opens the new conversation without constructing a source-link affordance. ChatRequestOriginPart supplies a neighboring source-navigation surface, but SessionsSideChatProviderContribution.observeSideChatOrigin explicitly excludes ordinary forks. The unresolved boundary is how durable fork provenance reaches shared rendering across the applicable providers; side-chat support alone is not coverage. Preserve existing fork history, titles, top-level list placement, and side-chat behavior. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/304246 was consolidated into this issue and supports the parent-navigation goal.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should fork provenance appear at the copied-history boundary, as suggested in the report, or as a persistent conversation-level source indicator?
