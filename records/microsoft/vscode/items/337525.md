- **Trace:**

  > The action passes model.url to IOpenerService.open with openExternal: true. For non-http(s) URLs that method calls shell.openExternal. I couldn't determine where the failure occurs without running it. The first step is to reproduce and read the error or log. Don't change behavior for http(s) URLs.

- **main process:** NativeHostMainService.openExternal in src/vs/platform/native/electron-main/nativeHostMainService.ts sends only http(s) URLs through openExternalBrowser. Everything else, including file:, goes to shell.openExternal.
