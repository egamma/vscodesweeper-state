# Issue #298144 — Show when customizations are extension-contributed

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298144](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298144)

## Summary

Current main identifies extension-provided prompt customizations through an Extensions group and per-item provenance hovers, but does not provide the requested per-extension customization off switch. Keep this team-authored request open; the related reports do not establish an older canonical covering its full scope.

## Change summary

Identify which extension contributes each customization and let users quickly turn off that extension's customizations.

## Best solution

Complete extension-level customization management in the shared Chat Customizations editor, retaining existing provenance display and providing reversible control over a contributing extension's customizations. First settle whether turning them off means hiding them from selection or preventing their use by agents, without disabling unrelated extension functionality.

- **Validation:** With two extensions contributing multiple customizations, verify their provenance is identifiable and turning off one extension's customizations applies the agreed visibility or runtime semantics to all its contributions, leaves the other extension and unrelated extension functionality unchanged, and is reversible. Add focused regression tests in the customization list/action suites once the semantics are agreed.
- **Behavior:**

  > Users can identify the extension responsible for a contributed customization and quickly turn off customizations from that extension. Existing provenance hovers partially satisfy identification, but individual built-in-skill controls do not satisfy extension-wide control. Acceptance must demonstrate reversible control across several contributions from one extension without affecting another extension's contributions. The maintainer must first define whether off changes presentation, runtime availability, or both.
- **Trace:**

  > AICustomizationItemRenderer.renderElement in src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/aiCustomization/aiCustomizationListWidget.ts reads extensionId for provenance hovers, while groupMatchedItems groups extension contributions together rather than by contributing extension. aiCustomizationManagement.disableItem and enableItem in aiCustomizationManagement.contribution.ts operate on individual prompt URIs; their menu conditions expose them only for built-in skills. isUserToggleableCustomization in common/promptSyntax/service/promptsService.ts reflects that same boundary. No extension-wide control was found in these paths; the trace has not established how a new control would propagate across every customization type and harness. Preserve unrelated extension functionality and distinguish presentation filtering from runtime disablement. Coordinate with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/304847 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/290290 rather than treating their narrower scopes as completion of this request.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should turning off an extension's customizations hide them from the customization/agent selection UI, prevent agents from consuming them, or do both?
  - Should the extension-wide choice apply globally across projects and harnesses, or only within a selected workspace or harness?
