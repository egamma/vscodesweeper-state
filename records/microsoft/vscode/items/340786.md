## Change summary

Dragging the terminal panel vertically while running an interactive TUI causes severe stuttering and temporary window unresponsiveness.

## Best solution

Establish the resize/output bottleneck in the terminal layout-to-PTY path, then bound redundant resize work while preserving responsive TUI rendering and correct final dimensions. Cover small buffers, split terminals, hidden terminals, and simultaneous row/column changes.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalResizeDebouncer.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalInstance.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/xterm/xtermTerminal.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalProcessManager.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/test/browser/terminalInstance.test.ts`
- **Validation:** Run a SIGWINCH-handling TUI in one terminal and then two split terminals; continuously drag the panel vertically across several dozen rows while recording renderer responsiveness and resize-call timing. Verify no multi-second UI stall or unresponsive-window dialog, and that both xterm and the child process reach the final dimensions. Add deterministic scheduling checks for rapid resize bursts, buffers below and above 200 lines, combined column/row changes, explicit immediate resizing, visibility changes, and disposal.
- **Behavior:**

  > Continuous vertical resizing of the integrated terminal while an interactive TUI is running must not make the workbench unresponsive. The terminal display and child process must converge to the final panel dimensions after the gesture. Acceptance includes single and split terminals, small and large buffers, and simultaneous width/height changes, without introducing persistent rendering corruption or lost final updates.
- **Trace:**

  > TerminalInstance.layout reaches _resize, which calls TerminalResizeDebouncer.resize. The debouncer immediately invokes the row callback for visible large-buffer terminals, and immediately invokes the combined callback for buffers shorter than 200 lines; the constructor callbacks in terminalInstance.ts resize xterm and forward dimensions to the process manager. XtermTerminal.resize delegates directly to raw.resize, while TerminalProcessManager forwards ready-process updates without rate limiting. These paths support the reporter's scheduling concern but do not independently prove the claimed signal/redraw/flushSync feedback loop; profiling the supplied reproduction is the first diagnostic step. Preserve explicit immediate resizing, hidden-terminal restoration, disposal safety, final dimension correctness, and horizontal reflow behavior. The related flicker work cited at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/330901 and https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/328100 is not a verified resolution of this hang.

- **Current scheduling behavior:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/terminal/browser/terminalResizeDebouncer.ts calls _resizeYCallback(rows) immediately for visible terminals with normal buffers of at least 200 lines, while only columns use the 100 ms scheduler.
