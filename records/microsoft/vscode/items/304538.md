Keep open: a maintainer confirmed the disappearing/resetting pickers, although they could not reproduce the duplicate CLI sessions. Current source still exposes a provider/model initialization mismatch worth investigating; no issue-referencing fix or qualifying canonical duplicate was established.

## Change summary

Reopening the welcome page can lose Copilot CLI picker state, and submitting a worktree chat can produce duplicate session-list entries.

## Best solution

Make reopened welcome-page CLI chats retain consistent provider, session-model, and picker state, with one session-list entry per submitted session. Diagnose the duplication separately rather than assuming it shares the picker defect.
