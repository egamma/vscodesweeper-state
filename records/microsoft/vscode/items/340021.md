## Evidence

- **attached mapping mismatch:** The attachments supplied in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340021#issuecomment-6037856479 both identify layout es, model pc105, group 0. However, vscode-key-layout.bad.txt has US raw mappings: Digit7.withShift='&', BracketLeft.value='[', and Backquote.value='`'. The good report instead has Digit7.withShift='/' and Spanish punctuation mappings.
- **shortcut outcomes explained:** The bad attachment resolves ctrl+/ to ctrl+[Slash], ctrl+[ to ctrl+[BracketLeft], and ctrl+` to ctrl+[Backquote]. The good attachment resolves ctrl+/ to ctrl+shift+[Digit7] and ctrl+` to ctrl+[BracketLeft]. These differences explain why the Spanish terminal shortcut instead invokes outdent and the comment shortcut stops working.
- **native acquisition and caching path:** src/vs/platform/keyboardLayout/electron-main/keyboardLayoutMainService.ts reads native-keymap.getKeyMap() and getCurrentKeyboardLayout() separately, caches their results, and refreshes on native layout-change notifications. src/vs/workbench/services/keybinding/electron-browser/nativeKeyboardLayoutService.ts maintains renderer-side mapping state; nativeKeyboardLayout.ts caches the resulting mapper and its validateCurrentKeyboardMapping() is a no-op. This identifies the diagnostic boundary, but does not prove which component produced the incorrect snapshot.
- **mapping consumer:** src/vs/workbench/services/keybinding/common/macLinuxKeyboardMapper.ts consumes rawMapping.value, withShift, withAltGr, and withShiftAltGr to construct shortcut mappings. src/vs/workbench/services/keybinding/browser/keybindingService.ts refreshes its mapper and resolver on layout-change notifications, consistent with the reporter's recovery after a delayed US-to-Spanish switch.

Its maintainer discussion addresses custom remappings and immutable scan codes, not intermittent US raw maps for an ordinary Spanish layout. Shared keyboard infrastructure does not establish the same root cause.
