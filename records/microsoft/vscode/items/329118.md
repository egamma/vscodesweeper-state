## Summary

Active, well-diagnosed investigation: a VS Code collaborator traced the CPU/memory spike to continuous xterm WebGL glyph-atlas rasterization via a captured performance trace, and has handed off to the terminal owner for further root-cause work — this should stay open and not be closed prematurely.

## Change summary

In a Remote-SSH window, the renderer process enters sustained >100% CPU and grows to 10+ GB physical footprint with thousands of IOSurface regions, apparently due to continuous terminal WebGL glyph-atlas invalidation/rasterization.

## Best solution

Terminal team (per the assignment to @anthonykim1) should determine why the xterm WebglRenderer/TextureAtlas is repeatedly rasterizing glyphs (rapid atlas invalidation) in this scenario, and whether the fix belongs in VS Code's terminal integration (e.g. render/debounce triggers) or needs to be reported upstream in xterm.js's addon-webgl; confirming whether `terminal.integrated.gpuAcceleration: off` avoids the issue (already asked in the thread) will help scope the fix.
