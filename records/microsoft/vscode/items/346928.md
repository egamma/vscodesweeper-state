# Issue #346928 — Inbox mode: Empty sections have no chevron and cannot be expanded

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346928](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/346928)

## Summary

The missing chevron and disabled expansion are explicitly intentional for empty Inbox categories, as documented in the linked test plan and asserted by a dedicated test. Propose closing as designed rather than treating this difference as broken behavior.

## Change summary

Make empty Inbox categories expandable with chevrons, matching empty chats and groups.

## Best solution

Retain the intentional empty-category behavior: show expansion controls once a category contains sessions, without changing ordinary chats or groups.
