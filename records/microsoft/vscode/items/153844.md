# Issue #153844 — Line Has Inline Suggestion Audio Cue — Pain Point Solution

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/153844](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/153844)

## Summary

Keep open: current inline-suggestion signals have limited deduplication, but no typing-rate-based suppression or quieter repeat cue. The maintainer accepted investigating this improvement, and no verified older canonical covers the same symptom.

## Change summary

Reduce distracting inline-suggestion audio repetition during typing through rate limiting or less intrusive repeated sounds.

## Best solution

Preserve useful inline-suggestion notifications while reducing rapid repeated playback in the inline-completions accessibility path. A maintainer should settle whether repeated suggestions are suppressed or remain audible with a quieter variant, then establish a measurable timing contract.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/controller/inlineCompletionsController.ts`, `src/vs/platform/accessibilitySignal/browser/accessibilitySignalService.ts`, `src/vs/workbench/contrib/accessibility/browser/accessibilityConfiguration.ts`, `src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/test/browser/inlineCompletions.test.ts`
- **Validation:** After choosing the repeat policy and thresholds, add a fake-clock controller test with a changing inline-completion provider and recorded accessibility signal calls. Exercise rapid same-line typing, a pause followed by a fresh suggestion, and unchanged suggestion identity; assert the selected playback budget or variant behavior. Manually check audible feedback with accessibility.signals.lineHasInlineSuggestion.sound enabled.
- **Behavior:**

  > When inline suggestions update repeatedly while typing on one line, audio should remain informative without producing distracting rapid repetitions. The first useful suggestion indication should remain available, and fresh feedback after a pause should not be permanently suppressed. Acceptance must specify a measurable repetition window and whether repeated indications are omitted or use a less intrusive sound. Existing cue enablement and ordinary suggestion acceptance must continue to work.
- **Trace:**

  > InlineCompletionsController in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/controller/inlineCompletionsController.ts schedules accessibility playback on changes to currentInlineCompletionBySemanticId, with a cancellable 50 ms wait and suggest-widget coordination. InlineSuggestionItemBase.semanticId in src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/model/inlineSuggestionItem.ts hashes the suggestion action, so changed replacement text or range can generate a fresh signal on the same line. AccessibilitySignalService.playSound prevents overlapping copies of a sound but has no repetition-window policy. The configurable getDelayMs path is used by editorTextPropertySignalsContribution.ts, not this inline-completion playback call. Preserve cue enablement, suggestion behavior, screen-reader announcement behavior, and unrelated signals; do not assume a shared-service change should affect every consumer. https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/180176 is related sound-design context, not a duplicate canonical. The remaining uncertainty is the audible repeat policy and its timing thresholds, not whether a configuration switch can disable the cue.
- **Open decisions:**
  - Should rapid repeated inline-suggestion indications be suppressed, or remain audible using a quieter or alternate sound?
  - What repetition window and threshold should apply, and which events should reset it so fresh suggestions receive useful feedback?

## Evidence

- **accepted direction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/153844#issuecomment-1172200934 records the maintainer's intention to investigate and seek a fix. The issue is in Backlog, so low upvotes or age do not justify declining accepted work.
- **current inline-suggestion path:** src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/controller/inlineCompletionsController.ts observes currentInlineCompletionBySemanticId, cancels superseded work, waits 50 ms, and calls playSignal(AccessibilitySignal.inlineSuggestion) for ghost text. This deduplicates unchanged semantic identities and closely spaced updates, but does not impose a typing-window playback budget or select quieter repeat sounds.
- **semantic identity:** src/vs/editor/contrib/inlineCompletions/browser/model/inlineSuggestionItem.ts defines semanticId as a hash of the suggestion action, including its replacement text and range. Different suggestion actions can therefore trigger fresh audio while the user remains on the same line.
- **existing controls are partial coverage:** src/vs/workbench/contrib/accessibility/browser/accessibilityConfiguration.ts registers accessibility.signals.lineHasInlineSuggestion.sound and accessibility.signalOptions.volume. Disabling the cue or lowering all signal volumes does not provide adaptive repetition control. AccessibilitySignalService.playSound in src/vs/platform/accessibilitySignal/browser/accessibilitySignalService.ts prevents overlapping playback of the same sound, but permits another playback after it finishes.
- **debounce precedent has a different call path:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/273731 is closed; its maintainer comment at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/273731#issuecomment-3469768167 recommends accessibility.signalOptions.debouncePositionChanges for repeated diagnostic/navigation cues. src/vs/workbench/contrib/accessibilitySignals/browser/editorTextPropertySignalsContribution.ts uses getDelayMs for those cues, whereas the inline-completions controller calls playSignal directly without consulting that delay. This precedent does not establish coverage of the reported typing scenario.
