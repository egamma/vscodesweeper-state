# Issue #52131 — cursor up/down is confused by selection

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52131](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52131)

## Summary

The current behavior is intentional and confirmed by a team member: when a selection exists, down/right move relative to the selection end, and up/left relative to the selection start. The request for an option to change this has zero upvotes over 8 years, and the behavior matches SublimeText and other editors.

## Change summary

When shift-selecting leftward then pressing down without shift, the cursor jumps to the selection end's column rather than the selection start's column.

## Best solution

Close as designed — the cursor-relative-to-selection-end behavior is intentional (confirmed by alexdima in https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/52131#issuecomment-405108456), consistent with SublimeText, and codified in `cursorMoveOperations.ts` lines 207–210. The request for an option has had no community traction in 8 years.
