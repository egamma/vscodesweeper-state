[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349914](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/349914)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still allows a secondary profile's effective sandbox settings to overwrite shared host configuration. The assigned 1.149.0 milestone and security-sensitive sandbox isolation concern independently prohibit a cleanup close.

## Change summary

Prevent cross-window sandbox configuration clobbering and make effective sandbox changes attributable in diagnostics.

## Best solution

Ensure connecting or switching a secondary profile cannot silently change other windows' sandbox behavior, while preserving managed restrictions. Add appropriately scoped diagnostics for effective flags and change provenance without logging credentials or raw enterprise rules.
