# Issue #340163 — Feature: separate Autopilot settings to require approval for plans and structured questions

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340163](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/340163)

## Summary

Keep open: current source still couples Autopilot to skipping structured questions, and the existing settings do not provide the two independent checkpoints requested. The older plan-approval report covers only part of this request, not a canonical duplicate.

## Change summary

Allow users to independently require human plan approval and structured-question answers while retaining Autopilot's other automation.

## Best solution

Provide independently controllable plan-review and question-answering checkpoints without changing unrelated tool approvals or autonomous continuation. Agree on their defaults and supported session scope before implementation, coordinating the plan behavior with https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/339675.
