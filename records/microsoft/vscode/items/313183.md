## Summary

The reported session and worktree loss is potentially severe, but the exact update-time failure path is unconfirmed and the relevant persistence architecture has changed substantially since 1.119. Current reproduction details and logs are needed before deciding whether this remains actionable.

## Change summary

Preserve forked agent sessions and their worktrees across application updates, retaining recoverable session metadata when restoration fails.

## Best solution

A failed post-update restore must keep the durable session and chat catalog intact, avoid destructive worktree cleanup, and expose a retryable recovery state. First confirm the behavior on current Insiders and identify whether the failure is in session enumeration, peer-chat restoration, or worktree recovery.
