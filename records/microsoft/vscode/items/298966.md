# Issue #298966 — Chat history not visible in Copilot Chat panel after restart (Remote-WSL, v1.109)

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298966](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298966)

## Summary

This matches the confirmed session-list loading defect tracked by https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/issues/298507 and was fixed on main by resolving chat-session providers independently, preventing a slow or hanging provider from hiding otherwise available history.

## Change summary

Persisted Copilot chats briefly appeared during reload and then disappeared from the Chat panel, although they remained accessible through the Agents session window.

## Best solution

Keep the provider-isolated session resolution implemented in commit 2d27541e2d69ab9d5dbaaab51db99472f06bc01d so one provider cannot block or remove sessions supplied by another provider.
