# Issue #1559 — client.needsStop() and client.stop() disagree on whether it's valid to call stop()

[https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-languageserver-node/issues/1559](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-languageserver-node/issues/1559)

## Summary

The mismatch remains on current main: needsStop() returns true during startup, but stop() rejects in that same state. Keep this accepted bug open; the discussion also identifies unresolved requirements for stopping externally managed servers cleanly.

## Change summary

Make needsStop() and stop() consistent and provide reliable cleanup when shutdown is requested before initialization completes.

## Best solution

Establish a consistent startup/shutdown contract in client/ that addresses early shutdown without leaving registrations behind. Decide how that contract handles an unresponsive initialization and externally owned processes, preserving existing graceful shutdown and timeout behavior.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `client/src/common/client.ts`, `client/src/node/main.ts`, `client-node-tests/src/integration.test.ts`, `client-node-tests/src/servers/startStopServer.ts`
- **Validation:** Extend the client-node-tests lifecycle integration suite with controlled delayed initialization: exercise the needsStop()-guarded stop() sequence, verify the agreed shutdown outcome, and verify that late initialization cannot leave commands/providers registered after cleanup. Run npm test --prefix client-node-tests in a writable implementation checkout, retaining the existing shutdown-crash, shutdown-timeout and restart checks.
- **Behavior:**

  > A client currently reports that it needs stopping while initialization is pending, yet immediately rejects stop() in that state. The reporter needs to dispose and recreate clients safely, including clients whose processes are managed by the extension. The finished contract must make the predicate and operation consistent and define what shutdown during pending or unresponsive initialization does. Acceptance should cover delayed initialization, cleanup of registrations, and successful recreation without duplicate-command errors, while preserving normal graceful shutdown and timeout behavior.
- **Trace:**

  > In client/src/common/client.ts, start() enters Starting before asynchronous connection creation and initialization; needsStop() accepts Starting, but activeConnection() and shutdown() require Running. Consequently stop() rejects before cleanUp(), and dispose() follows the same path. client/src/node/main.ts restart() also calls stop(), while process termination only applies to the tracked _serverProcess. The reporter supplies custom stream transports and owns the spawned process, as documented at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-languageserver-node/issues/725#issuecomment-2383255325. The maintainer moved that related cleanup discussion into this issue at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-languageserver-node/issues/725#issuecomment-2385150759. client-node-tests/src/integration.test.ts explicitly tests rejection of stop() immediately after start(), so startup shutdown semantics remain the public-contract uncertainty. Preserve normal initialized-client shutdown, timeout reporting and restart behavior; the separate connection-close notification issue at https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-languageserver-node/issues/1608 does not own this startup mismatch.
- **Open decisions:**
  - When shutdown is requested during Starting, should the existing stop() operation support bounded teardown, or should stop() retain its initialized-client restriction with a separate supported early-teardown operation and a predicate that accurately reflects that distinction?
  - For externally managed servers, should the supported teardown contract accept a termination callback, and what completion guarantee should it provide when initialization never finishes?
