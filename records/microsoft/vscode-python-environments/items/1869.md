## Evidence

- **Fallback defect confirmed in source:** In the checkout and live main, src/features/terminal/runInTerminal.ts constructs fallback text with quoteArgs([executable, ...allArgs]).join(' ') and passes it to terminal.sendText. src/features/execution/execUtils.ts:quoteStringIfNecessary wraps cost$AUDIT_UNSET.py in double quotes without escaping the dollar sign. Zsh therefore expands AUDIT_UNSET instead of preserving the literal filename, explaining the reported failure when that variable is unset.
- **Separate integration ownership:** src/features/terminal/runInTerminal.ts delegates the integration branch to terminal.shellIntegration.executeCommand(executable, allArgs), not quoteArgs. Its deferred resolves only upon a matching onDidEndTerminalShellExecution event, with no timeout or cancellation path. This establishes the waiting mechanism, but does not independently prove why the apostrophe case fails to produce completion.
- **Concrete development-host reproduction:** https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-python-environments/issues/1869 supplies macOS arm64/Zsh steps, the required python.useEnvironmentsExtension routing setting, disabled auto-activation, fresh terminals, and an integration-on/off filename matrix. The probe checks __file__ and sys.prefix; dollar-sign execution fails in both branches, while the apostrophe case exceeds 15 seconds only with integration enabled.
