# Issue #6 — Slugify leaves a trailing hyphen and drops accented letters

[https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/6](https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/6)

## Summary

The defect is directly reproducible from current source: slugify() converts punctuation runs to hyphens without trimming them and removes accented letters before they can be folded. The issue is actionable, correctly labeled, and not owned by another open issue.

## Change summary

Slugify Current Line should fold accented letters and omit leading or trailing hyphens from the generated slug.

## Best solution

Update slugify() in src/stats.ts to normalize and fold diacritics before replacing unsupported characters, then trim leading and trailing hyphens. Add test/stats.test.ts cases for `## Café menu, part 2!` and punctuation at both ends.

## Evidence

- **source reproduction:** src/stats.ts slugify() lowercases the heading and applies `.replace(/[^a-z0-9]+/g, '-')` without Unicode normalization or trimming. For `## Café menu, part 2!`, this necessarily drops `é` and leaves the replacement hyphen produced from the final punctuation.
- **command wiring:** src/extension.ts registers `noteStats.slugifyHeading` and replaces the current line directly with `slugify(line.text)`, so the helper's incorrect result reaches the document unchanged.
- **test gap:** test/stats.test.ts covers only lowercasing and removal of a markdown heading marker; it has no coverage for accented characters or boundary punctuation.
