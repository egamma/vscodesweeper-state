# Issue #8 — Show estimated reading time next to the word count

[https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/8](https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/8)

## Summary

Keep this as the canonical reading-time request. Current main has no reading-time capability, the project explicitly lists reading time as in scope, and the newer, thinner https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/9 corroborates demand without superseding this better-described issue.

## Change summary

Show an estimated reading time alongside the document's word count, with suitable placement and formatting for short documents.

## Best solution

Retain this issue as the canonical request and decide the words-per-minute assumption, short-document representation, and whether the estimate belongs in the status text, tooltip, or behind a setting. The estimate's pure calculation and formatting belong in src/stats.ts, with status-bar wiring in src/extension.ts and unit coverage in test/stats.test.ts; package.json is involved only if a setting is selected.

## Plan brief

- **Likely files:** `src/stats.ts`, `src/extension.ts`, `test/stats.test.ts`, `package.json`
- **Validation:** Run `npm test` with unit cases covering the chosen words-per-minute rate, zero and sub-minute documents, minute-boundary rounding, and status formatting; then verify the selected status-bar or tooltip presentation in an Extension Development Host.
- **Behavior:**

  > The status-bar experience should expose an estimated reading duration derived from the current document's word count. The result must follow a documented words-per-minute rate and have unambiguous output for empty, sub-minute, and minute-boundary inputs. Its placement and configurability must preserve the project's glanceable single-item design.
- **Trace:**

  > Current document text flows through `refresh()` in src/extension.ts to `computeStats()` and `formatStats()` in src/stats.ts, which currently model and render only words and characters. package.json contains no reading-time setting or command, so the existing surface does not satisfy the request. Pure estimation and formatting remain within the src/stats.ts/test/stats.test.ts boundary, while editor and status-item integration remains in src/extension.ts. The uncertainty begins at product presentation: the issue deliberately leaves the rate, short-duration notation, placement, and optional setting unresolved. https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/9 asks for the same outcome but is newer and less detailed, making this issue the canonical request rather than a duplicate.
- **Open decisions:**
  - What words-per-minute rate should define the estimate, and should it be fixed or configurable?
  - Should reading time appear in the status-bar text, only in the tooltip, or be controlled by a setting?
  - How should empty and sub-minute documents be represented, and what rounding rule should apply at minute boundaries?
