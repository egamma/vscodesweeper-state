# Issue #15 — Word count counts numbers as words

[https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/15](https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/15)

## Summary

The reported count is intentional and explicitly documented: Note Stats treats every whitespace-separated token, including numbers and standalone punctuation, as a word. Excluding those tokens would replace the documented simple counting model with a different definition of a word.

## Change summary

Change the word-count algorithm to exclude numeric values, dates, and standalone punctuation.

## Best solution

Keep the documented whitespace-based counting behavior, including the five-word result for `3 apples and 2 pears`, rather than introducing linguistic token classification.
