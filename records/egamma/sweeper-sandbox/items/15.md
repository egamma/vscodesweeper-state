# Issue #15 — Word count counts numbers as words

[https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/15](https://github.com/egamma/sweeper-sandbox/issues/15)

## Summary

The reported count is intentional and explicitly documented: Note Stats uses a whitespace-delimited, `wc -w`-style definition in which numbers, dates, and standalone punctuation count as words.

## Change summary

Change the word-counting rules to exclude numeric values, dates, and standalone punctuation.

## Best solution

Keep the documented whitespace-based counting behavior, which is predictable and consistent with the project's deliberately simple statistics model.

## Vision fit

- **Fit:** rejected
- **Reason:** Semantic filtering of tokens conflicts with the project's guardrail that word counting remain deliberately simple rather than judging what qualifies as prose.
- **Evidence:** The README's "Behavior notes" state that anything separated by whitespace counts as a word, including numbers and standalone punctuation, and give `3 apples and 2 pears` as an explicit five-word example.
