Independent verification — the 2nd review

A blinded second review re-judges duplicate and implemented-on-main close proposals before anyone acts (the dashboard's "2nd review" badge). "Disputed" is a dispute to read, not a proven error — the blinded judge sees less context than the original review. Coverage counts open proposals (the verify sweep's selection universe); stale stamps (the record was re-reviewed since verification) are excluded from the mix, and live issue drift is re-checked by the nightly verify sweep, not this page. Scope is these two close reasons only.

Reversals — reopened after a close

A reversal of a sweeper-comment close is the strongest false-positive signal the pipeline produces — worth a manual look (and a feedback issue) each. A reopen isn't automatically a wrong verdict: issues also reopen to track follow-up work or get re-closed differently.
